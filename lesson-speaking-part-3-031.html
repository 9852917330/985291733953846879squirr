Dance

Popular dances and attitudes to dancing

Part 3

Prompt

What dances are popular in your country? What is the general attitude towards dancing in your country? Do young people dance any differently from 10 years ago?

What dances are popular in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Traditional dances appear at weddings and festivals, while younger people often learn international styles such as K-pop online. Adults also attend dance exercise classes like Zumba. Traditional forms seem less secure because fewer young people learn them fully. Some now survive mainly through festival performances rather than being part of ordinary community life.

Key words and phrases

Traditional forms
Styles passed down through a culture over time.

Advanced Model Answer

There's quite a range, and it splits fairly clearly by age. Traditional dances still appear at festivals and weddings, and older people take them seriously as part of cultural heritage. Among young people, styles picked up from social media dominate — K-pop choreography especially, which spread incredibly fast because anyone can learn it from a video. Then there's dance purely as exercise: Zumba and similar classes are popular with adults who'd never call themselves dancers at all. The traditional forms are the ones losing ground, though — fewer young people are learning them properly, and a couple are kept alive mainly by festival performances rather than everyday practice.

Key words and phrases

part of cultural heritage
something belonging to a community's inherited traditions
festival performances
music, dance, or other shows at a festival

What is the general attitude towards dancing in your country?

Simple Model Answer

People seem more relaxed about dancing now, particularly in cities. They see it as entertainment or exercise, and classes are common. Some older people still feel embarrassed dancing in public. Younger people are often more comfortable because online dance videos have made it familiar, although smaller towns may remain more reserved.

Key words and phrases

reserved
Not showing feelings or behaviour very openly.

Advanced Model Answer

It's become noticeably more relaxed. Most people now see dancing as either entertainment or a form of exercise, and dance classes are common in cities. There's still some shyness about dancing in public, especially among men of my parents' generation, who'd feel embarrassed doing it in front of others. Among younger people that's largely disappeared, partly because posting dance videos online has made being seen doing it completely normal. That relaxed attitude is really a city thing, though; in smaller towns the old self-consciousness hasn't shifted nearly as much.

Key words and phrases

embarrassed doing it in front of others
feeling uncomfortable performing it while people watch
the old self-consciousness
the earlier worry about how others would judge them

Do young people dance any differently from 10 years ago?

Simple Model Answer

Yes. Young people now learn styles from around the world through online videos, rather than mainly from local teachers. Many routines are also designed for short recordings, so movements are brief and repeated. This can encourage creativity, but it may weaken the connection to local dance traditions. I think there are gains and losses.

Key words and phrases

routines
Planned sequences of movements in a dance.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, in two ways. First, they learn from global platforms rather than from family or local teachers, so the styles are far more international and they mix influences freely. Second, a lot of dancing's now done for the camera rather than for a room of people, which changes the movements — routines are shorter, more repetitive, and designed to look good in a fifteen-second clip. Something's gained in creativity, but the link to local tradition has weakened, and that's a genuine loss, not just a stylistic shift.

Key words and phrases

mix influences freely
combine different styles without strict rules
the link to local tradition
the connection with traditions from the area

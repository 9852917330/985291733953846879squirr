A time you travelled by public transport

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time you travelled by public transport.

You should say: - what type of public transport you used - where you travelled from and to - what you did during the journey - and explain how you felt about travelling in this way

Simple Model Answer

A memorable public transport journey was a train trip from Manchester to London last year. It was a Sunday morning, and I expected a quiet journey. I hoped to do some work because I thought the train would be fairly empty.

My first surprise was discovering that my ticket did not include a reserved seat. Then I heard singing in the station and realised many football supporters were travelling to a match. Luckily, I found a seat before the train filled up.

The fans talked, laughed and sang throughout the journey. I could not concentrate on work or even read my newspaper properly. Eventually, I gave up, bought coffee and watched what was happening around me. Although it was much noisier than expected, the people were friendly and in a good mood. I still had a comfortable seat, so I could relax for a couple of hours. I would have preferred more peace, but it was not a bad experience. However, I did choose a quieter option for my journey home.

Key words and phrases

reserved seat
A seat kept for a particular passenger in advance.
in a good mood
Feeling happy and pleasant.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe a train journey that I took last year between Manchester and London. I've made the same journey loads of times, but I'll never forget this particular one, because something happened that I really wasn't expecting. It was a Sunday morning and I was due in London that afternoon. There was no traffic at all as I drove into Manchester, and I was expecting a nice, peaceful trip on a half-empty train.

But my first surprise came when I tried to print my prepaid ticket at one of the machines: there wasn't a reserved seat number on it, which meant I'd have to find an available seat myself. I was still puzzling over this when I heard loud voices and singing coming from the main station concourse. Suddenly I realised what was going on - I was about to be on a train full of football fans travelling to a big match! Luckily, I managed to find a seat before the train completely filled up.

However, the noisy fans made sure I couldn't concentrate on any work - I couldn't even read the newspaper without getting interrupted by laughter, singing and football stories. In the end, I just sat back with a cup of coffee and watched the excited fans instead. All in all, it wasn't a bad journey; I love travelling by train, and at least I had a comfortable seat so I could sit back and relax for a couple of hours. I'd have preferred a quieter trip, but the fans were all friendly and good-natured, so I can't really complain.

Even so, I made sure I avoided them on the way home later that day!

Key words and phrases

a train journey
A trip using rail transport.
half-empty train
A train with many empty seats.
football fans
People who enjoy or support football.
sit back and relax
Rest comfortably without doing anything demanding.
friendly and good-natured
Kind and pleasant towards others.

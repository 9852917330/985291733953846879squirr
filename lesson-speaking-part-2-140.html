Prompt

Describe a recent happy event

You should say: what it was when and where it happened who is involved And explain why it was happy.

Simple Model Answer

A recent happy event was my brother's wedding a couple of months ago. He is my only sibling, so the occasion was especially important to me. His wife is Catholic, and the day included both Catholic and traditional Vietnamese customs.

We got up early and accompanied him to the bride's house. There, he formally asked her parents' permission before the families continued to a restaurant for the celebration. The room had sparkling lights and purple ribbons, my sister-in-law's favourite colour.

During the ceremony, the couple exchanged promises, and I was moved by how happy they looked together. It was the first wedding where I had been closely involved instead of attending only as a guest. That made every part feel more personal. The whole day went smoothly, and people enjoyed the meal and reception. I returned home tired but very happy. I remember it not just as a beautiful wedding, but as the moment our family welcomed someone new and saw my brother begin an important new stage of life.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about my brother's wedding, which I went to a couple of months ago — it's probably one of the happiest events of my whole life, and I still remember the day pretty clearly. My brother, who's my only sibling, decided to get married last summer.

Since his wife is Catholic, the wedding ended up being a mix of Catholic and traditional Vietnamese customs. Because we were on the groom's side, my family and I had to get up really early to go with my brother to the bride's house, where he formally asked her parents for permission to take their daughter into our family. Once that was done, everyone headed off to a fancy restaurant for the ceremony itself.

I remember how beautifully the room was decorated, with strings of sparkling lights and purple ribbons everywhere, which happens to be my sister-in-law's favourite colour. During the ceremony we all watched the two of them exchanging vows, and I have to admit it was genuinely moving — it was the first wedding I'd ever really been a proper part of, rather than just a guest, so it felt like a once-in-a-lifetime experience for me too.

What struck me most was how obviously completely in love they were, just from the way they looked at each other while they were speaking. The whole day went smoothly from start to finish, and by the time the reception ended, I think every single guest had had a brilliant time.

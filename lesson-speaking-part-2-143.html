An important event that you celebrated

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an important event that you celebrated

You should say: - What it was - Where it happened - Why it was important - How people felt about it

Simple Model Answer

An important event I celebrated was my younger sister Linh's eighteenth birthday in 2015. It happened just after she passed her university entrance exam and received offers from three leading universities in Ho Chi Minh City.

I wanted to celebrate both achievements, so I arranged a small dinner at home with our parents and a few of her closest friends. During the afternoon, I cooked some of her favourite dishes, including bun cha and pho. I also arranged for a small band to play songs she liked, which made the evening feel different from a normal family meal.

When we brought out the cake, everyone sang and congratulated her. She blew out the candles, and we wished her well for university. Later, Linh said it was the best birthday she had ever had. That made me feel very pleased with the effort. I think the celebration brought our family closer together. It showed her that we were proud of her work and excited about the new opportunities ahead of her.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about the time I organised a birthday party for my younger sister, Linh. It was back in 2015, when she passed the university entrance exam with flying colours and got offers from three of the top universities in Ho Chi Minh City, which honestly came as a surprise to the whole family — we knew she was clever, but not that clever.

So, to mark her resounding success as well as her turning eighteen, I threw her a birthday party just a few days after the acceptance letters arrived. It wasn't a huge event, more of a family dinner at home that included just my parents and a handful of her closest friends, but I still wanted to make it one of the most memorable nights of her life.

I spent the whole afternoon in the kitchen cooking several of her favourite dishes, from bun cha to pho, and I also booked a small live band to play some of her favourite songs, which made the atmosphere feel far cosier than a normal family dinner. When the cake came out, everyone sang the birthday song together while she blew out the candles, and we all congratulated her and wished her a brilliant few years at university.

Afterwards, Linh told me it was the best birthday she'd ever had, and I could tell my parents were genuinely moved too — it definitely brought us closer together as a family. That's why, even now, I think of that evening as one of the happiest our family has shared.

Shopping

Shopping online and what you dislike about shopping

Part 1

Prompt

Do you like shopping and why? Do you like shopping on the internet? What don't you like about shopping? What time do you do the shopping and why?

Do you like shopping and why?

Simple Model Answer

I don't treat it as a hobby. It is more enjoyable when I know what I need and have time to compare products. Walking around shops without a purpose makes me tired.

Key words and phrases

compare products
Look at different items to see how they are similar or different.

Advanced Model Answer

I don't really see shopping as a hobby, but I enjoy it when I've got a clear purpose and enough time to compare things properly. I find aimless shopping tiring rather than relaxing.

Key words and phrases

a clear purpose
A well-defined aim.
aimless shopping
Browsing shops without knowing what you want.

Do you like shopping on the internet?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I buy everyday products online when they are easy to compare. It saves time and offers more choice. For clothes or things I need to examine closely, I still visit shops.

Key words and phrases

examine closely
Look at something carefully to check its details.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, for everyday products that are easy to compare online. It saves time and gives me more choice, although I still go to actual shops for clothes or anything that needs careful inspection.

Key words and phrases

careful inspection
A close check of condition or quality.

What don't you like about shopping?

Simple Model Answer

I dislike discounts that are not really good deals, salespeople who pressure me and long lines. I want time and clear information to choose what is right for me.

Key words and phrases

pressure me
Try strongly to make me do something.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm not keen on misleading discounts, pushy sales staff or long queues. I think a good shop should let you compare things properly and make an informed decision, without all the pressure.

Key words and phrases

make an informed decision
Choose after understanding the relevant facts.

What time do you do the shopping and why?

Simple Model Answer

I usually shop early in the morning or on weekday evenings because the shops are quieter. It is easier to find what I need and finish quickly then.

Key words and phrases

weekday evenings
The evenings from Monday to Friday.

Advanced Model Answer

I usually go shopping early in the morning or on weekday evenings, when it's quieter. Avoiding the busy times just makes the whole thing far more efficient.

Key words and phrases

far more efficient
Using much less time or effort.

Patience & Politeness

Patience, politeness and rude behaviour

Prompt

Are you a patient person? Is it important to be polite? What do you do when others are rude?

Are you a patient person?

Simple Model Answer

I'm usually patient when someone cannot control the situation. Repeated mistakes can annoy me, though. I try to stop and think before responding so I don't speak only out of frustration.

Key words and phrases

frustration
The annoyed feeling of being unable to do or change something.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd say I'm fairly patient, especially when something's out of someone's control. But I do get a bit frustrated when the same avoidable mistake keeps happening. I try to pause for a second before reacting, so I don't let frustration control my response.

Key words and phrases

out of someone's control
Something a person cannot change.
control my response
Choose how I react.

Is it important to be polite?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, being polite shows respect and helps prevent arguments. You can still disagree with someone, but you should explain your opinion without being hurtful or insulting them.

Key words and phrases

insulting
Speaking to someone in a rude or offensive way.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, definitely, because it stops unnecessary arguments and shows respect. Being polite doesn't mean you have to agree with everyone, though; it just means you can disagree without attacking the person.

Key words and phrases

attacking the person
Criticising someone personally rather than their idea.

What do you do when others are rude?

Simple Model Answer

I try to stay calm and explain what behaviour I will not accept. If the person continues being rude, I end the conversation before it becomes a bigger argument.

Key words and phrases

stay calm
Keep your feelings under control and avoid becoming angry.

Advanced Model Answer

I try to stay calm and set a clear boundary, without matching their tone. If it carries on, I'll just end the conversation rather than letting things escalate further.

Key words and phrases

set a clear boundary
State clearly what behaviour is acceptable.
escalate further
Become even more serious.

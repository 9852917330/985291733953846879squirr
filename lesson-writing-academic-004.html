Cities & Housing

House prices in five cities

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The diagrams provide some information about the housing prices of five cities around the world from 1990 to 2000. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The two bar charts compare changes in house prices in five cities during two periods: 1990–1995 and 1995–2000.

Overall, San Francisco and Beijing recorded increases in both periods, whereas Rome experienced a decline in each. Hong Kong and Sydney moved from positive changes in the first period to falls in the second.

Between 1990 and 1995, Hong Kong had the largest rise, at 5%. Beijing followed with an increase of 4%, while prices rose by 3% in Sydney and 2% in San Francisco. Rome was the only city to record a fall, with a figure of minus 3.5%.

The pattern changed considerably in the later period. Beijing recorded the strongest increase, at 5.8%, compared with 4% previously. San Francisco's figure also rose, from 2% to 3%. By contrast, Hong Kong registered a 6% decline and Sydney a 2% fall. Rome's figure remained unchanged at minus 3.5%.

Hong Kong therefore showed the largest difference between the two reported changes, moving from a 5% increase to a 6% decrease, a swing of 11 percentage points. Rome was the only city with negative figures in both periods.

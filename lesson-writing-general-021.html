Dog care while on holiday

Informal

Prompt

You are going on holiday next week and you need to ask your friend to watch your dog while you are away. Write a letter to your friend. In your letter, - state how long you will be away - tell them what needs to be done - state how you will compensate them

Model answer

Hi Daniel,

I hope you're well. I'm writing to ask whether you could look after my dog, Milo, while I'm away in Da Nang from 12 to 19 July. You know him well, so I think staying with you would be far less stressful for him than going to a kennel.

His routine is simple. He needs a thirty-minute walk every morning and evening, fresh water throughout the day, and one cup of dry food at 7 a.m. and another at 6 p.m. I've left his food, lead and vet's number in a labelled box by the front door. Please also give him his allergy tablet every second evening.

I know this is a significant favour, and I don't want you to be out of pocket. I'll cover all expenses and take you out for dinner when I return. I'd also be happy to help with your house move next month.

Thanks for considering it. Please let me know whether this arrangement would work for you.

Best, Alex

Key words and phrases

a significant favour
A big act of help.
out of pocket
Having spent money without repayment.
170 words · minimum 150

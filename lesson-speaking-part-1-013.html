Colours

Your favourite colour, then and now

Part 1

Prompt

What is your favourite colour and why? Do you like the same colours now as you did when you were younger and why? What can you learn about people from the colours they like? Do colours have special meaning in your culture?

What is your favourite colour and why?

Simple Model Answer

My favourite colour is navy blue because it looks calm and smart. It matches many other colours, and it also makes me think of the sea and wide, open places.

Key words and phrases

matches
Looks good together with something else.

Advanced Model Answer

Navy blue is my favourite colour because it looks calm and professional, and it's easy to combine with other colours. I also associate it with the sea and open space.

Key words and phrases

easy to combine with
Goes well with other things.

Do you like the same colours now as you did when you were younger and why?

Simple Model Answer

Not exactly. I liked bright red and orange as a child. Now I prefer blue, grey and dark green because I enjoy quieter, less noticeable colours.

Key words and phrases

less noticeable
Less easy to see or attract attention.

Advanced Model Answer

Not entirely. As a child I preferred bright red and orange, but I now go for blue, grey and dark green. My taste has become more understated as I've grown older.

Key words and phrases

more understated
Less showy or attention-seeking.

What can you learn about people from the colours they like?

Simple Model Answer

Colours may show whether someone likes a bright or quiet style, but they do not tell us much about personality. Culture, fashion and practical reasons also affect what people choose.

Key words and phrases

practical reasons
Reasons connected with usefulness or everyday needs.

Advanced Model Answer

Colour preferences might give a small clue about whether someone likes bold or quieter styles, but I wouldn't read too much into it. Culture, fashion and practical needs can influence choices just as much as personality, so it's not a reliable test.

Key words and phrases

not a reliable test
Not a dependable way to judge.

Do colours have special meaning in your culture?

Simple Model Answer

Yes. In my culture, red is often used for luck and celebrations, while white can be connected with death and sadness. The same colour can mean different things in different cultures.

Key words and phrases

connected with
Related to something.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, definitely. Red's often linked to luck and celebrations, while white can be linked to mourning, so a colour's meaning really can depend on the culture.

Key words and phrases

depend on the culture
Change according to cultural expectations.

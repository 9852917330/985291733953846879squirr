Situation

An experience where you were late for an event

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when you were late for an event.

You should say: - When did this happen. - Why were you late. - What consequence have you caused. - How you felt afterwards.

Simple Model Answer

An experience of being late was missing a job interview a couple of months ago. I had applied online for a part-time role that matched my interests, and the company invited me to an interview at nine the next morning.

I was excited and prepared carefully that evening. I chose and ironed my clothes, packed a notebook and pen, and set an alarm for seven. I expected to have plenty of time for breakfast and travel. Unfortunately, I forgot to charge my phone, and its battery died overnight.

The alarm did not ring. When I woke, it was already nine, exactly when the interview should have begun. I panicked and decided not to go. I considered calling to ask for another appointment, but I felt too embarrassed and let the opportunity pass. Looking back, I should have contacted them instead of disappearing. I lost a job chance because of one small preparation mistake and my poor response to it. Since then, I have checked both my alarm and my phone battery before an important early appointment.

Advanced Model Answer

I like to think I'm an organised person and that tardiness is not my problem - but I'd be lying if I said I'm never late.

As a matter of fact, there was one time I was late for a job interview. It was just a couple of months ago, when I was looking for a part-time job to gain experience in my field. There was a position at a company whose job description suited me perfectly, and I really wanted it. I filled out the application online the moment I heard about the vacancy, and to my surprise, they called me for an interview the very next day, at 9 a.m.

I was thrilled, obviously, and I spent the evening before getting everything ready, picking out what to wear, ironing my clothes to leave a good impression, even packing a notebook and pen in case I needed to take notes. I set my alarm for 7 a.m. so I wouldn't have to rush breakfast before heading over.

The problem was, I'd forgotten to charge my phone, and it died overnight, so the alarm never went off. I woke up in a panic at 9 a.m., exactly when the interview was supposed to start. I decided I'd rather just skip it than turn up looking like a disorganised latecomer. I did think about calling to ask if they'd reschedule, but I couldn't come up with a decent excuse, so I just let the whole thing go. In the end, I missed out on a job I'd genuinely wanted, and I felt pretty ashamed of myself for such a silly mistake. Still, I learned from it - I'm much more careful about that sort of thing now.

Key words and phrases

an organised person
someone who plans and arranges tasks well
tardiness is not my problem
I do not tend to arrive late
As a matter of fact
in fact
gain experience in
learn practical skills and knowledge in
leave a good impression
Make others remember you positively.

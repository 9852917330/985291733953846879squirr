Health & Fitness

Your health and health concerns in your country

Part 1

Prompt

Is your health important to you? What do you do to improve your health? What is a major health concern in your country? What can be done about it?

Is your health important to you?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, very important. Good health helps me feel energetic, focus and do things without help. I think it is better to look after it before problems appear.

Key words and phrases

energetic
Having plenty of energy to do things.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, because health affects energy, concentration and independence. It's easy to take it for granted until a problem appears, so prevention is a worthwhile investment.

Key words and phrases

take it for granted
Assume it will always be available.
a worthwhile investment
Time or money well spent.

What do you do to improve your health?

Simple Model Answer

I exercise several times a week, keep regular sleeping habits and try to eat mostly fresh, simple food. These small habits work together to help me feel healthier.

Key words and phrases

sleeping habits
Your usual patterns of going to sleep and resting.

Advanced Model Answer

I exercise several times a week, sleep consistently and try to eat mostly unprocessed food. None of these habits is dramatic, but together they make a noticeable difference.

Key words and phrases

make a noticeable difference
Cause a change people can clearly see.

What is a major health concern in your country?

Simple Model Answer

I think obesity, diabetes and stress are important concerns linked to people's lifestyles. These problems can develop slowly, so people need clear information and health checks before things get worse.

Key words and phrases

health checks
Examinations to find out whether someone has a health problem.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd say lifestyle-related conditions such as obesity, diabetes and stress are growing concerns. They develop gradually, which means public education and early screening should play a central role.

Key words and phrases

play a central role
Be very important to something.

What can be done about it?

Simple Model Answer

Governments can provide better health services, safe places to exercise and advice about food. People also need healthy habits they can keep. I think both sides have a part to play.

Key words and phrases

a part to play
A role or responsibility in something.

Advanced Model Answer

Governments can improve preventive care, safe public spaces and nutrition education, while individuals need realistic habits they can maintain. In the end, I think it's a shared responsibility.

Key words and phrases

a shared responsibility
A duty divided among several people.

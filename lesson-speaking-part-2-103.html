Object & Favourite

An interesting advertisement

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an interesting advertisement

You should say: Where you saw it What it's about Why you think it was an interesting advertisement

OUTLINE

>>> Air New Zealand safety advert
airline safety advert by Air New Zealand, seen on a flight a few months ago
filmed like action trailer, flight attendants as adventurers, still explains safety rules

interesting bc turned a legally-required boring briefing into entertainment people watch

Simple Model Answer

An interesting advertisement I saw was an Air New Zealand safety video. I watched it on the seat screen before a flight a few months ago. Instead of a normal safety demonstration, it looked like a short adventure film.

There was dramatic music, quick changes between scenes and flight attendants moving through a jungle setting. At the same time, they explained seatbelts, oxygen masks and other safety information. It lasted about four minutes, and I watched the whole thing. I do not usually pay such close attention to these videos.

I found it clever because it made a familiar message interesting. Passengers have often heard the instructions before, so it can be difficult to keep their attention. This video used entertainment to help them listen. It also changed my impression of the airline. I felt the company had put effort into doing an ordinary task creatively. For me, an effective advertisement does more than repeat a name. It gives people a clear reason to remember the company afterwards.

Key words and phrases

flight attendants
Staff who help and look after passengers on a plane.
keep their attention
Continue making people interested enough to watch or listen.

Advanced Model Answer

I want to talk about an airline safety advert I saw on a flight a few months ago, made by Air New Zealand.

Instead of the usual, quite boring safety demonstration, this one was filmed like a proper action film trailer, with dramatic music, quick cuts between scenes, and the flight attendants dressed as adventurers running through jungle sets while still somehow explaining seatbelts and oxygen masks. It was about four minutes long, and I watched the entire thing, which never normally happens with safety videos.

I saw it on the plane itself, right before take-off, on the little screen on the seat in front of me.

I found it interesting mainly because of how well it solved a genuinely hard problem: nobody wants to watch a safety briefing, but airlines are legally required to show one on every single flight. Instead of fighting that, they turned a boring requirement into entertainment, and by doing that they probably got far more passengers to actually pay attention to safety information that could matter in an emergency.

It also made me look at the airline differently. Before that flight I'd never really thought about Air New Zealand's brand at all, but the advert left me with the impression that they're a company willing to take a creative risk rather than just doing the bare minimum the regulations require.

I think that's what separates a genuinely interesting advert from a forgettable one — it has to make you feel something about the brand, not just inform you.

Key words and phrases

quick cuts between scenes
fast changes from one filmed scene to another
turned a boring requirement into entertainment
made a dull obligation enjoyable to watch
pay attention to
Notice and think carefully about.
take a creative risk
try an unusual artistic idea even if it might fail
the bare minimum
only the smallest amount of effort needed
make you feel something
cause an emotional reaction in you

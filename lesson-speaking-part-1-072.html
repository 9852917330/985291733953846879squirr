Sports

Sports played in your country and local facilities

Part 1

Prompt

What sports do you like? What sports are played in your country? What are the sporting facilities like in your hometown? What is your favourite sport?

What sports do you like?

Simple Model Answer

I like badminton and swimming because many people can try them, but learning to do them well takes skill. I also lift weights, although I think of that more as exercise.

Key words and phrases

takes skill
Requires ability developed through practice.

Advanced Model Answer

I like badminton and swimming because they're both accessible and technically demanding. I also do strength training, although I see that more as structured exercise than a competitive sport.

Key words and phrases

accessible and technically demanding
Easy to start but requiring good skills.
competitive sport
Sport in which people try to win.

What sports are played in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Football, badminton, volleyball and running are common. Football has the most spectators, while badminton is easy to play in schools, parks and local spaces, so many people can join in.

Key words and phrases

spectators
People who watch a sport or event.

Advanced Model Answer

Football, badminton, volleyball and running are all common. Football attracts the largest audiences, while badminton's easy to play in schools, parks and community spaces, making it open to almost anyone.

Key words and phrases

open to almost anyone
Available to nearly everyone.

What are the sporting facilities like in your hometown?

Simple Model Answer

There are more gyms and sports centres than before, so facilities have improved. However, public places to exercise are not equally available everywhere, and some options are still too expensive for people.

Key words and phrases

facilities
Places and equipment provided for a particular activity.

Advanced Model Answer

Facilities have improved, with more private gyms and sports centres than before. Public spaces are still unevenly distributed, so affordable access remains a practical concern.

Key words and phrases

unevenly distributed
Not shared equally among people or places.
a practical concern
A real issue affecting what can be done.

What is your favourite sport?

Simple Model Answer

Badminton is probably my favourite. It is fast and fun to play with others, and you don't need costly equipment. Good timing and skill matter, not just being big or strong.

Key words and phrases

equipment
The things needed to do a particular activity.

Advanced Model Answer

Badminton's probably my favourite sport because it's fast, social and easy to play without expensive equipment. It also rewards timing and technique rather than physical size alone.

Key words and phrases

rewards timing and technique
Success depends on when and how you act.

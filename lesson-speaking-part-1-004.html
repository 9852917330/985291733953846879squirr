#004
Books

Reading in English and where you read

Part 1

Prompt

What kinds of things do you enjoy reading in English? Are you someone who can read anywhere, or do you have a favourite place? Do you need peace and quiet to read, or can you concentrate in noisy places? How did you first learn to read?

Model answer

What kinds of things do you enjoy reading in English?

Answer 1

I mostly read non-fiction in English — psychology, technology, that kind of thing, and books on personal development. They're great for my vocabulary, and they give me ideas I can apply in real life.

Are you someone who can read anywhere, or do you have a favourite place?

Answer 2

I can read almost anywhere, but I concentrate best at a quiet desk by a window. That's what helps me stay fully absorbed, without checking my phone every few minutes.

Do you need peace and quiet to read, or can you concentrate in noisy places?

Answer 3

If it's something difficult, I need a fairly quiet environment. I can skim an article in a cafe, but serious reading needs enough silence for me to process the ideas properly.

How did you first learn to read?

Answer 4

I first learned by sounding out short words with my parents, then reading picture books at school. Once I found books I genuinely enjoyed, reading didn't feel like homework any more — it just became a habit of my own.

Key phrase meanings

personal development
Improving your abilities and character.
apply in real life
Use in actual situations.
stay fully absorbed
Keep giving something your full attention.
process the ideas properly
Think about and understand the ideas.
a habit of my own
Something I regularly choose to do.

Object & Favourite

A piece of furniture that you own

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a piece of furniture that you own.

You should say: - what it is - where you bought it - how you use it - and explain why you like it.

Simple Model Answer

A piece of furniture I own is a large leather armchair in my living room. It stands beside the window and is my favourite place to relax. A friend gave it to me when he moved house and decided to buy new furniture.

Getting it home was harder than I expected. It would not fit in my car, so I hired a small van. We then needed two people to carry it upstairs to my flat. The effort was worth it because the chair is extremely comfortable.

I use it for reading, watching television and sometimes working. Its wide arms can even support my laptop. The window lets in pleasant light during the afternoon, making it an especially nice place to sit. Last night, I fell asleep there while watching a film, which happens fairly often. I like the chair because it makes my home feel comfortable without costing me anything to buy. The only disadvantage is that once I sit down, I sometimes find it difficult to get up and do something useful.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about my favourite chair. It's a big, leather armchair that sits in my living room at home, just under my living room window. I didn't actually buy the armchair.

One of my friends was moving house and was going to buy some new furniture. I had always liked his leather armchair, so he said I could have it if I managed to move it. The chair wouldn't fit in my car, and it was tricky to get it out of my friend's apartment.

I had to hire a small van to take the armchair home, and it took two of us just to carry it up the stairs to my flat. Obviously I use the armchair for sitting! It's my favourite place in the house to relax, read a book, watch TV or even do some work; the chair's arms are quite wide and flat, so my laptop fits nicely on either of them.

Last night I fell asleep in my armchair while I was watching a film, which happens more often than I'd like to admit. The main reasons why I like this piece of furniture are that it's comfortable and it's in the perfect position in my living room, right by the window where the light comes in during the afternoon. The only drawback might be that I enjoy sitting in the armchair too much, and sometimes I think it makes me lazy!

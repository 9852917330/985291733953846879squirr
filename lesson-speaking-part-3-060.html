Leisure & Holidays

Why holidays matter, and why some people stay at home

Prompt

Do you think that it is important for people to go on holiday? Why do you think some people prefer not to go abroad on holiday?

Do you think that it is important for people to go on holiday?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, people need breaks to rest rather than work all year. A holiday can remove normal responsibilities and help us return with more energy; a trip to France did that for me. However, it does not have to mean travelling abroad. A quiet week at home without work messages can provide many of the same benefits for less money.

Key words and phrases

responsibilities
Duties or tasks you are expected to manage.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I think we all need to go on holiday at least once or twice a year. It isn't healthy to work all year round without some time off to relax; we all need to take a break and recharge our batteries from time to time. Last summer, for example, I went on holiday to France for a couple of weeks, and it was great to leave all of my usual responsibilities behind me — I came home feeling properly refreshed. It doesn't have to mean going abroad, though; a quiet week at home away from work emails can do almost the same job for a fraction of the cost.

Key words and phrases

take a break
Stop briefly to rest.
recharge our batteries
rest and regain our energy
a quiet week at home
several peaceful days spent at home

Why do you think some people prefer not to go abroad on holiday?

Simple Model Answer

Cost is one reason, especially for families paying for travel and hotels. Some people also dislike unfamiliar languages or the stress of a foreign place. Others genuinely enjoy their local area and feel no need to leave. Environmental concerns can matter too, so staying home is not always only about money or comfort.

Key words and phrases

Environmental concerns
Worries about damage to nature or the climate.

Advanced Model Answer

Cost is usually the biggest factor — it's simply more expensive to fly somewhere and stay in a hotel than to holiday at home, especially for a family. There's also the stress of a foreign country where you don't speak the language and can't easily integrate with the locals, which some people would rather avoid altogether. And plenty genuinely just love where they live and don't feel any pull to leave it. That's not the full story for everyone, though — some people avoid flying specifically because of the environmental impact, which has very little to do with cost or comfort at all.

Key words and phrases

integrate with the locals
become part of the community of residents
the environmental impact
the effect something has on nature and pollution

Chocolate

Chocolate: what you eat and when

Prompt

Do you like chocolate? What type of chocolate do you prefer? When do you usually eat chocolate? Do people give chocolate as a present in your country?

Do you like chocolate?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I really like chocolate. I enjoy its taste and how it feels when I eat it. It also tastes good with coffee, which is my favourite drink.

Key words and phrases

tastes good with
Has a pleasant flavour when eaten or drunk with something else.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I love chocolate. I like the taste, the texture and the feeling that you get when you eat it. And it goes really well with my favourite drink, coffee.

Key words and phrases

goes really well with
Combines pleasantly with.

What type of chocolate do you prefer?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer dark chocolate because its flavour is stronger than milk chocolate. I also enjoy chocolate cake and hot chocolate, so I like it in different forms.

Key words and phrases

in different forms
In several different types or ways.

Advanced Model Answer

I prefer dark chocolate to milk chocolate, because it has a stronger taste. I also like chocolate when it's in a cake, and I love drinking hot chocolate too.

Key words and phrases

dark chocolate
Chocolate with much cocoa and little milk.
a stronger taste
A more intense flavour.

When do you usually eat chocolate?

Simple Model Answer

I try to limit how much chocolate I have. Still, I usually eat or drink something with chocolate in it about once every two days.

Key words and phrases

limit
Control something so there is not too much of it.

Advanced Model Answer

I try not to eat too much, but I probably eat or drink something that contains chocolate every couple of days.

Key words and phrases

every couple of days
About once every two days.

Do people give chocolate as a present in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, boxes of chocolates are common gifts in my country. People give them for birthdays and celebrations such as Valentine's Day, Easter or Christmas.

Key words and phrases

common gifts
Presents that people often give.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, it's very common to give boxes of individual chocolates as a gift. You can give chocolates on any special occasion really: birthdays, Valentine's Day, Easter, Christmas, and so on.

Key words and phrases

give boxes of individual chocolates
Present boxes of separate small chocolates.

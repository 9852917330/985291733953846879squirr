Environment

Prevent climate change or adapt to it?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

Some people say that instead of preventing climate change we should find a way to live with it. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

Adaptation has moved from a fringe idea to a central part of climate policy, and some now argue it should replace the attempt to cut emissions. I disagree with that substitution, although adaptation is necessary and currently underfunded.

The argument for adapting has a factual basis. A degree of warming is already unavoidable, because emissions released decades ago continue to act, so sea defences, drought-resistant crops and heat-proofed buildings are needed whatever happens next. Adaptation also has the advantage that a country benefits directly from its own spending, unlike emission cuts, whose benefit is shared with everyone including those who do nothing. Given how slowly international agreements progress, protecting yourself is at least within your own control.

The case against treating it as an alternative is decisive, however. Adaptation has limits that prevention does not: a wall can be built against a metre of sea level rise but not against ten, and no engineering protects a region whose rainfall has stopped. Costs also rise faster than the temperature, so each further degree makes adaptation less affordable, and the ability to pay is unequal, meaning a wealthy country builds defences while poorer ones absorb losses they did not cause. Some consequences cannot be adapted to at all, since a species that cannot move or a glacier feeding a river system does not respond to engineering.

The two are also not competing for the same money in the way the argument suggests. Preventing further warming reduces how much adaptation will eventually be required, so spending on emissions lowers the future adaptation bill rather than diverting funds from it.

In conclusion, adapting to changes already under way is unavoidable and deserves far more investment than it receives. Abandoning prevention would raise the eventual cost beyond anything adaptation could meet, so both must be pursued together.

Key words and phrases

absorb losses they did not cause
Bear damage caused by other people.
does not respond to engineering
Cannot be solved through technical construction alone.
lowers the future adaptation bill
Reduces later spending on coping with change.

A visitor in your home

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a visitor in your home

You should say: - Who this visitor was. - When he/she visited your home. - Why he/she came to visit. - Whether you liked him/her or not

Simple Model Answer

A memorable visitor to my home was Armane, a French business partner of my brother. They had worked together for more than two years after meeting at a yoga and meditation class. He visited us last week to say goodbye before returning to Paris.

Armane had large blue eyes and a strong-looking face. During dinner, I noticed that he understood many Vietnamese customs. He used chopsticks well and knew how to roll vegetables in rice paper. He even tried the strong shrimp paste because he did not want to seem impolite. Later, he admitted that learning our table manners had been difficult at first.

After the visit, he sent us a thank-you card that he had made by hand. My whole family was touched. We do not expect visitors from other countries to know all our traditions, but we appreciated his effort. I remember him as a friendly and thoughtful guest. His visit showed me how learning a little about another culture can make people feel respected.

Key words and phrases

table manners
Polite ways of behaving while eating with others.
made by hand
Created personally rather than produced by a machine.

Advanced Model Answer

Today I'd like to talk about one of the guests who visited my family and left a great impression on me. His name was Armane, a Frenchman and my brother's business partner for more than two years. I heard from my mother that my brother happened to meet him at a yoga and meditation class. He came to our house last week to say goodbye before returning to Paris.

Armane had a masculine look, with large blue eyes, a wide forehead and a sharp, straight nose. Although he's European, he seemed to have a genuinely deep understanding of Vietnamese culture — the way he used chopsticks and rolled vegetables in rice paper told me he was well aware of our table manners. I still remember him insisting on trying every single dish at dinner, even the strongest fermented shrimp paste, just so he wouldn't seem rude — you could tell he was struggling not to pull a face! He even admitted that at first he'd found it pretty stressful trying to follow such a minefield of social do's and don'ts.

Within a week of the visit, Armane surprised us with a thank-you card he'd made himself, by hand, which really touched us all. My parents are usually pretty understanding when foreigners aren't familiar with Vietnamese customs, but they were genuinely impressed that Armane had picked up so many of our traditions. He earned our utmost respect, and I'd say he was easily one of the most likeable guests we've ever had to stay.

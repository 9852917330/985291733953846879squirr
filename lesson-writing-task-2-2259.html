Children & Family

Starting a family later

Reasons / Advantages outweigh disadvantages

Prompt

In some countries today, many people are having their first child at an older age. What are the reasons for this trend? Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

Model answer

In many wealthy countries the average age at which women have a first child has risen by several years within a generation. The causes are mainly economic and educational, and in my view the advantages of the trend slightly outweigh its costs.

The principal reason is that the years once used for starting a family are now spent in education and early careers. A woman completing a degree and establishing herself professionally is unlikely to be ready for a child before her late twenties, and taking leave at the point when a career is being built carries a lasting penalty in pay. Housing reinforces this: where deposits require years of saving, couples postpone children until they have space for them. Reliable contraception has made this delay a genuine choice rather than an accident.

The advantages are real. Older parents are typically more financially secure, so a child grows up with fewer of the pressures that money problems create, and parents who have already travelled and worked are less likely to feel that a child cost them the life they wanted. Greater maturity also shows in parenting, and children of older parents perform slightly better at school on average.

The disadvantages are chiefly biological. Fertility declines with age, so some couples who delay discover that conceiving is difficult and face expensive treatment, and the risk of certain complications rises. Older parents also have less energy for young children and are more likely to be caring for their own parents at the same time.

In conclusion, the trend is driven by longer education and the cost of housing. On balance the benefits are greater, provided couples understand that delaying narrows the window rather than closing it later.

Key words and phrases

postpone children until they have space for them
Delay becoming parents until life can accommodate children.
cost them the life they wanted
Prevent them from having their desired lifestyle.
caring for their own parents
Looking after the needs of their parents.
narrows the window rather than closing it later
Reduces the available time for having children.

Environment

Where should recycling be taught?

Discuss both views + opinion

Prompt

Some people think that children should be taught at school to recycle materials and avoid waste. Other people believe that children should be taught this at home. Discuss both opinions and give your own opinion.

Model answer

Environmental habits form early, which raises the question of whether schools or parents should be responsible for teaching them. Both have a part to play, and my own view is that the two do different jobs and neither can be dropped.

The argument for schools is that they reach every child and can explain the reasoning. A parent can say that a bottle goes in a particular bin, but a teacher can show what happens to plastic that reaches the sea and why some materials can be reprocessed while others cannot, and a child who understands the reason will apply it to situations nobody has covered. Schools also reach children whose families recycle nothing, which is where teaching changes the most behaviour. Pupils frequently take the habit home and press their parents to adopt it.

The case for the home rests on repetition. Recycling is a daily routine rather than a body of knowledge, and it is learned by sorting rubbish several times a week from the age of three. Children also copy what adults actually do rather than what they are told, so a lesson at school carries little weight in a household where everything goes into the same bin.

In my view the objection to each is answered by the other. Schools supply the understanding but only a few hours of it; homes supply the daily practice but not always the reason, and sometimes not the example. A child who receives both learns a habit that survives leaving home.

In conclusion, this is not a choice between two options. Schools should explain why waste matters and homes should make sorting normal, since knowledge without routine is forgotten within a term.

Key words and phrases

changes the most behaviour
Produces the biggest change in people's actions.
goes into the same bin
Is mixed with other household rubbish.
survives leaving home
Continues after someone moves away from family.
forgotten within a term
Not remembered by the school term's end.

Technology & Science

Is the Internet the greatest invention?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

There have been many inventions in human history, such as the wheel. Some people think the most important one is the Internet. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

The Internet has reshaped work, learning and friendship within a single generation, which makes the claim that it leads all inventions tempting. I disagree, because a handful of older inventions did something the Internet has not: they made human life possible at its present scale.

The case for the Internet is strong on reach. It has given ordinary people access to almost all recorded knowledge, allowed a farmer to check a market price and a student in a village to attend a lecture, and it carries the science, banking and logistics that modern economies run on. It also removed distance as a barrier to working together, which is why research now moves faster than at any earlier point.

The comparison still fails. Writing, agriculture, the printing press, antibiotics and electricity each altered what a human being could be. Farming allowed settlement and therefore cities; writing allowed one generation to speak to the next; antibiotics turned a scratch from a probable death into an inconvenience. Without electricity there is no Internet at all, and an invention that depends on an earlier one can hardly outrank it.

There is a further difference in kind. Older inventions expanded what was available to humanity; the Internet mostly changes the speed and cost of moving what already exists. That is an enormous gain, but the world managed without it thirty years ago, whereas it could not manage a month without electricity or clean water. Its harms, from surveillance to the collapse of shared and reliable information, also complicate any simple verdict.

In conclusion, the Internet is arguably the most significant invention of recent decades and has changed daily life profoundly. Measured against inventions that made settlement, memory and survival itself possible, it belongs high on the list rather than at the top of it.

Key words and phrases

removed distance as a barrier to working together
Enabled collaboration despite being far apart.
depends on an earlier one
Relies on a previous discovery or invention.
collapse of shared and reliable information
Loss of a common base of trustworthy facts.

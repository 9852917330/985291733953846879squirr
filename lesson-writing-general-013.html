Formal

Lost watch on a train

Formal

Prompt

You left a valuable item on a train. Write to the railway company. In your letter: - describe the item - state where and when you lost it - explain what you would like the company to do

Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to report a watch that I believe I left on one of your trains on Monday, 8 June.

The item is a silver Seiko wristwatch with a dark-blue face and a narrow metal strap. There is a small scratch beside the number six, and the initials “A.M.” are engraved on the back. Although the watch is not especially expensive, it is of considerable sentimental value because it was a graduation gift from my grandfather.

I travelled on the 18:10 service from Manchester Piccadilly to Leeds and was seated in Coach C, seat 24A. I removed the watch while working on my laptop and probably left it on the fold-down table when I got off at Leeds at approximately 19:05.

Could you please check the lost-property records for that service and ask the cleaning team whether the watch was found? If it has been handed in, I can collect it from Leeds station or pay any reasonable postage costs to arrange for its return.

I have attached a photograph of the watch and a copy of my ticket.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

of considerable sentimental value
Important because of personal memories.
arrange for its return
Organise getting it back.

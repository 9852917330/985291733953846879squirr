Transport

Cycling to work in Britain

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The table shows changes in the number of residents cycling to work in selected areas of the United Kingdom between 2001 and 2011.

Model answer

The table compares the numbers of people who cycled to work in twelve areas of the UK in the years 2001 and 2011.

Overall, cycling to work increased in all twelve areas shown over the 10-year period. Inner London had by far the highest number of cycling commuters in both years.

In 2001, well over 43 thousand residents of inner London commuted by bicycle, and this figure rose to more than 106 thousand in 2011, an increase of 144%. By contrast, although outer London had the second highest number of cycling commuters in each year, the percentage change, at only 45%, was the lowest of the twelve areas shown in the table.

Brighton and Hove recorded the second biggest increase (109%) in the number of residents cycling to work, but Bristol had the highest number of cycling commuters among the areas shown outside London, with 8,108 in 2001 and 15,768 in 2011. Figures for the other eight areas were below the 10 thousand mark in both years.

Key words and phrases

had by far the highest number
Had many more than all others.
an increase of 144%
Rose by 144% of its starting value.
By contrast
Used to introduce a clear difference.
recorded the second biggest increase
Had the second largest rise.
were below the 10 thousand mark
Were fewer than ten thousand.

Technology

Inventions and the complexity of modern life

Prompt

What is the most important invention of the last century? Can technology make life harder?

What is the most important invention of the last century?

Simple Model Answer

I would choose the internet because it has changed communication, shopping, learning and entertainment so widely. It now supports daily life like roads or electricity. However, that is a broad answer, since the internet depends on many other inventions, such as transistors and communication networks. Those deserve credit too, even if the internet is the change people notice most.

Key words and phrases

deserve credit
Should be recognised for making an important contribution.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd say the internet, though the transistor's a reasonable answer too, since nothing else would exist without it. The reason I'd choose the internet is the sheer breadth of what it changed — communication, commerce, education, entertainment and access to information all work in a fundamentally different way now. It's stopped being a product and become basic infrastructure, in much the same way as electricity or the road network. The honest problem with an answer this broad, though, is that it's a bit of a cheat — you could just as easily break it down into the specific technologies underneath it, like fibre optics or mobile networks, and credit those instead.

Key words and phrases

the sheer breadth of what it changed
the extremely wide range of things it affected
basic infrastructure
essential structures and services such as roads and electricity
a bit of a cheat
something that feels like an unfair shortcut

Can technology make life harder?

Simple Model Answer

Yes. Devices can distract us, and being reachable all the time can extend the working day. We may also depend on systems so much that a failure leaves us unable to manage basic tasks. Technology can still be helpful, though. Clear boundaries and sensible habits often decide whether it saves time or makes life more complicated.

Key words and phrases

boundaries
Limits that protect your time, privacy or well-being.

Advanced Model Answer

Definitely. The obvious problem is distraction, since a device designed to save time ends up consuming your attention unless you set firm boundaries around it. Beyond that, there's the expectation of being permanently available, which has quietly extended the working day for a great many people. And there's a dependency issue: when a system fails, we often discover we've lost the ability to manage without it. The technology itself is fairly neutral, though; it's really the habits people build around it that matter.

Key words and phrases

set firm boundaries
make clear rules about what you will accept
lost the ability to manage without it
can no longer cope when it is unavailable

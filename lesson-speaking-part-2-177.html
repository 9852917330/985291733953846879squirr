Situation

A time when you were not satisfied with a service

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when you were not satisfied with a service.

You should say: - what the service was - who provided it - what happened - and explain why you were unhappy with the service.

Simple Model Answer

A time I was dissatisfied with a service was during a restaurant visit a couple of months ago. Some relatives from abroad were staying with us, and eight of us went to a nearby town. We became hungry and chose a restaurant with several empty tables.

After waiting for someone to greet us, a waitress said we had used the wrong entrance. At the main door, the manager then told us there was no table for eight. I suggested joining two empty four-person tables, but he refused and said we would have to sit separately.

We felt unwelcome because the restaurant was not busy and nobody seemed interested in finding a solution. In the end, we decided to leave. Fortunately, another restaurant around the corner welcomed us warmly, and we enjoyed our meal there. I understand that restaurants have practical limits, but the way staff explain a problem matters. A polite explanation or an effort to help would have changed our experience. Instead, the manager's attitude made us feel that our family was an inconvenience.

Key words and phrases

dissatisfied
Unhappy because something is not as good as expected.
an inconvenience
Something or someone causing extra trouble or difficulty.

Advanced Model Answer

I'll tell you about a time I got poor service from staff in a restaurant. It happened a couple of months ago, when some family members who live abroad came to stay with us. We'd decided to visit a nearby town, and at some point everyone got a little hungry, so we thought we'd grab a bite to eat. The first restaurant we came across looked perfect.

It had plenty of empty tables - we hadn't booked in advance - and we were all happy with the menu. But as soon as we walked through the door, things started to go wrong, and we weren't at all impressed with the staff's attitude. After standing around for a few minutes waiting for someone to greet us, I finally managed to get the attention of a waitress, who told me we'd come in through the wrong door. So I went round to the main entrance, only to be told by the manager that there weren't any tables free for a party our size - there were eight of us.

I could see several free tables with four seats each, so I suggested we simply push a couple of them together. But the manager was adamant that this was impossible; our only option, apparently, was to split the family across two separate tables. By that point some of my family were getting fed up - the restaurant was half empty, yet we were being treated as though they couldn't be bothered with us.

In the end we decided we didn't want to sit apart or stay somewhere we felt unwelcome, so we left, feeling pretty disappointed by how dismissive the manager had been. Fortunately, we found a much friendlier place just around the corner, and the whole episode made us appreciate good service a lot more.

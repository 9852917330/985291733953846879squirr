Situation

A time when someone helped you

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when someone helped you

You should say: Who helped you When it was Why s/he helped you And describe how you felt about it.

OUTLINE

Everything fine until sudden torrential rain poured down raincoat nowhere found
rather skeptical 1st mother always warns not trust anyone street after
received raincoat w/ hands looked w/ deepest appreciation
However kindness woman warmed inside heart
Obviously no one ever met before willing help no reward

Simple Model Answer

A time someone helped me was during a journey home from work about a month ago. It was late at night when heavy rain suddenly began. I searched my bag but realised I had forgotten my raincoat.

There was nowhere nearby to shelter, so I continued riding and expected to arrive completely wet. Then a young woman on a motorbike caught up with me. She called out and offered me a spare raincoat. At first, I was unsure because she was a stranger, but her manner seemed kind and honest.

I accepted the raincoat and thanked her. She smiled, told me to keep it and rode away before I could ask her name. I was still wet when I reached home, but I felt much better emotionally. She had no responsibility to help and received nothing in return. That made the action especially memorable. The experience reminded me how much a small act of kindness can matter at the right time. I hope I would notice a similar opportunity to help someone else.

Key words and phrases

spare raincoat
An extra coat used for protection from rain.
in return
As something given back after receiving help or a gift.

Advanced Model Answer

About a month ago I received support from a stranger, in a moment that's stuck with me ever since. I was on my way back home from work quite late at night. Everything was fine until a sudden torrential rain poured down, and to make things worse, my raincoat was nowhere to be found in my bag.

I couldn't find any shop or house along the road with any kind of cover where I could shelter for a bit, so I just kept moving on, resigning myself to arriving home looking like a drowned rat.

Then, in my rear-view mirror, I noticed a young woman on a motorbike trying to catch up with me. When she got closer, she called out loudly enough for me to hear that she wanted to give me her spare raincoat. I was a bit sceptical at first, since my mother's always warned me not to trust strangers on the street, but one look at her face told me her offer was completely genuine. I took the raincoat with both hands and thanked her as sincerely as I could. She just smiled warmly, told me to keep it, and rode off into the distance before I could even ask her name.

I was still soaked by the time I got home, but it was the kindness of that woman that really warmed me up from the inside. She was a complete stranger who had no reason to help me at all, and yet she did, and that small act of humanity is something I'll always remember.

Key words and phrases

received support from a stranger
was helped by a person I did not know
on my way back home from work
while travelling home after my job
a sudden torrential rain poured down

nowhere to be found

kept moving on

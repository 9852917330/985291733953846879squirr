Technology & Science

Do printed books still matter?

Discuss both views + opinion

Prompt

Some think that printed books are not necessary in the digital era, because all writing can be stored electronically. Others think printed books still play an important role. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Model answer

Now that any text can be stored and read on a device, some people regard printed books as an expensive habit. Others argue that the physical object still does something a file cannot, and I find myself closer to the second position.

The argument against print is largely practical. A single device holds thousands of titles, weighs less than one hardback and can be carried anywhere, which matters greatly to students and to anyone living in a small flat. Digital text is also cheaper to produce and distribute, so a reader in a country with few bookshops can obtain a title immediately, and adjustable type makes reading possible for people with poor eyesight. Printing consumes paper, ink and transport, none of which a download requires at any stage.

Those who defend print point to how reading actually works. Studies of comprehension consistently find that readers remember more from a printed page, partly because the physical position of text helps memory and partly because a book does not interrupt with notifications. A printed book is also permanent in a way that a licensed file is not, since a publisher can withdraw a digital edition from devices that have already paid for it, which has happened more than once.

My own view is that the two suit different purposes rather than competing. Reference material and casual reading belong on a screen, whereas anything that must be read closely or kept for decades is better held in print.

In conclusion, digital storage has genuine advantages of cost and portability, and it has made reading possible for people who lacked access. Printed books remain necessary nonetheless, because they support deeper reading and because ownership of a physical copy cannot be revoked.

Key words and phrases

requires at any stage
Needs at any point in the process.
better held in print
More securely kept as physical printed copies.
cannot be revoked
Cannot be withdrawn or cancelled.

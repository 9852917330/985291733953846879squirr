Person

A leader or politician who you admire

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a leader or politician who you admire.

You should say: Who is he/ she? What is so appealing about him? What type of leader is he/ she And explain why do you admire him /her.

Simple Model Answer

A leader I admire is David Cameron, a former Prime Minister of the United Kingdom. I met him briefly during a trip to England about five years ago. I was visiting Number 10 Downing Street when he walked past and stopped to shake hands with some people.

My first impression was that he looked professional and confident. He also seemed calm, even when discussing difficult political questions. From what I saw and read about him, I felt that he considered different choices carefully before making decisions.

Another reason I admired him was his approach to working with other people. He appeared willing to listen to different opinions instead of immediately refusing them. I also appreciated his interest in public services such as healthcare and support for people in need. Although our meeting was short, it was memorable because I had never met such a well-known politician before. I would enjoy visiting the UK again and learning more about its political life.

Key words and phrases

first impression
The opinion you form when you first meet someone.
public services
Services provided for people, often by the government.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about Mr David Cameron, the former Prime Minister of the UK, and I honestly think he's one of the most professional people I've ever come across.

To get the ball rolling, I should probably explain how I actually met him. It was about five years ago, more or less, when I travelled to England and had a chance to meet him in front of Number 10 Downing Street — I was there as a tourist, and he happened to walk past and stopped to shake a few hands.

On the subject of his appearance, I have to admit I was struck by how sharp and presentable he looked the moment I saw him. He came across as so charismatic and confident that everyone around him seemed to want to listen, even when he was debating political issues. What stood out even more, though, was how calm he stayed in every situation — it always felt like he'd weighed up the pros and cons before making any decision, rather than jumping to conclusions.

There are a couple of reasons I really admire him. For one, he led his party in a pretty democratic way — he'd listen to everyone's opinions and comment fairly on them rather than shutting people down. He also played a real part in improving things like healthcare and social welfare while he was in office.

For all these reasons, I'd say David Cameron is the most honourable leader I've ever met, and if I ever get the chance, I'd love to go back to the UK and meet him again.

Object & Favourite

A book that made me think

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a book that you enjoyed reading because it made you think deeply

You should say: - what this book was - why you decided to read it - what reading this book made you think about - and explain why you enjoyed reading this book.

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

A book that made me think is Atomic Habits by James Clear. It explains how small actions can lead to important changes if we repeat them over time. I read it after hearing the author in a podcast interview about a year and a half ago.

His ideas interested me enough to order the book that evening. While reading, I began comparing his examples with my own life. I realised that I often checked my phone as soon as I woke up without making a conscious decision. The book helped me understand how habits form and how to make helpful actions easier.

For example, I now put my running shoes out the night before I plan to exercise. It is a small change, but it makes starting easier in the morning. I enjoyed the practical examples because I could use them immediately. The book did not make my life perfect, but it encouraged me to pay attention to everyday choices. It showed me that improvement does not always require one dramatic decision.

Key words and phrases

a conscious decision
A choice you think about and make deliberately.
everyday choices
Small decisions made during ordinary daily life.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about a book that really made me think, and that's had a positive effect on my life. It's called Atomic Habits, by James Clear, and it's basically about the compounding effect that small decisions and tiny changes can have on someone's life if you stick with them for long enough.

I decided to read it after I saw an interview with James Clear on a podcast about a year and a half ago. He was talking about the science of tiny habits and telling stories about how something as small as making your bed every morning can eventually change your whole mindset. I found the whole idea so fascinating that I ordered the book that same evening.

While I was reading it, I couldn't help comparing the ideas to my own life, and to be honest it wasn't always comfortable. I realised I wasn't anywhere near as productive as I thought I was, and that most of my bad habits, like checking my phone the second I wake up, were happening on autopilot without me even noticing. The book gave me a clear framework for understanding why habits form and how to break the ones that aren't serving me, and I've been trying to apply it ever since, starting with small things like laying out my running shoes the night before.

I thoroughly enjoyed reading Atomic Habits because it wasn't just the author's opinions dressed up as advice; everything was backed by research and real examples. I love books that change the way I think or give me something practical I can actually use, and this one definitely did that.

Key words and phrases

made me think
Caused me to consider an idea.
had a positive effect on
Helped something improve.
compounding effect
Small effects adding up to larger results.
on autopilot
Without thinking consciously about each action.
a clear framework for understanding
An understandable structure for making sense of something.
backed by research
Supported by scientific or other investigation.

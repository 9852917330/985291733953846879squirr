Object & Favourite

A photo you have taken

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a photo you have taken

You should say: - Where you took it. - When you took the photo. - What/who is in the photo. - How you felt about the photo.

OUTLINE

>>> family photo at Phan Thiet Beach
photo that captured the memory of a happy family, taken around age 14 on a family holiday
looked at it hundreds of times, dad's rare sparkling smile, mum glowing next to him
younger siblings not photogenic, both mid-laugh about something now forgotten
background: sun dipping below the horizon, sky red/orange/yellow, water reflecting it
know little about photography technically, took it based on instinct not professional skill
not professionally shot but still the most treasured photo I own

Simple Model Answer

A photo I treasure is one I took during a family holiday to Phan Thiet when I was about fourteen. It shows my parents and my younger brother and sister at the beach near sunset.

Everyone looks happy in the picture. My father, who is usually serious in photographs, has a clear smile. My mother is standing beside him, and my brother and sister are laughing at something I can no longer remember. Behind them, the sky is red, orange and yellow, and the sea reflects those colours.

I do not know much about professional photography. I simply took the picture because I liked what I saw. However, it became one of my most important possessions. Whenever I look at it, I remember how relaxed we all felt on that holiday. It is special because it records a real family moment rather than a carefully arranged pose. I hope to keep it for the rest of my life, even as other photos and memories become less clear.

Key words and phrases

treasure
Value something very highly because it is important to you.
arranged pose
A position people deliberately take for a photograph.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about a photo I took a few years ago, back when I was around fourteen, on a family holiday to Phan Thiet beach. It's a photo that captured the memory of a really happy family, and it's honestly a priceless possession to me — I'd like to keep it with me for the rest of my life.

I've looked at it hundreds of times, and it still gives me this indescribable warm feeling. Everyone in it is smiling — my dad's eyes are actually sparkling, with these little crinkles at the corners, which is rare because he's normally quite serious-looking in photos. My mum's standing right next to him, glowing with this really warm happiness. My younger brother and sister aren't exactly photogenic in it, though — they're both mid-laugh about something I can't even remember anymore.

In the background, the sun's just about to dip below the horizon, and the whole sky's this mix of reds, oranges and yellows, with the water reflecting it all back. It's honestly a beautiful scene on its own, even without the family standing there in it.

I'll admit I know next to nothing about the technical side of photography — I just take photos based on what looks good to me rather than doing anything a professional would do. But even though it wasn't shot professionally, it's still the most treasured photo I own.

Key words and phrases

captured the memory of
Preserved a moment in an image.
priceless possession
An object whose personal value cannot be measured.
for the rest of my life
For all the remaining years I live.
indescribable warm feeling
A happy, comforting emotion difficult to describe.
dip below the horizon
Move below the visible line where sky meets land.
most treasured photo
The photograph I value the most.

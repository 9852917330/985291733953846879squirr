A situation when someone made noise

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a situation when someone made noise

You should say: when it happened who made the noise where it was And explain how you felt about it.

Simple Model Answer

A noisy situation I experienced was a neighbour's house renovation a few months ago. A new family had bought the house next to mine and needed to repair water damage before moving in.

For about a month, workers used drills, hammers and saws almost every day, including weekends. They often began around seven in the morning. This woke my family earlier than we wanted and made it hard to relax at home. One particularly difficult moment was an important work video call. The drilling was so loud that people struggled to hear me, and I kept apologising.

I understood that the family needed the repairs completed, but the constant noise still left me tired and stressed. Sometimes I even found it difficult to sleep because I could not relax properly in the evening. When the work finally ended, I felt enormous relief. I noticed how peaceful my own home could be without machinery next door. The experience made me more aware that building work can affect neighbours even when the reason for doing it is understandable.

Key words and phrases

renovation
Work to repair or improve a building.
enormous relief
A very strong feeling of comfort when a difficult situation ends.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about the time my neighbour made a lot of noise, renovating his house a few months ago. The sound of drills, hammers and other power tools going all day was honestly one of the most annoying things I've dealt with in ages.

A new family had bought the house right next to mine, and because of some problems with water leaking through the walls, they'd decided to gut the place and redo it properly. From that point on, the noise of hammers, saws and drills seemed completely endless around my house, all day, every day, even at weekends. My neighbour clearly wanted to move in as soon as possible, so the workers would turn up and start banging away by about seven every morning, which meant my whole family ended up waking up much earlier than we wanted to.

During that time I felt properly exhausted, and there was one particularly bad day when I had an important video call for work and had to keep apologising because the drilling kept drowning out my voice. I really wanted to make a complaint so my neighbour would do something about it, but since the renovation was completely legal, there wasn't actually anything I could do. Hearing that noise constantly drove me crazy and had a genuinely negative impact on my work, and some nights I was so wound up by it all that I could barely sleep.

But luckily, after about a month, my neighbour finished the renovations and the construction noise finally stopped. I was so relieved when things went quiet again, I actually noticed how peaceful my own flat could be.

Key words and phrases

made a lot of noise
produced loud sounds
power tools
machines powered by electricity to build or repair things

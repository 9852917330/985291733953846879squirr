Formal

Trying to reconnect with a childhood friend

Formal

Prompt

You saw a newspaper article about a childhood friend whom you have not contacted for many years. Write to the editor. In your letter: - identify the article and edition - explain how you lost contact - ask for help reconnecting with your friend

Model answer

Dear Editor,

I am writing about the profile of Dr Linh Tran published in the Sunday edition of the City Herald on 14 June.

Linh and I were close friends at Nguyen Du Secondary School, but we lost touch after her family moved overseas in 2008. At the time, neither of us used social media regularly, and the email address I had for her later stopped working. I have tried searching professional networks, but I have not been able to confirm whether the person I found is the same Linh.

The details in your article, particularly the name of her school and her interest in environmental science, strongly suggest that she is my former classmate. I understand that you cannot disclose private contact information. However, I would be grateful if you could forward my details to her on my behalf. She could then decide whether she wishes to respond.

Your assistance could put us back in contact after nearly two decades, and I would greatly appreciate it.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

strongly suggest that she is my former classmate
Provide strong signs we studied together.
you cannot disclose private contact information
You must not share personal details.
I would be grateful if you could
A polite way to request help.
forward my details
Pass my information to someone.
put us back in contact
Help us communicate again.

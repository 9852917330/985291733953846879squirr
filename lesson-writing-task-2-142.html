Economy & Money

Saving money or spending it

Discussion

Prompt

Some people think that it is best to save money, for example in a bank or savings scheme. Other people feel that money should be spent whenever it is available. Discuss these views and reach an opinion on this debate. Give reasons for your answer, and support your essay with ideas and examples from your own experience. You should write at least 250 words.

Model answer

The question of whether to save or spend money is very topical today, as so many people are struggling with financial worries. There are valid arguments on both sides, but I believe that putting money aside whenever possible is the wiser course.

On the one hand, it is often said that spending money is one way to relax and enjoy life, for instance by shopping or taking holidays, and there is certainly some truth in this. We also have little choice but to spend a large proportion of our salary on day to day expenses such as food, housing and transport, so there is surprisingly little left to save anyway. Spending on products and services also boosts the economy and creates employment, which benefits everyone.

On the other hand, there are real dangers in reckless spending, particularly on impulse purchases financed by credit. Advertising and, more recently, buy-now-pay-later schemes make it very easy to commit future income without noticing, and young people who have never had to manage a budget are especially vulnerable to this.

The stronger argument for saving, however, is security. Without a reserve of funds for emergencies, a single period of unemployment or illness leaves people putting themselves at risk of hardship or even bankruptcy, which is why financial advisers commonly suggest keeping three months of expenses in reserve. Saving also allows people to accumulate wealth over a lifetime, so that their children inherit money or property which will increase their security and standard of living.

Overall, I tend to agree that it is wiser to save money wherever possible, for reasons which benefit both individuals today and future generations.

Key words and phrases

reckless spending
Spending without considering risks or consequences.
a reserve of funds for emergencies
Money saved for unexpected urgent needs.
putting themselves at risk of hardship
Increasing their chance of financial difficulties.
accumulate wealth
Build up money and valuable possessions.

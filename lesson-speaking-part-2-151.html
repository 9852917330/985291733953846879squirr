Event

An unusual dinner that you had

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an unusual dinner that you had

You should say: - When you had this dinner - Who you had it with - What dishes you had - And explain why you think this dinner was unusual

Simple Model Answer

An unusual dinner I had was at a French restaurant in central Ho Chi Minh City about three years ago. My family usually ate simple Vietnamese meals at home, so this was a different experience. My father arranged it to celebrate my good final exam results.

The restaurant felt much more formal than anywhere we normally went. I remember having mushroom soup followed by steak with a red wine sauce. My mother and I were surprised by the surroundings, and the food was very different from our usual dishes.

The meal was expensive for my father, which made his decision especially meaningful. He did not earn a great deal, but he wanted to reward my effort and give us a special evening together. After weeks of revision, I finally felt relaxed. We also had time to talk properly after being busy for so long. I would like to take my parents somewhere similar when I can afford it. For now, that dinner remains a happy memory of both a personal achievement and my father's generosity.

Key words and phrases

revision
Studying material again before an exam.
generosity
Willingness to give time, money or help to others.

Advanced Model Answer

My family usually has dinner at home with simple, traditional dishes, so the one time we went somewhere really different stands out. It was about three years ago, right after my final exams. I'd been tied up in revision for weeks, and when I passed with flying colours, my dad decided to take the whole family out to a French restaurant downtown, partly to ease my mind and partly to celebrate.

What made it unusual was that we'd never eaten anywhere that fancy before — my mum and I were honestly a bit stunned when we walked in. I remember starting with a rich mushroom soup, then having steak in a red wine sauce, about as far from our usual home cooking as you could get. The restaurant's in the centre of Ho Chi Minh City, in a busy area, and it's normally packed every night. The food wasn't cheap at all; it cost my dad a small fortune, which made the evening feel even more special. He's not the kind of person who earns huge amounts of money, so the fact he was still willing to spend that much on us, as a reward for my results, meant a lot to me.

The meal helped me properly unwind after months of stress — I remember just sitting there, completely relaxed for the first time in ages. It was also a good chance to reconnect, since the three of us had barely seen each other in weeks.

If I ever have the money, I'd love to take my parents back to a place like that, maybe try lobster or salmon this time. For now, that one dinner is still the meal I think of whenever anyone asks about a special family occasion.

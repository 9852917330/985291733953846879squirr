Situation

A time when you had to work hard to achieve a goal

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when you had to work hard to achieve a goal

You should say: what the goal was when this happened what you did to achieve this goal and explain why the goal was important to you.

Simple Model Answer

A time I worked hard for a goal was my final year of high school. I wanted to enter Hanoi Medical University because becoming a doctor had been an important dream for me. I knew the entrance exam was highly competitive.

To prepare, I spent many hours in the library reading textbooks and practising questions. After school, I reviewed the work from that day and tried to understand anything I had found difficult. The routine lasted several months and required me to give up some free time.

Although I felt tired at times, thinking about my goal helped me continue. I did not want to depend only on luck when so many other students were preparing seriously. In the end, I passed with results I was proud of and received a place at the university. I was so happy that I cried when I saw the news. The achievement mattered because it showed me what sustained effort could do. Looking back, the preparation was difficult, but reaching a goal that meant so much made the work feel worthwhile.

Key words and phrases

highly competitive
Difficult to succeed in because many people are trying for limited places.
sustained effort
Work that continues steadily over a long period.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm the type of person who's never liked relying on luck, I believe everything worthwhile can only be achieved through hard work and determination, and my final year of high school was one of the times I really spared no effort to reach a goal.

Back then, like every other senior, my main goal was to pass the university entrance exam and get into my dream university, but the pressure felt even higher for me because I'd set my sights on Hanoi Medical University, one of the top universities in the country. Thousands of students sit that exam every year, so it's highly competitive, to put it mildly.

To keep up with the other candidates, I'd spend hours in the library every single day, reading as many textbooks as I could get my hands on, and even after school I'd stay up late going back over everything I'd covered that day. It was an exhausting few months, but I didn't feel tired at all, becoming a doctor was one of my biggest dreams, and I was determined not to let anything get in the way of it.

In the end, the hard work paid off: I passed the entrance exam with results I was genuinely proud of and got a place as a medical student. When the results came out, I was so happy I actually burst into tears. Looking back, that whole year of effort helped me reach one of the biggest goals I've set myself so far.

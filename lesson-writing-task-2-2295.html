Technology & Science

Holidays in space

Positive or Negative

Prompt

Space travel has been possible for some time, and some people believe that space tourism could be developed in the future. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?

Model answer

Several companies now sell brief flights beyond the atmosphere, and longer trips are promised within a decade. I regard the development as negative at present, though the reasons are practical rather than a hostility to space exploration.

There is a reasonable case in its favour. Paying passengers fund the engineering that reduces the cost of reaching orbit, and much of what makes satellites and scientific missions cheaper has been developed by companies whose income comes partly from tourism. The industry creates skilled employment and encourages young people into science, and the experience itself appears to change how travellers regard the planet, since almost every astronaut describes seeing the earth without borders as the strongest impression of the journey.

The objections are stronger. Each launch releases a large quantity of carbon and particles directly into the upper atmosphere, where the effect on the climate is worse than at ground level, and this is done for a journey lasting minutes. It is difficult to justify at a time when ordinary people are being asked to fly less. The cost is the second objection: tickets are priced beyond almost everyone, so the resources go towards an experience available only to the very wealthy while problems on the ground remain unfunded.

There are further difficulties. Orbit is already crowded with debris, and increasing traffic raises the risk of collisions that could damage the satellites weather forecasting and communication depend on. Safety regulation has not kept pace with the industry either, and a serious accident involving paying passengers would set the whole field back many years.

In conclusion, space tourism advances useful technology and inspires people, and those benefits are real. Its environmental cost, extreme exclusivity and the pressure it places on orbit make it, for now, a luxury the moment cannot justify.

Key words and phrases

seeing the earth without borders
Viewing the planet as one shared place.
an experience available only to the very wealthy
Something affordable only to extremely rich people.
would set the whole field back many years
Delay progress across the entire area for years.
a luxury the moment cannot justify
An expensive extra inappropriate to current priorities.

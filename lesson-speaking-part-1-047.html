Laughing

What makes you laugh and why it matters

Prompt

What kinds of thing make you laugh? Do you like making other people laugh? Do you think it's important for people to laugh? Is laughing the same as feeling happy?

What kinds of thing make you laugh?

Simple Model Answer

Funny stories with friends make me laugh the most, especially when we talk about things that happened to us. I also enjoy watching comedians and funny films.

Key words and phrases

comedians
People whose job is to make an audience laugh.

Advanced Model Answer

I laugh most when I'm with friends talking about funny things that have happened to us. I also like watching comedians and comedy films.

Key words and phrases

happened to
Did something by chance.

Do you like making other people laugh?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I enjoy it because it feels good to make someone happy. Sharing a joke or a funny moment can be a nice way to connect with another person.

Key words and phrases

connect with
Build a friendly or understanding relationship with someone.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, it's a nice feeling when you can make someone laugh because you can see that you've made them happy. It's great to share a funny moment with someone.

Key words and phrases

make someone laugh
Cause someone to find something funny.

Do you think it's important for people to laugh?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, laughter helps us enjoy life and not worry about everything too much. Being able to find something funny can help us relax, and I think that is good for us.

Key words and phrases

relax
Feel calmer and less worried.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, of course. It's important to see the funny side of things; humour helps us not to take life too seriously. I think that laughter is good for our health.

Key words and phrases

take life too seriously
Be too serious about ordinary problems.

Is laughing the same as feeling happy?

Simple Model Answer

Not exactly. You can feel happy without laughing, and people sometimes laugh during difficult times. Humour can help us deal with problems even when we are not feeling very happy.

Key words and phrases

Humour
The ability to find things funny or make people laugh.

Advanced Model Answer

It's not exactly the same because you can be happy without laughing, and sometimes we laugh when something bad has happened; a sense of humour can help us to cope with difficult situations.

Key words and phrases

a sense of humour
The ability to appreciate funny things.
cope with difficult situations
Manage challenging circumstances.

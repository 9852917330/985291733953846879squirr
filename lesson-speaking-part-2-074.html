Object & Favourite

A photograph of you that you like

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a photograph of you that you like.

You should say: - where the photo was taken - what you were doing when it was taken - who took it - and explain why you like the photo.

Simple Model Answer

A photograph of myself that I like is my university graduation picture. It was taken just before the ceremony, in the student cafe beside the main hall. The university had hired a professional photographer to take portraits of the graduates.

In the photo, I am wearing a black graduation gown and a flat academic cap. I am holding a rolled-up certificate and smiling at the camera. The photographer used a white background and special lights, so there is not much else to see. An assistant organised payments and recorded everyone's details.

I like this picture because it represents the end of four years of hard work. I had finished my degree and made my family proud. At the same time, I knew a new stage was beginning, including looking for a job. When I see the photo now, I remember the friendships and experiences of university, as well as the relief I felt that day. It is a simple portrait, but it captures an important achievement in my life.

Key words and phrases

graduation gown
The long formal clothing worn at a ceremony for completing a degree.
a new stage
A different period of someone's life.

Advanced Model Answer

The photograph that immediately comes to mind when I think of one I like is my university graduation photo. It was taken just before the ceremony that marked the end of my undergraduate degree. You can't really see anything in the background, but it was actually taken in the students' cafe next to the hall where the ceremony was held. In the photo, I'm wearing the traditional graduate outfit — a black gown and one of those flat academic caps, the mortarboard.

I'm also holding my rolled-up degree certificate. It's obviously a formal portrait, so I'm just standing there, smiling, looking straight into the camera. A professional photographer took it — the university had hired him to set up in the cafe and photograph graduates before the ceremony started.

He'd brought a white screen backdrop, some lighting equipment, and an assistant who was taking payments and noting down everyone's details. The reason I love this photo so much is that it captures a turning point in my life. Getting handed that certificate at the ceremony was the payoff for four years of hard work, and it felt like closing one whole chapter of my life.

I'd achieved what I set out to do and made my family proud, and I remember knowing that the next step was job-hunting. Whenever I look at that photo now, it brings back such happy memories — of my time at university, and of the relief and contentment I felt that day.

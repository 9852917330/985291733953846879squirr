Collecting

Collecting things as a hobby

Prompt

Have you ever collected anything as a hobby? What kinds of thing do people often like collecting? Why do you think people collect certain objects? Is there anything that you would like to collect in the future?

Have you ever collected anything as a hobby?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I collected keyrings when I was younger. My favourite was a little Lego man that someone gave me. I remember liking it more than the others in my collection.

Key words and phrases

my collection
The group of similar objects I have gathered.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes. When I was younger I used to collect keyrings. I remember that my favourite was a Lego man keyring that someone bought me as a present.

Key words and phrases

used to collect
Collected regularly in the past.

What kinds of thing do people often like collecting?

Simple Model Answer

People collect many things, from inexpensive stamps, keyrings and toys to valuable old objects. Some also collect watches, jewellery or even cars if they have enough money.

Key words and phrases

valuable
Worth a lot of money or important to someone.

Advanced Model Answer

Some people collect cheap items like stamps, keyrings or toys. Others collect expensive antiques, watches, jewellery or even cars.

Key words and phrases

expensive antiques
Valuable old objects.

Why do you think people collect certain objects?

Simple Model Answer

Some people enjoy looking for the next object in their collection. Others collect things to remember experiences, or because they like arranging their objects and showing them to people.

Key words and phrases

showing them to people
Letting other people see them.

Advanced Model Answer

Maybe they like the constant search for a new item, or they want to remember past experiences. Others might like the feeling of organising and presenting what they own.

Key words and phrases

constant search
Looking continuously for something.

Is there anything that you would like to collect in the future?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I'd like to collect guitars if I had enough money. It would be lovely to own several guitars with different colours, shapes and sizes.

Key words and phrases

if I had enough money
On the condition that I could afford it.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes. If I could afford it, I'd love to have a collection of guitars in different colours, shapes and sizes!

Key words and phrases

a collection of guitars
Several guitars gathered and kept together.

Writing Task 2 Template

6. Direct Opinion

Use for direct opinion questions such as “Do you think...?”, “Should...?”, “Is... the best way?” or “Which is more important?”
Core approach: Answer the exact question directly in the introduction. Build the essay around the judgement the prompt actually asks for, rather than forcing an agree/disagree formula.

Introduction

Paraphrase the issue.

In my view, give a direct answer to the question, primarily because reason 1 and reason 2.

Body Paragraph 1

The strongest reason for this view is that main idea 1.

This is important because explanation.

For example, example.

Consequently, link back to the direct answer.

Body Paragraph 2

A second consideration is main idea 2.

Although reasonable counterpoint, explain why your judgement still stands.

This means that result / implication.

Conclusion

Overall, I believe that direct answer restated because brief synthesis of the two reasons.

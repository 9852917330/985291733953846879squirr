Communication

Keeping in touch: phone, email and letters

Prompt

How do you usually keep in touch with members of your family? Do you prefer to speak to people by phone or by writing emails? Do you ever write letters by hand? Is there anything you dislike about mobile phones?

How do you usually keep in touch with members of your family?

Simple Model Answer

I usually speak to my family on the phone, and I have also started making video calls. I try to visit them whenever I can because meeting in person is important too.

Key words and phrases

in person
Being physically together rather than using a phone or computer.

Advanced Model Answer

I mostly keep in touch with my family by phone, and I've started using the Internet to make video calls. I also try to visit members of my family in person as often as I can.

Key words and phrases

keep in touch with
Continue communicating with.
make video calls
Talk while seeing each other online.

Do you prefer to speak to people by phone or by writing emails?

Simple Model Answer

It depends on who I'm speaking to. I often email or text my friends, but my parents prefer a phone call, so I usually call them.

Key words and phrases

depends on
Is decided or affected by something.

Advanced Model Answer

It depends on the situation. I use email and text messages to communicate with friends, but my parents prefer it if I phone them.

Key words and phrases

communicate with friends
Exchange messages or talk with friends.

Do you ever write letters by hand?

Simple Model Answer

No, I don't usually write letters by hand. Sending an email is much easier, and the other person receives it straight away, so I find it more useful.

Key words and phrases

straight away
Immediately, without waiting.

Advanced Model Answer

No, I don't write letters by hand because it's so much easier to send an email that will arrive immediately.

Key words and phrases

write letters by hand
Use a pen rather than typing.

Is there anything you dislike about mobile phones?

Simple Model Answer

I dislike phones ringing during films or lessons because they disturb other people. I also don't enjoy hearing someone's private conversation when they talk loudly on their phone in public.

Key words and phrases

disturb
Interrupt someone or stop them feeling comfortable or focused.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, I really don't like it when someone's phone goes off in the cinema or during a lesson, and I'm not keen on overhearing other people's conversations in public either.

Key words and phrases

goes off
Makes a sudden sound, such as an alarm.

An interesting event

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an interesting event

You should say: What the event was How you knew about this event Why it was so interesting And explain your feelings towards this event.

Simple Model Answer

An interesting event I attended was my best friend Huong's wedding three weeks ago. She had phoned about a month earlier to tell me the news. I knew both her and her future husband well, so I was excited to be there.

The room was decorated with small lights, candles and roses. It felt warm and romantic. The ceremony was more modern than the weddings I had attended before, which made it especially interesting. My favourite moment was when the groom sang to Huong. Guests joined in, and some people began dancing.

He had also prepared a video explaining why he loved her. I found it touching and became a little tearful. Later, I met old friends I had not seen for a long time, and we took many photographs together and with the couple. I enjoyed the event because it combined a close friend's happiness with the chance to reconnect with others. It was not just a formal ceremony. The personal songs and video helped us understand the couple's relationship and share their joy.

Key words and phrases

touching
Making someone feel moved by kindness, love or emotion.
reconnect with others
Renew relationships with people after time apart.

Advanced Model Answer

The event I want to talk about is my best friend Huong's wedding, which happened three weeks ago and left a deep impression on me. She'd called me about a month before to tell me she was finally tying the knot, and honestly, I'd been waiting for that call for years, since I know both her and her husband really well. There was no question I'd take part in the day, whatever else I had on.

What made it such a wonderful occasion was partly the setting — the room was decorated with fairy lights, candles and roses everywhere, which gave it a really cosy, romantic feel — and partly how modern the whole ceremony was. I'd only ever been to fairly traditional weddings before, so it felt refreshingly different.

The moment that really got me, though, was when the groom stood up and sang a song to the bride, with all of us joining in and a few people getting up to dance. He'd also put together a video of himself talking about why he loved her, and I'm not ashamed to say I welled up a bit watching it.

On top of all that, the wedding gave me a chance to catch up with old friends I hadn't seen in ages. We spent a good chunk of the evening taking photos with each other and, of course, with the newly-weds.

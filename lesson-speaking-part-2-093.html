Object & Favourite

A vehicle you would like to buy in the future

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a vehicle you would like to buy in the future.

You should say: - what kind of vehicle it is - what you would use it for - and why you would like to buy it.

Simple Model Answer

An unusual vehicle I would like to buy is a small helicopter. The idea came from a television programme about someone who owned one. His helicopter was blue and had two seats, which is the kind I imagine having.

Of course, I would first need proper training and permission to fly. If I could use one, I would enjoy taking short trips and seeing places from the air. Flying above cities or mountains with a family member would be a memorable experience. Avoiding long traffic jams is another part of the attraction.

I am also interested in helicopter landing areas on top of tall buildings. Landing there seems very different from arriving by car. However, I realise owning a helicopter is not a realistic plan for me now. It would be expensive, and finding suitable places to land would require care. I treat it mainly as a dream rather than a serious shopping decision. Still, if I ever had enough money, perhaps through winning the lottery, I would love to explore the possibility.

Key words and phrases

suitable places
Locations that are appropriate for a particular purpose.
explore the possibility
Find out whether something could actually be done.

Advanced Model Answer

I would like to buy my own helicopter. I recently saw a TV programme about someone who flew around in his own helicopter, and I remember thinking that it would be great to have one. The helicopter on the programme was a small, blue, two-seater and it seemed quite easy to fly - that's the kind of thing I'd like to buy.

Ideally, I would use my helicopter instead of a car. First, I would need to learn to fly it, but then I would use it to go on short trips or holidays. It would be fantastic to be able to avoid traffic jams, and get everywhere in a fraction of the time it normally takes.

I would take friends and family sightseeing, over cities or mountain tops, and maybe I could even use it to do the shopping! The reason I would like to buy a helicopter is that you can park one almost anywhere. You do not need to find a runway or an airport like you would for a plane.

So I would be able to park my helicopter in my garden - if I had a bigger garden. Also, I have always been intrigued by those helipads on the tops of tall buildings - I think it would be fun to land on top of a building. A helicopter probably isn't a realistic choice of vehicle, but you never know - I might win the lottery!

Key words and phrases

avoid traffic jams

park one almost anywhere

intrigued by
Very interested in discovering more about.
a realistic choice of vehicle

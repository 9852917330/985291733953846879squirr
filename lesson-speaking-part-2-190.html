Situation

Good service at a music shop

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an occasion when you received good service from a company or shop.

You should say: - What the service was. - When and where it happened. - Whom you were with. - How you felt about it. - Explain why you think it was good service.

Simple Model Answer

An occasion when I received good service was at a music shop a couple of months ago. After watching an inspiring concert, I decided to learn guitar. The following weekend, my flatmate came with me to help choose an instrument because he already played a little.

We noticed a shop window full of guitars and went inside. A young assistant greeted us warmly. I explained that I was a complete beginner and did not understand the differences between acoustic guitars. He patiently described the models, materials, prices and the way different types of wood affected the sound.

I appreciated that he listened to what I needed instead of immediately recommending the most expensive guitar. There was a lot to understand, but he did not make me feel foolish for asking basic questions. My flatmate was impressed too. We left agreeing that we would return when I had a clearer idea of what to buy. For me, the good service came from sharing knowledge honestly and giving a new customer time to make a sensible choice.

Key words and phrases

complete beginner
Someone who has no previous experience of an activity.
sensible choice
A practical and reasonable decision.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about how impressed I was with the service I got at a music shop a couple of months ago.

I'd just watched a really inspiring concert with my flatmate, and by the end of it I'd decided, completely on a whim, that I wanted to learn guitar. So the following weekend, my flatmate came with me into town to help me find a shop, since he actually plays a bit himself and knew roughly what to look for. As we were walking along one of the main shopping streets, a window full of guitars and other instruments caught my eye, so we went in for a look.

A young shop assistant came over almost straight away and greeted us with a genuine smile. After a bit of friendly small talk, I explained that I'd never played guitar in my life and had no idea what actually separated one acoustic guitar from another. He talked us through the different makes and models, whether they were factory-made or handmade, the type of wood used, how that affected the sound, and, of course, the price range. I'll admit I felt a little out of my depth as a total beginner, but I really appreciated how patient he was about it.

Honestly, it's rare these days to come across such attentive customer service. This assistant clearly knew his stuff, and more importantly, he took the time to actually listen to what I needed rather than just pushing the most expensive option. My flatmate ended up being just as impressed, and we both agreed, walking out, that we'd definitely be back once I'd learned enough to know what I was doing.

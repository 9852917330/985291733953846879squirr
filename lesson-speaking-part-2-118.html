A long walk you had

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a long walk you had

You should say: where you went to when you went who you went with And explain how you felt about this walk.

Simple Model Answer

A long walk I remember was around Hoan Kiem Lake one evening. I often walk there alone when work or study makes me feel stressed, but that particular night I had also argued with my best friend.

He had questioned personal decisions that I felt were not his business. The conversation upset me enough to make me think about our friendship. Rather than stay at home feeling angry, I went out for some fresh air.

The lake was peaceful, with a wide pavement and space to walk without rushing. There were police nearby, which helped me feel comfortable in the area. After walking for a while, I sat on a bench and looked at the stars. I did not solve every problem that evening, but the quiet surroundings helped me calm down and think more clearly. By the time I returned home, I felt better prepared for the next day. The experience reminded me that a simple walk can create useful distance between a difficult conversation and my reaction to it.

Key words and phrases

calm down
Become less angry, worried or excited.
think more clearly
Understand a situation with a calmer and more organised mind.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about a long walk I took recently, around Hoan Kiem Lake. I actually do this walk quite often, usually late in the evening and always on my own, whenever I'm feeling under pressure from work or school.

That night, the fresh air and tranquil atmosphere around the lake made me feel relaxed but still quite energetic, and it helped me clear my mind and get ready for whatever the next day would bring. There's also a visible police presence around that whole area, so I felt completely safe and didn't have to worry about being pickpocketed or anything like that - I could just focus on enjoying the walk.

There's a long, spacious pavement all the way around the lake where people go jogging or walking, so after I'd walked for a while, I just sat down on a bench and took in the atmosphere for a bit - mostly just staring up at the stars, trying to let my mind switch off and unwind.

The reason I went for that walk in the first place was because I'd just had a huge argument with my best friend - he'd started questioning things about my life that really weren't his business, and it left me feeling pretty awful, to the point where I even started questioning our whole friendship. Everything just felt a bit surreal that evening, so I went out looking for something that would calm me down.

under pressure

Clothes

Clothes you dislike, and how your style has changed

Part 1

Prompt

How important are clothes and fashion to you? What kind of clothes do you dislike and why? How different are the clothes you wear now from those you wore 10 years ago? What do you think the clothes we wear say about us?

How important are clothes and fashion to you?

Simple Model Answer

Clothes matter because I want to feel comfortable and dress properly for each situation. I like looking neat, but I don't follow every new style. People should choose what suits them.

Key words and phrases

what suits them
What is right for their needs, taste or appearance.

Advanced Model Answer

Clothes matter to me mainly in terms of comfort and appropriateness. I like to look presentable, but I don't follow every trend, because style is a matter of personal choice.

Key words and phrases

a matter of personal choice
Something each person should decide.

What kind of clothes do you dislike and why?

Simple Model Answer

I don't like very tight clothes, large brand names or clothes that are hard to wash. Even if they look fashionable, I won't buy them unless they feel comfortable for daily use.

Key words and phrases

daily use
Regular use in normal everyday situations.

Advanced Model Answer

I dislike clothes that are overly tight, heavily branded or difficult to care for. Even if something looks fashionable, I won't buy it unless it's comfortable enough for daily wear.

Key words and phrases

comfortable enough for daily wear
Pleasant to wear every day.

How different are the clothes you wear now from those you wore 10 years ago?

Simple Model Answer

My clothes are simpler now. Ten years ago, I tried brighter colours, but these days I prefer plain colours that I can wear in different places and situations.

Key words and phrases

these days
During the present period of your life.

Advanced Model Answer

My clothes are simpler and more practical now. Ten years ago I experimented with brighter colours, but these days I prefer neutral items that work in several settings.

Key words and phrases

work in several settings
Be suitable in different situations.

What do you think the clothes we wear say about us?

Simple Model Answer

Clothes may suggest someone's job or taste, but they do not tell us everything about their character. Sometimes people dress a certain way because others expect them to.

Key words and phrases

a certain way
A particular manner or style.

Advanced Model Answer

Clothing can suggest someone's profession, taste or attitude to formality, but it's not a complete picture of their character. People sometimes dress to meet expectations rather than to express their real personality.

Key words and phrases

express their real personality
Show who they really are.

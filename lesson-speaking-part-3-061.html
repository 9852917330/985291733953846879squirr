Leisure & Holidays

Leisure and the economy

Prompt

What is the relationship between leisure and the economy? How does the economy benefit from people's leisure activities? Do men and women enjoy the same type of leisure activities?

What is the relationship between leisure and the economy?

Simple Model Answer

Leisure is an important part of the economy because people spend money on restaurants, films, sport and holidays. Those activities provide many jobs. However, this spending can fall quickly when families have less money, since entertainment is easier to cut than food or housing. So leisure supports the economy but can also be especially vulnerable during difficult periods.

Key words and phrases

vulnerable
Easily affected or harmed by a problem.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, people spend a huge amount of money on all sorts of leisure activities, so I'd say leisure is a genuinely important part of the economy in most countries. Leisure could be anything people do in their free time — eating out, going to the cinema, watching a football match, or staying in a hotel — and millions of people make a living from these industries. What I'd add, though, is that leisure spending is one of the first things households cut when money's tight, so it's also one of the more exposed parts of the economy.

Key words and phrases

make a living from
earn enough money to support yourself by doing
exposed parts of the economy
parts of the economy most likely to be harmed

How does the economy benefit from people's leisure activities?

Simple Model Answer

Leisure businesses employ people who then spend their wages and pay taxes, so the money supports other parts of the economy too. Tourism also brings income from visitors outside the country. However, some jobs are seasonal or poorly paid. The industry may create wealth without giving every worker a secure or comfortable living.

Key words and phrases

seasonal
Available or happening only during certain parts of the year.

Advanced Model Answer

The leisure industry makes a huge contribution to the economy. It keeps millions of people in employment, and those employees pay tax and spend their wages on other goods and services, so the money keeps moving round the economy rather than sitting still. Tourism works the same way but on a bigger scale, because it pulls in money from outside the country altogether. The one qualification I'd make is that a lot of leisure jobs are seasonal or poorly paid, so the economy benefits even when the people doing the work don't benefit all that much.

Key words and phrases

poorly paid
receiving too little money for the work

Do men and women enjoy the same type of leisure activities?

Simple Model Answer

They share many interests, such as restaurants and cinema, although some activities attract more of one gender. Football matches have traditionally been an example. However, those differences are changing, and habits do not prove a natural preference. I would consider the individual rather than assume men and women must enjoy separate kinds of leisure.

Key words and phrases

gender
Being male, female or another gender identity.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes and no. I think both men and women enjoy things like eating in restaurants or going to the cinema, but I'm sure there are other activities that are more popular with one gender. For example, I think more men than women go to football matches. I'd be careful about generalising, though, because those differences seem to be narrowing. Women's football has grown enormously in the last few years, and a lot of the differences people assume are natural turn out to be mostly habit.

Key words and phrases

more popular with one gender
Attracting one gender more than another.
narrowing
becoming smaller or more limited
mostly habit
mainly something people do because it is familiar

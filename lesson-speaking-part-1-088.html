Warm-Up

Your name and its meaning

Part 1

Prompt

Could you tell me your full name please? What is your name? Does your name have any special meaning? Why do many people change their names?

Could you tell me your full name please?

Simple Model Answer

My full name is Nguyen Nhat Dien, but most people call me Dien. It is quite easy to use, so I rarely have to explain it or make it shorter.

Key words and phrases

full name
Your complete name, including all its parts.

Advanced Model Answer

My full name is Nguyen Nhat Dien, although people usually call me Dien. It's a straightforward name, so I rarely need to explain or shorten it.

Key words and phrases

a straightforward name
A name that is clear and uncomplicated.

What is your name?

Simple Model Answer

My name is Nguyen Nhat Dien. Most people just call me Dien, so that is the name I'm most used to hearing in everyday conversations.

Key words and phrases

used to hearing
Familiar with hearing something often.

Advanced Model Answer

My name's Nguyen Nhat Dien, but almost everyone just calls me Dien — it's the name I answer to without even thinking.

Key words and phrases

without even thinking
Automatically, without conscious attention.

Does your name have any special meaning?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, it has a positive meaning in Vietnamese. My family chose it, and it is important to me because it reminds me of them and feels like part of who I am.

Key words and phrases

positive
Expressing something good or hopeful.

Advanced Model Answer

It's got a positive meaning in Vietnamese, and it was chosen by my family. I value it mainly because it connects me with them and gives me a sense of identity.

Key words and phrases

a sense of identity
A feeling of knowing who you are.

Why do many people change their names?

Simple Model Answer

People change names for marriage, moving countries, personal reasons or work. A different name can be easier to use, but making the change official often means completing many forms.

Key words and phrases

official
Formally accepted or recorded by the relevant authorities.

Advanced Model Answer

People may change their names because of marriage, migration, personal identity or professional reasons. A new name can make communication easier, although changing it isn't always simple — it usually involves a great deal of paperwork.

Key words and phrases

a great deal of paperwork
Many forms and documents to complete.

Film & Television

Whether cinema is gaining or losing ground

Part 3

Prompt

Do you think the cinema has increased or decreased in popularity in recent years? In your opinion, will this trend continue into the future? What are the advantages and disadvantages of making films of real-life events?

Do you think the cinema has increased or decreased in popularity in recent years?

Simple Model Answer

I think cinema has become less common for everyday viewing because streaming offers many films for a low monthly cost. However, big films and large screens still attract audiences. The cinema has changed from the usual way to watch any film into a special outing that people choose and plan more carefully.

Key words and phrases

outing
A trip away from home for enjoyment.

Advanced Model Answer

Overall it's declined, and streaming is the obvious reason — for the price of one ticket you get a month of viewing at home. But it hasn't collapsed either. Big spectacle films still fill cinemas, and premium formats with large screens and proper sound have actually grown. What's changed is the occasion: going to the cinema used to be the default way to see a film, and now it's a deliberate outing that people plan.

Key words and phrases

premium formats
more expensive ways of seeing a film with special features
the occasion
the event or experience itself

In your opinion, will this trend continue into the future?

Simple Model Answer

Probably. Streaming is convenient, while cinemas will need to offer excellent sound, large screens and a shared experience. There may be fewer cinemas, but more special screenings and live broadcasts. Smaller independent cinemas could struggle because they cannot easily compete on either price or expensive technology, even if some audiences value their different choices.

Key words and phrases

independent cinemas
Cinemas run separately from large commercial chains.

Advanced Model Answer

I think so. Streaming will remain dominant for everyday viewing, because convenience nearly always wins. Cinemas will survive by offering what a living room genuinely can't — scale, sound, and the experience of watching with an audience, which does change how a comedy or a horror film feels. I'd expect fewer cinemas overall but better ones, used increasingly for live broadcasts and re-releases rather than only for new films on their opening weekend. Smaller independent cinemas are the ones I'd worry about, since they can't easily compete on either price or spectacle.

Key words and phrases

watching with an audience
seeing a film together with other people
live broadcasts and re-releases
shows sent out as they happen and old films shown again

What are the advantages and disadvantages of making films of real-life events?

Simple Model Answer

Films can introduce a historical event to many people and make it feel real. However, a clear dramatic story often needs shortened events or invented conversations. Viewers may then remember fiction as fact, which can unfairly affect real people. Still, an interesting film may encourage viewers to read the actual history afterwards, so it can start useful learning.

Key words and phrases

invented conversations
Dialogue created by a writer rather than taken from a real record.

Advanced Model Answer

The advantage is reach. A well-made film about a historical event will be seen by far more people than any book on the subject, and it can make something abstract feel immediate. The problem is that drama requires simplification — you need clear heroes, a narrative arc and often invented dialogue. Audiences then remember the film version as fact, and that's considerably more serious when the events are recent and the people involved are still alive to be misrepresented. It's not all bad, though — a film that gets a few details wrong can still send people off to read the real history afterwards, which a textbook alone rarely manages.

Key words and phrases

requires simplification
needs to be made simpler
remember the film version as fact
incorrectly think the film's account is historically true

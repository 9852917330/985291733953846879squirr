Colours

Colours you like and how they affect mood

Prompt

What colours do you like? Do colours affect your mood? Do you wear bright clothes?

What colours do you like?

Simple Model Answer

I especially like blue because it makes me think of calm, clean places. It also looks good in clothes and rooms without being too bright or noticeable.

Key words and phrases

looks good
Has an attractive appearance.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm particularly fond of blue because it feels calm and clean. It also works well in clothing and interior design without drawing too much attention.

Key words and phrases

drawing too much attention
Making people notice you excessively.

Do colours affect your mood?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, a little. Soft, natural colours help me feel relaxed, but a large area of very bright colour can make me tired. The effect is small, but I notice it over time.

Key words and phrases

over time
Gradually, as time passes.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, to some extent. Soft, natural colours help me relax, but extremely bright colours can feel tiring when they cover a large space. The effect is subtle but noticeable over time.

Key words and phrases

to some extent
Partly, but not completely.
noticeable over time
Becoming clear as time passes.

Do you wear bright clothes?

Simple Model Answer

Sometimes, but not very often. I might wear one bright piece of clothing with plain colours. That way, my clothes still look simple and go well together.

Key words and phrases

go well together
Look or work nicely when combined.

Advanced Model Answer

Only occasionally. I may wear one bright item, but I usually combine it with neutral colours so that the overall look remains simple and balanced.

Key words and phrases

simple and balanced
Uncomplicated with parts that work together.

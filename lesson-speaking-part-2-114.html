Activity

A holiday you would like to take in the future

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a holiday you would like to take in the future

You should say: - When and where you would like to go - Who you would like to go with - What you would like to do on this holiday - Why you would like to take the holiday

Simple Model Answer

A holiday I would like to take is a family trip to Vinh Hy Bay during my children's summer break. A friend recommended it because of its beautiful scenery, quiet beaches and fresh seafood.

It is about four hours from our city by car. We would drive slowly and stop at interesting places along the way instead of rushing. I have wanted to visit for a long time, but my busy work schedule has made it difficult. This summer seems like a good opportunity for us to go together.

We would mainly relax on the beach and enjoy meals as a family. I would bring UNO cards and tools for making sandcastles so the children had something to do. We do not need a holiday full of organised activities. The main purpose is to spend time together away from the pressure of city life. I also think it would be a nice reward for the children after a year of studying. I hope we come home feeling rested and closer to one another.

Key words and phrases

busy work schedule
A working timetable with many tasks and little spare time.
organised activities
Planned events or things arranged for people to do.

Advanced Model Answer

I have itchy feet, so there's basically always a detailed plan for the next family holiday sitting in the back of my mind. This time, I'm waiting for my kids' summer vacation so the whole family can go away together. I'm a family person at heart, and summer just isn't complete without suntans and beach photos, so it has to be somewhere on the coast - and Vinh Hy Bay is my top choice. A friend of mine raved about it after her own trip there; apparently it's known for its stunning scenery and cheap, fresh seafood.

I've wanted to go for ages but could never find the time, thanks to my tight working schedule. This summer, though, we're finally making it happen. It's only about a four-hour drive from our city, so we'll take our time getting there, stopping wherever looks interesting along the way rather than rushing. One thing I love about Vinh Hy is that it's still pretty untouched - it's not overrun with tourists, so the food stays reasonably priced and the beaches stay quiet.

Unlike other beaches that are packed with water sports like canoeing, scuba diving and jet-skiing, there isn't really much to do in Vinh Hy besides sunbathing and eating seafood - but honestly, that suits us fine, since this trip is really just about being together as a family. In case the kids get restless, I'm planning to bring some games, like UNO, and a few sandcastle-building tools to keep them busy on the beach.

I'm honestly in serious need of this holiday. I want to escape the stress and pressure of city life for a while with my spouse and kids, and I think it's also a nice way to reward the children after a hard year of studying.

Key words and phrases

have itchy feet
Feel eager to travel.
a detailed plan
A plan with specific steps.
summer vacation
Time off during the summer.
tight working schedule
Very little spare time between work duties.
in serious need of
Needing something badly.

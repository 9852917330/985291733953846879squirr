Hobbies

A spare-time activity and how long you have done it

Part 1

Prompt

What do you do in your spare time? How long have you been interested in this activity? Do you do this alone or with other people? How much time do you spend doing this activity?

What do you do in your spare time?

Simple Model Answer

I usually go to the gym, read or take a quiet walk. I choose things that help me feel rested and ready for the next part of my day.

Key words and phrases

rested
Feeling less tired after relaxing or sleeping.

Advanced Model Answer

In my spare time I usually train at the gym, read or go for a quiet walk. I prefer activities that leave me feeling refreshed rather than just filling the time.

Key words and phrases

In my spare time
When I am not working or occupied.

How long have you been interested in this activity?

Simple Model Answer

I've been lifting weights for several years. At first, I just wanted to get fit. Now I enjoy seeing small improvements, which makes the activity feel worthwhile.

Key words and phrases

worthwhile
Useful or enjoyable enough to deserve your time.

Advanced Model Answer

I've been strength training for several years now. I started just to get fit, but tracking small improvements is what's made it personally rewarding.

Key words and phrases

personally rewarding
Giving me satisfaction and a sense of benefit.

Do you do this alone or with other people?

Simple Model Answer

I do some hobbies alone because I can concentrate better. I also exercise or go out with friends, so I get both time to myself and time with other people.

Key words and phrases

time to myself
Time spent alone doing what I choose.

Advanced Model Answer

I do some hobbies alone because being on my own helps me concentrate, but I also exercise or go out with friends. That combination lets me strike a healthy balance between independence and social contact.

Key words and phrases

strike a healthy balance
Give different needs sensible amounts of attention.

How much time do you spend doing this activity?

Simple Model Answer

I spend about four or five hours a week on it. That gives me enough practice to improve while still leaving time for my other responsibilities.

Key words and phrases

responsibilities
Things you need to do or take care of.

Advanced Model Answer

I spend roughly four or five hours a week on it. That's enough to make progress without letting the hobby take over my schedule.

Key words and phrases

take over my schedule
Use up nearly all my available time.

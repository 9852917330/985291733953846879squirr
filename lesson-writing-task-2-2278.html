Health

Healthier in the countryside or the city?

Discuss Both Views

Prompt

Many people believe that it is easier to have a healthy lifestyle in the countryside. Others believe that there are health benefits to living in cities. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

The countryside is usually pictured as clean air and fresh vegetables, and the city as noise, traffic and takeaway food. Both settings carry genuine advantages, and I believe the city has the stronger claim once medical care is taken into account.

Rural living clearly helps in some respects. The air is cleaner, which matters enormously for children with asthma and for anyone with a weak heart. Food is often grown nearby and eaten in season. Physical work and long walks build activity into the day rather than requiring a gym, and quieter surroundings and darker nights produce better sleep, which affects almost every measure of health.

Cities answer with different strengths. Hospitals, specialists and emergency services are concentrated there, and for a stroke or a difficult birth the distance to a surgeon decides the outcome. Urban residents also have more choice: a supermarket carries fresh food all year, and pools, parks and clubs make exercise easy to sustain. Walking to a station or a shop means many city dwellers move more than they realise, while rural life increasingly means driving everywhere. Loneliness, which harms health as much as smoking does, is easier to escape where people are close together.

My own view is that the countryside offers the better environment and the city the better safety net, and the safety net wins. Clean air cannot compensate for an ambulance that takes fifty minutes, and rural areas now suffer from shortages of doctors and few services for the old. The healthiest arrangement is a city with parks, cycle routes and controlled traffic, since it keeps medical access while borrowing what the countryside does well.

In conclusion, each setting protects health in its own way. Given the weight of medical care and social contact, city living is on balance the healthier option, provided that pollution and inactivity are actively managed.

Key words and phrases

move more than they realise
Get physical activity without consciously noticing it.
borrowing what the countryside does well
Adopting beneficial features of rural life.

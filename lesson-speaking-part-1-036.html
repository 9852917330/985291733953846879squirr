Health & Fitness

The exercise you do and why it matters

Part 1

Prompt

What kind of exercise do you do regularly? What kind of exercise do you enjoy least? Why is exercise good for health? What kind of exercise would you suggest to someone who is very unfit?

What kind of exercise do you do regularly?

Simple Model Answer

I lift weights and walk quickly several times a week. Together, these activities help me stay fit, and I can fit them into my normal schedule without needing special facilities for every session.

Key words and phrases

fit them into
Find time for them in a schedule.

Advanced Model Answer

I do strength training and brisk walking several times a week. The combination improves fitness without requiring specialised facilities and fits naturally into my routine.

Key words and phrases

fits naturally into my routine
Works easily with my existing habits.

What kind of exercise do you enjoy least?

Simple Model Answer

I like long-distance running least because the repeated movement can feel uncomfortable. I prefer activities that build strength, help me move better and let me improve gradually.

Key words and phrases

gradually
Slowly, through small changes over time.

Advanced Model Answer

I enjoy long-distance running least because repetitive impact can be uncomfortable. I prefer exercise that combines strength, mobility and steady progress.

Key words and phrases

steady progress
Regular, continuing improvement.

Why is exercise good for health?

Simple Model Answer

Exercise helps the heart and muscles, improves sleep and makes stress easier to manage. It can also help us keep doing everyday things as we get older, which matters more than appearance.

Key words and phrases

appearance
The way someone or something looks.

Advanced Model Answer

Exercise strengthens the heart and muscles, improves sleep and helps regulate stress. It's also good for long-term independence, which is often more important than appearance.

Key words and phrases

more important than appearance
Matters more than how something looks.

What kind of exercise would you suggest to someone who is very unfit?

Simple Model Answer

I'd suggest walking every day and doing simple exercises using their own body weight. These are cheap and can be made easier or harder, helping the person improve and feel more confident.

Key words and phrases

body weight
The weight of your own body used as resistance in exercise.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd suggest daily walking and simple body-weight exercises. They're low-cost, easy to adjust and allow an unfit person to build confidence gradually.

Key words and phrases

build confidence gradually
Become more self-assured over time.

Food & Agriculture

Fruit production and fertiliser use

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The first graph below shows the weight (in tonnes) of fruit produced by a farm during each month in 2013, and also the amount (in tonnes) of fertiliser used. The second graph shows the volume of rainfall in each month (in millimetres). Write a report summarising the information. Select and describe the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The charts give information about the tonnage of fruit produced by a farm in each month of 2013, the amount of fertiliser used, and the volume of rainfall in that year.

Overall, the farm generally used the most fertiliser at times of high rainfall, and by contrast it produced the most fruit when both rainfall and fertiliser use were around their lowest.

Fruit production rose steadily from just over 2 tonnes in January to a peak of roughly 4.2 tonnes by August. Output then fell rapidly, declining to around the initial 2 tonne mark in December.

Fertiliser use followed a largely reversed seasonal pattern. The greatest amount, just under 1 tonne, was used in April, and this was followed by a marked decline to zero in June and July. Fertiliser consumption then increased slightly in the autumn, rising to a total of around 0.7 tonnes before dipping to just below 0.5 tonnes at the end of the year.

Rainfall appeared to reflect the use of fertiliser, with an early high of 16mm in February, falling consistently to 4mm in July, August and September. Rainfall then increased steadily to reach 14mm in December.

Key words and phrases

followed a largely reversed seasonal pattern
Seasonal rises and falls were mostly opposite.
a marked decline to zero
A clear fall to nothing.
before dipping to just below 0.5 tonnes
Then falling slightly below 0.5 tonnes.
appeared to reflect the use of fertiliser
Seemed connected to fertiliser use.

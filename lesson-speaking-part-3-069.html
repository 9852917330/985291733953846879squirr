Music

Music young people like, and what technology changed

Part 3

Prompt

What kinds of music are popular with young people in your country? How has technology affected the music that is popular with young people? What do you think influences a young person's taste in music?

What kinds of music are popular with young people in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Pop, hip-hop, electronic dance music and K-pop are very popular. Young people discover songs through streaming and short videos, so their choices are much more international than my parents' were. A song from another country can become familiar quickly. Local music still matters, but it now competes with a much wider range of music.

Key words and phrases

streaming
Listening to or watching online content without downloading the whole file first.

Advanced Model Answer

Pop and hip-hop dominate, along with electronic dance music and Korean pop, which has got an enormous following here. What strikes me is how international the taste's become. My parents' generation listened almost entirely to domestic music because that's what was available, whereas young people now discover things through streaming and short videos, so a song from anywhere can become popular here within days. Local music's still strong, but it competes globally now in a way it never used to.

Key words and phrases

an enormous following
a very large group of fans
competes globally now
now competes with others worldwide

How has technology affected the music that is popular with young people?

Simple Model Answer

Technology makes it easier to create music on a laptop, so artists do not always need a record company. It also changes discovery through recommendations made by apps. These can help listeners find songs, but often suggest similar music repeatedly. As a result, people may have more choice available while actually exploring fewer different styles.

Key words and phrases

recommendations
Suggestions about things you might like or find useful.

Advanced Model Answer

In two significant ways, really. Production first — you can record something decent on a laptop these days, so artists don't need a record label to be heard, and that's opened things up to people who'd never have got through the old system. The second is discovery: recommendation algorithms decide a huge amount of what people actually hear, and because they keep suggesting what resembles what you already like, taste can narrow even while choice technically expands.

Key words and phrases

recommendation algorithms
computer systems that suggest content based on behaviour

What do you think influences a young person's taste in music?

Simple Model Answer

Friends strongly influence teenagers because music helps show which group they belong to. Family introduces music earlier, while films, games and online platforms add new choices. Personal memories matter too. A song linked to an important period can remain special even when friends dislike it, so musical taste is not only a result of outside influence.

Key words and phrases

musical taste
The types of music a person enjoys.

Advanced Model Answer

Friends most of all, especially in the teenage years, because music's partly a way of signalling which group you belong to. Family matters earlier on, since people often keep an attachment to whatever was played at home. Beyond that, films, games and online platforms introduce a lot too. But there's something genuinely personal in it as well: people attach songs to particular periods of their life, and that connection has nothing to do with what anybody else thinks.

Key words and phrases

signalling which group you belong to
showing others which community you see yourself as part of
keep an attachment to
continue feeling connected to
nothing to do
No activity available to do.

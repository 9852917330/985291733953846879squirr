Global Issues

Do countries need each other more than ever?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

As we are facing more and more problems which affect the whole planet, good relationships between different countries are becoming more important than ever before. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

The largest problems now facing humanity do not respect borders, which has led to the claim that cooperation between states matters more than at any previous point in history. I agree with this, because the specific threats we face cannot be solved by any country acting alone.

The clearest case is the atmosphere. Emissions released in one country warm the whole planet, so a state that reduces its own output while others expand theirs achieves nothing except its own economic disadvantage. This is why agreements matter more than national policies: cooperation is not a moral preference here but the only arrangement in which any effort produces a result at all.

Disease works the same way. A virus that appears in one city reaches every continent within weeks, and the speed of detection depends entirely on whether governments share information honestly and quickly. Countries that concealed early outbreaks damaged themselves as much as their neighbours, and vaccine development during the last pandemic depended on laboratories in several countries exchanging results within days.

The main counter-argument is that cooperation often fails when interests genuinely conflict, and that agreements are ignored the moment they become expensive. This is a fair description of how international politics frequently works, but it argues for building stronger institutions rather than for abandoning the attempt, since the alternative leaves every country worse off.

In conclusion, I agree that good relations between countries have never mattered more. Climate change, pandemics and the regulation of new technologies are problems whose causes and effects cross borders, and no government, however powerful, can protect its own citizens from them by acting within its own territory.

Key words and phrases

produces a result at all
Achieves any useful outcome.
exchanging results within days
Sharing findings within a short period.
leaves every country worse off
Makes the situation worse for all countries.
by acting within its own territory
Through measures taken inside its own borders.

A future aim

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a future aim.

You should say: - what the aim is - when you hope to achieve it - what you need to do to reach your goal - and why this goal is important to you.

Simple Model Answer

A future aim of mine is to write a book, probably a novel. I enjoy reading and have often wondered whether I could create a story that other people would want to follow. I imagine using some of my experiences and ideas while inventing the characters and events.

At the moment, I do not have a clear plot, so this is a long-term ambition rather than an immediate plan. Perhaps I would begin after retirement, although a good idea might encourage me to start sooner. I know writing a complete novel would require much more than an exciting first chapter.

To achieve it, I would need a routine, such as writing for a fixed time every day. I would also need patience to continue when the story became difficult or my motivation fell. What attracts me is the challenge of making something from my imagination. My favourite authors have given me many enjoyable hours, and I would like to try creating a similar experience for someone else, even if the book never became widely known.

Key words and phrases

plot
The main events and structure of a story.
long-term ambition
Something important that someone hopes to achieve in the future.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe a future aim of mine, which is to write a book. I'd love to write a novel - something fictional, but probably be influenced by a lot of my own experiences, ideas and opinions. The trouble is, I honestly have no idea where I'd start or what it would even be about, so realistically I don't think I'll achieve this until much later in life.

It might end up being something I take up as a hobby once I retire, or maybe I'll suddenly get inspired and start much sooner than that - right now it's really just a vague objective in the back of my mind. I imagine it must be incredibly hard to find the motivation to write a novel, so more than anything, I'd need time, commitment and enough passion to keep working until I actually finished it.

I'd also need to set myself a target, like writing for a fixed amount of time every day, and of course I'd need a decent idea for a story in the first place! What draws me to this goal is that I've always wondered how people manage to bring a story to life out of nothing, and whether I'd be capable of doing the same. I've always loved reading, so I'd really like to follow in the footsteps of my favourite authors one day.

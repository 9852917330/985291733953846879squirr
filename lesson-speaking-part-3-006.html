Animals

How humans differ from animals, and what animals are for

Part 3

Prompt

In what ways are humans different from other animals? What are the main roles of animals in your country?

In what ways are humans different from other animals?

Simple Model Answer

One important difference is how humans share knowledge across generations. We use complex language to record discoveries, so each generation can build on earlier work. We also plan far into the future, such as saving for retirement. Some animals learn traditions too, so the difference is not complete, but human knowledge develops on a much larger scale.

Key words and phrases

across generations
From older people to younger people over time.

Advanced Model Answer

The fundamental difference is cumulative culture. Other animals learn, and some use tools, but each generation largely starts again, whereas humans pass down knowledge in a form that accumulates, so we build on what previous generations worked out — nobody has to reinvent the wheel every generation. Complex language is what makes that possible. Long-term planning is the other major difference: humans will plant an orchard they won't see fruit from, or save for a pension decades away, which almost no other species does in any meaningful sense — though a few animals, like some whales, do pass on narrow local traditions of their own, so the line isn't quite absolute.

Key words and phrases

cumulative culture
knowledge and practices built and passed on over generations
reinvent the wheel
create again something that already exists
in any meaningful sense
in a way that makes a real difference

What are the main roles of animals in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Animals provide food, including meat, eggs, milk and fish. They also support farming and nature by spreading pollen and controlling pests. Machines have replaced many jobs once done by working animals. Meanwhile, more families keep pets for company. People often care deeply about pets while thinking less about farm animals, which shows that our attitudes can be inconsistent.

Key words and phrases

inconsistent
Not following the same ideas or standards in different situations.

Advanced Model Answer

Agriculture's still the largest, providing meat, eggs, milk and fish, though mechanisation has removed most of the working roles that buffalo and horses once had. Then there's the ecological function, which people rarely think about — pollination, pest control, maintaining soil — and agriculture depends on it entirely. The fastest-growing role is companionship: urban families increasingly keep pets, and in smaller households the animal's treated much like a family member. It's a slightly uneven picture, though — people who'd never eat their own dog give barely a thought to the animals raised for their dinner, which shows how much the "role" of an animal depends on which category we've mentally put it in.

Key words and phrases

pest control
methods used to stop harmful insects or animals
treated much like a family member
cared for almost as if it were part of the family

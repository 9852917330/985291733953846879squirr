Situation

A recent change in your life

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a recent change in your life

You should say: What the change was When it happened How you felt about it And explain if it was a positive or negative change for you

OUTLINE

>>> graduate + start full-time work
suppose one biggest turning points life graduated uni couple months
looked job began working full-time right after not time take deep
not get learn lot school always irritated poor attitude staff there
element working bc constantly learn new things deal w/ new challenges
already worked part-time uni made not nearly enough save rainy day
Personally feel change positive impact me

Simple Model Answer

A recent change in my life was graduating from university and starting full-time work a couple of months ago. I began looking for jobs immediately and found one quickly, so the move from student life happened quite fast.

Unlike some people, I was relieved to leave university. I had often felt disappointed by the course and wanted to become financially independent. Although I worked part time while studying, the income was not enough to save much or help my family.

Working full time has suited me better. I learn new things regularly and deal with practical challenges, which makes me feel more involved. Earning enough to cover my own needs is especially important. I no longer have to depend on my parents in the same way, and I hope to support them more in the future. The job is not always easy, and I am still adjusting to the routine. Even so, I see this as a positive change because it has given me greater responsibility, confidence and control over my own life.

Key words and phrases

financially independent
Able to pay for your needs without relying on someone else's money.
adjusting to
Gradually becoming comfortable with a new situation.

Advanced Model Answer

I suppose one of the biggest turning points in my life was when I graduated from university just a couple of months ago. I started job-hunting straight away and began working full-time almost immediately, so I haven't really had time to take a deep breath and reflect on such a big change.

People always say university is one of the best periods of your life and that you'll miss it once you're working, but that's not true for me at all. I was honestly relieved when I finally got my degree. I didn't feel like I learned much there, and I was constantly irritated by how poor the staff's attitude was. All I wanted for those four years was to get a job so I could support myself, and eventually my family too, and now that's finally come true.

I feel much more in my element working than I ever did studying, mainly because I'm constantly learning new things and having to deal with fresh challenges every week. But honestly, the biggest change is that I can now take care of myself financially and don't have to rely on my family any more. I did work part-time during university, but what I earned then wasn't nearly enough to save for a rainy day, let alone help support my family.

So although the job isn't all plain sailing, I'm still really glad I've made it this far, and personally, I feel this change has had a very positive impact on my life.

Key words and phrases

take a deep breath and reflect on
pause, breathe slowly, and think carefully about
in my element
doing something I am especially comfortable and good at
save for a rainy day
keep money for unexpected future needs
isn't all plain sailing
has difficulties and does not always go smoothly

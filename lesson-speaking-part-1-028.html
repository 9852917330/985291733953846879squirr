Food

Cooking at home and family meals

Part 1

Prompt

Do you like cooking? Who does the cooking in your home? Do you think family meals are important? What kind of food do you like?

Do you like cooking?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, but I mainly make simple meals. I enjoy choosing the ingredients myself and changing the flavour until the food tastes the way I like it.

Key words and phrases

ingredients
The foods used to make a dish.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, although I cook simple meals rather than elaborate dishes. I enjoy being able to control the ingredients and adjust flavours to my own taste.

Key words and phrases

to my own taste
Matching what I personally enjoy.

Who does the cooking in your home?

Simple Model Answer

We share the cooking, depending on who has time. This works well because one person does not have to prepare all the meals on their own.

Key words and phrases

share the cooking
Divide the work of preparing meals between people.

Advanced Model Answer

Cooking is shared in my home, depending on who's got time. That arrangement is practical and prevents one person from carrying the entire responsibility.

Key words and phrases

the entire responsibility
The whole duty of managing something.

Do you think family meals are important?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, family meals are important because they give us a regular chance to talk. Even a quick meal together can help us stay close if we give each other our attention.

Key words and phrases

a regular chance
An opportunity that happens often or repeatedly.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, because they create a regular opportunity for people to talk without distractions. Even a short meal can help family members stay connected.

Key words and phrases

stay connected
Keep communicating and feeling close.

What kind of food do you like?

Simple Model Answer

I enjoy fresh Vietnamese dishes, especially vegetables, herbs and light soups. I don't like food with too much oil because it can make me feel full in an uncomfortable way and tired.

Key words and phrases

herbs
Plants or leaves used to add flavour to food.

Advanced Model Answer

I like fresh Vietnamese food, particularly dishes with vegetables, herbs and light broths. I'm less keen on food that's excessively oily because it leaves me feeling heavy and tired.

Key words and phrases

heavy and tired
Feeling physically sluggish and lacking energy.

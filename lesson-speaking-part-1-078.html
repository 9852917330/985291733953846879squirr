Toys

The toys you played with as a child

Prompt

Did you play with toys when you were a child? What kinds of toys did you like? In your country, do boys and girls play with the same types of toys? Do you think that toys help children to learn?

Did you play with toys when you were a child?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I loved playing with toys as a child. They were an enjoyable part of my childhood, and I think many other children feel the same way.

Key words and phrases

childhood
The time in your life when you were a child.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, of course. I loved playing with toys when I was a child; I think all children do.

Key words and phrases

playing with toys
Using toys for enjoyment.

What kinds of toys did you like?

Simple Model Answer

I remember playing mostly with toy cars, planes and figures from films or cartoons. I also liked Lego because I could use the pieces to build different things.

Key words and phrases

figures
Small models shaped like people or characters.

Advanced Model Answer

As far as I remember, I mostly played with toy cars, planes and action figures from films or cartoons. I also liked building things with Lego.

Key words and phrases

As far as I remember
According to what I can recall.

In your country, do boys and girls play with the same types of toys?

Simple Model Answer

Not always. Many boys choose cars or action figures, while some girls prefer dolls. For example, my niece likes putting different clothes on dolls rather than playing with toy cars, although every child is different.

Key words and phrases

action figures
Small toy models of characters from films, games or stories.

Advanced Model Answer

Not really. I think boys like the kinds of toys that I mentioned before, whereas girls play with dolls. My niece, for example, doesn't like toy cars; she prefers dressing dolls in different outfits.

Key words and phrases

different outfits
Different sets of clothes.

Do you think that toys help children to learn?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, toys can help children use their imagination and make new things. Even simple toys let them create situations and invent their own games and rules.

Key words and phrases

invent
Think of or create something new.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I do. All toys encourage children to use their imagination and creativity. Even with simple toys, children imagine situations and invent games and rules.

Key words and phrases

imagination and creativity
The ability to invent ideas and possibilities.

An unforgettable dinner

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an unforgettable dinner

You should say: - When was the dinner. - Where was the dinner. - Who did you dine with. - Why is it unforgettable.

Simple Model Answer

An unforgettable dinner was at my boyfriend's parents' home last summer. It was the first time I met them properly as his girlfriend, so I felt excited and nervous. We chose the tenth because that was the date our relationship had begun.

I decided to prepare a meal instead of eating out, hoping to make a good impression. Unfortunately, I was not a confident cook. After searching online, I chose roast chicken and an olive salad. My boyfriend arranged the table and small lights on the veranda while I worked in the kitchen.

When his parents arrived, the conversation was awkward at first. Then his mother praised the food and began telling funny stories about his childhood. I relaxed, and we ended up talking for hours. The meal itself was a little too salty and looked different from the recipe photos, but nobody seemed to mind. What mattered was getting to know each other. By the end of the evening, I felt welcome in the family, which made the experience much more important than the quality of my cooking.

Key words and phrases

make a good impression
Help someone form a positive opinion of you.
veranda
A covered outdoor area along the side of a house.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about an evening meal I'll never forget: dinner at my boyfriend's parents' house last summer, the first time I met them properly. I was excited but pretty nervous — we'd been at the same high school, so I'd met his mum before, just never as his girlfriend. I picked a weekday, the tenth, because that's the date we started going out, and his parents would both be home by six.

At first we were going to eat out, but then I decided a home-cooked meal would leave a good impression on his parents. That turned out to be a fairly reckless decision, because I genuinely couldn't cook to save my life. After hours of googling recipes and watching cooking videos, I settled on an olive salad and roast chicken, figuring they'd be hard to mess up. While I panicked in the kitchen, my boyfriend was outside setting up the table and fairy lights on the veranda to create a warm atmosphere.

Just as we'd finished, his parents arrived home. We sat down around a small round table, and the first few minutes were pretty awkward because I didn't know what to say. Halfway through the meal, though, his mum complimented me on the food and started telling embarrassing stories about my boyfriend as a kid, which completely eased my nerves. We ended up talking for hours, like old friends.

I'll admit the food itself wasn't a great success — a bit too salty, and nothing like the photos I'd copied — but none of that mattered, because we had such a great time talking. That evening really helped strengthen our relationship, mostly because it made me feel like I was already part of his family.

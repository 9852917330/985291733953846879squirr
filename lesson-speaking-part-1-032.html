Friends

One close friend or a group of friends

Part 1

Prompt

Do you prefer to have one particular friend or a group of friends? What do you like doing most with your friends? Do you think it's important to keep in touch with childhood friends? What makes a good friend?

Do you prefer to have one particular friend or a group of friends?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer a small group of close friends. Each person has different ideas, and I can learn from them. At the same time, I have several people I can depend on.

Key words and phrases

depend on
Trust someone to help or do what you need.

Advanced Model Answer

I prefer a small group of close friends rather than one exclusive friendship. Different friends bring different perspectives, while the group still gives me a reliable support network.

Key words and phrases

a reliable support network
People you can depend on for help.

What do you like doing most with your friends?

Simple Model Answer

We usually have coffee, exercise or go on short trips. I mainly enjoy having time to talk honestly and share things from our daily lives, whatever activity we choose.

Key words and phrases

talk honestly
Say what you really think or feel.

Advanced Model Answer

We usually have coffee, exercise or take short trips. The activity itself isn't the main point; I mainly enjoy having time to talk openly and share everyday experiences.

Key words and phrases

share everyday experiences
Talk about or do ordinary activities together.

Do you think it's important to keep in touch with childhood friends?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, if the friendship is still good for both people. Childhood friends know a lot about your past. However, people change, so staying friends should be a choice rather than a duty.

Key words and phrases

a duty
Something you are expected or required to do.

Advanced Model Answer

It's valuable when the friendship remains healthy, because childhood friends understand your background. But people change, so staying in touch should feel natural rather than a social obligation.

Key words and phrases

a social obligation
Something you feel expected to do socially.

What makes a good friend?

Simple Model Answer

A good friend is honest, reliable and willing to listen. You don't have to agree on everything, but you should respect one another and feel comfortable talking openly.

Key words and phrases

reliable
Someone you can trust to do what they say.

Advanced Model Answer

A good friend is trustworthy, honest and able to listen without immediately judging. Mutual respect matters more than constant agreement because it creates a genuine connection.

Key words and phrases

a genuine connection
An honest, meaningful relationship.

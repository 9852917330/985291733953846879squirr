Situation

A situation when you had to be polite

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a situation when you had to be polite

You should say: - What situation it was - Who were there - Why you had to be polite - How you showed politeness

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

A time I behaved politely was when I offered my bus seat to a pregnant woman. This happened on a rainy day about three years ago, during my first year at university. I was travelling from my dormitory to a music festival in the city centre.

The bus was already crowded when she got on. After a short time, she looked pale and held the rail tightly. I stood up and offered her my seat, then opened the window to let in some fresh air. She seemed more comfortable after sitting down.

We talked for the rest of the journey. She was visiting her aunt in hospital and had not wanted to wait for another bus. She thanked me warmly because she had been feeling faint. I did not think of the action as anything special; it simply seemed right. My mother has always encouraged me to notice when people need help. The experience made me feel pleased because a small, easy action had made someone else's journey much more comfortable.

Key words and phrases

dormitory
A building where students live while attending school or university.
feeling faint
Feeling weak or dizzy, as if you might lose consciousness.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about a time when I gave up my bus seat for a pregnant woman.

It was a rainy day about three years ago, when I was still a freshman at university. I was on a bus from my dormitory into the city centre to go to a music festival, and partway through the journey, a heavily pregnant woman got on at one of the stops. The bus was already swarming with students by that point, so many of them were standing because there weren't enough seats to go round. In that stuffy atmosphere, I noticed her face suddenly turn pale, and she had to grip the hand rail tightly, with sweat rolling down her forehead.

Without really thinking about it, I stood up straight away and offered her my seat, then leaned over and opened the window to let some air in. After taking a few deep breaths she seemed to feel a lot better, and we ended up chatting for the rest of the journey. She told me she was on her way to visit her aunt in hospital and hadn't wanted to wait for the next bus in case she missed the visiting hours. She thanked me warmly, saying that if she'd had to stand any longer, she might actually have fainted.

I did it simply because it felt like the right thing to do, it wasn't really a decision, more just instinct. My mother has always told me to pay close attention to the people around me and help out whenever I can, because small moments like that can matter more than we realise. By the end of that day I felt genuinely thrilled, knowing I'd helped not just one person, but two, her and the baby.

Key words and phrases

a music festival
an event with many live music performances
swarming with
full of large numbers of
stuffy atmosphere
air and mood that feel hot, closed, and uncomfortable
the right thing to do
the morally correct action
pay close attention to
Watch or consider something carefully.

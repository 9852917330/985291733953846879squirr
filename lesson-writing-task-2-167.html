Crime & Punishment

Cybercrime and what can be done about it

Causes / Solutions

Prompt

Many people today are worried about ‘cybercrime’ such as hacking and identity theft. What problems does ‘cybercrime’ cause, and what solutions can you suggest for ordinary people and businesses to take? Give reasons for your answer, and provide ideas and examples from your own experience. You should write at least 250 words.

Model answer

Cybercrime is a cause of widespread concern today, as so many people use technology to store and transmit sensitive data. There are a number of problems that people suffer as a result of these crimes, but also some straightforward measures we can take to protect ourselves. Perhaps the worst problem stemming from this is the financial impact. If someone has his or her bank account emptied, or has false credit lines created under their name, they are potentially liable for large sums of money. Even if they can avoid the consequences, their bank or finance company has to cover the losses, which can damage their performance.

These losses do not stay contained, either: once stolen money enters a criminal network, it can just as easily fund drug trafficking or terrorism as ordinary theft, and by the time it has passed through several accounts there is no realistic way to trace where it has gone. Victims rarely emerge unscathed even when a bank eventually refunds the loss. Months of disputed transactions, replacement documents and the fear of it happening again take a genuine toll on people who did nothing to invite it.

The most effective response starts with individuals. Strong, unique passwords and the simple habit of shredding old bank statements deny criminals the raw material they need in the first place. Public education matters just as much, since scammers deliberately target the elderly and the very young; campaigns that raise awareness of the risks in schools and community centres can close off an easy source of victims before a crime even happens. The greatest responsibility, though, lies with banks and technology firms, whose fraud-detection systems and closer cooperation with the police can stop an attack before a customer notices anything is wrong.

Overall, the problems caused are both financial and social, and the solutions should involve coordinated action by individuals, corporations and the state.

Key words and phrases

a cause of widespread concern
Something that worries many people.
potentially liable for large sums of money
Possibly legally responsible for substantial payments.
deny criminals the raw material they need
Stop criminals obtaining the information or tools required.
raise awareness of the risks
Help people understand possible dangers.

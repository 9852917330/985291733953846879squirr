Situation

A time that you arrived early

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time that you arrived early

You should say: When it was; How early you arrived; Why you arrived early; And explain how you felt about it.

Simple Model Answer

A time I arrived early was my best friend's wedding last week. I was her maid of honour, so I wanted to help with preparations and make the morning easier for her. I reached her home at about five o'clock.

Getting up that early was difficult because I normally stay awake late. However, seeing her smile made me glad I had made the effort. There was plenty to do. We arranged flowers, washed dishes and helped prepare food. Starting early meant we had time to finish without rushing at the last moment.

By about nine, everything was ready and the ceremony began. Watching the couple make their promises was emotional, and I felt very happy for my friend. After the celebration, I was exhausted but also pleased. I knew my help had contributed to a special day. The experience reminded me that arriving early can be more than simply avoiding lateness. Sometimes it gives you a chance to support someone when they are busiest and show that an important event matters to you too.

Key words and phrases

maid of honour
The bride's main female helper at a wedding.

Just before something begins or must be completed.

Advanced Model Answer

So last week I actually had to show up early for my best friend's wedding. As her maid of honour, I felt it was my responsibility to help her have the best day possible, so on the morning of the wedding I decided to turn up early and help with all the preparations.

I think it was about five in the morning when I arrived at her front door. I'm honestly quite the night owl, so it was pretty tough for me to get up that early, but the second I saw the huge smile on her face, I was wide awake and got straight to work. The to-do list was seriously long, decorating the room with flowers, washing up dishes, helping prepare the food, so I was really glad I'd made the effort to come early.

By about nine, once everything was finally sorted, the ceremony began in such a warm, sincere atmosphere. Watching the bride and groom exchanging vows was genuinely moving, I even teared up a little, and I felt so happy for her that she'd finally found the right person.

After the party, even though I was completely exhausted, I also felt relieved and really delighted, because I like to think my help was part of what made her day so special.

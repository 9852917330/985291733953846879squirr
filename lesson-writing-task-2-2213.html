Education

Team sports and cooperation

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

The best way to teach children to cooperate is through team sports at school. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

It is often claimed that school team sports are the most effective method of teaching children to work together. I agree that sport teaches cooperation extremely well, but I do not accept that it is the best method for every child.

The strengths of team sport are clear. In a game of football or basketball, cooperation is not an abstract idea to be explained in a classroom; it is immediately and visibly necessary. A player who refuses to pass will lose, and the consequences are obvious to everyone within seconds. Children also learn to accept different roles, to trust others to do their part, to support a teammate who has made a mistake and to deal with defeat as a group. These lessons are memorable precisely because they are physical and emotional rather than theoretical.

However, sport suits some children far better than others. A child who is unathletic, physically small or simply uninterested may spend years being chosen last and blamed for defeats, and the lesson learned is exclusion rather than cooperation. Team sports can also encourage the wrong attitudes, since aggressive competition and pressure to win sometimes produce selfish behaviour instead.

In my opinion, schools should therefore use a range of methods. Group projects in science, drama productions, orchestras, debating teams and school newspapers all require pupils to plan together, divide responsibilities and depend on one another, and they engage children whose talents are not physical.

In conclusion, team sports are a valuable way of teaching cooperation, but they work best alongside other collaborative activities rather than as the only approach.

Key words and phrases

immediately and visibly necessary
Clearly needed straight away.
the lesson learned is exclusion rather than cooperation
Children learn rejection instead of teamwork.
alongside other collaborative activities
Together with other activities involving cooperation.

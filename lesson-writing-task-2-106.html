Economy & Money

Putting economic growth above everything else

advdis

Prompt

In many countries, the government prioritises economic growth above all other concerns. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of this.

Model answer

Economic growth is necessary for jobs, public revenue and better living standards, but it should not be the government's only priority. A country can become richer while its environment, health system and social trust deteriorate. Governments should therefore pursue growth alongside broader measures of progress.

The benefits of a growing economy are clear. Successful businesses create employment, wages rise and the state collects more tax. This money can fund schools, hospitals and infrastructure. In poorer countries, sustained growth can also reduce extreme poverty and give families access to basic services. For these reasons, governments cannot simply ignore economic performance.

However, growth can produce serious costs if it depends on pollution, unsafe work or extreme inequality. Higher national income means little to people who cannot afford housing or healthcare. Governments should evaluate whether growth improves ordinary lives, not only whether production increases. Measures such as life expectancy, educational outcomes, air quality and income distribution provide a fuller picture of national progress.

The central question is how the gains are distributed. When productivity rises across an economy, ordinary households usually see it in wages as well as taxes; but when growth is driven by a handful of highly automated or extractive industries, profits can rise sharply while employment and average pay barely move, as has happened in some oil-exporting and heavily automated economies. Governments therefore need environmental limits and social investment so that higher output translates into a broader improvement in living standards.

In conclusion, economic growth should remain an important objective, but not the objective that overrides every other concern. The best policy is growth that is environmentally sustainable and widely shared, so that higher output translates into better lives.

social trust deteriorate
People become less trusting of one another.
a fuller picture of national progress
A more complete view of national improvement.
translates into a broader improvement in living standards
Leads to better everyday living for more people.

Semi-formal

Requesting extended leave from work

Semi-formal

Prompt

You need more than one month of leave from work for personal reasons. Write to your manager. In your letter: - explain why and how long you need leave - describe how you will remain informed - suggest how your work can be covered

Model answer

Dear Ms Reynolds,

I am writing to request six weeks of unpaid leave from 3 August to 13 September so that I can support my father during his recovery from major surgery.

He lives alone in another city and will need help with transport, meals and medical appointments for the first month after leaving hospital. My siblings can assist later, but they are unable to cover the initial period.

I am committed to ensuring a smooth handover during my absence. Before leaving, I will complete the July performance report, prepare detailed handover notes and brief Minh on the two client accounts I currently manage. He has already agreed to act as the main contact, while Hoa can approve routine expenses.

To keep up to date, I will check email each weekday and join the Monday team meeting remotely whenever possible. I will also be available by phone for genuinely urgent questions, although I will avoid interfering with day-to-day decisions.

I hope this plan provides adequate cover for the department. I would be grateful for an opportunity to discuss the request this week.

Yours sincerely, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

I am writing to request
I want to ask for.
ensuring a smooth handover
Helping the next person take over easily.
prepare detailed handover notes
Write instructions for your replacement.
act as the main contact
Be the main person to approach.
avoid interfering with day-to-day decisions
Do not interrupt ordinary management choices.

Object & Favourite

Your favourite piece of clothing

Part 2

Prompt

Describe your favourite piece of clothing

You should say: Where you got it When you got it How often you wear it And explain why it is your favourite piece of clothing.

Simple Model Answer

My favourite piece of clothing is a pair of ripped jeans. I bought them cheaply in a second-hand shop when I was still at school. At that time, they were fashionable, and I felt pleased when classmates asked where I had found them.

Although fashions have changed, I still wear the jeans regularly. They are comfortable enough for relaxing at home or doing everyday jobs outside. I choose something smarter for formal events such as weddings, but these jeans suit most ordinary days.

I like them because they go well with different shirts, so getting dressed is easy. The denim has also lasted a long time, which has saved me from buying replacements. They are now old and no longer look new, but that does not bother me much. I have worn them through many different periods of my life, so they feel familiar. For me, useful clothes do not have to follow the latest trend. Comfort, durability and the ease of wearing them matter more than looking fashionable every season.

Key words and phrases

second-hand shop
A shop selling things that have already belonged to someone else.
durability
The ability to last a long time without breaking or wearing out.

Advanced Model Answer

A pair of ripped jeans is honestly my favourite item in my entire wardrobe, even though they're years old now and a bit past their best.

I picked them up for next to nothing in a second-hand clothing store back when I was still at school, right when ripped jeans were really popular and everyone I knew wanted a pair. I remember wearing them on my first day back after the holidays and having three different classmates ask me where I'd got them, which felt like a pretty big deal at fifteen.

Trends moved on eventually, the way they always do, but I never really stopped wearing mine. I wear them almost everywhere — round the house, to school, running errands — and I only swap them out for something smarter if it's an actual formal event, like a wedding or a ceremony. They're honestly so comfortable that I've fallen asleep in them more than once without even meaning to.

There are a few reasons I'm still this attached to a single pair of jeans. For one, they go with almost anything, so they're incredibly easy to mix and match — I can throw on nearly any shirt without worrying I'll look ridiculous. They're also just tough; denim doesn't wear out the way cheaper fabrics do, so I've never had to think about replacing them, which has probably saved me a fair bit of money over the years.

All in all, I can't really imagine getting through a normal week without them. If I'm honest, I think ripped jeans deserve to come back into fashion properly, because younger people today don't seem to rate them the way we did.

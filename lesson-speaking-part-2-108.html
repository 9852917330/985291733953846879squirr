Object & Favourite

Something you bought recently that made you happy

Part 2

Prompt

Describe something you bought recently that made you happy.

You should say: - What it is. - When and where you bought it. - How it was used/what is was used for. - Why it made you happy.

Simple Model Answer

Something I bought that made me happy is my Dell laptop. I purchased it about three years ago, in my final year at university. My old laptop was noisy and often stopped working while I was writing my thesis, which made me anxious.

I asked people for advice and read reviews before choosing a replacement from an electronics shop in the city centre. I wanted dependable performance at a price I could afford. The new laptop helped me continue writing without worrying about losing my work.

My happiest memory is finishing the thesis at four in the morning, three days before the deadline. I felt enormously relieved, and the laptop had helped make that possible. Since then, I have used it for assignments, job applications, films and games. It supports both serious tasks and relaxation after work. I know it is only a machine, but I have become attached to it because of the experiences connected with it. I plan to keep using it for as long as it works well.

Key words and phrases

thesis
A long piece of research writing completed for a university qualification.
dependable performance
Working well and reliably when needed.

Advanced Model Answer

My laptop is what I want to talk about — I bought this Dell about three years ago, in my final year of university, and it's still the thing I use every single day.

At the time, my old laptop had basically given up — the fan was so loud it sounded like it was about to take off, and it kept freezing halfway through writing my thesis, which was terrifying given the deadline. I asked around, read a stack of reviews, and picked out a Dell from an electronics shop downtown, because it gave me solid performance without the price tag of brands like ASUS or Lenovo, which can cost an arm and a leg.

I use it for pretty much everything — finishing my thesis was the original reason, but since then it's been assignments, job applications, watching films, and gaming late at night when I can't sleep. It's the one object I'd grab first if there were ever a fire.

What made me happy about it wasn't really the laptop itself, if I'm honest — it was what it let me do. I finished my thesis on it at four in the morning, three days before the deadline, and I remember just sitting there staring at the screen, completely relieved. It's also become my go-to way to unwind after a long day — putting a film on or playing a game helps me switch off from everything else.

I'm planning to hang onto it for as long as it'll survive, honestly, because it's been through a lot with me, and I'm genuinely fond of it in a way that probably sounds a bit silly for a piece of plastic and metal.

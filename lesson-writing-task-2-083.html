Education

Is television useful for education?

discuss

Prompt

Some people say that television is useful for education, while others say it is useful only for entertainment. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

Television can educate and entertain, and these functions often overlap. In my view, its educational value is significant, but most viewers use it mainly for relaxation. The quality of the programme matters more than the medium itself.

Educational television can make difficult subjects easy to grasp. A documentary can take a class to the deep ocean or inside a volcano, and film shot in slow motion can show a chemical reaction that no school laboratory could safely run. News programmes also improve public knowledge when they explain an issue carefully rather than simply report the headline. For children and adults with limited access to books or courses, television can bring useful knowledge into the home at no extra cost.

At the same time, entertainment is the main reason many people switch on the television. Drama, sport and comedy provide a simple way to rest after work. This is not necessarily negative, because good entertainment can reflect social problems, introduce new cultures and encourage discussion. The problem begins when viewers watch passively for many hours or when channels rely on sensational content to hold attention.

What television does well, though, is not what a book or a classroom does. It can show a process, a place or a period in a way that makes it feel real, but it moves at its own pace and cannot be questioned, so viewers tend to keep the impression and lose the detail. This is why the strongest programmes are supported by reliable information elsewhere — a website, a booklet or a lesson that follows — which is what allows a broadcast to be engaging without becoming superficial.

In conclusion, television is neither purely educational nor purely entertaining, and the two functions are not easily separated. What decides its value is what is broadcast and how it is watched, and the most useful change would be for viewers to choose programmes deliberately rather than leave the set running.

Key words and phrases

make difficult subjects easy to grasp
Explain complex topics so people understand easily.
bring useful knowledge into the home
Help people learn useful things at home.
rely on sensational content to hold attention
Use shocking or exciting material to retain viewers.
keep the impression and lose the detail
Remember a general feeling but forget facts.
engaging without becoming superficial
Interesting while still offering serious information.

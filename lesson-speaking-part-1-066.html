Shoes

Buying shoes: comfort or style

Prompt

Do you like to buy expensive shoes? When you buy shoes, do you prefer convenience or style? Why do you think some people buy many pairs of shoes? Have you ever bought shoes online?

Do you like to buy expensive shoes?

Simple Model Answer

No, I usually buy shoes at a reasonable price. I care more about how comfortable they feel than whether they come from an expensive or famous brand.

Key words and phrases

reasonable price
A cost that seems fair and not too high.

Advanced Model Answer

No. I tend to buy average-priced shoes because I'm more interested in comfort than expensive brands.

Key words and phrases

average-priced shoes
Shoes that cost a normal amount rather than being very cheap or very expensive.

When you buy shoes, do you prefer convenience or style?

Simple Model Answer

Comfort and ease of use matter most to me. I still think about appearance, though, because I want the shoes to look good with the clothes I usually wear.

Key words and phrases

appearance
The way something looks.

Advanced Model Answer

I mainly look for comfort and convenience. But obviously I consider the style too, because I do choose shoes that match the clothes I wear.

Key words and phrases

comfort and convenience
Feeling at ease and avoiding difficulties.
match the clothes I wear
Go well with my clothing.

Why do you think some people buy many pairs of shoes?

Simple Model Answer

Some people enjoy following new fashions. Others like having different shoes for work, parties and other situations, so they buy several pairs to suit different occasions.

Key words and phrases

occasions
Particular events or situations.

Advanced Model Answer

I suppose they either like following the latest fashions, or they just enjoy having a different pair for every occasion.

Key words and phrases

following the latest fashions
Wearing what is currently popular.

Have you ever bought shoes online?

Simple Model Answer

No, I haven't. I prefer to put shoes on in a shop before buying them. That way, I can check that they are the right size and feel comfortable.

Key words and phrases

the right size
A size that fits properly.

Advanced Model Answer

No, I haven't. I always like to try shoes on before I buy them, to make sure that they fit properly and feel comfortable.

Key words and phrases

try shoes on
Wear shoes briefly to check their fit.

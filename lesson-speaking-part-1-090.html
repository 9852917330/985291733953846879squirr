Work

Your job, and working alone or with others

Prompt

Do you work or are you a student? Do you enjoy your job? What's the best thing about your job? Do you prefer working alone or with others?

Do you work or are you a student?

Simple Model Answer

I'm a trained doctor, but I'm currently studying English. I want to improve my English so I can work as a doctor in this country.

Key words and phrases

trained
Having learned the skills and knowledge needed for a job.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm a qualified doctor, but at the moment I'm studying English so that I can work in this country.

Key words and phrases

a qualified doctor
A doctor with the required professional training.

Do you enjoy your job?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I do. I've wanted to be a doctor since I was a child. Helping people get better and supporting them during difficult times still makes the work feel important to me.

Key words and phrases

get better
Recover or become healthier.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes. I've wanted to become a doctor since childhood, and I still find the work meaningful because it allows me to help people recover and make difficult periods more manageable.

Key words and phrases

help people recover
Assist people in becoming healthy again.

What's the best thing about your job?

Simple Model Answer

The best part is helping patients and their families. Sometimes, explaining a situation clearly makes them feel less worried. It is rewarding to know that my work can improve their lives.

Key words and phrases

rewarding
Giving you a feeling of satisfaction because you have done something useful.

Advanced Model Answer

The best thing about my job is that I can make a difference in people's lives. It's a great feeling when patients and their families feel reassured just because we took the time to explain things clearly.

Key words and phrases

make a difference
Have a useful or important effect.

Do you prefer working alone or with others?

Simple Model Answer

I work with others because hospital work depends on a team. I'm used to it now, and I think we can do more together than one person could achieve alone.

Key words and phrases

achieve
Succeed in doing something you have worked towards.

Advanced Model Answer

In a hospital you have to work as part of a team. I've got used to working with others, and I think you can achieve a lot more as a team than you would alone.

Key words and phrases

work as part of a team
Cooperate with others on shared work.
achieve a lot more as a team
Accomplish more by working together.

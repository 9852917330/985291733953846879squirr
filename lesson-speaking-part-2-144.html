An interesting conversation you had with someone

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an interesting conversation you had with someone

You should say: - who the person was - where the conversation took place - what you talked about - and explain why you found the conversation interesting.

Simple Model Answer

An interesting conversation I had was with a music shop assistant a couple of weeks ago. I was walking home from work when a window full of instruments attracted me. I went inside and reached the acoustic guitar section upstairs.

The assistant asked if I needed help. I explained that I had not played guitar for years but was curious about the different models. He described how materials, construction and price affected the instruments. He also explained the difference between handmade and factory-made guitars.

What made the conversation special was his enthusiasm. He clearly enjoyed the subject and did not hurry me, even after I said I was not planning to buy anything. He played several guitars so I could hear how their sounds differed. This helped me understand much more than reading price labels would have done. I left feeling that I had learned something useful from someone who cared about his work. It was an unexpected reminder of how enjoyable an ordinary conversation can be when another person is willing to share their knowledge.

Key words and phrases

acoustic guitar
A guitar that produces sound mainly through its body rather than electronic amplification.
enthusiasm
A strong feeling of interest and enjoyment.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about an interesting conversation I had a couple of weeks ago in a music shop. I was walking along one of the main shopping streets in the city centre, just browsing on my way home from work, when a big window full of musical instruments caught my eye. Out of curiosity, I went in to have a look around, and I ended up chatting to a shop assistant on the second floor, in the section dedicated to acoustic guitars.

I hadn't planned on talking to anyone, but the assistant came over in a really friendly way and asked if I had any questions. I told him I hadn't played guitar in years, but that I was curious about the differences between all the acoustic guitars on display. He talked me through the different makes and models, whether they were factory-made or handmade, the types of wood and varnish used, how they each affected the sound, and of course the price range.

I found the whole conversation fascinating because the assistant was so knowledgeable — it was obvious he genuinely had a passion for guitars. He didn't seem to mind spending ages talking to me either, even though I'd made it pretty clear I wasn't planning to buy anything. He even picked up three or four of the guitars, one after another, and played them so I could hear the differences in their sound for myself, which I thought was a genuinely generous thing for him to do.

Key words and phrases

Walking

Walking in the city and in the countryside

Prompt

Do you like walking? Do you think walking is important? Do you think walking in the countryside is better than walking in the city? What could be done to improve the experience of walking in cities?

Do you like walking?

Simple Model Answer

I don't really enjoy very long country walks. However, I like walking around towns and cities, especially when I'm visiting a new place and looking at the sights.

Key words and phrases

the sights
Interesting or famous places that visitors go to see.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm not one for going on really long walks in the countryside, but I don't mind walking when I'm in a town or city, for example when I'm sightseeing in a new place.

Key words and phrases

not one for
Not someone who enjoys.
going on really long walks
Walking for a long time or distance.

Do you think walking is important?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, walking is a simple way to keep moving and stay active. It is one of the easiest forms of exercise to include in everyday life.

Key words and phrases

stay active
Keep moving your body regularly.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I think it's important to be active, and walking is the most basic physical activity that we can do.

Key words and phrases

the most basic physical activity
The simplest form of bodily movement.

Do you think walking in the countryside is better than walking in the city?

Simple Model Answer

Not for me. I prefer walking in cities, especially places with interesting history, such as London or Paris. There is more that I personally enjoy looking at there.

Key words and phrases

personally
In my own opinion or experience.

Advanced Model Answer

Personally, no. As I said before, I like walking in the city, especially if it's somewhere with a lot of history like London or Paris.

Key words and phrases

a lot of history
Many important events from the past.

What could be done to improve the experience of walking in cities?

Simple Model Answer

Cities should give people more space and make walking safer. Wider pavements would help when streets are busy, and we need safe places to cross the road.

Key words and phrases

pavements
Paths at the side of a road for people to walk on.

Advanced Model Answer

I suppose the main issues are space and safety. Pavements need to be wide enough to accommodate lots of pedestrians, and we need safe places to cross the street.

Key words and phrases

safe places to cross the street
Protected places for pedestrians to cross.

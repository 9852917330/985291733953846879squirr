Sports

Watching and taking part in sport

Part 1

Prompt

Do you like sports? Do you enjoy watching sports? Do you enjoy participating in any sports? What is your favourite sports activity?

Do you like sports?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I enjoy lifting weights, swimming and sometimes playing badminton. I like activities where regular practice helps me improve and where I can see the progress I make.

Key words and phrases

progress
Improvement or movement towards a goal.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes. I enjoy strength training, swimming and occasionally badminton. I prefer activities where progress can be measured and technique improves through consistent practice.

Key words and phrases

consistent practice
Regular, continued work on a skill.

Do you enjoy watching sports?

Simple Model Answer

I sometimes watch important events, but I prefer doing sport myself. Watching is more interesting when I understand how the game works and care about who wins.

Key words and phrases

care about
Feel interested in or concerned about something.

Advanced Model Answer

I watch major events occasionally, but I'd rather participate than sit through an entire season. Watching is most enjoyable when I understand the strategy and have a reason to care about the result.

Key words and phrases

a reason to care about the result
Something that makes the outcome matter personally.

Do you enjoy participating in any sports?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, especially exercise I can do by myself and relaxed games with friends. Being active makes me feel happier and gives me a physical goal to work towards.

Key words and phrases

work towards
Make an effort to reach a goal.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, particularly in individual exercise and casual games with friends. Participation improves my mood and gives me a clear physical challenge.

Key words and phrases

a clear physical challenge
A definite test of strength or fitness.

What is your favourite sports activity?

Simple Model Answer

Lifting weights is my favourite. It helps me stay healthy, stand better and keep moving well as I get older. I also enjoy seeing small improvements over time.

Key words and phrases

over time
Gradually, as time passes.

Advanced Model Answer

Strength training's my favourite because it supports health, posture and long-term mobility. I also like the fact that progress is gradual and easy to track.

Key words and phrases

easy to track
Simple to monitor over time.

Processes & Cycles

Hydroelectricity production

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The diagram below shows the process of using water to produce electricity. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The picture explains the process through which water is used to produce electrical power.

Overall, the process shows ten stages, using the movement of water to generate electricity for a range of different users. It begins with a natural cycle of evaporation and rainfall, and ends with power being distributed from a transformer station.

The process begins when seawater is heated by the sun and evaporates to form small clouds in the sky. These clouds then merge into a storm cloud, which rains over the mountain, and the rainwater is gathered in the reservoir behind a dam.

The following steps involve a pipe which connects the reservoir to a turbine and a pump, and which is controlled using a valve. Once this valve is opened, water flows into the turbine and rotates it to produce an electrical current, after which the water is pumped back into the reservoir.

The electricity produced by the turbine is then transferred to the transformer station through high voltage cables. At the last stage, the electrical power is delivered to domestic and industrial consumers, as well as to educational and medical facilities.

Object & Favourite

A toy that you received when you were a child

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a toy that you received when you were a child.

You should say: - What the toy was. - Who gave it to you. - How you used it. - An explanation of how you felt when you got this toy.

Simple Model Answer

A toy I received as a child was a soft toy called Marsupilami. My parents gave it to me for my birthday when I was about five or six. I had seen the character on television and asked for the toy for several weeks.

It looked just like the cartoon creature: yellow with black spots and an extremely long tail. In the programme, the tail could help it swing through trees and pick things up. When I opened the present, I was delighted because the character seemed almost real.

I carried it around the house and kept it on my pillow at night. I even told it about my day before sleeping. For the first week of kindergarten, I took it in my school bag until my mother encouraged me to leave it at home. The toy made unfamiliar situations feel more comfortable. I no longer treat toys like living friends, of course, but seeing a Marsupilami in a shop still reminds me of that happy period of childhood.

Key words and phrases

soft toy
A toy animal made from fabric and filled with soft material.
unfamiliar situations
Experiences or places that are new and not yet comfortable.

Advanced Model Answer

The first sentimental item that came to mind when I saw this topic was a stuffed toy called Marsupilami. It was a birthday present from my parents, and I can't remember my exact age, but I was probably around five or six. I'd been asking for it for weeks after seeing the character on TV, so when I finally unwrapped it, I was genuinely thrilled.

It was identical to the character from the cartoon, so it felt like he'd actually stepped out of the screen — a black-spotted yellow creature, a bit like a monkey crossed with a dog, with this incredibly long tail that in the show could do almost anything, from swinging between trees to picking things up.

I carried Marsupilami around the house constantly, and for the first few weeks I wouldn't even let my cousins touch him when they came over. He sat on my pillow every single day, and I actually got into the habit of telling him about my day before I went to sleep, the way some kids talk to a diary. When I started kindergarten not long after, he even came with me in my school bag for the first week, until my mother gently convinced me to leave him at home.

Looking back, that toy is tied up with some of my happiest early memories, and even now, whenever I see a Marsupilami toy in a shop, it takes me straight back to being five years old and utterly convinced he was real.

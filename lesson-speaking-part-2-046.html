A place where you often visit with your friends or family

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a place you often visit with friends or family.

You should say: Where the place is How you know the place How you go there Why you want to visit

Simple Model Answer

A place I visit with people close to me is Freedom Park in District 4. I have lived in the area for about five years, and the park is only a five-minute walk from my flat. I discovered it while exploring the neighbourhood after moving in.

It is especially pleasant early in the morning, before the traffic becomes busy. People walk, exercise and use the public sports equipment. There is also a small cafe on a hill where visitors can have a simple breakfast after exercising.

My favourite memory there is an evening walk with my girlfriend about two years ago. The sun was setting and the park lights had just come on. We walked together along paths between the trees, and everything felt peaceful. That memory is one reason the park remains special to me. I do not visit as often as I would like now, but I hope to go more regularly with family and friends. It is a convenient place to enjoy fresh air and slow down.

Key words and phrases

fresh air

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about Freedom Park, which is right in the heart of District 4, where I've lived for about five years now.

I first got to know the park just after I moved into the area. I didn't know anyone nearby yet, so one evening I just went out for a walk to explore, and I stumbled across it almost by accident. It's actually only a five-minute walk from my flat, so these days I just head there on foot whenever I feel like some fresh air.

What I love about it is that early in the morning, before the traffic builds up, it's wonderfully quiet. Locals go there to take a stroll or work out before starting their day, and the park's got a decent range of public sports facilities that people use to keep fit and boost their mental wellness. There's also a little cafe right at the top of the hill that does a simple breakfast — fried rice, sausages, that sort of thing — so you can grab something to eat once you've finished exercising.

If my memory serves me right, the first time I actually spent real time there, rather than just passing through, was about two years ago, when I went one evening with my girlfriend. It was incredibly peaceful — the sun was going down, the park lights had just come on, and we walked hand in hand along the little paths that wind through the trees. It's honestly one of my favourite memories of that whole year.

That's really why Freedom Park always comes out on top whenever I want to get back to nature and just switch off for a while. I don't get there as often as I'd like these days, so I'm hoping that once things settle down a bit, I can start going back regularly with my family and friends.

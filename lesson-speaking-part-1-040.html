Home & Accommodation

Your favourite room and what you would change

Prompt

Do you live in a house or an apartment? Which is your favourite room in your home? Would you change anything about your home? Would you like to move to a different home in the future?

Do you live in a house or an apartment?

Simple Model Answer

I live in a three-bedroom house outside central Manchester. It is joined to one other house, and I have lived there for a little over a year.

Key words and phrases

a little over
Slightly more than a particular amount.

Advanced Model Answer

I live in a semi-detached house with three bedrooms in a suburb of Manchester. I've lived there for just over a year.

Key words and phrases

just over a year
A little longer than twelve months.

Which is your favourite room in your home?

Simple Model Answer

I don't have a strong favourite, but I would probably choose the living room. It is the place where I sit down and relax, so I enjoy spending time there.

Key words and phrases

living room
The room at home used for sitting and relaxing.

Advanced Model Answer

I don't really have a favourite room. But if I had to choose, I'd say the living room because that's where I go to sit and relax.

Key words and phrases

if I had to choose
If selecting one option were necessary.

Would you change anything about your home?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I want to decorate the house again. I have planned to do that since moving in last year. The kitchen needs replacing, so I would start there.

Key words and phrases

replacing
Removing something and putting a new one in its place.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I've been meaning to redecorate it since I moved in last year. It needs a new kitchen, so that's what I'd change first.

Key words and phrases

been meaning to
Have intended to do something.

Would you like to move to a different home in the future?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, if I had enough money. I'd like to try living in the countryside, or perhaps another city or country, at some time in the future.

Key words and phrases

countryside
Areas outside towns and cities, with fields or natural land.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I'd definitely move again at some point if I could afford to. I'd like to live in the countryside, or maybe in a different city or country.

Key words and phrases

at some point
At an unspecified future time.
live in the countryside
Have your home outside towns and cities.

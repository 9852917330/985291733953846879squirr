Object & Favourite

An electronic machine you want to buy

Part 2

Prompt

An electronic machine you want to buy

You should say: What it is When you know this machine What specific features you want And explain why you want this machine.

Simple Model Answer

An electronic device I want to buy is an Aqua air conditioner. I first noticed it in an advertisement before an online film about two weeks ago. I liked its simple, modern appearance and began looking at its features.

The main reason I want it is the summer heat. My old fan is no longer enough to keep my room comfortable. The model I am considering uses inverter technology, which is advertised as using less electricity than older models. It can also adjust its operation according to the room temperature.

The difficulty is the price. I spent too much on clothes last month, so I would need to save more, reduce spending on takeaway food or ask my mother for some help. I have not bought it yet because I want to make sure I can afford it. Still, I think a comfortable room would help me sleep and work better during hot weather. It is a practical purchase rather than an exciting luxury, but it could improve daily life considerably.

Key words and phrases

features
The useful functions or qualities of a product.
afford it
Have enough money to buy it without financial difficulty.

Advanced Model Answer

Let me tell you about an air conditioner I'm hoping to buy in the next few weeks. It's an Aqua — the brand's Vietnamese, but the units are manufactured under licence from a big Japanese company, so I'd expect the quality to be decent. I first noticed it about two weeks ago: I was watching a film online, an advert for it popped up before the video started, and I was struck straight away by how sleek it looked.

I made up my mind pretty much on the spot to buy one, mainly because it's the middle of summer here and my old fan just isn't cutting it any more. There's just one problem — I'm short on cash right now, since I overspent on clothes last month, and this air conditioner isn't cheap. I might have to borrow a bit from my mother and cut back on takeaway food for a week or two to afford it, but I've more or less decided it's worth it.

As for the features, what really sold me is the inverter technology, which is supposed to cut electricity use quite a lot compared with older models — that matters because my bill practically doubles every summer. It's also got an auto-adjust function, so it can sense the temperature in the room and regulate itself instead of just blasting cold air at one fixed setting all day.

Honestly, it's not a glamorous purchase, but a good air conditioner makes a real difference to how well I sleep and work once the heat sets in properly, so I think it'll be money well spent in the long run.

Key words and phrases

manufactured under licence from
Made legally with permission from another company.
on the spot
Immediately, in that location.
short on cash
Having little money available.
inverter technology
Technology that changes motor speed to save electricity.
makes a real difference to
Has a significant effect on.
money well spent
A purchase providing good value.

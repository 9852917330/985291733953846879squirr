Economy & Money

Development and the values it costs

Advantages / Disadvantages

Prompt

Many countries aim to improve living standards through economic development, but some important social values are lost as a result. Do the advantages of economic development outweigh the disadvantages?

Model answer

Rapid development transforms what a country can offer its citizens and also changes how they live together. I believe the advantages outweigh the losses, though the losses are real and are felt most by those who did not choose the change.

The gains are measurable and enormous. Development brings clean water, electricity, vaccination and schooling, and the result is visible in child mortality that falls by more than half within a generation. It offers choice as well: young people can work outside farming, women can earn independently, and families are no longer confined to the village they were born in. Governments also gain the revenue that funds hospitals, pensions and courts, and it is worth remembering that the values romanticised in poor societies came with early death and rigid hierarchy.

The costs are nonetheless genuine. Extended families disperse as work moves to cities, so the care that grandparents and neighbours once provided is replaced by services people must buy. Communities that shared work and celebrations become collections of households with little contact. Time is measured differently, since hospitality and obligation give way to schedules, and a competitive economy encourages people to compare rather than to help. Traditional crafts, dialects and festivals fade because nobody has the hours to maintain them.

The important question is whether the losses are unavoidable, and largely they are not. Some countries have developed while protecting rest days, family time, language and public space, whereas others allowed the market to decide everything. What destroys social values is usually the speed and the absence of any policy rather than prosperity itself.

In conclusion, development delivers health, education and freedom that no traditional society provided, and few who have gained them wish to return. The social costs deserve attention, so governments should pursue growth while deliberately protecting the time and places that hold communities together.

Key words and phrases

nobody has the hours to maintain them
People lack time to preserve these relationships.
the speed and the absence of any policy
Rapid change without government planning.

Place

An ideal house

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an ideal house

You should say: where it is what it looks like when you would like to live in And explain why you would like to live in it

OUTLINE

>>> ideal house in the hills near Da Lat
not a palace, somewhere small, right location: hills outside Da Lat, cool quiet
single-storey, big windows, natural light, cross-ventilation, wooden floors, reading corner, garden
when: not any time soon, once career stable and not tied to city, maybe in 40s, long-term goal
why: peace and quiet, job pretty full-on, weekends exhausted, want to potter around garden/read
don't need anything fancy, just somewhere to properly switch off

Simple Model Answer

My ideal house would be a small home in the hills outside Da Lat. I would choose that location because the weather is cool and the surroundings are quieter than the city. I do not need a very large or expensive-looking building.

I imagine one floor, wooden floors inside and large windows along one side. These would let in daylight and fresh air. There would be a reading corner by a window and a garden with flowers and a few fruit trees. I would like somewhere to sit with coffee in the morning.

This is a long-term plan rather than something I expect to do soon. Perhaps I could move there in my forties, when my career is stable and I do not need to work in the city every day. My current job is busy, so the idea of a quiet home is attractive. I would enjoy reading, looking after plants and resting properly. For me, comfort and peace matter more than having a grand house.

Key words and phrases

long-term plan
A plan for something you hope to do years in the future.
my career is stable
My working life feels secure and well established.

Advanced Model Answer

OK, so my ideal house isn't really a palace or anything grand — I'd actually like somewhere fairly small, but in the right location: up in the hills just outside Da Lat, where it's cool and quiet all year round, away from the noise of the city.

I picture a single-storey house with big windows all along one side, so there's plenty of natural light and good cross-ventilation without needing the air-con on constantly. It'd be simply furnished — wooden floors, a small reading corner by the window — with a garden out front full of flowers and a couple of fruit trees, just enough space to sit outside with a coffee in the morning.

As for when I'd want to live there, honestly, not any time soon. I'd say once I've got a stable career going and I'm not tied to the city for work any more — maybe in my forties. It's more of a long-term goal than something I'm planning next year.

The main reason I want it is just peace and quiet. My job right now is pretty full-on, and by the time the weekend comes I'm usually exhausted, so the idea of waking up somewhere cool and silent, with nothing to do but potter around the garden or read a book, genuinely sounds like the perfect way to unwind. I don't need anything fancy — just somewhere I can properly switch off.

Key words and phrases

cross-ventilation
air flowing through a room from openings on different sides
peace and quiet
a calm place without noise
pretty full-on
quite intense or demanding
potter around the garden
do small relaxing jobs in the garden
properly switch off
fully stop thinking about work and stress

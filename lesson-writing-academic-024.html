Transport

Airline crews, complaints and baggage

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The first chart below shows the number of airline crew (pilots, stewards etc) needed to fly a passenger plane between Australia and a number of other countries. The second chart shows the number of passenger complaints per plane journey made on these routes, and average kilogram (kg) weight of luggage (or baggage) carried per passenger. Write a report summarising the information. Select and describe the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The charts give data about four flight destinations from Australia in terms of crew numbers, passenger complaints made and luggage weight per passenger.

Overall, it is apparent that flights to Germany require the largest crews and carry the most luggage per passenger, while flights to New Zealand are at the opposite end of the scale on both counts. However, the pattern for customer complaints is reversed, with German flights attracting the fewest complaints and New Zealand flights the most. The Malaysia and Indonesia flights lie roughly between these two extremes.

The largest crew, of 15 people, is needed for the flight to Germany, followed by Indonesia with 11 and Malaysia with 8, while New Zealand flights need the smallest crew of only 6. This pattern is reflected in the weight of luggage per passenger, with Germany having the heaviest baggage at 38 kg, and Indonesia the second heaviest at 32 kg. Flights to Malaysia carry 30 kg per passenger, and New Zealand has the lightest figure at 15 kg.

Turning to passenger complaints, New Zealand flights receive the most, at 6 per journey, followed by Indonesia and Malaysia with 3 each. Flights to Germany produce the fewest, with only 1 complaint per flight.

Education

What education should consist of

Opinion

Prompt

It is generally believed that education is of vital importance to individual development and to the well-being of societies. What should education consist of in order to fulfil both of these functions?

Model answer

Education is important both for personal development and for the well-being of society. In my opinion, a system that serves these purposes must combine a strong foundation in knowledge with independent thinking, practical skills and the ability to cooperate with others.

The first requirement is an effective academic and vocational foundation. Literacy and numeracy help people understand information, manage everyday tasks and continue learning. Scientific knowledge also supports informed decisions about the world. Alongside academic study, students should have access to respected vocational routes that prepare them for useful work. Training an electrician or a technician can benefit both the learner and a community that needs those skills. Neither route should be treated as a substitute for basic educational quality.

A second requirement is the ability to evaluate ideas. Easy access to information does not guarantee that the information is accurate or relevant. Students need opportunities to examine evidence, recognise weak arguments and revise a conclusion when new facts emerge. For example, comparing two explanations of the same issue can be more valuable than memorising a single approved response. Knowledge and reasoning should support each other, since critical thinking is difficult without enough understanding of the subject.

Education should also develop social responsibility and personal independence. Group projects can teach cooperation, while discussion allows students to practise disagreeing respectfully. Teachers' behaviour matters too: fairness and honesty are more convincing when students see them demonstrated. Explicit lessons about citizenship can reinforce these experiences, rather than being expected to produce good judgement on their own.

In conclusion, education should help people earn a living, understand their choices and participate constructively in society. This requires a balanced curriculum in which useful knowledge, careful reasoning and responsible behaviour develop together.

Key words and phrases

vocational routes
Paths of education that prepare people for practical occupations.
evaluate ideas
Judge ideas carefully rather than accept them immediately.
examine evidence
Look closely at the information supporting a claim.
revise a conclusion
Change a judgement after considering more information.
social responsibility
A duty to consider how actions affect other people.
disagreeing respectfully
Expressing a different opinion without treating someone badly.
a balanced curriculum
A programme that gives suitable attention to different learning needs.

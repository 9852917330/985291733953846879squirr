Formal

Applying for a part-time restaurant job

Formal

Prompt

A local restaurant is recruiting a part-time employee. Write to the restaurant manager. In your letter: - apply for the position - describe relevant experience - state the days and hours you can work

Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to apply for the part-time front-of-house position advertised in the window of Green Table Restaurant.

I have eighteen months of relevant customer-service experience from working at a busy cafe near my university. My duties included taking orders, handling card payments, resolving minor complaints and preparing the dining area between sittings. The role taught me to remain polite and work efficiently under pressure, even during crowded weekend shifts. I also hold a current food-safety certificate and speak both English and Vietnamese fluently.

I am available from 5 p.m. until closing on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and at any time on Saturday or Sunday. During university holidays, I could take additional daytime shifts if required.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss the position and am available for interview this week. My CV and the contact details of my previous supervisor are attached.

Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

eighteen months of relevant customer-service experience
A year and a half helping customers.
resolving minor complaints
Settling small customer problems.
work efficiently under pressure

available for interview
Free to attend an interview.

A polite closing for a job application.

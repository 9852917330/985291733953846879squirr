A couple you know who have a happy marriage

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a couple you know who have a happy marriage

You should say: Who they are How you know them What they usually do together And how you feel about their marriage.

Simple Model Answer

I'd like to talk about my parents, who have been happily married for over thirty years. They are both in their sixties and work at a government office, although they plan to retire this year.

They enjoy doing ordinary things together. After dinner, they often walk around our neighbourhood. On Sundays, they prepare a family meal, with my father cutting vegetables while my mother cooks. These small activities give them time to talk and enjoy each other's company.

Their life has not always been easy, but they have supported each other through difficult times. They are honest about their feelings and understand each other well. Of course, they sometimes argue about little things, such as the television remote. However, my father usually makes peace first. I think their kindness and willingness to listen explain why their marriage has lasted. I admire their relationship, and I hope to have the same kind of loving partnership in the future.

Key words and phrases

enjoy each other's company
Like spending time together.

Helps people stop arguing and become friendly again.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, I'd like to talk about my own parents, whose marriage is, without a doubt, a happy one. They've been married for over thirty years now, and to me they're the perfect couple.

My mum and dad are both in their sixties, and they're still really busy with their nine-to-five jobs at a government office, though they're both planning to retire this year. Obviously, I've known them my whole life!

What I love is that they still do simple things together — they go for a walk around the neighbourhood most evenings after dinner, and on Sundays they cook a big family meal together, my dad chopping vegetables while my mum handles the stove. It's nothing fancy, but they clearly look forward to it.

Over the years, my parents have been through thick and thin together but have never lost their love for each other. They show their affection simply by always being honest with each other and sharing their true feelings. Their mutual understanding is so strong that sometimes it only takes one look to realise what the other is thinking.

Of course, they still have minor tiffs now and then — things like whose turn it is to walk the dog, or who's hogging the TV remote — but my dad's always first to give in and comfort my mum. I think little things like that are actually the key to their long-lasting relationship.

To me, my parents are a perfect match, and I admire their love for each other so much. Hopefully, one day, I'll have a partner who cares for me just as much as they care for each other.

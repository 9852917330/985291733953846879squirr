Place

A sports centre near home

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a place of entertainment (e.g. a theatre, cinema or sports centre) that you would like to have near your home.

You should say: - what this place would be - where it should be - and why you think this place of entertainment is needed

Simple Model Answer

A place I would like near my home is a sports centre. I imagine a building with a gym, swimming pool, football pitches and courts for sports such as tennis and badminton. A shared exercise area could also be used for fitness classes.

There is an empty piece of land a few minutes' walk from my house where an old warehouse used to stand. It is near a main road and a train station, so people could reach it easily. Although houses are planned for the site, I think sports facilities would also be useful.

At present, residents have to travel to another town for many activities. A local centre could provide swimming lessons for children, exercise sessions for adults and competitions for the whole community. It would give young people something enjoyable to do after school and at weekends. I would use it regularly because being close to home would make exercise easier to fit into my routine. I believe it could improve both health and social life in our area.

Advanced Model Answer

The place of entertainment I wish we had closer to where I live is a sports centre. My ideal one would have a gym, a swimming pool, indoor and outdoor football pitches, courts for tennis, badminton and squash, and an all-purpose area for things like gymnastics and circuit training. I can actually picture the perfect spot for it too — there's a patch of disused land just a few minutes' walk from my house, where an old warehouse got knocked down a while back.

It's close to the main road and the train station, so it'd be easy for people to get to. There are plans to build houses on that plot instead, but I honestly think a sports centre would do a lot more for local residents. The great thing about a sports centre, after all, is that literally everyone can use it.

There could be lessons and clubs for children and teenagers — things like swimming lessons and football practice sessions. I'm sure plenty of adults round here would join the gym, use the pool, or book the courts for a game of tennis or badminton. The centre could even host sports events, like local competitions, that would attract spectators as well as participants.

I think we genuinely need something like this where I live, because at the moment people have to travel all the way to the next town for most of the facilities, activities, lessons and clubs I've just mentioned. I also think it'd give young people, in particular, something fun and productive to do in the evenings and at weekends, rather than nothing at all. Playing sport keeps people healthy and makes them happier, so a sports centre like this would brighten up my local area and improve our quality of life.

Transport

Train passengers and ticket prices

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The chart below shows the number of passengers arriving at a train station per hour across a day, and also the average price (in US Dollars) of their tickets. Write a report summarising the information. Select and describe the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The chart gives data about passenger volumes reaching a station every two hours during the day, and also the average ticket cost at those times.

Passenger numbers peak three times during the day, and ticket prices broadly follow the same pattern, rising when passenger traffic is high, except during the evening rush hour when they actually fall. Notably, the midday period combines the highest passenger total with the highest average fare.

At 6am, the number of passengers is 480, rising to 690 by 8am before falling again. There is then a peak of 700 at midday, followed by an afternoon decline to a low of 230 at 4pm. A third spike occurs at 6pm, reaching 670 before tailing off.

Turning to ticket prices, the 8am passenger surge is matched by a high price of $29, followed by a peak of $31 at midday and a decrease to $17 by 4pm. However, the 6pm spike in passenger numbers is accompanied by a low of $11 in prices, with a subsequent rise to $22 at 8pm before falling to $18 at 10pm.

Key words and phrases

broadly follow the same pattern
Change in roughly similar ways.
a peak of
The highest level reached by a figure.
before tailing off
Before gradually decreasing.
is accompanied by
Occurs together with another feature or change.

Object & Favourite

An advertisement that you saw or heard or liked

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an advertisement that you saw or heard or liked

You should say: Where you saw or heard it What kind of advertisement it was What the content of the advertisement was (or, what product /service was advertised) And explain how you felt about this advertisement/ why you liked it.

Simple Model Answer

An advertisement I like is for Dulux Easy Clean paint. I first saw it during a television commercial break several years ago. It shows children making a mess on the walls of their home with paint, mud and crayons.

When their mother returns from work, she finds her little boy drawing on a newly painted wall. Instead of becoming angry, she smiles. The message is that marks can be wiped off easily because of the paint, so the children's mistakes do not become a major problem.

I like the advertisement because the children seem playful and natural. The song, I'm Sorry, also suits the story. Most importantly, the idea connects the product with an ordinary family experience. It explains the benefit without giving a long technical description. I still remember the message after many years, which suggests that the advertisement worked well. It made the brand familiar to me and showed how a practical product could be presented through a warm, funny story rather than just a list of features.

Key words and phrases

commercial break
A pause in a television programme when advertisements are shown.
list of features
A description of the functions or qualities of a product.

Advanced Model Answer

I want to tell you about my all-time favourite advert, for a paint called Dulux Easy Clean. I first saw it years ago, during a commercial break on TV, and I've loved it ever since.

It starts with a group of children covering the walls of their house in paint, mud and powder, having the time of their lives. Then their mother gets home after a long day at work, walks in the front door, and finds her little boy happily drawing all over the newly painted wall with his crayons. Instead of getting angry, which is what you'd expect, she just looks at him and smiles.

That's the whole point of the advert — because the walls are painted with Dulux Easy Clean, any mark can be wiped off in seconds. It ends with a slogan that works two ways at once: with Dulux Easy Clean, forgiveness comes easily, because the mess itself is never really a problem.

I love this ad for a few reasons. First, the kids act exactly like real kids — mischievous but completely innocent — which I find genuinely sweet. I can't take my eyes off the screen whenever it comes on. The song playing over it, “I'm Sorry”, fits the mood perfectly too. But honestly, it's that clever slogan that stuck with me the most, and I think it's exactly why the campaign did such a good job of building brand awareness — you remember the ad long after you've forgotten most others.

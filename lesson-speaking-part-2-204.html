Object & Favourite

Your favourite season in your country

Part 2

Prompt

Describe your favourite season in your country.

You should say: - when that season is - what the weather is like at that time of year - how that season is different from other seasons - and why it is your favourite season.

Simple Model Answer

My favourite season in England is winter, especially the coldest weeks when there is snow. I live near the countryside, and the fields and trees look beautiful under a layer of white.

One morning last winter, I looked outside and saw the whole area covered in snow. The familiar landscape seemed completely different. I enjoy clear, freezing mornings when the ground feels hard and the air is still. Winter also includes Christmas and New Year, which are my favourite celebrations because of the time spent with family and friends.

Of course, the season has disadvantages. Snow can block roads and close schools, while short days mean it may be dark by four in the afternoon. Much of the weather is grey and wet rather than bright and snowy, and I understand why some people dislike it. Still, the good days make it special to me. I would not claim that every winter afternoon is enjoyable, but the combination of snowy scenery and important celebrations gives the season a feeling I do not get at other times of year.

Advanced Model Answer

My favourite season in England is winter. A typical English winter would last around 4 or 5 months between November and March, but when I say that I like the winter, I'm really thinking about the coldest month or two each year. During those really wintry months, the temperature drops below zero and it often snows. Over the last few years we've had periods of quite heavy snowfall, to the extent that roads have been blocked and schools have had to close. Last winter I remember looking out of the window one morning and seeing everything covered in a layer of white. Obviously the snow is what differentiates winter from the other seasons, but when it doesn't snow there isn't much difference between winter, autumn and spring; winter is just a bit colder. The reason I've chosen winter as my favourite season is that I like the snow. I live near the countryside, and the landscape looks beautiful when everything is covered in white. We also celebrate Christmas and New Year in the winter, and these are my favourite festivals. I'll be honest and say that winter has its drawbacks too. The days are extremely short — it can be dark by four in the afternoon — and a lot of people find that genuinely difficult by about February. When it's grey and wet rather than crisp and snowy, which is most of the time, it's not a season anyone would choose.

But on the good days it's unbeatable. There is a particular kind of bright, still, freezing morning where the sky is completely clear and the ground is hard underfoot, and for me that is worth putting up with the rest of it.

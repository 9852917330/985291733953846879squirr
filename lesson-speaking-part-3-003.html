Advertising

Popular forms of advertising, and working in the industry

Part 3

Prompt

What's the most popular form of advertising in your country? Would you like to work for an advertising company? Do you agree that advertisements for smoking should be banned?

What's the most popular form of advertising in your country?

Simple Model Answer

I'd say social media adverts, especially short videos, are very common. They cost less than television adverts and can reach particular groups. People may also share them with friends. Influencers are important too, but their recommendations can be confusing because viewers may not know whether the opinion is honest or paid for.

Key words and phrases

particular groups
Specific types of people with shared interests or characteristics.

Advanced Model Answer

Social media advertising, without much doubt, and short video particularly. It's overtaken television because it's far cheaper, it can be targeted at very specific groups, and people share it themselves, which effectively makes the advertising free. Influencer marketing is a large part of it, where a recommendation looks like a personal opinion rather than a paid promotion. That's precisely what makes it effective, and also what makes a lot of people uncomfortable about it — you often can't tell whether someone genuinely likes a product or was simply paid to say so.

Key words and phrases

targeted at very specific groups
designed to reach particular sets of people
a paid promotion
an advertisement someone pays to show

Would you like to work for an advertising company?

Simple Model Answer

Possibly, if I believed in the products. The mix of research, writing and design sounds interesting, and you can see the results quickly. However, I would dislike making people feel bad about themselves just to sell something. Advertising useful products or sharing public information would feel more suitable for me.

Key words and phrases

believed in
Trusted or thought something had real value.

Advanced Model Answer

Possibly, depending on the clients. The work itself looks genuinely interesting — it combines research into how people think, writing and visual design, and you see results quickly. What would put me off is the type of product. I'd have no difficulty advertising something useful, but a good deal of the industry works by creating insecurity and then selling the remedy — skincare that promises to fix a flaw you didn't know you had, for instance — and I don't think I could keep doing that for long. That said, plenty of agencies work purely on brand image or public information campaigns, so it wouldn't necessarily be every client.

Key words and phrases

creating insecurity and then selling the remedy
making people feel inadequate and then offering a product as the solution

Do you agree that advertisements for smoking should be banned?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I think they should be banned because smoking causes serious harm. Adverts can make it seem normal or attractive, especially to young people. The health costs also affect society, not only smokers. Although businesses normally need freedom to advertise, I think protecting people from this level of harm is more important in this case.

Key words and phrases

banned
Officially not allowed.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, and the evidence supports it fairly clearly. Tobacco's unusual in that there's no safe level of use, and the public health costs fall on everybody through the health system. Advertising's main effect was normalising it and recruiting new smokers, who are almost always teenagers. Countries that banned it saw rates fall within a few years. It does restrict commercial freedom, certainly, and I wouldn't apply that argument to every product. But with tobacco the harm's severe enough to justify it.

Key words and phrases

no safe level of use
every amount can cause harm
severe enough to justify it
serious enough to make this action reasonable

Music

Listening habits and how your taste has changed

Prompt

How often do you listen to music? Has the music you like changed since you were younger? Do you prefer listening to music alone or with other people? Do you listen to music while you are working or studying?

How often do you listen to music?

Simple Model Answer

I listen almost every day, usually while doing something else. I pay more attention to the music when I travel to and from work, because that is time I can really listen.

Key words and phrases

to and from work
Going to your workplace and returning home.

Advanced Model Answer

Pretty much daily, though it is usually on in the background rather than something I sit down for. The commute is when I actually pay attention to it.

Key words and phrases

on in the background
Playing quietly while something else happens.

Has the music you like changed since you were younger?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, quite a lot. As a teenager, I listened to the music my friends liked. Now I prefer quieter music, such as acoustic songs and some jazz, and know those styles better.

Key words and phrases

acoustic
Using instruments whose sound is not produced electronically.

Advanced Model Answer

Quite a lot, yes. As a teenager I mostly listened to whatever my friends were into, and I have drifted towards quieter things since then — acoustic stuff, a bit of jazz. I think my taste got narrower but deeper.

Key words and phrases

drifted towards
Gradually became more interested in.

Do you prefer listening to music alone or with other people?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer listening alone. Music feels quite personal to me, and with other people I worry about whether they enjoy my choice instead of simply enjoying it myself.

Key words and phrases

personal
Connected with your own feelings, taste or private life.

Advanced Model Answer

Alone, definitely. Music is quite a private thing for me, and when other people are around I end up worrying about whether they like it instead of listening properly.

Key words and phrases

worrying about whether they like it
Being concerned about their approval.

Do you listen to music while you are working or studying?

Simple Model Answer

Only when the task is simple and familiar. Words in songs can distract me when I need to think, so I choose music without singing or work with no music at all.

Key words and phrases

distract me
Take my attention away from what I am doing.

Advanced Model Answer

Only if the work is routine. Anything that needs concentration and lyrics get in the way, so I either put on something instrumental or work in silence.

Key words and phrases

get in the way
Create a difficulty or obstruction.

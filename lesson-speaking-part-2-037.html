Place

A good place where people go to swim

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a good place where people go to swim.

You should say: What is this place? Where is it situated? Why is it so popular?

Simple Model Answer

A good place to swim is the Central Pool in District 4 of Ho Chi Minh City. I enjoy swimming because it helps me become fitter, so I go there most weekends.

The pool is very large, with separate areas for adults and children. The staff and lifeguards are professional, and I appreciate the way they manage the place. There is also a large restaurant nearby serving Vietnamese and Japanese food. After swimming for a while, it is pleasant to sit down and have something to eat.

The pool is open throughout the day and night, which makes visiting convenient. However, it gets particularly busy at weekends, so visitors often need to book tickets ahead of time. I bought a yearly ticket because I wanted to go regularly. I like having somewhere I can exercise and relax in the same visit. If I had more free time, I would invite friends to come with me so we could swim together rather than always training alone.

Key words and phrases

lifeguards
Trained people who help keep swimmers safe.
ahead of time
Before something happens or before it is needed.

Advanced Model Answer

To be honest, I'm pretty obsessed with swimming — it's a great way for me to build up my stamina. So I'd like to tell you about the Central Pool in District 4, which I go to most weekends.

First, let me mention the size of the place, because it's what everyone talks about. It's the biggest swimming pool in HCM City, around 50 metres wide and 100 metres long, and it's split into two separate areas — one for adults and one for children.

The pool's open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and right next to it there's a huge restaurant specialising in Vietnamese and Japanese food. It's the perfect spot to unwind with something to eat after a long training session in the water. On top of that, the staff and lifeguards there are properly professional, and — more importantly to me — they're always punctual, which gives everyone swimming there a real sense of respect and satisfaction.

For all these reasons, the pool's always packed at weekends, so you have to book tickets in advance if you want any chance of getting in. I actually bought a season ticket for the whole year so I can just gain access to the pool whenever I feel like it. If I had a bit more free time, I'd definitely invite more friends along so we could all train together.

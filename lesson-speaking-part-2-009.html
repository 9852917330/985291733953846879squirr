A new friend you have made recently

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a new friend you've made recently.

You should say: Who the person is What kind of person he/she is How you met this person And explain why you made friends with him/her

Simple Model Answer

A new friend I have made is Mrs Kim, who sells noodles near my university. We met on my first day of college about three months ago. I was looking for the HCMC University of Education, but I could not find it, even with Google Maps.

I finally asked Mrs Kim at her food cart. She smiled and pointed to the large building across the road. I felt embarrassed because I had been so close all along. We started talking, and I discovered that she came from Ninh Thuan, my hometown too. Now I often eat lunch at her cart.

I enjoy talking to her because she is warm and friendly. She has had a difficult life, but she still treats people kindly. Coming from the same place also gives us plenty to talk about. Our friendship was unexpected, and that makes it special to me. A stressful first morning at university ended with meeting someone who now helps me feel more at home.

Key words and phrases

had been so close all along
Had been near something for the whole time.
feel more at home
Feel more comfortable and relaxed in a place.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about Mrs Kim, a lovely lady I've recently become friends with.

I started college three months ago, and on my very first day I couldn't find my way to the university at all. Google Maps wasn't much help, and asking the locals didn't get me very far either. So, after wandering around for what felt like hours, I finally resorted to asking a lady who was selling noodles from a food cart near where I was standing. I asked her if she knew the way to the HCMC University of Education, and she just smiled and pointed at the huge five-storey building right across the road — turns out I'd been standing there the whole time! After thanking her, we ended up chatting for a bit, and I found out she's also from Ninh Thuan, which is my hometown too. We hit it off straight away, and now her food cart is my favourite lunch spot whenever I'm at university.

I think the main reason Mrs Kim and I get on so well is that she's just a genuinely sweet person. The more I've got to know her, the more I've realised she's had a pretty tough life, yet she's managed to hold on to a warm, gentle personality that plenty of other people would have lost after going through what she has. I guess the other reason we click is that we share the same hometown, and I tend to get on well with people from back home. Sometimes I think the best friendships are the ones you never see coming.

Key words and phrases

wandering around
Walking around without a strict destination.
resorted to asking
finally asked because other approaches did not work

come from the same town
get on well
have a friendly relationship

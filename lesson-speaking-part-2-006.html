Person

A famous person that you are interested in

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a famous person that you are interested in

You should say: - Who this person is. - How you know about this person. - What this person is famous for. - Why you are interested in this person.

Simple Model Answer

A famous person I am interested in is My Tam, a Vietnamese pop singer from Da Nang. She started her singing career in 1999. I first saw her performing on television several years ago and immediately liked her voice.

Since then, I have listened to many of her songs. I especially enjoy her slower songs because she expresses the feelings clearly. I also saw her at a concert in my city. Even though I was standing near the back, her singing made the performance feel special. Her album Tam 9 also helped her reach listeners outside Vietnam.

I admire her personality as well as her music. In interviews, she seems friendly and funny. She has organised free concerts for university students and worked with her fans to help people who are poor, ill or homeless. I think this combination of talent and kindness explains why so many people respect her. She makes me feel that a successful artist can also have a positive effect on others.

Key words and phrases

expresses the feelings
Shows the emotions in something clearly.

Make a situation or someone's life better.

Advanced Model Answer

A famous person I'm genuinely interested in is My Tam. To me, she's the best pop singer in Vietnam, and she embarked on her singing career back in 1999. She's originally from Da Nang, and she recently became Vietnam's first-ever artist to appear on Billboard's World Albums chart, after releasing her ninth studio album, 'Tam 9'.

I first came across her by accident. Some years ago, I caught a glimpse of her on TV, singing live, and I was immediately hooked by how effortless she made it look. I've been her loyal fan ever since. What I love most is the way she sings — she doesn't just hit the notes, she really seems to feel every line of the song, especially in the slower, more emotional tracks. I've watched her live once, at a concert here in the city, and even from the back of the crowd her voice carried the whole room.

She's also just as admirable as a person. She's known to all her fans for being down-to-earth and for having a great sense of humour in interviews, which isn't always the case with singers at her level of fame. On top of that, she's devoted her time to organising free concerts for university students nationwide, and together with her fans, she's quietly done a lot of charity work over the years to help the poor, the sick and the homeless.

Honestly, it's her combination of talent and genuine kindness that makes her my biggest idol, and I don't think that's going to change any time soon.

Transport

A driving test every year

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

The best way to make road transport safer is to require drivers to take a driving test every year. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

Most drivers pass one examination in their late teens and are never assessed again, which is a strange arrangement for an activity that kills over a million people a year. I agree that periodic checks would help, but annual testing for everybody would be an expensive way to address the wrong problem.

The reasoning behind re-testing is sound. Skills decay and bad habits accumulate, so a driver of thirty years' experience may have forgotten rules that have since changed and acquired several dangerous routines. Eyesight and reaction times also decline gradually, and the person concerned is usually the last to notice. A test would correct faults before they cause an accident rather than after.

The objections are practical and substantial. Testing every licence holder each year would require an enormous administration, and the cost would fall on drivers, which penalises those who need a car to work. It is also poorly targeted, since most accidents are not caused by an inability to drive: speeding, alcohol, tiredness and phone use cause the majority, and all of them are committed by people who would pass any test on the day of the examination. A driver on their best behaviour for forty minutes proves very little about the other days.

Measures with stronger evidence behind them exist. Enforcement of speed limits by camera, serious penalties for drink-driving and phone use, graduated licences that restrict new drivers, safer road design and vehicle technology such as automatic braking have all reduced deaths wherever they were introduced. Periodic checks still have a place, particularly eyesight tests and assessments for older drivers, but every few years rather than annually.

In conclusion, drivers should be re-checked from time to time, since skills and eyesight change. Annual testing would cost far more than it saved, so enforcement, road design and vehicle safety remain the better routes to safer roads.

Key words and phrases

correct faults before they cause an accident
Fix mistakes before they lead to a collision.
reduced deaths wherever they were introduced
Led to fewer fatalities in places using them.
enforcement, road design and vehicle safety
Applying laws and improving roads and vehicles.

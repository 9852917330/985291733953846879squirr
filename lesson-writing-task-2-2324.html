Health

Looking up symptoms instead of seeing a doctor

Reasons / Positive or Negative

Prompt

Nowadays, many people use the Internet to get medical advice instead of going to see a doctor. Why is this? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?

Model answer

Most people now search their symptoms before deciding whether to book an appointment. The reasons are largely practical, and the development is positive if patients use the Internet to prepare for a consultation rather than to replace one.

The main cause is difficulty of access. Waiting weeks for an appointment, or paying for one, makes a free answer in ten seconds extremely attractive, and shift workers and parents of small children often cannot attend during opening hours at all. Embarrassment plays a part too, since people will type a question about a rash or a mental health symptom that they would hesitate to say aloud to a stranger. There is also a rise in the wish to understand rather than simply obey, and patients arrive wanting to discuss options instead of receiving instructions.

The benefits are real. Reliable health sites explain conditions clearly and repeatedly, which no appointment allows, and a well-informed patient asks better questions and follows treatment more faithfully. Online guidance also prevents unnecessary visits for minor complaints and, in remote areas, is the only medical information available at all. Support communities help people with rare conditions find others in the same position.

The dangers are equally clear. Search results are not sorted by accuracy, and commercial sites sell remedies while pretending to inform. Serious illness is missed when a symptom is explained away, and the opposite error is common too: ordinary tiredness read as something grave produces anxiety out of all proportion to the evidence. Nothing online can examine a patient, which is where diagnosis actually happens.

In conclusion, people search because care is slow, costly or awkward to obtain, and good information genuinely helps them. The development is positive only where the Internet prepares the patient rather than replaces the examination.

Key words and phrases

hesitate to say aloud to a stranger
Feel uncomfortable describing it to an unfamiliar person.
the only medical information available at all
The sole accessible source of health knowledge.
anxiety out of all proportion to the evidence
Worry much greater than the facts justify.
prepares the patient rather than replaces the examination
Helps someone prepare for care without replacing assessment.

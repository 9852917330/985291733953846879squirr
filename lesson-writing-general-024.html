#024
Informal

Instructions for looking after a flat

Informal

Prompt

A friend will stay in your apartment and look after it while you are away. Write to your friend. In your letter: - give useful information about the apartment - provide important instructions - explain what to do if a problem occurs

Model answer

Hi Sophie,

Thanks for staying in my flat while I'm away. I thought I'd run through the essentials before I leave on Friday.

The spare key is in the lockbox beside the front door; the code is 4821. The hot-water switch is inside the kitchen cupboard, and the Wi-Fi password is written underneath the router. Please use anything in the kitchen and feel free to invite Mark over for dinner.

The main things are to water the balcony plants every three days, collect the post and keep the place secure whenever you go out. The windows in the bedroom can look closed even when they're not locked, so please check them carefully. The rubbish bins are taken downstairs on Wednesday evening.

In the unlikely event of a leak or electrical fault, turn off the main switch beside the fridge and call the building caretaker, Mr Hoang, on 0908 222 410. For anything less urgent, just message me.

Have a comfortable stay, and thanks again for helping.

Best, Alex

Key phrase meanings

run through the essentials
Explain the main things briefly.
In the unlikely event
If this unexpected situation happens.

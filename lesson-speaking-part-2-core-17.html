Excellent Help After a Cancelled Flight

Core Story

Prompt

Describe a good service you received from a company or organisation.

You should say: - what the service was - when and where you received it - why you used the service - and explain why you thought it was good.

OUTLINE

>>> Excellent Help After a Cancelled Flight
excellent service • hotel • cancelled evening flight
late arrival • stranded travellers • expected chaos
fast queue • clear room, breakfast and transport information
toiletries + larger room for a family • taxi arranged proactively
practical help + calm staff • good service reduces stress

Simple Model Answer

I received excellent service at a hotel after my evening flight was cancelled. I arrived late, tired and worried. Many other passengers were waiting in the lobby, so I expected a slow check-in. Instead, the receptionist organised the queue and explained the rooms, breakfast and transport clearly.

The staff stayed calm even when guests were upset. One family could not get their luggage, so the hotel gave them basic washing supplies and arranged a larger room. When I mentioned my early replacement flight, the receptionist booked a taxi before I needed to ask.

What impressed me was the way they noticed practical problems and solved them without making things complicated. They did not make unrealistic promises or use fancy language. Their actions simply made us feel less stressed. This changed my idea of good service. It does not have to mean luxury; sometimes it means helping someone through a difficult situation calmly and efficiently.

Key words and phrases

replacement flight
Another flight provided because the original one cannot be taken.
unrealistic promises
Claims about future help that cannot reasonably be kept.

Advanced Model Answer

One of the best examples of customer service I have experienced happened at a hotel after my evening flight was cancelled.

By the time I reached the hotel, it was late and the lobby was full of other stranded travellers. I expected a long wait and a fairly mechanical check-in. Instead, the receptionist organised the queue quickly, explained the available rooms and gave clear information about breakfast and transport back to the airport.

What impressed me most was how calmly the staff handled people who were tired and frustrated. One family had lost access to their luggage, so the hotel provided basic toiletries and arranged a larger room without making the situation complicated. When I mentioned that I had an early replacement flight, the receptionist also booked a taxi for me before I even asked.

The service was good because it solved practical problems while also reducing stress. Nobody used exaggerated language or made promises they could not keep; they simply noticed what people needed and acted efficiently. That experience changed my idea of good service. It is not about luxury. It is about making a difficult situation noticeably easier.

Key words and phrases

stranded travellers
People unable to continue their journey.
What impressed me most
The thing I admired the most.
without making the situation complicated
Keeping the situation simple.
solved practical problems
Fixed difficulties in everyday life.
noticeably easier
Clearly less difficult.

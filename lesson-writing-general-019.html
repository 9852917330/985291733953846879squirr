Advice about choosing work instead of college

Informal

Prompt

A friend has asked whether they should attend college or start working. You think employment would suit them better. Write to your friend. In your letter: - explain why college may not suit them - describe the advantages of getting a job - suggest suitable kinds of work

Model answer

Hi Jenny,

It was great to hear from you. I've thought carefully about your choice, and I honestly think starting work would suit you better than going straight to college.

You have always been a practical, hands-on learner, whereas most college courses involve long lectures, extensive reading and several years of theoretical study. I'm not saying you couldn't succeed, but I suspect you'd find that environment frustrating and lose motivation quite quickly.

A job would allow you to gain real-world experience, earn your own money and discover which type of work you genuinely enjoy. It would also give you more independence, and you could still take a professional course later once you have a clearer goal.

Because you're confident, organised and good with people, I'd look at customer service, hotel reception or event support. An apprenticeship in sales or digital marketing might also be worth considering because it combines training with paid work.

Of course, the final choice is yours, but there's no one-size-fits-all answer. Starting work now doesn't close the door to education in the future.

Take care, Alex

Key phrase meanings

straight to college
To college without a break.
a practical, hands-on learner
Someone who learns by doing.
find that environment frustrating
Feel annoyed in that setting.
combines training with paid work
Includes learning and earning money.
no one-size-fits-all answer
No solution suits everyone.

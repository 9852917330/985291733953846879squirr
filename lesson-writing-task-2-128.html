Technology & Science

The problems of mobile phones

Benefits & Drawbacks

Prompt

There are social, medical and technical problems associated with the use of mobile phones. What form do these problems take? Do the problems of using mobile phones outweigh the benefits?

Model answer

Mobile phones create difficulties that are social, medical and technical in nature. In my opinion these problems are real but manageable, and they do not outweigh the considerable benefits that the technology provides.

The social problems are the most visible. People frequently attend to their phones rather than to those physically present, which weakens conversation within families, and constant availability means that work now intrudes into evenings and holidays. Children are exposed to bullying that follows them home, and online harassment is far harder to escape than the playground variety ever was.

Medical concerns are more limited than often claimed. There is no reliable evidence that phone radiation causes cancer, but the effects on sleep are well established, since using a screen before bed delays the onset of sleep. Phone use while driving is a proven cause of fatal accidents, and repetitive strain in the hands and neck is now widespread. Technical problems include the loss of personal data through hacking, the theft of devices, and the environmental cost of manufacturing and discarding hundreds of millions of handsets.

Against these must be set benefits that were unimaginable a generation ago. A phone is a telephone, camera, map, bank, library and translator combined. It allows a farmer to check market prices, a traveller to navigate an unfamiliar city and anyone to summon emergency help immediately. In poorer countries, mobile banking has given millions their first access to financial services.

In conclusion, mobile phones create genuine social, medical and technical difficulties, but these arise mainly from how the devices are used. The advantages remain substantially greater.

Key words and phrases

intrudes into evenings and holidays
Interrupts personal time after work and during breaks.
delays the onset of sleep
Makes falling asleep happen later.
manufacturing and discarding
Making products and throwing them away.
summon emergency help immediately

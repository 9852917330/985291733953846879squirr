Disputing an increase in car insurance

Formal

Prompt

Your insurer has informed you that your monthly car-insurance payment will increase, and you disagree with the decision. Write to the insurer. In your letter: - explain why you are writing - state why the increase seems unjustified - say what you want the company to do

Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing regarding your notice dated 10 June, which states that my monthly car-insurance premium will rise by 28 per cent from August.

I would ask you to review this decision because my circumstances do not appear to justify such a substantial increase. I have held policy number MC-44821 for six years and maintained a completely claim-free record. My vehicle, annual mileage and home address have not changed, and the car is parked overnight in a locked garage. I have also completed an approved defensive-driving course since my last renewal.

Please provide a clear breakdown of the factors used to calculate the new premium. If the increase resulted from incorrect information, I would like the policy amended immediately. If it reflects a general pricing change, please reconsider the quotation in light of my history and confirm whether a higher excess or revised level of cover would reduce the cost.

I look forward to receiving a written response before the current policy expires.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

Key phrase meanings

clear breakdown
An understandable list of separate costs.
in light of
Considering this information.
maintained a completely claim-free record
Never requested an insurance payment.
do not appear to justify such a substantial increase
Do not explain such a large rise.
a higher excess or revised level of cover
Pay more yourself or change insurance protection.

Daily Routine

A typical weekday and a typical weekend

Part 1

Prompt

What would you like to change in your daily routine? Are all your days the same? Tell me about a typical weekday and a typical weekend. What is the balance of work/ study and free time in your normal days?

What would you like to change in your daily routine?

Simple Model Answer

I'd like to use screens less in the evening. I could read or take a short walk instead, which would help me relax before going to bed.

Key words and phrases

relax
Become calmer and less worried or tense.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to spend less time looking at screens in the evening. Reading or going for a short walk instead would help me wind down properly before bed.

Key words and phrases

wind down properly
Fully relax after being busy.

Are all your days the same?

Simple Model Answer

No. My weekdays are quite similar, but I have more freedom at weekends. I still keep regular exercise and meal times because they help me organise my day.

Key words and phrases

regular
Happening at similar times or in a usual pattern.

Advanced Model Answer

Weekdays follow a fairly stable pattern, but weekends are much more flexible. I still keep a few fixed habits, such as exercise and meal times, because they give my day structure.

Key words and phrases

give my day structure
Organise my day into a clear pattern.

Tell me about a typical weekday and a typical weekend.

Simple Model Answer

On weekdays, I work, exercise and get ready for the next day. At weekends, I get up a little later, meet friends or do personal tasks, so life feels less busy.

Key words and phrases

get ready
Prepare yourself for something.

Advanced Model Answer

On weekdays I work, exercise and prepare for the following day. At weekends I sleep a little later, meet friends or handle personal errands, so the pace is far less demanding.

Key words and phrases

far less demanding
Requiring much less effort.

What is the balance of work/ study and free time in your normal days?

Simple Model Answer

Work fills most of my weekdays, but I still make time for exercise, meals and family. The balance could be better, although I try to keep a routine I can manage.

Key words and phrases

make time for
Arrange to have enough time to do something.

Advanced Model Answer

Work takes up most of my weekdays, but I protect time for exercise, meals and family. The balance isn't perfect, but I make a conscious effort to maintain a sustainable routine.

Key words and phrases

make a conscious effort
Try deliberately rather than automatically.
a sustainable routine
A habit you can keep following.

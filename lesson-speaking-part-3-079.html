Sports & Exercise

Why sport matters and sportspeople as role models

Prompt

Why do you think sport is important? Do you think famous sportspeople are good role models for children? Do you agree that sports stars earn too much money?

Why do you think sport is important?

Simple Model Answer

Sport is enjoyable, helps fitness and creates chances to make friends. Training also teaches patience because improvement is not always quick. When I play football, I can forget other worries and enjoy the moment. However, these benefits depend partly on enjoying the activity. Forcing someone into a game they dislike may put them off sport for years.

Key words and phrases

put them off
Make them dislike or avoid something.

Advanced Model Answer

I think sport's important for a few different reasons. For me, doing a sport's mainly about having fun — when I play football, for example, I forget about everything else and just enjoy myself. It also helps you keep fit and healthy, and it's a good way to socialise and make friends. There's a discipline side too: training regularly teaches you that progress is slow and uneven, and that lesson transfers to almost everything else you do. That's assuming it stays enjoyable, though — plenty of people are put off sport for life by being forced into competitive games at school that they never actually liked.

Key words and phrases

keep fit
Remain healthy through exercise.

Do you think famous sportspeople are good role models for children?

Simple Model Answer

They should try to be because children admire them and copy their behaviour. Famous players therefore have a responsibility to act fairly and set a useful example. However, expecting perfection is unfair. Many are young and under constant pressure, so we should recognise their mistakes without assuming they must be ideal people in every part of life.

Key words and phrases

admire
Respect someone and think highly of them.

Advanced Model Answer

I think that sportspeople should be good role models. Children look up to their favourite football players, like David Beckham for example, so I think that these people have an enormous responsibility. They should try to be a good influence, and behave in the right way. That said, I think it's unfair to expect them to be perfect. They're usually young people under enormous public pressure, and we ask far more of them than we would of anyone else.

Key words and phrases

look up to
admire and respect
an enormous responsibility
a very large duty to others

Do you agree that sports stars earn too much money?

Simple Model Answer

I think high earnings can be fair because top athletes train hard and many fans pay to watch them. The money reflects that demand. What concerns me more is the difference within sport: a few stars earn enormous sums while many professionals have ordinary pay and short careers. That gap seems harder to justify than success itself.

Key words and phrases

justify
Give a convincing reason why something is reasonable or fair.

Advanced Model Answer

In my opinion, it's fair that the best sportspeople earn a lot of money. Being a top sportsperson requires hours of practice, and there are millions of sports fans who are willing to pay to see them play. If we don't want sportspeople to earn so much money, we shouldn't go to watch them. What does seem unfair is the gap within the sport itself. A handful of players earn extraordinary sums while most professionals earn an ordinary wage and retire in their thirties.

Key words and phrases

requires hours of practice
needs many hours of training
willing to pay
prepared to spend money on it

Lisbon and Its Waterfront

Core Story

Prompt

Describe a place you would like to visit again.

You should say: - where the place is - when you first visited it - what you did there - and explain why you want to return.

OUTLINE

>>> Lisbon and Its Waterfront
place to revisit • Lisbon • old neighbourhoods + Tagus waterfront
hilly streets • old trams • viewpoints • rooftops
sunset waterfront walk • coffee • locals + tourists • lively, not chaotic
next visit • slower pace • local food • museums • quieter areas
history + everyday life + beautiful public spaces

Simple Model Answer

I'd like to visit Lisbon again, especially its old neighbourhoods and the area beside the Tagus River. I first went on a short trip and thought the famous sights would be the best part. Instead, I most enjoyed simply walking around.

The streets were steep, old trams passed through narrow corners, and viewpoints opened onto rooftops and water. One afternoon, I reached the river just before sunset. Light shone on the pale buildings, and the area felt busy but not stressful. I bought a coffee, sat outside and watched local families, workers and tourists pass by.

I want to return because the trip was too short to understand the city fully. Next time, I would explore quieter areas, try more local food and spend longer in museums. Lisbon stays in my memory because its history and ordinary daily life sit naturally together. Even doing very little there felt interesting and enjoyable.

Key words and phrases

viewpoints
Places offering a clear or attractive view.
pass by
Go past a place or person.

Advanced Model Answer

A place I would genuinely like to visit again is Lisbon, especially the older neighbourhoods and the waterfront along the Tagus River.

I visited the city on a short trip and expected to spend most of my time seeing famous landmarks. In reality, the best part was simply walking. The streets rise and fall sharply, old trams move through narrow corners, and small viewpoints suddenly open onto rooftops and the river.

One afternoon I walked down towards the waterfront just before sunset. The light reflected off the pale buildings and the river, and the whole area felt lively without being chaotic. I bought a coffee, sat outside and watched commuters, tourists and local families pass by. Nothing dramatic happened, but the scene had a strong sense of place.

I want to return because I felt I only saw the surface of the city. Next time I would stay longer, explore areas away from the main tourist routes and take more time with local food and museums. Lisbon is memorable to me because it combines history, everyday life and beautiful public spaces in a very natural way.

Key words and phrases

the best part was simply walking
Walking itself was the most enjoyable activity.
open onto
Lead directly into or overlook.
without being chaotic
Without being confusing or disorganised.
a strong sense of place
A clear feeling of the location's identity.
away from the main tourist routes
In places that tourists visit less often.

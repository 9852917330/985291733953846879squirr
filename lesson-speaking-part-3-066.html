#066
Money

Why saving matters and teaching children about money

Prompt

Why is it important for people to save some of their money? In your view, should children be taught how to manage money? Do you think that girls are better at saving money than boys?

Model answer

Why is it important for people to save some of their money?

Answer 1

In the long term I think it's important to save for retirement, but it's also a good idea to have money set aside so you can deal with unexpected problems sooner than that. For example, I've learned to put some money aside for car repairs, and that came in useful recently when I needed my car's brake pads changed — if I hadn't had savings, the bill could have sent me into the red. The exception, I think, is people on very low incomes, where telling them to save doesn't help much if there's nothing left over at the end of the month.

In your view, should children be taught how to manage money?

Answer 2

Not at school, no — I think it's mainly the job of parents to teach children that gradually, and a lot of it they pick up naturally just by growing up around money. I remember being given money for sweets or an ice cream as a kid, and I soon learned what I could afford and what I couldn't. Where I'd make an exception is the more technical stuff — interest, credit, pensions — because that genuinely is easier to learn properly in a classroom than to pick up by trial and error.

Do you think that girls are better at saving money than boys?

Answer 3

I've never really thought about that before, and I don't see any obvious reason why girls would be better savers than boys, or the other way round. I suppose it depends more on the individual's personality than their gender — a child who likes cheap toys might not think about saving at all, whereas one who wants something expensive, like a bike or a games console, might summon the willpower to save up! If there is a pattern, I'd guess it's more about how parents treat sons and daughters differently around money than anything to do with the children themselves.

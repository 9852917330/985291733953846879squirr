Situation

A positive experience that you had as a teenager

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a positive experience that you had as a teenager.

You should say: - what the positive experience was - where you were - who you were with - and explain why you found the experience positive.

OUTLINE

>>> London school trip
never London before great share experience w/ school friends
far remember enjoyed whole trip coach journey visits various tourist attractions
got off coach near Houses Parliament one 1st memories seeing famous
said earlier school trip think around thirty including two teachers
struck London historic modern thriving same time
Coming relatively small town experience made keen visit capital cities

Simple Model Answer

A positive experience I had as a teenager was my first school trip to London. There were around thirty students and two teachers. I went with several close friends, which made the coach journey and visits especially enjoyable.

We got off near the Houses of Parliament. I remember looking up at the Big Ben clock tower and being surprised by how much larger it seemed than on television. We also visited Buckingham Palace, the Tower of London and Trafalgar Square. At the palace, I was fascinated by the guards standing so still.

Coming from a small town, I found London exciting. It had historic buildings but also felt modern, busy and international. My friends and I kept pointing things out and laughing together, so the experience was about more than seeing famous sights. It gave us shared memories outside the classroom. The trip made me want to explore other capital cities and showed me that travelling with school could be both educational and fun. I remember the whole day positively rather than only one particular attraction.

Key words and phrases

international
Including or connected with people from many countries.
shared memories
Experiences that several people remember having together.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe the first time I visited London, the capital of England, on a school trip when I was a teenager. I'd never been before, and it was brilliant to share that experience with my school friends rather than go with family.

The positive experience wasn't confined to any one place, as far as I remember, I enjoyed pretty much the whole trip, from the coach journey down to the visits to all the different tourist attractions. We got off the coach right near the Houses of Parliament, so one of my first memories is looking up at the famous Big Ben clock tower and just thinking how much bigger it was than it looked on TV. We also visited the Tower of London, Buckingham Palace and Trafalgar Square, and I remember being fascinated by the guards standing so still outside the palace gates.

As I said, it was a school trip, so there were around thirty of us altogether, including two teachers, and I was with a group of close friends, which definitely made the experience more enjoyable. We were constantly laughing and pointing things out to each other, which you wouldn't get on a trip with your parents.

What really struck me about London was that it felt historic and modern at the same time, lively, fashionable and properly cosmopolitan. Coming from a relatively small town myself, the whole experience left me really keen to visit more big capital cities in the future.

Object & Favourite

A kind of music or a song

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a kind of music or a song

You should say: What kind of music it is Where you usually listen to it Why you like it And say what kind of mood this music puts you in

Simple Model Answer

A kind of music I particularly enjoy is ballads. These are usually slower songs about love, sadness or important feelings. I listen to other styles too, but I return to ballads most often because they match my personality.

I normally listen on my phone with headphones. I play them on the bus, while doing housework or before going to sleep. When I feel low, a familiar melody can help me express feelings that are difficult to explain.

Many songs also bring back memories. A particular ballad might remind me of school friends, a family car journey or an old relationship. This can make me feel a little sad because that time has passed, but it is also comforting to remember it. Sometimes I fall asleep listening and wake up thinking about those happy moments. I like music that has an emotional connection with my own life. For me, ballads offer more than background sound; they help me relax and remember people and experiences that matter.

Advanced Model Answer

I like to think of myself as a lover of music that's kind of sentimental and romantic. I listen to loads of different music genres, but the one I particularly enjoy most is ballads — that slow tempo, sad, heart-breaking kind of song.

I mostly listen on my phone with my headphones in, so honestly you'll catch me playing ballads pretty much anywhere — on the bus, doing chores, lying in bed before I fall asleep. I'm quite a sentimental person by nature, so whenever I'm feeling low, I'll just put one on and let the melody say what I can't.

Ballads give me a sense of nostalgia — they bring back memories of my childhood, old friends, past relationships, all of it. Every time a particular ballad comes on, I'm suddenly back at some specific moment, like messing around with my friends after school or a long car ride with my family. It's like the genre unlocks my past for me.

The mood it puts me in is a strange mix of sad and comforting at the same time. I feel a bit wistful that those days are behind me, but I also find myself looking forward to whatever's still ahead. And if I happen to fall asleep with a ballad playing, I sometimes dream about those exact memories, which honestly leaves me smiling when I wake up. It really ticks every box I look for in a genre of music.

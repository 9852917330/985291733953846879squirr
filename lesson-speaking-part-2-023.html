An old friend you enjoy talking with

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an old friend you enjoy talking with

You should say: Who this person is How you knew him/her What you usually talk about And explain why you want to talk to this person

OUTLINE

>>> Nguyen
university friend Nguyen, approachable and funny, met by chance at a citywide singing contest
didn't make much impression at first, only became close after bumping into each other again at school
what we talk about: music/guitar mostly, also uni gossip, family, where our lives are heading, relationships (he never judges)
why I enjoy talking to him: his warm, playful voice and jokes always crack me up
he's abroad finishing further education now so we don't talk as often, but still the person I most enjoy talking to

Simple Model Answer

An old friend I enjoy talking to is Nguyen. We met during a city singing competition in our second year at university. We were competing against each other, but we became friends after meeting again at school a few months later.

We both play guitar, so music is our favourite subject. We talk about bands, university life, our families and our plans for the future. I can also speak honestly about relationships because he listens without judging me. He has a warm voice and often makes jokes, which helps when I am in a bad mood.

Once, we spent almost a whole day talking in a quiet cafe, from seven in the morning until ten at night. I never felt bored. He is now studying abroad, so we do not meet as often. However, I still enjoy every conversation we have. I value our friendship because talking to him feels easy, and I can be myself rather than worrying about saying the perfect thing.

Key words and phrases

without judging me
Without deciding that I am wrong or a bad person.
be myself
Behave naturally without pretending to be different.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about Nguyen, one of my university friends, who's probably the most approachable and funny guy I've ever met. We weren't classmates and we weren't even in the same clubs — I actually met him purely by chance, when we ended up as rival candidates in a citywide singing contest back in our second year.

At the time, he was just an average-looking guy with a silly grin permanently on his face, so if I'm honest, he didn't make much of an impression on me then. It wasn't until we bumped into each other again at school a few months later that we actually started talking properly, and we hit it off almost straight away.

We talk about pretty much everything, but music's probably our favourite subject — we're both into guitar, and we'll argue for hours about which bands are actually any good. Beyond that, it's mostly university gossip, our families, and increasingly, where our lives are heading now we've both graduated. He's also one of the only people I can be properly honest with about relationships — he never judges, he just listens.

If there's one thing that makes Nguyen so easy to talk to, it's his voice — he's got this warm, playful way of speaking, always slipping in a joke, and it somehow cracks me up even when I'm in a foul mood. We once spent an entire day, from seven in the morning until ten at night, just lying on the floor of a quiet cafe talking about nothing in particular, and I never once got bored.

These days he's abroad, finishing off his further education, so I don't see him nearly as often as I'd like — but he's still, without question, the person I most enjoy talking to.

Key words and phrases

bumped into each other
met each other unexpectedly
hit it off
became friendly immediately
properly honest with
completely truthful with
cracks me up
makes me laugh a lot
further education
study after secondary school, often at college or university

Music

Instruments you enjoy and learning at school

Part 1

Prompt

Which instruments do you like listening to most and why? Have you ever learned to play a musical instrument at school and why? Do you think children should learn to play a musical instrument at school? How easy would it be to learn to play an instrument without a teacher?

Which instruments do you like listening to most and why?

Simple Model Answer

I most enjoy piano and acoustic guitar. They can sound gentle or strong and fit many styles of music, while still allowing me to hear the tune clearly.

Key words and phrases

tune
The main series of notes in a piece of music.

Advanced Model Answer

I enjoy the piano and acoustic guitar most. Both can sound intimate or powerful, and they work across loads of musical styles without overwhelming the melody.

Key words and phrases

overwhelming the melody
Becoming louder or more noticeable than the tune.

Have you ever learned to play a musical instrument at school and why?

Simple Model Answer

I had a few simple music lessons at school, but I never became very good. Still, I learned about rhythm and realised that musicians need to practise regularly.

Key words and phrases

rhythm
The pattern of beats in music.

Advanced Model Answer

I had a few basic music lessons at school but never really got good at it. Even so, they taught me rhythm and showed me how much consistent practice a musician needs.

Key words and phrases

consistent practice
Regular, continued work on a skill.

Do you think children should learn to play a musical instrument at school?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I think they should learn the basics. Playing an instrument helps children listen carefully, control their movements and practise regularly. It also gives them a way to be creative.

Key words and phrases

creative
Able to use imagination to make or express something.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, at least at a basic level. It's great for their listening, coordination and discipline, and it gives them a creative form of expression too.

Key words and phrases

a creative form of expression
An imaginative way to share feelings or ideas.

How easy would it be to learn to play an instrument without a teacher?

Simple Model Answer

You can learn from videos and apps, but it may be harder without a teacher to correct mistakes. Learning alone works better if you have a clear plan for practice.

Key words and phrases

correct mistakes
Show what is wrong and help make it right.

Advanced Model Answer

It's possible with videos and apps, but a teacher can correct your technique before bad habits set in. Self-study works best when you've already got a clear practice plan.

Key words and phrases

a clear practice plan
A specific schedule for improving a skill.

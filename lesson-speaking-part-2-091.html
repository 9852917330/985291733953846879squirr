A TV series you enjoy watching

Prompt

Describe a TV series you enjoy watching

Simple Model Answer

A television series I enjoy is Friends. It follows six people in New York as they deal with jobs, relationships and family problems. Much of the story takes place in an apartment or a nearby coffee shop.

I discovered it about two years ago, during a stressful exam period at school. A classmate played it while we were studying, and after a couple of episodes I wanted to see more. I watched much of the first season that week, although I probably should have spent more time revising.

I like the programme because it is easy and comforting to watch. Episodes last about twenty minutes, so I can enjoy one without following a complicated plot. Chandler is my favourite character because his quick jokes often make me laugh, even when I have seen the episode before. Some clothes and objects look old now, but the friendship still feels familiar. After a bad day, watching the group support each other usually helps me feel better. It is a simple way to relax without too much effort.

Key words and phrases

revising
Studying material again to prepare for an exam.
comforting
Making someone feel calmer, safer or less unhappy.

Advanced Model Answer

The TV series I want to talk about is Friends, the American sitcom about a group of six friends living in New York. I know it finished decades ago, but I only discovered it myself about two years ago, during the exam stress of my final year at school, when I badly needed something light to switch my brain off with.

It follows six friends — Ross, Rachel, Monica, Chandler, Joey and Phoebe — through their twenties and thirties, mostly hanging out in Monica's apartment or the coffee shop downstairs, dealing with ordinary things like jobs, break-ups and family drama, but always with a joke close behind. There isn't really one big storyline; it's more about watching the same group grow up together over ten years.

I first watched it completely by accident. A classmate had it running in the background during a study session, and within about two episodes I was completely hooked. I ended up watching the entire first season that same week instead of revising, which I probably shouldn't admit.

The reason I love it so much is that it's just endlessly comforting. Every episode wraps up neatly in about twenty minutes, so it's perfect when I'm tired and don't want to commit to anything complicated. Chandler is definitely my favourite character — his sarcastic one-liners genuinely make me laugh out loud, even on a rewatch, and I think that's rare for a show this old.

What surprises me most is how well it's held up. The fashion is obviously dated, but the friendships and the humour still feel completely relevant, and I think that's exactly why it's stayed so popular with people my age who weren't even born when it first came out. Whenever I've had a bad day, I still put an episode on just to feel better, and it works pretty much every time.

Key words and phrases

switch my brain off

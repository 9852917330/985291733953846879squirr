#004
Formal

Complaint about a theatre visit

Formal

Prompt

You attended a good play at a theatre but could not enjoy it because of problems at the venue. Write to the theatre manager. In your letter: - identify the performance and date - describe the problems - suggest what should be done

Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing about the evening performance of The Last Station at the Grand Theatre on Friday, 5 June.

The play itself was excellent, but the experience was seriously undermined by several problems. When my partner and I arrived, another couple had been allocated the same seats. An usher moved us to the front row, where a stage light shone directly into our eyes throughout the first act. In addition, the air-conditioning was not working, and the auditorium became uncomfortably hot.

I appreciate that isolated technical problems occur, but a double-booking error should have been resolved before the performance began. The replacement seats were clearly unsuitable, yet staff did not offer us another option or explain how to make a complaint.

I suggest reviewing the booking system, checking lighting positions before each show and training ushers to handle seating errors consistently. Given the disruption, I would also consider a partial refund or replacement tickets to be a reasonable remedy.

I look forward to your response.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

Key phrase meanings

seriously undermined by several problems
Badly weakened by several difficulties.
a reasonable remedy
A fair way to fix things.
a double-booking error should have been resolved
The duplicate booking needed fixing.
training ushers to handle seating errors consistently
Teaching staff a consistent seating procedure.
I would also consider a partial refund
Some money back could be acceptable.

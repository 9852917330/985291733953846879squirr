Place

A shop that has opened in your hometown

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a shop that has opened in your hometown

You should say: What shop it is What it sells (or, what you usually buy there) When you first started going to this shop (or, how you came to know about this shop) And explain why you like (to go to) this shop

Simple Model Answer

A shop that recently opened in my hometown is Tranquil Cafe, a book-and-coffee shop in Hanoi. It has traditional-style decoration, paintings and shelves full of books that customers can read during their visit.

I first went there about three months ago after reading an online article. By chance, my visit was on opening day. I liked the place immediately because it felt warm and comfortable. It serves coffee, cakes and light snacks. I normally order coffee with condensed milk and choose a book from the shelves.

The main reason I return is the peaceful atmosphere. Hanoi's traffic can leave me feeling stressed, so sitting with a drink and a book is a welcome break. On some Saturday evenings, a member of staff plays acoustic music, which adds to the experience. The staff also remember regular customers and sometimes give me a small discount. I like having a place where reading and relaxing are equally welcome, and I expect to keep visiting it for a long time.

Key words and phrases

acoustic music
Music mainly played with instruments that do not need electronic sound effects.
regular customers
People who visit and buy things from the same business often.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, a shop that's just opened in my hometown that I want to talk about is Tranquil Cafe, which I'd say is one of the best book-and-coffee shops in Hanoi.

It's decorated in an old, traditional Hanoi style, with bookshelves everywhere, and it actually attracts quite a few foreign tourists too. They do lovely coffee — I always go for a black coffee with condensed milk — plus cakes and a few light snacks, and there's a whole wall of books you're welcome to borrow while you sit there.

I still vividly remember the first time I went, about three months ago, which happened to be the opening day. It was completely by chance — I'd read an article online about a new spot everyone in Hanoi was starting to talk about, and I was curious enough to go and check it out. The place caught my eye the second I walked in. The whole space was so unique, with gorgeous paintings on the walls and quirky little touches everywhere, and it somehow made me feel completely at home.

The main reason I love it, though, is the warm ambience — I haven't found that anywhere else in the city. Hanoi's such a busy, noisy place, and I'm always a bit wound up after fighting through the traffic, so I often go there as a hideaway, just to get away from the hustle and bustle for an hour. A coffee and a good book there is honestly one of the most soothing things I know. On Saturday nights, one of the staff sometimes plays live acoustic music too, which makes it even better. And the waiters remember their regulars — they always give me a little discount now, which is a nice touch.

So yeah, for all those reasons, I know I'll be going back in the long run.

vividly remember
can clearly recall
caught my eye

Study & Education

Your time at school and the subjects you liked

Part 1

Prompt

Do you enjoy your time at school? Would you recommend your school to others? What subject did you find most interesting when you were at school? Apart from classes, what else did you enjoy at school?

Do you enjoy your time at school?

Simple Model Answer

Overall, I enjoyed school. I made close friends and learned useful knowledge and skills. Exams were stressful sometimes, but looking back, I think school was a valuable experience.

Key words and phrases

looking back
Thinking about something that happened in the past.

Advanced Model Answer

Overall, yes. School gave me close friendships and a strong academic foundation, although the exam pressure wasn't always easy to handle. Looking back, it was a valuable learning experience.

Key words and phrases

a strong academic foundation
Strong basic knowledge and study skills.
a valuable learning experience
An event that teaches useful lessons.

Would you recommend your school to others?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, mainly because the teachers worked hard and supported us. The school had clear rules, but it wasn't an unfriendly place. It had problems like any school, but I would still recommend it.

Key words and phrases

recommend
Suggest that something is a good choice.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd recommend it, mainly because the teachers were committed and the environment was disciplined without being hostile. No school's perfect, but it offered a supportive environment.

Key words and phrases

a supportive environment
A place where people encourage and help you.

What subject did you find most interesting when you were at school?

Simple Model Answer

English interested me most. It helped me understand books and films and communicate with people from other countries. I could see how I would use it outside school as well as in exams.

Key words and phrases

communicate
Share information, ideas or feelings with other people.

Advanced Model Answer

English was the most interesting subject for me because it opened access to books, films and people from other countries. It felt like a practical skill I'd keep using, not just something for exams.

Key words and phrases

a practical skill
An ability that is useful in real situations.

Apart from classes, what else did you enjoy at school?

Simple Model Answer

I enjoyed clubs, trips and spending time with classmates outside lessons. These experiences helped me become more confident and learn to work with others in a different way.

Key words and phrases

classmates
Other students in the same class as you.

Advanced Model Answer

I enjoyed clubs, school trips and informal time with classmates. Those activities developed confidence and teamwork in ways that ordinary lessons couldn't fully replicate.

Key words and phrases

fully replicate
Copy every part accurately.

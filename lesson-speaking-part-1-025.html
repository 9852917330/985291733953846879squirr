Festivals

The main festivals in your country

Part 1

Prompt

Can you describe one of the main festivals celebrated in your country? What can you tell me about the most important festival in your country? What special food and activities are connected with this festival? What do you most enjoy about it?

Can you describe one of the main festivals celebrated in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Tet is the Lunar New Year and Vietnam's most important festival. Families come together, clean and decorate their homes, and get ready for a new year. It is a time for being together.

Key words and phrases

come together
Meet and spend time as a group.

Advanced Model Answer

Tet, the Lunar New Year, is the most important festival in Vietnam. Families reunite, clean and decorate their homes, and prepare for a fresh start, so the celebration brings people together.

Key words and phrases

brings people together
Creates opportunities for people to connect.

What can you tell me about the most important festival in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Tet marks the start of a new year and gives people a break from work. It is also a time to remember family members who have died and reconnect with relatives.

Key words and phrases

reconnect with
Become close to or communicate with someone again.

Advanced Model Answer

Tet matters because it marks a new beginning and gives everyone a proper break from work. Beyond the celebrations, it's a time to honour ancestors and renew family connections.

Key words and phrases

renew family connections
Reconnect with family members.

What special food and activities are connected with this festival?

Simple Model Answer

People eat sticky-rice cakes, sweet preserved fruit and special family dishes. They visit relatives, give children lucky money and remember their ancestors. These customs help keep Vietnamese traditions alive.

Key words and phrases

ancestors
Family members from generations before your parents or grandparents.

Advanced Model Answer

Traditional foods include traditional sticky-rice cakes, candied fruit and various family dishes. People also visit relatives, give lucky money to children and pay respect to ancestors, which helps preserve local identity.

Key words and phrases

preserve local identity
Keep the area's distinctive traditions alive.

What do you most enjoy about it?

Simple Model Answer

I most enjoy having a slower, quieter few days with my family. The celebrations are fun, but I especially like the feeling of starting again with fresh energy.

Key words and phrases

starting again
Making a new beginning.

Advanced Model Answer

I enjoy the slower pace and the chance to spend uninterrupted time with family. The atmosphere's festive, but the most valuable part is the sense of renewal.

Key words and phrases

sense of renewal
A feeling of making a fresh start.

Health

Doctor consultation times

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The table shows data about the average length of time (in minutes) that people of different ages spend in a consultation with family doctors in a number of countries. Write a report summarising the information. Select and describe the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The table gives information about the typical time that patients spend with their family doctors in a number of countries, broken down by age group.

Consultation times generally rise with age in France, the USA, the UK, Italy and Chile. Brazil and India reach a peak and then fall, while Japan fluctuates and Kenya shows an overall decline.

The figure for France more than doubles, from 16 to 38 minutes, between the youngest and the oldest people. Likewise, the USA figure rises from 13 to 31, the UK from 9 to 21, and Italy from 17 to 29. These are all substantial increases, while Chile has a consistent but much smaller rise, from 32 to 39 minutes.

Brazil also shows a rise, although after age 60 there is a decline from 26 minutes to an eventual 20. India has a more marked rise and fall, peaking at 21 minutes for people aged 40 to 60 and declining to 11 minutes for those aged 70 and over. In Japan the figures fluctuate across the age groups, between 12 and 16 minutes, while Kenya shows a non-increasing pattern as age rises, from 15 minutes for the youngest to 10 minutes for the oldest people.

Key words and phrases

more than doubles
Becomes over twice as much.
a more marked rise and fall
A more noticeable increase and decrease.
fluctuate across the age groups
Rise and fall between age categories.

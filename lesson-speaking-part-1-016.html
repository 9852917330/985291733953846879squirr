Cooking

Cooking at home and learning to cook

Prompt

Do you like cooking? Who did the cooking in your family when you were a child? Do you think that it's important to know how to cook well? Do you think that children should be taught cookery at school?

Do you like cooking?

Simple Model Answer

Sometimes. I enjoy making a special meal when family or friends visit. However, cooking every day feels more like a job I have to do than something fun.

Key words and phrases

have to do
Need to do because it is necessary.

Advanced Model Answer

Sometimes. I like preparing a special meal for family or friends who visit, but everyday cooking's a bit of a chore — it has to be done, but it's not really fun.

Key words and phrases

preparing a special meal
Making food for a special occasion.
a bit of a chore
An unpleasant task you must do.

Who did the cooking in your family when you were a child?

Simple Model Answer

My mother did most of the cooking when I was a child. I don't think she believed my father could prepare a good meal, so she usually did it herself.

Key words and phrases

most of
The largest part of something.

Advanced Model Answer

My mother almost always did the cooking when I was young. I don't think she trusted my father to make a nice meal.

Key words and phrases

make a nice meal
Prepare enjoyable food.

Do you think that it's important to know how to cook well?

Simple Model Answer

I think everyone should be able to cook a few simple meals, even if they are not excellent cooks. Eating ready-made meals or fast food all the time is not healthy.

Key words and phrases

ready-made meals
Meals prepared in advance that usually only need heating.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm not sure whether it's important to cook well, but I do think that everyone should know the basics. It definitely isn't healthy to rely on pre-prepared meals or fast food.

Key words and phrases

know the basics
Understand the most important simple skills.

Do you think that children should be taught cookery at school?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I think that would be useful. Children could learn to make a few simple, healthy meals using basic ingredients. Those skills would help them when they are older.

Key words and phrases

basic ingredients
The main foods used to prepare a dish.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, that's probably a good idea. If children learned to cook from scratch — even just a few basic, healthy meals — that would definitely be useful for later in life.

Key words and phrases

cook from scratch
Prepare food using basic ingredients.

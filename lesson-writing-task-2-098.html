Children & Family

Couples who choose not to have children

advdis

Prompt

There is an increasing trend around the world of married couples deciding not to have children. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages for couples who decide to do this.

Model answer

More married couples are choosing not to have children. This decision can give adults greater freedom and financial security, but it may also create personal and social disadvantages. On balance, the effects depend on the couple's circumstances and the scale of the trend.

For individuals, remaining child-free can reduce financial pressure and allow more time for careers, relationships and personal interests. Raising a child requires substantial money, energy and long-term responsibility. Some people also decide that they cannot provide the stable home they would want for a family. In such cases, choosing not to become parents is a responsible personal decision rather than a selfish one.

The disadvantages are less predictable. Some couples may later regret the decision or feel isolated in old age, although having children does not guarantee support. At a national level, a very low birth rate can shrink the future workforce and increase the cost of pensions and healthcare. Governments may then need immigration, later retirement or stronger family support policies to maintain a balanced population.

The scale of the trend also matters. A modest number of child-free couples has little effect on society, but the pattern becomes serious once it turns into a generational norm: South Korea's fertility rate, now among the lowest in the world, has already forced schools to close and left some regions with more retirees than working adults. This does not justify pressuring couples into parenthood, but it does mean governments cannot simply hope the trend reverses on its own; the more realistic response is policy that makes parenthood a genuine choice rather than a financial penalty.

In conclusion, couples should be free to decide whether parenthood suits them. The personal advantages can be considerable, but widespread childlessness may create demographic challenges. Policy should make raising children more affordable without pressuring people into parenthood.

Key words and phrases

a responsible personal decision
A carefully considered choice about your own life.
less predictable
Harder to know in advance.
maintain a balanced population
Keep a suitable mix of age groups.
turns into a generational norm
Becomes the usual behaviour for an age group.
a genuine choice rather than a financial penalty
A freely available option without financial punishment.

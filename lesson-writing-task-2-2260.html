Environment

Drilling in untouched places

Advantages outweigh disadvantages

Prompt

The increased world demand for oil and gas has made it necessary to locate these sources in remote and untouched natural areas. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages of damaging these natural areas?

Model answer

As accessible reserves are exhausted, energy companies have moved into the Arctic, deep oceans and rainforest. I do not believe the advantages outweigh the damage, although the arguments in favour deserve to be taken seriously.

The case for extraction rests on need. Modern economies still run on oil and gas, and a country that cannot secure supply faces rising prices that fall hardest on poorer households, who spend the largest share of their income on heating and transport. Domestic production also reduces dependence on unstable exporters, and for developing countries a discovery can fund schools and hospitals that would otherwise be unaffordable.

The damage, however, is of a different kind from the benefit. These regions are remote precisely because they are difficult to reach, which means that any spill is almost impossible to contain: oil in Arctic ice degrades over decades rather than years, and there is no equipment capable of cleaning it. Extraction also brings roads, pipelines and settlements into ecosystems that had none, so the disturbance extends far beyond the drilling site. Indigenous communities lose the land their way of life depends on.

The decisive objection is that this is investment in the wrong direction. A new field takes decades to repay its cost, which commits a country to burning fossil fuels long after it has promised to stop, and the same capital spent on renewable generation would deliver energy security without any of the damage.

In conclusion, the disadvantages outweigh the advantages. The energy obtained is real but temporary, whereas the harm to fragile environments is effectively permanent, and the money involved would buy more security if it were directed at replacing the demand instead.

Key words and phrases

would otherwise be unaffordable
Would cost too much without that support.
extends far beyond the drilling site
Affects places far beyond the extraction area.
without any of the damage
Without causing those harmful effects.
directed at replacing the demand instead
Focused on removing the need for it.

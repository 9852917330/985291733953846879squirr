An enjoyable day out in the countryside that you had

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an enjoyable day out in the countryside that you had.

You should say: - where you went on this day out - who you were with - what you did - and explain why you enjoyed the day

Simple Model Answer

An enjoyable countryside trip I took was to the Tissington Trail in Derbyshire. It follows an old railway route that is now used by walkers, cyclists and horse riders. I went with my wife, my brother, his wife and their daughter during a weekend visit.

Our relatives had come from London and wanted a break from city life. We parked in the village of Tissington, hired bicycles and followed the trail. It was mostly flat, with gentle slopes, so the ride was comfortable. We passed trees, open fields, wildflowers and a deep rocky valley.

My favourite part was riding through an old railway tunnel. Recorded steam-train sounds played as we went through, making it easy to imagine the route's past. Everyone enjoyed that unusual detail. Afterwards, we relaxed in a village tearoom. The day was memorable because it combined gentle exercise, beautiful views and family time without feeling rushed. I liked seeing how an old railway could become a place that people of different ages can continue to enjoy.

Key words and phrases

gentle slopes
Areas of ground that rise or fall gradually rather than steeply.
tearoom
A small place serving tea, cakes and light food.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe a recent trip to a lovely place called the Tissington Trail. The Tissington Trail is a path or track that follows the route of a former railway line through beautiful countryside in the county of Derbyshire, here in the UK. The old railway line was taken up many years ago, and it was transformed into a recreational trail that's ideal for walkers, cyclists and horse riders.

I went to the Tissington Trail because we had some family members, my brother and sister-in-law and their daughter, staying with us over the weekend. They'd come up to Manchester from London, and they wanted my wife and me to take them somewhere they could breathe in some fresh countryside air and get away from the hustle and bustle of the city. To reach the trail we had to park in Tissington itself, which is a charming, picturesque village.

We hired some bicycles and set off along the trail. It was an easy ride because the trail is mostly flat, with just a few gentle dips and inclines, and we were able to enjoy the stunning scenery — woodland, a dramatic limestone ravine, and grassy meadows covered with colourful wild flowers. The part of the day I enjoyed most was cycling through a historic railway tunnel.

The tunnel has been fitted with a sound installation that plays eerie steam train noises as you pass through; we all loved the idea that the sounds transport you back in time, like you're really on an old steam train with its whistles and clunking machinery. It was a really nice day out, and we all enjoyed relaxing in the village tearoom afterwards.

Key words and phrases

a former railway line
Railway tracks formerly used by trains.
fresh countryside air
Clean air outside the city.
the hustle and bustle
Busy, noisy activity.
picturesque village
A particularly pretty small settlement.
stunning scenery
Extremely beautiful views of nature.
transport you back in time
Make the past feel present again.

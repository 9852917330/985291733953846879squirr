Object & Favourite

A film that you would like to see in the future

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a film that you would like to see in the future.

You should say: - what the film is called - what it is about - how you heard about this film - and why you would like to see it.

OUTLINE

>>> Spectre
film like see comes out later year
It's new James Bond film saw news it's going called Spectre’
sure similar previous Bond films w/ plot line involving lots twists
James Bond films always big news UK probably bc Bond himself
like see Spectre’ cinema bc sure full action scenes special effects
they're always fast entertaining fun bit like roller-coaster ride

Simple Model Answer

A film I would like to see is Spectre, one of the James Bond films. I first heard about it in the news and later saw a trailer on television. The name refers to a criminal organisation in the story.

I have not watched the film, so I do not know every detail of the plot. However, I expect Bond to face a dangerous enemy and complete a difficult mission. I enjoy the surprises, action scenes and unusual equipment that appear in these films. The international locations are another attraction because they allow me to see places I might never visit myself.

I would prefer to watch it at the cinema. A large screen and strong sound would make the action and special effects more exciting. I know James Bond stories are not very realistic, but that does not stop me enjoying them. Sometimes I want a film that is fast and entertaining rather than serious. I hope Spectre gives me that kind of enjoyable escape for a couple of hours.

Key words and phrases

trailer
A short video showing parts of a film to advertise it.
special effects
Images or sounds created to show things that would be difficult to film normally.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about a film I'd love to see once it comes out later this year — the new James Bond film, Spectre. I saw in the news that Spectre is the name of the imaginary criminal organisation that Bond has to defeat in this one.

Since it hasn't been released yet, I don't know exactly what it'll be about, but I can guess that Bond's going to be on a mission to save the world from some arch-criminal, as usual. I'm sure it'll be similar to most of the previous films, with a plot full of twists and turns, and Bond using his skills to outsmart his nemesis right at the end.

I first heard about it while I was watching the news a couple of months back, and since then I've seen the official trailer on TV. There have also been several newspaper articles about which actors have been cast to play supporting roles in it. Bond films are always big news here in the UK, probably because Bond's an English character.

I'd like to catch Spectre at the cinema because I'm sure it'll be full of action scenes and special effects, and I love how Bond's missions always take place in spectacular locations all over the world. Bond films are completely unrealistic, ridiculous even, but they're always fast, entertaining and fun — a bit like a roller-coaster ride.

Key words and phrases

as usual
As normally happens.
twists and turns
Surprising changes in the story.
play supporting roles in
Act as less central characters.
big news
An important announcement or event.
spectacular locations
Beautiful and impressive places.
a roller-coaster ride
An exciting experience with many emotional changes.

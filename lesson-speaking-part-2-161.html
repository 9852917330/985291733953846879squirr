Simple Model Answer

A service I appreciated was Traveloka's online booking service. I used it last summer when my mother and I planned a trip to Taiwan. We wanted to organise flights and a hotel ourselves instead of joining a package tour.

I made the bookings on my laptop over a couple of evenings. The site let us compare prices, flight times and hotel locations clearly. It also explained payment choices and sent confirmation emails quickly. When we needed to change one detail, customer service replied promptly with instructions I could understand.

What impressed me most was the lack of confusion. Each stage showed what would happen next and what we would pay. My mother is not confident with technology, but she could still follow what I was doing. That made me feel the design was effective. The service saved time and reduced the pressure of planning an overseas holiday. I would use it again because good service, for me, means not being left uncertain or having to search repeatedly for basic information.

Key words and phrases

package tour
A holiday sold with transport, accommodation and sometimes activities arranged together.
confirmation emails
Messages checking that a booking or payment has been completed.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe an excellent service that I received from Traveloka, an online travel company. I used it last summer when my mother and I decided to visit Taiwan and wanted to organise the trip ourselves instead of buying a package tour.

The service was mainly a booking service for flights and hotel accommodation. I did the whole thing from my laptop at home over a couple of evenings. The website let us compare prices, check flight times and choose a hotel in a convenient location. What impressed me most was that the whole process was clear, fast and easy to follow. Even though I hadn't booked an overseas trip online before, I didn't feel confused at any stage.

Another good point was that the company offered a variety of payment methods and sent confirmation emails immediately. When we needed to change one detail in the booking, the customer-service team replied quickly and explained the steps in simple language.

Overall, I was completely satisfied with the service because it saved us time, reduced our stress and helped us arrange the trip at a reasonable price. I'd definitely use the same service again. Thinking about why it worked so well, I'd say the key was that the company anticipated the questions we were going to ask. Every screen told us what would happen next and what it would cost, so we never had to hunt for information or worry that a fee would appear at the end.

My mother isn't comfortable with technology at all, and she was able to follow what I was doing on the screen, which I thought was a good test of the design. Poor service usually means being left uncertain, and at no point during that booking were we uncertain about anything.

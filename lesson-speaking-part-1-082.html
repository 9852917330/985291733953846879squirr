Travel & Holidays

How you travel and what you do while travelling

Prompt

What form of transport do you prefer to use? How much time do you spend travelling on a normal day? What do you do while you are travelling? Do you ever have problems with transport?

What form of transport do you prefer to use?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer travelling by car because I have my own space and feel more comfortable. The buses and trains in my city are often very crowded, which makes them less pleasant for me.

Key words and phrases

my own space
An area where I can feel comfortable without being crowded by others.

Advanced Model Answer

I prefer to travel by car because it means that I have my own space. Also, the buses and trains in my city are usually packed out; my car's much more comfortable.

Key words and phrases

have my own space
Have privacy and room for myself.
packed out
Completely full of people.

How much time do you spend travelling on a normal day?

Simple Model Answer

I spend about an hour and a half going to work and coming home each day. I travel at busy times, so traffic adds quite a lot to the journey.

Key words and phrases

busy times
Periods when many people are doing the same activity or travelling.

Advanced Model Answer

I probably spend about an hour and a half travelling to and from work. I travel during the rush hour, so there's quite a lot of traffic.

Key words and phrases

the rush hour
The busy time when many people travel to or from work.

What do you do while you are travelling?

Simple Model Answer

I usually listen to the radio or a CD while travelling. In the morning, I like hearing the news so I know what is happening in the world.

Key words and phrases

the news
Reports about recent events.

Advanced Model Answer

I usually listen to the radio or a CD. In the morning I like to listen to the news to find out what is happening in the world.

Key words and phrases

find out what is happening
Learn about current events.

Do you ever have problems with transport?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I often get delayed by traffic on my way to and from work. It used to annoy me a lot, but it happens so often that I'm used to it now.

Key words and phrases

used to it
Familiar with something so it no longer feels unusual or surprising.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, as I said, I get stuck in traffic on my way to and from work. I used to get annoyed by traffic jams, but now I'm used to them.

Key words and phrases

get stuck in traffic
Be unable to move because roads are crowded.
get annoyed by
Become irritated because of.

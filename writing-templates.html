Writing Templates

Eight reusable IELTS Writing Task 2 structures. Choose a type to open the full framework.

8 Task 2 types
01Agree / DisagreeUse for: “To what extent do you agree or disagree?” / “Do you agree or disagree?”→02Advantages / DisadvantagesUse for: “What are the advantages and disadvantages?” / “Discuss the advantages and disadvantages.”→03Discuss Both Views + OpinionUse for: “Discuss both views and give your own opinion.”→04Cause / SolutionUse for: “What are the causes of this problem and what solutions can be suggested?” / “Why is this happening and what can be done?”→05Cause / EffectsUse for: “What are the causes and effects?” / “Why is this happening and what effects does it have?”→06Direct OpinionUse for direct opinion questions such as “Do you think...?”, “Should...?”, “Is... the best way?” or “Which is more important?”→07Outweigh / Positive–Negative DevelopmentUse for: “Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?” or “Is this a positive or negative development?”→08Two-Part QuestionsUse when the task asks two separate questions, for example: “Why is this happening? Is it a positive development?” or “What problems does this cause? Who should be responsible?”→
Use the structure, not a memorised essay. Replace every highlighted placeholder with language that directly answers the prompt. Keep your position consistent from the introduction to the conclusion.

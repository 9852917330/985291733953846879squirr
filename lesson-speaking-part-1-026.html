Films

Film stars' pay, violence and censorship

Part 1

Prompt

Are film stars paid too much money, in your opinion? Do you think there is too much violence in modern movies? What do you think about film censorship, and is it necessary? What was the last film you saw? Did you enjoy it?

Are film stars paid too much money, in your opinion?

Simple Model Answer

Some famous actors earn far more than seems reasonable to me. However, their names help attract money and audiences to films. Their pay reflects their business value more than their wider contribution to society.

Key words and phrases

attract
Make people interested in coming or taking part.

Advanced Model Answer

A few global stars are paid extraordinary sums, but their names can determine whether a film attracts investment and audiences. I think the salaries seem excessive, although that's more about commercial value rather than social value.

Key words and phrases

commercial value rather than social value
Profit matters more than community benefit.

Do you think there is too much violence in modern movies?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, some films use violence too often to make things exciting. Violence can be part of a story, but too many detailed, violent scenes cannot make up for a weak story.

Key words and phrases

make up for
Balance or improve a bad situation by adding something else.

Advanced Model Answer

Some films rely too heavily on violence because it creates immediate excitement. I don't think violence is a problem in itself, but repeated graphic scenes can become a substitute for good writing.

Key words and phrases

a substitute for good writing
Something used instead of a good script.

What do you think about film censorship, and is it necessary?

Simple Model Answer

I think films need age ratings so parents can protect children and viewers know what to expect. However, adults should usually choose for themselves rather than have whole films banned.

Key words and phrases

age ratings
Labels showing which age groups a film is suitable for.

Advanced Model Answer

I think some classification is necessary to protect children and warn viewers, but blanket censorship is risky. Adults should generally be free to choose what they watch, provided there's clear age guidance.

Key words and phrases

clear age guidance
Clear advice about suitable viewer ages.

What was the last film you saw? Did you enjoy it?

Simple Model Answer

The last film I saw was a drama about a family going through changes. I enjoyed it because the people seemed real and I could not easily guess how it would end.

Key words and phrases

going through
Experiencing a situation or period of change.

Advanced Model Answer

The last film I watched was a thoughtful drama about a family dealing with change. I enjoyed it because the characters felt believable and the story avoided an overly predictable ending.

Key words and phrases

an overly predictable ending
An ending too easy to guess.

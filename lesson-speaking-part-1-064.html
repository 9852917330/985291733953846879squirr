Robots

Robots at home and in childhood cartoons

Prompt

What do you think of robots? Did you watch any cartoons related to robots in your childhood? Do you like the idea of robots helping you at home or driving your car?

What do you think of robots?

Simple Model Answer

I think robots are a very clever invention. I'd enjoy having one that could talk to me and help with tasks, because it would make daily life easier.

Key words and phrases

invention
Something new that someone has designed or made.

Advanced Model Answer

I think robots are a clever idea. I'd love to have a robot that could speak to me and do things for me!

Key words and phrases

a clever idea
An intelligent or inventive thought.
do things for me
Carry out tasks on my behalf.

Did you watch any cartoons related to robots in your childhood?

Simple Model Answer

I don't remember any cartoons about robots, but I liked the robots in films such as Star Wars. Those are the ones I remember from childhood.

Key words and phrases

childhood
The period of life when you were a child.

Advanced Model Answer

I can't remember watching cartoons with robots, but I remember that I liked the robots in films like Star Wars.

Key words and phrases

watching cartoons with robots
Viewing animated programmes featuring robots.

Do you like the idea of robots helping you at home or driving your car?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I'd like a robot to help around the house. I also like the idea of cars that drive themselves, and I think they could make driving safer if the technology works well.

Key words and phrases

drive themselves
Move and control the journey without a person driving.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, definitely. It would be great to have a robot helper at home, and I like the idea of driverless cars - I think driving would be safer if cars were controlled by robots.

Key words and phrases

a robot helper
A machine that assists with tasks.
driverless cars
Cars that operate without a human driver.

Arts & Museums

Live performance or the screen at home?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

In today's world, it is no longer necessary to attend live performances such as shows or concerts, because watching them at home on television or a computer is a better option. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

High-quality recordings of concerts and plays are now available for a fraction of the price of a ticket. I disagree that this makes attendance unnecessary, although watching at home is genuinely better in several respects.

The advantages of the screen are considerable. Cost is the largest: a family can watch an opera for the price of a coffee, while seats would be beyond them entirely. Recordings also remove distance, so people in small towns and those with disabilities or young children gain access to performances they could never reach. The view is frequently better than from a real seat, since cameras show the performer's hands and face, and a recording can be paused, repeated and kept. For learning an instrument or studying a play, that ability to watch the same moment again is invaluable.

What the screen removes, however, is the reason live performance exists. An audience changes a performance: musicians respond to it, actors adjust their timing to laughter, and no two nights are identical, which is why recordings of the same work differ so much. There is also a physical dimension that no equipment reproduces, since sound in a hall arrives through the body as well as the ears. Watching among strangers who are all absorbed in the same thing at the same moment produces a shared experience that a living room cannot.

Attendance matters for the art form too. Ticket income supports musicians, technicians and venues, and an audience that only ever watches at home removes the money that pays for the performances it is watching.

In conclusion, home viewing has made performance far more accessible and is often the more practical choice. It reproduces the content of an event rather than the event itself, so live attendance remains worth the cost and the journey.

Key words and phrases

ability to watch the same moment again
Being able to replay a recorded event.
remains worth the cost and the journey
Still offers enough value to justify attending.

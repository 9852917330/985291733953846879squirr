Shopping

Who shops in your household and what you enjoy buying

Prompt

Who does most of the shopping in your household? What kind of shopping do you like doing? Is shopping a popular activity in your country? What types of shops do teenagers like best in your country?

Who does most of the shopping in your household?

Simple Model Answer

We share the shopping rather than leaving it to one person. We often go together as a family, so everyone takes part and no one does most of it alone.

Key words and phrases

takes part
Joins in an activity.

Advanced Model Answer

There isn't one person who does most of the shopping. I'd say it's a shared responsibility because we tend to go shopping together as a family.

Key words and phrases

a shared responsibility
A duty divided among several people.

What kind of shopping do you like doing?

Simple Model Answer

I enjoy buying presents for birthdays or Christmas. My favourite shops are bookshops because I like looking at books, so that is the shopping I enjoy most.

Key words and phrases

presents
Things given to someone as gifts.

Advanced Model Answer

I quite like shopping for presents for people's birthdays or for Christmas. My favourite type of shop would have to be a bookshop.

Key words and phrases

shopping for presents
Looking for gifts to buy.

Is shopping a popular activity in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, shopping is very popular. Saturdays are especially busy, and many people go shopping for enjoyment rather than just to buy things they need.

Key words and phrases

for enjoyment
To have fun or feel pleased.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, it's very popular. Saturday is the busiest shopping day, and lots of people treat shopping as a kind of leisure activity, rather than something practical.

Key words and phrases

leisure activity
Something enjoyable done in free time.

What types of shops do teenagers like best in your country?

Simple Model Answer

I'm not completely sure, but I think teenagers enjoy shops selling clothes, music and electronic gadgets. Those seem like things many young people are interested in buying.

Key words and phrases

gadgets
Small useful electronic devices.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm not an expert on teenage behaviour, but I guess they like buying clothes, music, gadgets, that kind of thing.

Key words and phrases

not an expert on
Do not have specialist knowledge of.

Situation

A time when you had a problem with a piece of equipment

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when you had a problem with a piece of equipment

You should say: - What equipment it was - What problem you had - When you had this problem And explain what you did about this problem.

Simple Model Answer

A problem I thought I had with equipment involved my first smartphone about seven or eight years ago. Most classmates still had basic phones, so my new one attracted a lot of attention on its first day at school.

Several people asked to borrow it and added apps. Later, someone told me another student had secretly installed something that would damage the battery. Soon afterwards, the phone switched off. I panicked because it was brand new, and I did not know how I could explain a repair bill to my parents.

My desk mate noticed how upset I was and asked what had happened. When I explained, she started laughing and told me to press the power button. The phone turned on immediately. My classmates had been playing a joke, and there had been no serious technical problem at all. I felt relieved and rather embarrassed. Looking back, the situation is funny because I imagined the worst before checking the simplest explanation. I learned to stay calmer and investigate before assuming equipment is broken.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm usually pretty quick when it comes to technology, but I want to tell you about the one time I completely panicked over what turned out to be a fake problem with my phone.

This story dates back to when I got my very first smartphone, about seven or eight years ago. Back then, most students still had a basic phone, or what we used to call a dumbphone, that could barely do more than call and text, so turning up to school with a brand-new smartphone was a huge deal, and I instantly became the target of quite a bit of jealousy.

At first it was actually pretty nice being the centre of attention, but after a while I noticed my classmates kept borrowing it to download apps onto it behind my back. Later, one of them let slip that a boy in our class had secretly installed something that would drain the battery fast, and sure enough, right after that, the phone just switched itself off. I was horrified, it was literally the first day I'd owned it, and I had no idea how to explain it to my parents, especially since I was too broke to get it fixed at a shop.

I sat there in silence, on the verge of tears, convinced my life as I knew it was over. My desk-mate noticed and asked what was wrong, and when I told her, instead of comforting me, she just burst out laughing until her face went red. Confused, I asked what was so funny, and she just told me to turn the phone back on. It worked instantly, my classmates had fooled me the whole time.

So that's how I "fixed" the huge technical problem with my phone, by pressing one button.

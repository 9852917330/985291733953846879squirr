Cities & Urban Life

Turning parks into vegetable gardens

Advantages / Disadvantages

Prompt

In some cities, public parks and open spaces are being turned into gardens where local residents can grow their own fruit and vegetables. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

Model answer

Allotments and community plots have reappeared in cities where land is scarce and food is expensive. I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages, provided the gardens take only part of the open space available.

The clearest benefit is what growing food does for the people doing it. Gardening is steady physical activity that continues into old age, and it produces fresh vegetables at a cost that matters to households on low incomes. The mental effect is well documented, since working with plants reduces stress and gives structure to the days of the retired and the unemployed. Community plots also bring neighbours into contact who would otherwise never speak, and shared work builds the kind of familiarity that makes a street feel safe.

There are wider gains. Children who have grown a carrot understand where food comes from and eat vegetables more willingly. Food grown a few streets away needs no transport or packaging, and gardens absorb rainwater and support insects that a mown lawn does not.

The objections deserve attention. A garden is not a park: it excludes the football, running and casual sitting that open space allows, and plots are usually allocated to a limited number of households, so land that belonged to everybody now belongs to a few. Gardens also require commitment, and an abandoned plot quickly becomes waste ground that nobody wants to look at. Cities that are short of green space cannot afford to convert much of it. The balance therefore depends on scale. Converting a corner of a large park serves both purposes, while converting the whole of a district's only open space does not.

In conclusion, community gardens improve health, food access and neighbourly contact at very low cost. They deserve support wherever they take a share of the open space rather than replacing it.

Key words and phrases

waste ground that nobody wants to look at
Unused, neglected and unattractive land.

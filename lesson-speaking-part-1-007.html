Hometown

The town you come from

Part 1

Prompt

Which town or city do you come from? What's the best thing about living there? Is there anything you don't like? Which places would you recommend to a tourist?

Which town or city do you come from?

Simple Model Answer

I'm from Hanoi, a large city in the north of Vietnam. It has old neighbourhoods as well as new business areas, so it feels traditional but is also changing quickly.

Key words and phrases

as well as
In addition to something else.

Advanced Model Answer

I come from Hanoi, a large city in northern Vietnam. It combines historic neighbourhoods with rapidly developing business districts, so it feels both traditional and constantly in motion.

Key words and phrases

constantly in motion
Always moving or changing.

What's the best thing about living there?

Simple Model Answer

The best thing is the choice of things to do. There are parks, cafes, interesting cultural places and great food close together. Shops and other daily services are also easy to reach.

Key words and phrases

easy to reach
Not difficult to get to.

Advanced Model Answer

I think the best thing is the variety — there are parks, cafes, cultural sites and excellent food, all within a relatively small area. I also like that daily services are within easy reach.

Key words and phrases

relatively small
Small compared with other examples.
within easy reach
Easy to get to.

Is there anything you don't like?

Simple Model Answer

I dislike the heavy traffic and dirty air. Travelling can be tiring during busy hours, even for a short distance. I think the city needs more dependable public transport.

Key words and phrases

heavy traffic
A large number of vehicles on the roads.

Advanced Model Answer

Traffic congestion and air pollution are the main drawbacks. At peak times, even a short journey can be exhausting, so the city needs a more reliable public transport network.

Key words and phrases

Traffic congestion
Too many vehicles causing slow travel.
air pollution
Harmful substances in the air.
public transport network
Connected bus, rail and other shared services.

Which places would you recommend to a tourist?

Simple Model Answer

I'd suggest the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake and the Temple of Literature. These places help visitors learn about Hanoi's history and see what daily life in the city is like.

Key words and phrases

daily life
The usual activities and experiences of each day.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd recommend the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake and the Temple of Literature. Together they give visitors a balanced picture of the city's history and everyday life.

Key words and phrases

a balanced picture
A fair view of different sides.

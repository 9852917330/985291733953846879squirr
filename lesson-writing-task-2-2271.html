Work & Employment

Banning age discrimination in hiring

Positive or Negative

Prompt

In some countries, it is illegal for companies to reject job applicants because of their age. Is this a positive or negative development?

Model answer

Several countries now prohibit employers from turning down candidates on grounds of age. I regard this as a positive development, both for the individuals protected and for the economies that adopt it.

The strongest argument is fairness. Age says almost nothing about whether a particular person can do a particular job, and rejecting a candidate at fifty-five because of an assumption about energy or adaptability is a judgement about a category rather than about the applicant. The consequences are severe, because older workers who lose a job take far longer to find another and many leave the workforce permanently, not by choice but by exhaustion.

There is also a practical case that will matter more as populations age. Countries with falling birth rates cannot afford to lose experienced workers a decade before they wish to retire, since the pensions and health care of an ageing society are paid for by those still in work. Older employees additionally bring judgement built over decades and lower turnover, and mixed-age teams consistently outperform uniform ones because they approach problems from different starting points.

The objection is that such laws are difficult to enforce, since an employer can always give another reason for a rejection, and that they may make firms reluctant to hire anyone they might struggle to dismiss. This has some force, but the same was said of laws on sex and disability discrimination, which changed behaviour substantially even though individual cases remained hard to prove.

In conclusion, this is a positive development. It protects people at the point in life when unemployment does the most lasting damage, and it keeps skills in an economy that will need them for longer.

Key words and phrases

not by choice but by exhaustion
Forced by tiredness rather than personal preference.
approach problems from different starting points
Use different experiences and assumptions to solve issues.
individual cases remained hard to prove
Specific examples were still difficult to demonstrate.
will need them for longer
Will continue requiring their skills for more years.

Telephoning

How often you call and who you talk to

Prompt

How often do you make telephone calls? Who do you spend most time talking to on the telephone? When do you think you'll next make a telephone call? Do you sometimes prefer to send a text message instead of telephoning?

How often do you make telephone calls?

Simple Model Answer

I make about three or four calls a day. Most are to arrange things with people at work or to speak to family and friends, so they are usually part of everyday life.

Key words and phrases

arrange things
Make plans or organise what needs to happen.

Advanced Model Answer

I probably make three or four phone calls every day, usually just to make arrangements with work colleagues or to speak to family and friends.

Key words and phrases

make arrangements with
Organise plans together with.

Who do you spend most time talking to on the telephone?

Simple Model Answer

Probably one of my closest colleagues. We often phone each other instead of meeting in person, so I spend more time on the phone with that person than with most others.

Key words and phrases

colleagues
People you work with.

Advanced Model Answer

I think it would be one of my closest work colleagues. We often call each other rather than meeting face-to-face.

Key words and phrases

face-to-face
Meeting in person.

When do you think you'll next make a telephone call?

Simple Model Answer

I'll probably call my family as soon as this exam finishes. They are expecting to hear from me because they want to know how it went.

Key words and phrases

expecting
Thinking or waiting for something to happen.

Advanced Model Answer

My family are expecting me to phone them as soon as I finish this exam. They'll want to know how it went.

Key words and phrases

how it went
What happened and how successful it was.

Do you sometimes prefer to send a text message instead of telephoning?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, for simple plans I prefer texting because each person can reply when they are free. I still phone if something is urgent or we need to discuss it properly.

Key words and phrases

urgent
Needing attention or action very soon.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes. For a simple arrangement, a text message is more efficient than a phone call because both people can deal with it in their own time. I still call when it's urgent or needs a proper discussion.

Key words and phrases

more efficient than a phone call
Achieving the aim with less time than calling.
deal with it in their own time
Handle it when convenient for them.

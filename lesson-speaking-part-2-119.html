Describe a new skill you would like to learn

Simple Model Answer

A skill I would like to learn is speaking German. I became interested after travelling in Europe a couple of years ago. I especially liked Berlin, but one shopping mistake made me realise the value of understanding the language.

I wanted to buy my father a black Nike coat. After searching several shops, I found one at Kaufland. Unfortunately, when I got home, I discovered that I had bought a women's size. I thought better German might have helped me understand the information before paying.

The language would also be useful beyond shopping. It is spoken in several European countries, and learning it could make travel easier. If I became good enough, I might consider studying in Germany. I would probably start with an app to learn basic words and then join a class or find a tutor. Some vocabulary seems similar to English, although I know the grammar would take effort. I want to learn because it could turn an enjoyable travel experience into a longer-term personal and educational opportunity.

Key words and phrases

tutor
A teacher who gives lessons to one person or a small group.
longer-term
Continuing or being useful over a longer period of time.

Advanced Model Answer

Today I'd like to talk about a skill I've always desired to learn, which is speaking German.

A couple of years ago I went on a fantastic trip to Europe, and I was deeply impressed by the beauty and style of Berlin. While I was there, I spent ages wandering around different shopping malls trying to find my dad his favourite black Nike coat, and I finally tracked one down in a place called Kaufland. The only problem was, when I got home, I realised I'd bought the women's size by mistake! That's actually the moment I decided to try my hand at learning German - I figured if I understood the language a bit better, I wouldn't make silly mistakes like that again.

German appeals to me for a few reasons. For one thing, it's an official language in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, so it'd genuinely be useful across quite a few countries. It bears a close resemblance to English too - they're both Germanic languages, so a lot of the vocabulary feels oddly familiar, which should make it easier to pick up. And if I get good enough, it could even open the door to studying in Germany one day.

I also just think learning another language is genuinely useful, whether that's for travelling or just feeling more confident generally. As for how I'd actually go about it, I'd probably start with an app to build up the basics, then look for a proper tutor or an evening class once I've got the fundamentals down. So sometime soon, once work and study calm down a bit, I'd love to finally give it a go.

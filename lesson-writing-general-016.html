Formal

Requesting help from a language tutor

Formal

Prompt

Your children are learning a foreign language at school but require extra support. A friend has recommended a private tutor. Write to the tutor. In your letter: - explain why you are contacting them - describe the areas your children need help with - ask about their experience and teaching suggestions

Model answer

Dear Ms Patel,

I am writing on the recommendation of my colleague, Sarah Nguyen, to ask whether you could provide additional French tuition for my two children, aged eleven and thirteen.

Both children understand basic classroom vocabulary, but they are falling behind in speaking and listening. My younger son is hesitant to answer questions aloud, while my daughter finds it difficult to follow normal-speed recordings and often confuses verb endings. Their school teacher believes that regular one-to-one practice would give them the targeted support they need.

Could you please outline your experience of teaching learners in this age group and explain how you normally assess a new student? I would also appreciate your view on whether they should study together or have separate lessons. Ideally, we are looking for one or two sessions each week after 5 p.m.

Please let me know your fees, availability and any materials the children would need. I would be happy to arrange an introductory meeting at your convenience.

Yours sincerely, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

they are falling behind in speaking and listening
Their language skills are progressing too slowly.
hesitant to answer questions aloud
Nervous about speaking answers publicly.
outline your experience
Briefly describe your past work.
at your convenience
Whenever it suits you.

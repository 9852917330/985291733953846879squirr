Person

One of your best friends

Part 2

Prompt

Describe one of your best friends

You should say: - What this person looks like - When and where you met this person - What you do when you are together And explain why he/she is one of your best friends

OUTLINE

>>> Mai
good friend Mai, down-to-earth, gorgeous and tall, pays attention to her appearance
known since high school, met on the first day when I got lost and she helped, became desk-mates
what we do together: shopping on Saturdays, bubble tea, watching a drama series together weekly
why best friend: hit it off from the start, see eye to eye on things, protective, has shortcomings (late, absent-minded) but a strong sense of responsibility
friendship matters most to me, proud to have a friend as loyal as Mai

Simple Model Answer

One of my best friends is Mai. We met on the first day of high school when I was lost and she helped me find our classroom. The teacher then put us at the same desk. We later attended the same college and have stayed close.

Mai is tall, with straight brown hair and brown eyes. She likes fashionable clothes, but she is friendly and easy to talk to. We try to meet every week, either to shop or have bubble tea. We also enjoy the same television drama and often discuss what might happen next.

We have similar interests, including fashion, sport and games. Although Mai is sometimes late and forgets small things, she takes our friendship seriously. She is protective and helps me when I need support. I know I can rely on her. I value having someone who understands me so well and also makes ordinary afternoons enjoyable. To me, a loyal friend like Mai is more important than having a large number of friends.

Key words and phrases

takes our friendship seriously
Treats our friendship as something important.
rely on her
Trust her to help or support me.

Advanced Model Answer

The person I'd like to talk about is my good friend Mai. She has to be one of the nicest and most down-to-earth people I know. Mai's gorgeous — tall, with straight brown hair and deep brown eyes — and she attracts a fair amount of attention wherever we go, which makes standing next to her a little embarrassing! She pays a lot of attention to her appearance too, and always dresses in whatever's trendiest — I've never once seen her in a plain old T-shirt.

We've basically known each other since forever, because we were in the same class from high school through to college. The first time I met her, she left a deep impression on me — on the first day of school I got completely lost trying to find my classroom, and she turned out to be my saviour. As luck would have it, we were then put together as desk-mates by the teacher.

These days we try to see each other weekly — shopping on a Saturday, or just grabbing bubble tea and talking for hours about nothing. We're also both obsessed with the same drama series, so we'll watch an episode together and message each other our theories afterwards.

Honestly, the main reason we're best friends is that we just hit it off from the very start, and we see eye to eye on nearly everything, from fashion to sport to video games. What I admire most is how protective she is. She's not perfect, mind you — she's never on time, and she can be a bit absent-minded, so people sometimes assume she doesn't take things seriously. But she's genuinely got a strong sense of responsibility, and I know I can always rely on her.

Friendship's the most important thing in the world to me, and I'm proud to say I've got a friend as loyal and as much fun as Mai — she really is one in a million.

Key words and phrases

pays a lot of attention to her appearance
cares greatly about how she looks
turned out to be my saviour
unexpectedly became the person who rescued me
hit it off
became friendly immediately
see eye to eye
agree with each other
strong sense of responsibility
a strong feeling that one should do one's duties well
one in a million
an unusually wonderful person

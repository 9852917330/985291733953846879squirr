An old building that you like

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an old building that you like.

You should say: - where it is - what it is used for - and why you like it.

Simple Model Answer

An old building I like is the Empire State Building in New York. It stands in Manhattan and was completed in 1931. Although many people think of skyscrapers as modern, this one has been part of the city for many decades.

Much of the building is used for offices, but visitors can also go to viewing areas. There is a famous outdoor viewing floor on the eighty-sixth level and another area higher up. From there, people can see the city in many directions.

I like the building because its shape is so easy to recognise. It has appeared in many photographs and films, so it feels closely connected with New York. I also admire the fact that a building constructed so long ago still attracts so much attention. The views are the main reason I would recommend it to a visitor. Even if it is a popular tourist attraction, seeing the streets and towers from above would make the visit worthwhile. It combines an important piece of the city's past with a striking view of its present.

Key words and phrases

viewing areas
Places designed for people to look out over the surroundings.
worthwhile
Good or useful enough to deserve the time, effort or money spent.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe a really famous building in New York: the Empire State Building. People might not think of it as an old building, but it was actually built back in 1931, so I don't think it really counts as new any more. It's right in the middle of Manhattan, and it's probably the most distinctive and recognisable building on the whole New York skyline.

As far as I know, it's mainly an office building, but visitors can also go up to an observation deck on the 102nd floor, right at the top. There's also a 360-degree viewing area on the 86th floor, which is actually the one most tourists go to. Apparently, the building makes more money these days from ticket sales to those observation floors than it does from renting out office space, which I find quite funny given it's meant to be an office building first.

I think the Empire State Building's still one of the most impressive skyscrapers in the world. It's such an iconic structure, and it's kind of amazing to think it's still standing strong nearly a hundred years after it was built. The best part is definitely the viewing deck on top, which gives you spectacular views of the whole city — it's genuinely the best vantage point in New York, in my opinion.

I'd recommend it to anyone visiting the city — just go up there once, even if it's touristy, because the view alone makes it totally worth it.

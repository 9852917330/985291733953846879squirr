Activity

A holiday or vacation you have been on

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a holiday or vacation you have been on

You should say: when you had it where you went what you did and explain how you felt about it

OUTLINE

>>> Nha Trang holiday
few months ago went South Vietnam vacation w/ family
decided go plane nearly 1200 kilometres Hanoi take long time car
trip began w/ incredible view airplane window remember overwhelmed beauty city
think memorable moment trip definitely bought fresh ingredients local market grilled
Sitting fire breezy night w/ delicious smell freshly barbequed fish amazing
definitely enjoyed definitely go again chance

Simple Model Answer

A holiday I enjoyed was a family trip to Nha Trang a few months ago. We travelled from Hanoi by plane because the distance was almost twelve hundred kilometres. I was so excited that I packed my clothes and camera a week early.

The flight left on time, and I enjoyed looking down at the city through the window. We arrived as the sun was setting. During the holiday, we tried activities such as surfing, parasailing and diving near coral. I especially liked the clear blue water and white sandy beaches.

My favourite memory was an evening when we bought fresh seafood at a local market and grilled it on the beach. We sat together near the fire while a cool breeze came from the sea. The food tasted wonderful, but being with my family made the moment special. The trip gave us new experiences and a proper break from our normal routines. We returned home with many photographs and happy memories, and I would love to go back if we had the opportunity.

Key words and phrases

parasailing
Being pulled above the water in a parachute attached to a boat.
a proper break
Enough time away from normal duties to relax well.

Advanced Model Answer

A few months ago, I went to the south of Vietnam on a family holiday. We went to the lovely coastal city of Nha Trang, which is also a really famous tourist destination. We decided to fly there since it's nearly 1,200 kilometres from Hanoi, and going by car or train would've taken forever.

I was so excited about the trip that I'd packed everything - clothes, camera, all the bits and pieces - a week in advance. The flight only took about an hour, and luckily it left right on time. What I remember most is the view from the window as we took off: the whole city looked incredible from above, and we landed just as this amazing sunset was starting.

Our time in Nha Trang was brilliant. We tried out so many things we'd never done before, like parasailing, surfing, and diving to see the coral reef. The beaches there are honestly some of the best in Vietnam - deep blue water, pearly white sand - and the food was incredible too. But if I had to pick the best moment, it'd be the night we bought fresh seafood from a local market and grilled it ourselves right on the beach. Sitting by the fire with that smoky, freshly barbecued fish smell drifting around us on a breezy night - I don't think I'll ever forget it.

That trip really was the holiday of a lifetime for us - so much fun, and so much incredible scenery. We'd honestly go back in a heartbeat if we ever got the chance.

Key words and phrases

tourist destination
A place people visit for pleasure.
a week in advance
Seven days before an event.
never done before
Something I had not previously experienced.
the holiday of a lifetime
An exceptionally memorable trip.
go back in a heartbeat
Return without hesitation.

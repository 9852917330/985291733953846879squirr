Formal

Complaint about noise at a recreation ground

Formal

Prompt

A school regularly brings pupils to a recreation ground near your home, and recent visits have caused problems. Write to the head teacher. In your letter: - explain why you enjoy living near the ground - describe the recent problems - suggest what the school should do

Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I live opposite Maple Recreation Ground and am writing about the sports sessions held there by pupils from your school.

Residents generally value the open space because it provides trees, walking paths and a safe place for local children to play. We also understand that school sport is valuable and have never objected to normal daytime activity.

However, during the past three weeks, several groups have left plastic bottles and food packaging behind. Footballs have repeatedly struck parked cars, and pupils have gathered outside nearby houses shouting for up to thirty minutes after the sessions ended. These incidents disrupt local residents, particularly older people and parents with young children.

Could teachers please supervise pupils more closely, ensure all rubbish is collected and keep pupils within the marked playing area? It would also help if groups left promptly once the lesson had finished. These simple measures would allow the school to continue using the ground without creating unnecessary conflict.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

have never objected to normal daytime activity
Never complained about ordinary daytime noise.
disrupt local residents
Disturb people living nearby.
supervise pupils more closely
Watch students more carefully.
keep pupils within the marked playing area
Keep children inside the designated space.
without creating unnecessary conflict
Without causing avoidable disagreements.

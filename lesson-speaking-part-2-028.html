A beautiful garden that you like

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a beautiful garden that you like.

You should say: - where it is - what you can see there - what people do there - and explain why you like it.

Simple Model Answer

A beautiful garden I like is the one behind my home. My house is in the suburbs, about an hour's drive from San Francisco. There is a large tree in the middle of the garden, along with a hot tub, a small fountain, bushes, flowers and a vegetable area.

My wife and I bought the house a year ago. The garden was one of the main reasons we chose it because the previous owner had kept it in excellent condition. We now look after it ourselves. We are not experienced gardeners, but we enjoy growing roses, tulips and tomatoes.

When friends and relatives visit, we invite them to relax in the hot tub and enjoy the view. Looking after the garden takes time and hard work, but seeing the plants grow gives us a sense of achievement. It is also my favourite place to rest after work. I like having a peaceful outdoor space that we can gradually make our own.

Key words and phrases

a sense of achievement
A pleased feeling because you have completed something successfully.
make our own
Change something so it reflects our personal choices.

Advanced Model Answer

Today I'd like to describe my home garden, which I think is a beautiful one and which I like very much. The garden is part of my home's backyard, and my house is located in the suburbs, about a 1-hour drive from the city of San Francisco. There is a big tree right in the middle of my garden, which is surrounded by a hot tub, a small water fountain, several bushes, a section for vegetables, and different kinds of flowers.

This garden was actually one of the reasons why my wife and I chose to buy our house because it had been beautifully maintained by the previous owner's gardener and landscaper. It's been only 1 year since we bought the house and now we're taking care of the garden ourselves. Even though we're not experts in gardening, we still try our best to plant things that we like, such as roses and tulips, and we even grow our own tomatoes.

Whenever family and friends come to visit us, we invite them to have a relaxing time in our hot tub, and enjoy the view. So in other words, our garden has also become a kind of resort! It definitely requires lots of hard work to properly maintain the garden, but it gives us a great sense of accomplishment to "decorate" it the way we like.

All in all, the garden is one of the best features of my home and it's a place where I can relax after a long day of work or at weekends.

Key words and phrases

beautifully maintained
kept in very good condition
hard work to properly maintain the garden
a lot of effort to keep the garden healthy and tidy
a great sense of accomplishment
a strong feeling of satisfaction after doing something well

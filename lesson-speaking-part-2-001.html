Person

A businessman that you admire

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a businessman that you admire.

You should say: - Who this person is; - How you got to know this person; - What this person does; - How you think of this person.

OUTLINE

>>> Mr Vincent
businessman admire respect, neighbour got to know him ~3 years ago
moved in two doors down, met chatting over garden fence, stayed close since
Mr. Vincent 30 y/o established 1st company at 26, trades medical equipment (Viet Sun)
reputation as hard-working, early to work late home, weekends still on computer
not many have passion/skills to chase goals - built company from nothing at 26, inspiring
love his advice, learned about business and attitudes to life, owe him a lot

Simple Model Answer

A businessman I admire is my neighbour, Mr Vincent. His family moved into our street about three years ago. We first spoke over the garden fence, and we became friends because I was interested in his work.

He is thirty now and started a medical equipment company called Viet Sun when he was twenty-six. He works very hard. He usually leaves home early and returns late, and he often uses his weekends to work on his computer. Although he is nearly ten years older than me, he always seems young and full of energy.

I admire him because he built his company from nothing. He also makes time to help me. Whenever I feel worried about my future, I talk to him, and he gives me useful advice about business and life. To me, he is both a successful businessman and a good friend. His experience encourages me to keep working towards my own goals.

Key words and phrases

full of energy
Feeling active and ready to do things.
built his company from nothing
Started and developed his business without having an established company already.

Advanced Model Answer

Today I'm going to talk about a businessman that I really admire and respect. I live on a beautiful and peaceful street, and most of my neighbours work for companies or run their own businesses, but out of all of them, I'd pick Mr Vincent as the most admirable businessman I've ever met.

I got to know him about three years ago, not long after he and his family moved in two doors down from us. We got chatting over the garden fence one weekend, and once he found out I was interested in business, he was happy to tell me about what he does, and we've stayed close ever since.

Mr Vincent is only thirty, but he established his first company when he was just twenty-six. His company trades medical equipment, and it's called Viet Sun. He's built up a reputation as an incredibly hard-working person — he leaves for work early and gets home late, and even on weekends, instead of hanging out with friends, he's still glued to his computer screen.

He's nearly ten years older than me but looks really young and always seems full of energy. The fact that he built all this up from nothing by the age of twenty-six is honestly what I find most inspiring about him.

Another thing I love about him is his advice — whenever I'm feeling discouraged, I'll go and talk to him, and I've learned a lot from him, not just about business, but about his attitudes to life.

To me, he's not just a businessman I happen to live near — he's someone I can share things with and learn from. I really owe him a lot.

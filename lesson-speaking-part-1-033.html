Games

Popular games and how people learn them

Prompt

What games are popular in your country? Do you play any games? How do people learn to play games in your country? Do you think it's important for people to play games?

What games are popular in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Football is the most popular if we include sports. People also enjoy board games such as chess, so there are both active games and quieter ones to choose from.

Key words and phrases

board games
Games played on a board, often using small pieces.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, if we count sports as games, then football's by far the most popular game in my country, but we also love board games like chess.

Key words and phrases

by far the most popular game
A game much more popular than others.

Do you play any games?

Simple Model Answer

Not very often now. I sometimes play tennis with my brother-in-law. Recently, I also tried a bowling game on the Nintendo Wii with my nephew, but I wasn't very good.

Key words and phrases

Not very often
Only sometimes, rather than regularly.

Advanced Model Answer

Not regularly these days. I play the occasional game of tennis with my brother-in-law, and I recently played a bowling game on the Nintendo Wii with my nephew. I wasn't very good at it.

Key words and phrases

play the occasional game of
Play sometimes, but not regularly.

How do people learn to play games in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Children often learn games from other children. That is how I learned chess: a friend at primary school explained the rules and showed me how to play.

Key words and phrases

the rules
The instructions that tell you how a game works.

Advanced Model Answer

I think children mostly teach each other how to play games, and that's how I learned chess too. A friend at primary school taught me the rules when we were young.

Key words and phrases

taught me the rules
Explained how the game is played.

Do you think it's important for people to play games?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, especially for children. Playing games can help them think, use their imagination and learn to work with other people. So games can be useful as well as enjoyable.

Key words and phrases

imagination
The ability to form new ideas or pictures in your mind.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, it's really important, especially for children. I think research has shown that play can improve brain development, intelligence, creativity, and the ability to cooperate with others.

Key words and phrases

brain development
Growth in how the brain works.
cooperate with others
Work together with other people.

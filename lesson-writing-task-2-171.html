Technology & Science

Has technology given us more leisure time?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

Modern technology has given people more leisure time than in the past. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

It is often claimed that machines and digital devices have freed people from work and given them more time to relax. I largely disagree with this view, because although technology has removed a great deal of physical labour, it has also created new demands that consume the time it saves.

There is no doubt that technology has eliminated many hours of drudgery. Washing machines, dishwashers and vacuum cleaners have reduced housework from a full-time occupation to a few hours a week, which has been particularly important for women. In the workplace, computers perform in seconds calculations that once took clerks days, and online banking and shopping have removed the need for countless journeys. Measured purely in tasks completed per hour, we are far more productive than our grandparents were.

The difficulty is that the time saved has not become free time. Employers have simply raised their expectations, so employees now produce far more within the same working day. Smartphones have also destroyed the boundary between work and home, since emails arrive in the evening and at weekends and most people feel obliged to answer them. Much of what remains is absorbed by the devices themselves, because several hours a day now go to social media and streaming, which is distraction rather than genuine rest.

In conclusion, technology has certainly made individual tasks quicker, but I disagree that it has given us more leisure. The hours it saves are immediately filled by heavier workloads and by the constant demands of the technology itself.

consume the time it saves
Use up the free time it creates.

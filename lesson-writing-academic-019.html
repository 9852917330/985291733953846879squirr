Money & Spending

Youth spending and life milestones

Academic Task 1

Prompt

Charts 1 and 2 below show the proportion of income spent on different areas by young people in Canada and Poland. Chart 3 shows the average age of certain life events for these countries, and an estimated global average age. Write a report summarising the information. Select and describe the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The charts show the percentage of youth income spent on various areas in Poland and Canada, and also the average age at which young people in these countries reach three key life events, compared with an estimated global norm.

Overall, young people's expenditure in Canada is more evenly split than in Poland, where studies and transport account for around two-thirds of expenditure. In terms of life events, Poland is closer to the global average than Canada.

Looking first at income, in Canada the two largest areas of expense are leisure and clothing, at 20% and 19% respectively. The other areas (housing, food, transport and studies) are all roughly similar, at between 14% and 16%. In Poland, however, studies take almost half the expenditure, at 43%, followed by transport at 26%. Leisure and clothing are similar at 10% and 11%, and housing and food are the same at 5%.

Regarding life events, in Poland leaving home and buying a car happen at an age close to the estimated global average of around 26 years, whereas in Canada they happen much earlier, at 20 years or just under. Getting married, by contrast, happens at a similar age in both countries, at around 30 years, which is much later than the world average of just over 20.

Key words and phrases

compared with
Used to show a difference or similarity between two things.
more evenly split
Divided into more equal shares.
respectively
In the same order as the things already mentioned.
take almost half the expenditure
Use nearly half the spending.
by contrast
Used to introduce a clear difference.
much later than the world average
Considerably after the typical global timing.

Museums

Museums you have visited and would like to visit

Part 1

Prompt

Which museums have you visited recently? What did you enjoy seeing there? Which famous museum would you like to visit? Which collections would you particularly like to see there?

Which museums have you visited recently?

Simple Model Answer

I recently visited the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology in Hanoi. It has objects, photos and examples of traditional houses from different ethnic groups, so I learned a lot during the visit.

Key words and phrases

ethnic groups
Groups of people who share a cultural background or traditions.

Advanced Model Answer

I recently visited the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology in Hanoi. It shows the cultures of different ethnic groups through objects, photos and reconstructed houses, so the whole visit's highly informative.

Key words and phrases

highly informative
Providing a lot of useful knowledge.

What did you enjoy seeing there?

Simple Model Answer

I liked the traditional houses and everyday objects most. They helped me see how weather, local materials and traditions affect the way different groups of people live.

Key words and phrases

local materials
Things available in an area that people can use to make objects or buildings.

Advanced Model Answer

I particularly enjoyed the traditional houses and everyday objects. They showed how climate, materials and local customs shape the way communities organise daily life.

Key words and phrases

organise daily life
Plan ordinary tasks and activities.

Which famous museum would you like to visit?

Simple Model Answer

I'd love to visit the British Museum because it has objects from so many periods of history. Seeing the real things would help me feel closer to the past.

Key words and phrases

periods of history
Different times or ages in the past.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd love to visit the British Museum because its collection covers such an enormous span of history. Seeing the original objects would give me a more tangible connection with the past.

Key words and phrases

a more tangible connection
A connection you can experience more directly.

Which collections would you particularly like to see there?

Simple Model Answer

I'd especially like to see objects from ancient societies and the history of writing. They can show how people shared ideas and learned from other societies.

Key words and phrases

ancient
Belonging to a time very far in the past.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd be most interested in the ancient civilisations and the history of writing. Those collections show how ideas travelled and how societies influenced one another.

Key words and phrases

influenced one another
Changed each other's ideas or actions.

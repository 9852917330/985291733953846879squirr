Work & Employment

Fathers at home while mothers work

Essay

Prompt

These days more fathers stay at home and take care of their children while mothers go out to work. What could be the reasons for this? Do you think it is a positive or a negative development?

Model answer

It is true that men are increasingly likely to take on the role of househusband, while more women than ever are the breadwinners in their families. There could be several reasons for this, and I consider it to be a very positive trend.

In recent years, parents have had to adapt to various changes in our societies. Equal rights movements have made great progress, and it is no longer remarkable for a woman to out-earn her partner after years spent gaining the same qualifications and workplace experience that were once reserved for men. It has also become socially acceptable for a man to stay at home and raise his children, rather than being seen as failing to provide for his family. At the same time, the rising cost of living, especially childcare fees in large cities, means that once a nursery place costs almost as much as one partner earns, it often makes more financial sense for that parent, not automatically the mother, to stay at home instead. Couples therefore increasingly decide who works and who cares for the children based on income and preference rather than on tradition.

In my view, the changes described above should be seen as progress. We should be happy to live in a society in which men and women have equal opportunities, and in which women are not put under pressure to sacrifice their careers. Equally, it seems only fair that men should be free to leave their jobs to assume childcare responsibilities if this is what they wish to do. Couples should be left to make their own decisions about which parental role each partner takes, according to their particular circumstances and needs.

In conclusion, the changing roles of men and women in the family are a result of wider changes in society, and I believe that these developments are desirable.

Key words and phrases

adapt to various changes
Respond successfully to different new circumstances.
out-earn her partner
Earn more money than her partner.
failing to provide for his family
Not supplying enough income or support for his family.
the rising cost of living
Everyday necessities becoming more expensive.
sacrifice their careers
Give up career opportunities for another priority.
assume childcare responsibilities
Take on the duty of looking after children.

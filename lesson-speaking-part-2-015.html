Person

A person you know who is beautiful or handsome

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a person you know who is beautiful or handsome.

You should say: Who the person is When you met the person at the first time What the person's character And explain how you feel about the person.

OUTLINE

>>> my older brother
older brother, used to be a role model growing up; can't say exactly 'when I met him' (he's my brother) so describe the moment I started looking up to him instead
tall, strong, confident, not academic but brilliant at sport - opposite of me
ran with older boys, got into fights, never caused real trouble locally; dad (a teacher) disapproved
didn't change until badly hurt in a fight - knocked some sense into him
now has his own family, still look up to him but for his calmness and steadiness now, not the old reckless side

Simple Model Answer

A person I think is handsome is my older brother. He is eight years older than me, tall and strong, and he has always seemed very confident. When I was small, I wanted to be like him.

I remember seeing him deal with an argument between two bigger boys when I was about seven. He managed to end it by talking, without fighting. At school, he was good at sport, while I was better at subjects such as maths. However, he also spent time with some older boys and sometimes got into fights. My father, a secondary school teacher, worried about his behaviour.

After being badly hurt in one fight, my brother became more careful. He now has a wife and a son, and he is much calmer and more responsible. I still admire his appearance, but his personality matters more to me now. He is someone our family can depend on. I am pleased that he has grown into a person who handles pressure sensibly.

Key words and phrases

depend on
Trust someone to help or do what is needed.
handles pressure sensibly
Deals with difficult situations in a calm and reasonable way.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to tell you about my older brother, who used to be a role model for me when I was growing up. He's eight years older than me, and we've gone down completely different paths. I can't really say when I first met him, since he's my brother — but I remember the moment I first started looking up to him. I was about seven, and I watched him walk into an argument between two much bigger boys and talk his way out of it without throwing a single punch. That's the moment I decided I wanted to be like him.

He's tall, strong and ridiculously confident, and he looked a bit like Superman to me when I was small. At school he wasn't particularly academic — maths and physics just weren't his thing — but he was brilliant at every sport he tried, which was the complete opposite of me. I was small and skinny, but I could pick up new ideas and solve problems a bit faster than most of my classmates. Still, it was always my brother that people noticed first.

He used to run around with a group of older boys, and yes, they got into a fair few fights, though they never caused any real trouble in our local community. My dad, who's a secondary school teacher, hated it and was constantly punishing him, hoping he'd turn out a bit more like me, without realising I secretly admired every bit of it.

He didn't really change until he got badly hurt in a fight, and that seemed to knock some sense into him. He's got his own family now, a wife and a son, and if you met him today you'd never guess he used to be that reckless teenager. I still look up to him, for completely different reasons now — his calmness under pressure, and the steady, dependable man he's grown into.

Key words and phrases

used to be a role model for me
was once someone I admired and tried to imitate
when I was growing up
during my childhood
talk his way out of it

local community

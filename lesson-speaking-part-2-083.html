A special cake you had

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a special cake you had

You should say: What kind of cake it is When you ate the cake Who you ate with And explain why you think the cake is special

Simple Model Answer

A special cake I remember was a chocolate sponge cake made by my classmate Trang. This happened soon after I started high school, about eighty kilometres from my hometown. I lived alone in a small flat and found it difficult to settle in.

During the first few months, I became quite ill. I felt dizzy, missed school and stayed in bed for three days, hardly eating. Then Trang phoned and asked if she could visit. When I went downstairs, she was holding a homemade cake that was still warm.

I was deeply touched because I had not expected anyone to make such an effort for me. We sat on the floor of my room and ate the cake together. I started feeling better afterwards, but the kindness mattered more than the food itself. From that day, we became close friends. Although our lives took different directions after school, we still keep in touch. I remember that cake because it made a lonely and difficult period feel less frightening.

Advanced Model Answer

I want to talk about a special occasion when a simple cake genuinely changed how I felt about an awful few months in my life. It happened right after I'd started high school, which was 80km from my hometown, so I had to rent a small flat and live there on my own. Like a lot of people in that situation, I found it hard to adjust, and for the first couple of months I barely made any friends at all.

Then I got sick — properly sick, not just a cold. I couldn't even walk fifty metres without feeling dizzy, let alone go to school. Stubbornly, I convinced myself I'd get better on my own if I just waited it out, so after asking my teacher for a few days off, I spent three days basically just lying in bed, barely eating anything. If anything, I got worse.

Then, one afternoon, I got a call from a classmate called Trang, asking if she could come and check on me. When she turned up, I forced myself downstairs, and I was completely caught off guard — she was holding a homemade chocolate sponge cake, still slightly warm from the oven. She'd heard I was struggling and decided to bake it herself rather than just texting to ask if I was okay.

That small act nearly made me cry. We sat on the floor of my tiny room and ate the whole cake together that evening, and it's still one of the kindest things anyone's ever done for me. My body actually started recovering pretty quickly after that — maybe it was just good timing, or maybe I just needed to eat something again.

From that day, Trang and I became close friends, and even though we've gone our separate ways since finishing school, we still keep in touch with each other and swap stories whenever we can. All these years later, I'm genuinely thankful for a friendship that started with something as small as a cake.

Environment

Can the environment only be protected internationally?

Opinion

Prompt

Environmental problems are serious in many countries. It means that the only possible way to protect the environment is at an international level. To what extent do you agree or disagree on this hypothesis?

Model answer

Globally, environmental degradation has been recognised as one of the main challenges that people have to overcome in the 21st century. The rapid deterioration of the environment is believed to impose a severe threat on people's survival in the years ahead, if not solved effectively. In addressing this issue, countries worldwide should join forces and make a concerted effort, instead of working individually for inconsistent targets.

The clearest reason for international cooperation is that environmental harm rarely stays inside one border. Rivers and ocean currents connect countries whether governments intend this or not, so pollution created in one place quickly becomes another country's problem. Pesticide run-off from farms beside a shared river, for instance, can poison fishing grounds hundreds of kilometres downstream in a neighbouring state, and carbon released by factories in one country contributes to flooding and drought in others that had no part in producing it. Because no government controls an entire river, ocean or atmosphere alone, protecting shared natural resources is only realistic if neighbouring states act together.

A second benefit is the transfer of money and technology that no country can generate alone. Cleaning a polluted river, replacing coal-fired power stations or protecting a forest all require funding and expertise that many governments simply do not have; in the absence of outside funding, a government struggling with debt will rarely choose conservation over factories or farmland, however much it might prefer to. This is why funds financed mainly by wealthier, industrialised nations, which produced most of the world's historical emissions, matter: they give support from the global community to countries that would otherwise face a straight choice between economic survival and protecting their environment.

To summarise, cooperation between countries is inevitable in this global campaign, not only because the ecosystem is a unified mechanism but also because international engagement can make optimal use of financial and human resources. By collaborating with each other, countries can protect the global environment while using the fewest possible resources.

Equality

Women in the army

Opinion

Prompt

In many countries, women join the army as men do. However, some people argue that the army solely needs males. What is your opinion?

Model answer

In the 21st century, women have entered nearly every walk of life. However, the army is still a male-dominated area in much of the world. A great many people hold biases against women's enrolment in the army. In my view, women can contribute to the army just as men do, even though they face obstacles that men do not.

The first argument for recruiting women is that they now have far greater access to education. In comparison with the past, when recruitment concentrated on strength and fitness, armies today select heavily on technical and academic ability. Hand-to-hand fighting accounts for only a small part of modern warfare, which is dominated by tanks, armoured vehicles, missiles and aircraft, most of them computerised. In other words, women can perform military tasks as their male counterparts do, provided that they are well-educated and well-trained.

Secondly, women bring qualities that modern military organisations actually need. Much of service life consists of intelligence analysis, logistics, medical support and communications, roles that reward patience and meticulous attention to detail rather than physical force. Mixed units are also more effective in conflicts fought among civilians: in Afghanistan, armies deployed female engagement teams because male soldiers could not speak to local women without causing serious offence.

These strengths do not remove the obstacles that women in uniform still face. The first is social attitudes. Many women have to cope with pressure from their families and friends, because soldiering is still widely seen as a dangerous occupation that is suitable only for men. The second is discrimination and harassment, which remains common in most armies and discourages capable recruits from staying long enough to reach senior rank.

In conclusion, the presence of women in the army should be accepted and encouraged. Modern soldiering rewards the education and precision that women bring to it, and the prejudice they still meet is an argument for reforming military culture, not for excluding them.

Key words and phrases

a male-dominated area
A field where men hold most positions.
meticulous attention to detail
Extremely careful checking of small details.
an argument for reforming military culture
A reason to change military attitudes and practices.

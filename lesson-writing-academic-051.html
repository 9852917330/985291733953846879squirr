Education

Problems in primary schools

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The table compares the problems faced by children in two primary schools in 2005 and 2015.

Model answer

The table compares two primary schools in terms of the proportions of their pupils who experienced five different educational problems in the years 2005 and 2015.

It is noticeable that school A had higher proportions of children with all five educational difficulties in both years. However, while school A managed to reduce the incidence of all five problems between 2005 and 2015, school B recorded increases in two categories and unchanged figures in the other three.

In 2005, 42% of school A's pupils found it difficult to follow instructions, whereas only 6% of pupils in school B experienced this problem. Similarly, between 30 and 40 per cent of children attending school A had problems in the areas of spelling, listening, verbal expression and concentration in lessons, while the equivalent figures for school B stood at between 5 and 15 per cent.

In 2015, the difference between the two schools was less pronounced. Notably, the proportion of children who struggled to follow instructions fell by 24 percentage points in school A, and this school also saw falls of 22, 15, 14 and 5 percentage points in the figures for children who had problems with concentration, listening, verbal expression and spelling. In school B, however, the proportion of children who struggled with spelling and following instructions doubled, to 10% and 12% respectively, and there was no change in the incidence of listening, verbal or concentration problems.

Key words and phrases

the equivalent figures
The directly comparable numbers.
less pronounced
Less noticeable or less clearly marked.
percentage points
Units used to describe the difference between two percentages.
saw falls of
Experienced decreases of.
struggled with
Had difficulty dealing with.
respectively
In the same order as the things already mentioned.

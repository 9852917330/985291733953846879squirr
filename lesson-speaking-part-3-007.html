Animals

Caring for pets and changing attitudes to animals

Part 3

Prompt

What is involved in taking care of a pet? How do you think people's attitudes towards animals will change in the future? What do you think should be done about poaching endangered animals?

What is involved in taking care of a pet?

Simple Model Answer

A pet needs food, cleaning, exercise and medical care, and those needs continue every day for years. Vet bills can also be expensive. Beyond basic care, animals need attention and company. People should think about the long commitment before getting a pet. Different animals have different needs, though; some cope better alone than others.

Key words and phrases

long commitment
A responsibility that you agree to continue for a long time.

Advanced Model Answer

More than most people expect when they take one on. There's the daily routine — feeding, exercise, cleaning up — which sounds simple enough but has to happen every single day for years on end. Then vet care, which is genuinely expensive and unpredictable. Beyond the practical side, animals need attention and company, since a dog left alone for ten hours becomes distressed. What people underestimate most is the length of the commitment. That's not true of every animal, though — a cat manages considerably better on its own, which is one reason cats have overtaken dogs as the more popular pet in a lot of cities.

Key words and phrases

the length of the commitment
how long the responsibility will continue

How do you think people's attitudes towards animals will change in the future?

Simple Model Answer

I think people will care more about how animals are treated, including animals on large farms. Pets may also receive more special food, insurance and attention as families treat them like members of the household. However, people may still care more about their own pets than farm animals because they do not connect the two situations.

Key words and phrases

how animals are treated
The way people care for, handle or behave towards animals.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd expect concern about welfare to keep growing, particularly around intensive farming, which is already driving legislation on cage sizes and transport in several countries. At the same time, pets are increasingly treated as family members rather than animals, with specialised food and even insurance. Those two trends coexist a little uncomfortably, since people who adore their own dog will still eat industrially farmed pork without much reflection about the difference — it's not that they don't care, it's that the connection just doesn't occur to them at the supermarket.

Key words and phrases

treated as family members
cared for like members of the household
without much reflection
without thinking carefully first

What do you think should be done about poaching endangered animals?

Simple Model Answer

We need trained, well-equipped people to protect endangered animals and enforce the law. But we also need to reduce demand for animal products and help local communities benefit from protecting wildlife. Otherwise, people may see the animals only as a threat to crops or income. These steps should work together, and recovery may still take many years.

Key words and phrases

reduce demand
Make fewer people want to buy something.

Advanced Model Answer

Enforcement matters, and rangers need proper funding and equipment, since they're often outmatched by organised criminal groups. But enforcement alone doesn't work — you have to reduce demand at the other end, through prosecution and campaigns in the countries actually buying the products. And crucially, local communities need a reason to protect wildlife: if an elephant destroys your crops and brings you nothing, protecting it is asking a great deal. It's not a quick fix in any case; even where all three strands work together, populations of the worst-affected species can take decades to recover.

Key words and phrases

reduce demand at the other end
make fewer people want the product or service
asking a great deal
requiring a lot of effort or time

An interesting neighbour you know

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an interesting neighbour you know.

You should say: Who this person is How you know this person What this person likes to do And explain why you think this neighbour is interesting.

Simple Model Answer

An interesting neighbour I know is Duy. Our flats are next to each other. We first met during a power cut one evening. I could not find my torch and had no candles, so I knocked on his door for help.

He was friendly and immediately helped me. A few days later, I made some snacks to thank him, and we became close friends. Duy is in his fifties and lives alone because his family died in a car accident. Despite this terrible experience, he has remained positive about life.

He works and studies at the same time. At weekends, he also visits an orphanage to teach children maths without payment. I admire both his kindness and his determination to continue helping others. Since we both live alone, it is comforting to have a friend so close by. We look after each other, and I know I can ask him for help when I need it. He makes our building feel like a community.

Advanced Model Answer

I must say, I'm extremely fortunate to have such a wonderful neighbour, Duy, who's really friendly and always willing to assist me whenever I'm in trouble. Duy and I live in the same building, and our flats are right next to each other. We got to know each other when the power to the whole building was cut off one evening — I'd run out of candles and my torch was nowhere to be found, so I had to go and knock on the apartment next door for help, and that's when I first met him.

He was so helpful and cheerful about it — he even asked me to hold his torch while he lit candles in his own flat. I was really grateful, so a few days later I made him some snacks as a thank-you, and we've been pretty close friends ever since. The more I've got to know Duy, the more I've come to look up to him. He's well into his fifties and lives alone, since he lost his family in a car accident a while back, but he still somehow manages to see life through rose coloured glasses — his motto is simply to keep moving forward. He works and studies at the same time, and every weekend he travels out to an orphanage to teach the kids there maths, completely unpaid.

He really is a wonderful neighbour, and I know I could call on him any time I needed to. We look out for each other whenever we can, and since we both live on our own, it's really important to have a good friend that close by.

Hometown

Your hometown and whether you would move

Prompt

Where are you from? Do you like your hometown? Would you prefer to live somewhere else? Do you think your hometown is a good place for young people?

Where are you from?

Simple Model Answer

I'm from Manchester, a city in the north-west of England. That is my hometown, and it is the place I think of when someone asks where I'm from.

Key words and phrases

hometown
The town or city you come from.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm from Manchester, which is a city in the north west of England.

Key words and phrases

north west of England
The northwestern part of England.

Do you like your hometown?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I enjoy living there because most of my friends are nearby and there are many things to do. The weather is the only part I don't really like.

Key words and phrases

nearby
Not far away.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I like living in Manchester because it's where most of my friends live, and because there are plenty of things to do there. The only thing I don't like is the weather.

Key words and phrases

plenty of things to do
Many available activities.

Would you prefer to live somewhere else?

Simple Model Answer

I'm happy here for now, but I'd like to live somewhere warmer in the future. A place near a beach would be especially nice because I like that idea.

Key words and phrases

for now
At the present time, even if things may change later.

Advanced Model Answer

For the moment I'm happy living here, but at some point I'd like to live in a country with a warmer climate, and I'd like to live near a beach!

Key words and phrases

at some point
At an unspecified future time.

Do you think your hometown is a good place for young people?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I think it is. Manchester has places to play sport, enjoy music and watch films. Young people have plenty of different activities to choose from.

Key words and phrases

plenty of
A large or more than enough amount of something.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, Manchester has lots of things for young people, such as sports facilities, music and cinemas.

Key words and phrases

sports facilities
Places and equipment for exercise.

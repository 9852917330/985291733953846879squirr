Neighbours

Knowing your neighbours, and problems with them

Prompt

How well do you know the people who live next door to you? How often do you see them? What kinds of problem do people sometimes have with their neighbours? How do you think neighbours can help each other?

How well do you know the people who live next door to you?

Simple Model Answer

I know them quite well. They are friendly, and when I see them, I usually stop for a short conversation. We have a good relationship.

Key words and phrases

a good relationship
A friendly connection in which people get along well.

Advanced Model Answer

I know my neighbours quite well. They're really nice people, and I always stop to talk whenever I see them.

Key words and phrases

know my neighbours quite well
Am quite familiar with people living nearby.

How often do you see them?

Simple Model Answer

I see them a few times a week, often when we leave for work in the morning or return in the evening. Those are the times we tend to meet outside.

Key words and phrases

tend to
Usually or often do something.

Advanced Model Answer

I see my neighbours at least a few times a week. We usually see each other when we're leaving for work in the morning or coming home in the evening.

Key words and phrases

a few times a week
Several times each week.

What kinds of problem do people sometimes have with their neighbours?

Simple Model Answer

Noise is probably the most common problem. A dog barking at night or someone playing loud music can make it hard for the people next door to rest.

Key words and phrases

next door
In the home immediately beside yours.

Advanced Model Answer

I think the most common problem is probably noise. It's difficult to live next to people who have a dog that barks at night, or who play loud music.

Key words and phrases

play loud music
Play music at high volume.

How do you think neighbours can help each other?

Simple Model Answer

They can check your home while you are away or accept a parcel when you are out. Small helpful actions like these make living near each other easier.

Key words and phrases

a parcel
A package sent or delivered to someone.

Advanced Model Answer

Neighbours can do little things like watching your house if you go on holiday or taking a delivery if something arrives by post when you're not at home. I think these little favours are a good way for neighbours to help each other.

Key words and phrases

taking a delivery
Receiving a package, often for someone else.
these little favours
These small helpful acts.

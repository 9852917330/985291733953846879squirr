Activity

An activity you do to keep fit

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an activity you do to keep fit.

You should say: - What you do. - When and where you usually do it. - How you do it. - Why it can keep you fit.

Simple Model Answer

An activity I do to keep fit is jogging. I usually go in the morning with a close friend and spend about thirty minutes to an hour running at a comfortable pace. We prefer parks or paths beside a lake.

I like exercising outdoors because there is fresh air and plenty to see. Other people are walking, doing aerobics or playing games, so the surroundings feel lively. Jogging also needs little organisation. I do not have to book a court or collect a large amount of equipment.

The biggest benefit for me is the effect on my mood. Running gives me time away from work and daily worries. Afterwards, I feel more relaxed and ready to begin the day. I have also noticed improvements in my fitness since making it a regular habit. I try not to miss a session unless the weather is particularly bad. It no longer feels like an extra task I must force myself to do. It has become a normal and enjoyable part of my routine.

Key words and phrases

comfortable pace
A speed that someone can manage without too much difficulty.
regular habit
Something a person does repeatedly as part of normal life.

Advanced Model Answer

Today I'd like to talk about jogging - my favourite activity for staying healthy. It's basically running at a slow, comfortable pace, and it really helps to build up your physical fitness over time.

I much prefer jogging in open spaces, like parks or along the lakeside, because you get fresh air and you can watch other people out doing their own thing - running, aerobics, badminton, whatever. I usually go in the mornings with a close friend of mine, most days, for about thirty minutes to an hour, just to keep fit.

There are a few reasons it works so well for me. For one, it keeps me in decent shape without much effort, and it's done wonders for my immune system too - I hardly get ill compared to a few years ago. It's also refreshingly low-effort compared to other sports; I can just amble along and take in whatever's around me instead of concentrating hard the whole time. But honestly, the biggest thing is that it lets me ease my mind and switch off from my usual routine for a bit.

These days I try not to skip a session unless the weather's really against me, and I can already notice the difference it's made, both in how I look and in how I deal with stress generally. It's become such a normal part of my day now that I honestly can't imagine giving it up.

Key words and phrases

build up your physical fitness
Gradually improve your physical condition.
keep fit
Remain healthy through exercise.
in decent shape
Reasonably healthy and physically capable.
ease my mind
Help me feel less worried.
switch off
Relax and stop thinking about responsibilities.

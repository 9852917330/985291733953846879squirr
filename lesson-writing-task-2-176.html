Technology & Science

How technology has changed our relationships

positive

Prompt

Nowadays the way many people interact with each other has changed because of technology. In what ways has technology affected the types of relationships people make? Is this a positive or negative development?

Model answer

Technology has changed both the number and nature of people's relationships. It allows individuals to maintain contact across distance and to meet others with similar interests, but it can also make communication more superficial. Overall, the effect is positive when digital contact supports rather than replaces real interaction.

Messaging, video calls and social networks make long-distance relationships easier to maintain. Families separated by work or migration can speak regularly, and professional teams can cooperate across countries. Online communities also help people with rare interests or health conditions find support. Technology has therefore removed many barriers created by distance.

The weakness is that convenience can reduce depth. Short messages often replace longer conversations, and people divide their attention between a screen and the person sitting in front of them. Social media also encourages comparison, since users measure their ordinary days against the edited version of life that their friends choose to post. These problems, though, come less from the technology itself than from habits of use.

Technology has also expanded weak connections far more than close ones. Keeping in touch with former classmates or professional networks now costs almost nothing, whereas a close friendship is built by listening to someone at length when they are in difficulty, and no platform can do that on a user's behalf. It is quite possible to have several hundred online contacts and nobody to telephone at midnight.

In conclusion, technology has widened the range of people we can reach while making it easier for that contact to remain shallow. On balance I regard the change as positive, provided that people set deliberate boundaries around online communication and use digital tools to arrange meetings and share real experiences rather than as a substitute for time spent together.

Key words and phrases

removed many barriers created by distance
Made physical separation less of an obstacle.
convenience can reduce depth
Easy contact may become less meaningful.
expanded weak connections far more than close ones
Increased casual contacts more than deep friendships.
nobody to telephone at midnight
No close person available in a difficult moment.
deliberate boundaries around online communication
Intentional limits on digital conversations.

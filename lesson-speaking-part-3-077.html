#077
Shopping

Shopping for clothes and judging people by them

Part 3

Prompt

Do you enjoy shopping for clothes? What kind of clothes do you feel least comfortable in? How much do you judge a person by their appearance?

Do you enjoy shopping for clothes?

Simple Model Answer

Only when I have a clear purpose. I enjoy finding a well-made item that will last, but browsing without a plan is tiring and can lead to unnecessary purchases. I prefer trying most clothes in person because sizes vary. For basic things such as socks or plain T-shirts, though, ordering online is convenient enough.

Key phrase meanings

browsing
Looking around without necessarily deciding to buy something.

Advanced Model Answer

Answer 1

Only when I know what I'm looking for. If I need a particular thing, I quite enjoy the process of finding something well made that'll last. What I don't enjoy is browsing with no purpose — I find it exhausting, and that's exactly when I end up buying things I never wear. I also prefer buying clothes in person rather than online, because sizing's so inconsistent between brands that ordering blind's usually a waste of time. That said, for basics like socks or plain T-shirts, where fit barely matters, I'll happily buy online instead.

Key phrase meanings

browsing with no purpose
looking through content without a clear goal

What kind of clothes do you feel least comfortable in?

Simple Model Answer

I dislike tight, very formal clothes and heavy fabrics, especially in hot weather. I also feel uncomfortable if I am dressed very differently from everyone else. Ideally, I want to move freely and forget about my clothes. For a wedding or interview, however, I accept some discomfort because dressing appropriately matters more on that occasion.

Key phrase meanings

appropriately
In a way that suits the situation or occasion.

Advanced Model Answer

Answer 2

Anything too tight or too formal, really. I dislike stiff, heavy fabrics, and I'm uncomfortable in clothes that are wrong for the weather — being too hot in a suit is genuinely distracting. There's a psychological element too: if I'm dressed very differently from everyone around me, I spend the whole time aware of it. For me, comfortable means being able to move naturally and then forget about what I am wearing. The exception's somewhere like a wedding or an interview, where I'll happily put up with a few uncomfortable hours because looking right matters more that day than feeling right.

Key phrase meanings

a psychological element
a part connected with how people think and feel
forget about what I am wearing
stop thinking about the clothes on my body

How much do you judge a person by their appearance?

Simple Model Answer

Appearance affects first impressions, so I cannot honestly say it has no influence. However, clothing may reflect someone's work, culture or budget rather than character. I try to treat it as limited information and get to know the person. Judging quickly can simply repeat stereotypes, and I have been wrong about people often enough to remember that.

Key phrase meanings

stereotypes
Fixed general ideas about groups that may not fit individuals.

Advanced Model Answer

Answer 3

I'd be dishonest if I said not at all, because first impressions form automatically and appearance's a large part of that. What I try to do is treat it as information rather than evidence of character. Someone's clothing usually tells you about their job, their culture or simply what they could afford that month — it doesn't tell you who they actually are. I've been wrong about people often enough to have learned that judging quickly mostly just reinforces stereotypes.

Key phrase meanings

first impressions
opinions formed when you first meet someone
evidence of character
something used as proof of a person's nature
reinforces stereotypes
makes unfair generalisations seem more believable

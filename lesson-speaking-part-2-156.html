Situation

A difficult decision that you once made

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a difficult decision that you once made

You should say: - What the decision was - How you made your decision - What the results of the decision were - Why it was difficult to make.

Simple Model Answer

A difficult decision I made was choosing a university about two years ago, after finishing high school. I wanted to study English, but several universities in Hanoi offered the subject. Their fees, locations and reputations were different, so deciding was not easy.

At the same time, I was completing applications and preparing for the entrance exam. Some friends were choosing schools mainly to stay together, but I wanted a reason connected with my own future. Fortunately, my older brother helped me think through the options. He had graduated from the University of Pedagogy and understood the process.

We compared teaching quality, distance from home and possible jobs after graduation. He advised me to choose a university with a strong focus on my subject rather than the most impressive-looking campus. After considering this, I applied to the specialised option. I am now happy with the learning environment. The experience taught me to ask for advice without letting others make the whole decision. I am grateful that my brother helped me focus on what mattered most.

Key words and phrases

think through the options
Consider the available choices carefully.
learning environment
The conditions and atmosphere in which someone studies.

Advanced Model Answer

I've made plenty of difficult decisions in my life, but the one I'd like to talk about today is choosing which university to study at.

It was about two years ago, right after I'd graduated from high school. I remember being incredibly busy at the time, filling in application forms for the university entrance exam while also trying to revise for it, so on top of all that stress, I also had to make this huge decision about my future. I was genuinely at a crossroads: my main interest was English language, but that subject was taught at several different universities in Hanoi, each with its own reputation, tuition fees and location, so it wasn't obvious which one to pick. Some of my friends were choosing based on which school their friends were going to, which I didn't think was a good enough reason for such an important decision.

Fortunately, my older brother, who'd graduated from the University of Pedagogy a few years earlier, gave me some really sound advice. We sat down together one evening and he helped me weigh up the pros and cons of each option, things like teaching quality, job prospects after graduating and how far each campus was from home. In the end, he encouraged me to go for the university that specialised in my subject rather than the one with the flashier campus, and after thinking it over carefully, I applied there.

At present, I feel really satisfied with the learning environment at this university, and I'm constantly thankful for that decision, and for my brother's advice. Without his guidance, I honestly don't think I'd be anywhere near as content with my university life as I am now.

Key words and phrases

at a crossroads
at a point where an important choice is needed
tuition fees
money paid for education
sound advice
sensible and reliable guidance
weigh up the pros and cons
consider the good and bad sides before deciding
job prospects
chances of getting a job in the future

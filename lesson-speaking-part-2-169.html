Situation

A time when someone visited your home

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when someone visited your home

You should say: - Who he or she was - When it happened - What you did - How you felt about the visit

Simple Model Answer

A memorable home visit was when I invited an American friend for dinner on Christmas Eve about four months ago. He lived far from his family, and I did not want him to spend the evening alone. My mother decided to cook turkey because he had missed it at Thanksgiving.

Unfortunately, the electricity failed an hour before dinner. Most of our cooking equipment needed power, so the food was not ready when he arrived. I felt embarrassed, but he stayed relaxed. We lit candles, and my father bought bread and ham to eat while we waited.

We spent the delay talking about differences between our countries and his experience of living in Vietnam. The conversation was so enjoyable that the problem mattered less than I expected. We finally ate turkey two hours late. It tasted different from his family's version, but he said he loved it. My parents enjoyed his company, and he has visited more often since then. The evening showed me that a warm welcome matters more than a perfectly organised meal.

Key words and phrases

electricity failed
The supply of electrical power stopped working.
a warm welcome
Friendly and kind treatment when someone arrives.

Advanced Model Answer

It was around four months ago that I invited one of my closest friends round for dinner. It happened to be Christmas Eve, and since he's an expat living far from his own family, I didn't want him spending it alone, so I asked him to join us for a proper family dinner instead.

My friend's American, and since he'd missed out on turkey that Thanksgiving, my mother decided to cook turkey specially for him that night instead, as a kind of belated treat.

One of the funniest things about that evening was that almost every cooking appliance in our house runs on electricity, even the stove, and of course we had a blackout exactly an hour before dinner was due to start. By the time my friend arrived, none of the food was anywhere near ready. I was mortified, but he was completely relaxed about the whole thing. We lit candles all over the kitchen, my father dashed out to buy some bread and ham, and we ended up sitting round talking and snacking while we waited for the power to come back on. Honestly, we had an amazing time discussing the cultural differences between our countries, how hospitable Vietnamese people are, and how the language barrier never stopped him from getting to know the locals.

We finally sat down to the turkey after a two-hour delay, and even though my mother's version turned out quite differently from how his family makes it back home, he genuinely loved it. My parents absolutely adored him after that night, and he's been coming round much more often ever since, he's practically part of the family now.

Key words and phrases

belated treat
a pleasant reward given later than expected

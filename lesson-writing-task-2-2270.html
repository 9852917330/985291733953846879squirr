Technology & Science

Has technology made life complicated?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

Some people think the developments of technology make people's lives more complex, so we should make life simpler without using technology. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

Modern life certainly feels busier than it once did, and some conclude that we should simplify it by using less technology. I agree that technology has added complexity, but I disagree entirely with the remedy proposed.

The observation itself is fair. Tasks that were once single actions now involve accounts, passwords and updates, and a person may manage a dozen applications simply to pay bills and arrange a journey. Technology has also removed the boundaries that used to structure a day, since messages arrive at any hour and work follows people home, so many feel that they are never entirely off duty. The pace of change adds to this, because skills learned once now have to be relearned repeatedly.

The proposed solution nonetheless collapses on inspection. Almost everything that makes contemporary life bearable rests on the technology being rejected: medicine that treats illnesses which were once fatal, the systems that put food in shops, and the networks that let families separated by migration speak daily. Abandoning technology would not produce a simpler life but a shorter and harder one, and the people who romanticise simplicity rarely propose giving up antibiotics or clean water.

The complexity is also better explained by how technology is used than by its existence. A phone interrupts because notifications are switched on, and work reaches people in the evening because employers expect it to, so both can be changed without discarding the device.

In conclusion, I agree that technology has made daily life more complicated and more difficult to switch off from. The answer is to set limits on how it is used rather than to reject it, since removing it would take the benefits along with the problems.

Key words and phrases

never entirely off duty
Always potentially expected to work or respond.
giving up antibiotics or clean water
Losing essential benefits such as medicines and safe water.
without discarding the device
While keeping the technology itself.
take the benefits along with the problems
Accept both advantages and accompanying drawbacks.

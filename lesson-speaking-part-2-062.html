Part 2

Prompt

Describe an open-air or street market which you enjoyed

You should say: - where the market is - what the market sells - how big the market is - and explain why you enjoyed visiting it.

Simple Model Answer

An outdoor market I enjoy is the Manchester Christmas Market. It takes place in the city centre for about a month from late November. The main area is in Albert Square, beside the Town Hall, but stalls also fill nearby streets and squares.

Visitors can buy handmade jewellery, decorations, wooden toys and Christmas gifts. There is also food and drink from different parts of Europe. A popular drink is warm, sweet wine served in a souvenir mug that customers can keep. I enjoy looking around even when I am not planning to buy anything.

The market has grown a lot from its small beginnings and now attracts many visitors. My favourite thing is the atmosphere. When I went last December, the weather was cold and wet, but the streets still felt lively and cheerful. People were eating, shopping and spending time together. The lights and busy stalls made the city feel different from an ordinary winter evening. That festive feeling is why I keep returning.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe a street market that I've really enjoyed visiting, many times now, in Manchester. It's the Manchester Christmas Market, and it comes to the city for about a month from the end of November every year. The market stalls are spread across several sites in the city centre, but the centrepiece is the large European market in Albert Square, next to the Town Hall.

The Christmas Market stalls sell an array of Christmas gifts and mouth-watering food and drink from all over Europe. It's a great place to find handmade crafts such as jewellery, ornaments and wooden toys, but it's the food and drink that seem to be most popular. Probably the biggest seller is the 'Gluhwein', a hot, sweet wine sold in a souvenir mug that you get to keep.

The market was originally quite small, occupying just one of the central squares, but it's grown quickly in recent years, spilling over into maybe five other pedestrian streets and a few more squares. Apparently there are over 200 stalls now, so it's become a genuinely huge event that pulls in thousands of visitors. The main reason I keep going back is the atmosphere, honestly.

When I went last December, it didn't matter how cold or wet it was — Manchester just seemed to come alive the moment the market opened. The streets were packed, and there was a real festive feel to the whole city.

Dancing

Learning to dance and traditional dance

Part 1

Prompt

Do you enjoy dancing and why? Has anyone ever taught you to dance? Tell me about a traditional dance in your country. Do you think that traditional dancing will be popular in the future?

Do you enjoy dancing and why?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I enjoy dancing at social events, even though I'm not very good at it. People are usually relaxed and want to have fun rather than dance perfectly.

Key words and phrases

have fun
Enjoy yourself.

Advanced Model Answer

I enjoy dancing at social events, although I wouldn't call myself skilled. It's enjoyable because the atmosphere is relaxed and people are more concerned with having fun than performing perfectly.

Key words and phrases

performing perfectly
Doing something without mistakes.

Has anyone ever taught you to dance?

Simple Model Answer

I've never had proper dance lessons. I have learned a few easy steps by watching my friends and copying them, so I only dance in a casual way.

Key words and phrases

copying them
Doing the same actions as other people.

Advanced Model Answer

No one has taught me formally. I've picked up a few basic steps by watching friends and copying them, so my dancing is entirely informal.

Key words and phrases

entirely informal
Completely relaxed and without formal rules.

Tell me about a traditional dance in your country.

Simple Model Answer

There are several traditional dances in Vietnam, often performed during festivals. Dancers move together, wear colourful clothes and use music and movement to tell stories about local culture.

Key words and phrases

local culture
The traditions and way of life of people in a particular area.

Advanced Model Answer

Vietnam has several traditional dances linked to festivals and regional cultures. They often use coordinated movements, colourful costumes and music to tell a cultural story.

Key words and phrases

tell a cultural story
Express a community's history or traditions.

Do you think that traditional dancing will be popular in the future?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I think people will keep doing it, especially if schools and cultural groups make it interesting. It may not become a common hobby, but it is worth keeping.

Key words and phrases

worth keeping
Important or useful enough to preserve.

Advanced Model Answer

I think it'll survive, especially if schools and cultural groups present it in an engaging way. It may not become an everyday hobby, but it remains worth preserving.

Key words and phrases

worth preserving
Valuable enough to keep for the future.

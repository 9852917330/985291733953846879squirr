Television

How much television you watch, and your favourite programme

Part 1

Prompt

Do you watch a lot of TV? Tell me about television in your country? What is your favourite TV programme? Is TV more important to you now than in the past?

Do you watch a lot of TV?

Simple Model Answer

No, I don't watch much. I prefer choosing programmes online and watching when I want, rather than following a TV schedule or watching whatever happens to be on.

Key words and phrases

schedule
A plan showing when programmes or activities happen.

Advanced Model Answer

No, not much. I'd rather watch selected programmes online than follow a fixed television schedule, because that way I can choose content and avoid passive viewing.

Key words and phrases

avoid passive viewing
Watch thoughtfully instead of without attention.

Tell me about television in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Many people still watch TV for news, sport and big entertainment shows. Younger people often choose programmes online, so television companies are changing how they provide their content.

Key words and phrases

content
Programmes, videos or other material that people watch or read.

Advanced Model Answer

Television's still got a broad audience for news, sport and major entertainment programmes, but younger viewers increasingly stream content, so broadcasters are adapting to changing viewing habits.

Key words and phrases

changing viewing habits
Changes in how people watch programmes.

What is your favourite TV programme?

Simple Model Answer

I especially enjoy documentaries about science and society. Good pictures and clear, trustworthy explanations help me understand complicated subjects and see why they matter.

Key words and phrases

trustworthy
Able to be believed or relied on.

Advanced Model Answer

I enjoy well-produced documentaries about science and society. They've got strong visuals and reliable explanations, and they often put complex issues into perspective.

Key words and phrases

put complex issues into perspective
Explain difficult topics within a wider picture.

Is TV more important to you now than in the past?

Simple Model Answer

No, it is less important now. I can find information and entertainment online whenever I want. Television is still useful, but it is only one of several choices at home.

Key words and phrases

whenever I want
At any time I choose.

Advanced Model Answer

It's less important to me now because I can access information and entertainment on demand. Television's become just one option among many rather than the centre of home entertainment.

Key words and phrases

the centre of home entertainment
The main source of entertainment at home.

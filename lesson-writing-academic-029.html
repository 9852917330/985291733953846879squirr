School layout redevelopment

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The diagrams below show the site of a school in 2004 and the plan for changes to the school site in 2024.

Model answer

The two pictures compare the layout of a school as it was in the year 2004 with a proposed site design for the year 2024.

It is clear that the main change for 2024 would involve the addition of a new school building. The school would then be able to accommodate a considerably larger number of students.

In 2004, there were 600 pupils attending the school, and the two school buildings were separated by a path running from the main entrance to the sports field. Under the proposed layout for 2024, the school would have 1,000 pupils and a third building. The plan is also to join the two original buildings together, creating a shorter path that links the buildings only.

As the third building and a second car park would be built on the site of the original sports field, a new, smaller sports field would need to be laid. A new road would also be built from the main entrance to the second car park. Finally, no changes would be made to the main entrance and original car park.

Key words and phrases

accommodate a considerably larger number of students
Have room for many more students.
separated by a path
With a walkway between them.
sports field
An area of ground used for outdoor sports.
the proposed layout
The arrangement suggested in a plan, rather than an existing arrangement.

Garlsdon supermarket sites

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The map below is of the town of Garlsdon and shows two possible sites for a new supermarket. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The map shows two possible sites for a supermarket in Garlsdon, a town with a population of 65,000. One site, S1, is in the countryside to the north-west, while S2 is in the town centre.

Overall, the main contrast is between an out-of-town location beside a main road and a central location within a no-traffic zone. Both sites are close to the railway, and the surrounding towns are connected to Garlsdon by main roads.

S1 lies outside the housing area, near the road towards Hindon, which is 12 kilometres away and has a population of 10,000. The railway passes nearby before continuing through Garlsdon towards the south-east. This proposed site is also north-west of the town's industrial and residential areas.

S2 is surrounded by housing and occupies a position within the central no-traffic zone. The railway crosses the northern part of this central area, but the main roads do not run through it. Roads from the town lead south-west towards Bransdon, 16 kilometres away with 15,000 residents, and south-east towards Cransdon, 25 kilometres away with a population of 29,000. The map therefore presents different transport settings for the two locations without identifying a preferred site.

Key words and phrases

a no-traffic zone
An area where ordinary road traffic is not allowed.

Technology

How the internet will change our lives

Prompt

How do you think the internet will affect our lives in the future?

How do you think the internet will affect our lives in the future?

Simple Model Answer

I think the internet will become built into more ordinary services, so people notice it less. Systems may order supplies automatically or warn of health problems earlier. That could be useful, but it requires collecting personal information. Greater dependence also means a breakdown or attack could affect more of life at once. Privacy and reliable systems will therefore become increasingly important.

Key words and phrases

breakdown
A failure that stops a machine or system working.

Advanced Model Answer

I think it'll become almost invisible, in the sense that it'll be built into everything rather than something we consciously log into. Healthcare, education, transport and government services are all moving online, and systems will increasingly anticipate what we need before we ask — a fridge that reorders milk before you run out, or a health app that flags a problem before you'd have noticed the symptoms yourself. The benefits are obvious, but there are two things societies will have to manage. One is privacy, because prediction requires collecting enormous quantities of personal data. The other is dependence — if everything runs through a single network, a failure or an attack becomes far more damaging than it would once have been.

Key words and phrases

built into everything
included as a standard part of almost every system
government services
help provided by public offices and officials
anticipate what we need
predict our needs before we ask
dependence
needing something in order to function
a failure or an attack
when a system stops working or is deliberately harmed

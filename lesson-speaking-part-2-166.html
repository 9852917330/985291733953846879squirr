Situation

A success in your life

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a success in your life

Simple Model Answer

A success I am proud of was helping protect Thong Nhat Park in my hometown about five years ago. I was a first-year college student when I heard plans to replace the park with a shopping centre.

Some trees were already being cut down, so I gathered a group of friends who shared my concern. The park was the area's main green space. Residents used it for walking, jogging and tai chi, and my grandmother went there daily. It was also home to birds, which made losing it feel even worse.

We organised a petition and collected several hundred signatures. We also contacted local news organisations to explain why residents wanted the park kept. Eventually, the shopping centre plan was cancelled. I felt proud because our effort had helped protect something valuable for the community. The experience showed me that students can contribute to local decisions when they work together and explain their concerns clearly. I still see it as a success because the benefit was shared by many people, rather than belonging only to me.

Key words and phrases

petition
A written request that many people sign to show support.
contribute to local decisions
Help influence choices that affect the area where people live.

Advanced Model Answer

The success I'd like to talk about today is my part in a bit of local activism that happened about five years ago, when I was just a freshman in college. I still feel really proud of it, because I genuinely think I helped do something good for my community.

At the time, the local authorities in my hometown had announced plans to knock down a public park, Thong Nhat Park, and build a shopping mall in its place. I noticed workers had already started cutting down some of the older trees, so I got a group of friends together and we decided to protest, even though I knew it wasn't going to be a simple fight against the local council.

The reason I got involved was that this park was the only real green space in the area. People came every single morning to jog, do tai chi or just sit and get some fresh air, and my own grandmother used to walk there daily too. It's also home to quite a few wild birds, which felt like something genuinely worth protecting rather than losing to another shopping centre.

I knew that going up against the authorities wasn't going to be easy, but I felt I had to try, for the sake of the whole neighbourhood. We put together a petition, collected a few hundred signatures, and reached out to a couple of local news outlets to help make some noise about it. In the end, our campaign worked, the mall project was cancelled, and honestly, that whole experience still feels like a real success in my life.

Key words and phrases

local authorities
officials who manage a town or local area
green space
land covered with grass or trees for people to enjoy
for the sake of
in order to help or protect
make some noise about it
complain or speak publicly until people pay attention
success in my life
an achievement that mattered in my life

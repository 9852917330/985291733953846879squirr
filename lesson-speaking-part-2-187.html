Situation

An important stage of your life

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an important stage of your life

You should say: What part it is Where you were then What memorable things you did And explain why this is the most enjoyable part / stage in your life.

Simple Model Answer

An important stage of my life was preparing for the university entrance exam about five years ago. The test covered a large amount of high school work, and I felt strong pressure to achieve a good result.

During the final month, I spent many late evenings revising. I worried about remembering enough material and competing with other students. At the same time, I had to decide which subject to study at university. Some classmates already had clear plans, but I was still uncertain, which made the period more stressful.

It was not a particularly enjoyable time, but it was important for my development. I had to think seriously about my future rather than simply follow the next school timetable. I also learned that important goals require preparation and personal decisions. Now, when I meet old classmates, we can laugh about some of those difficult evenings. The pressure feels smaller from a distance. I remember the stage because it helped me become more independent and think about who I wanted to be, not only which exam I wanted to pass.

Key words and phrases

revising
Studying something again in preparation for a test.
more independent
Better able to make decisions and manage life without constant help.

Advanced Model Answer

I want to talk about the time I was preparing for my university entrance exam, which is still probably the most important stage of my life so far. It happened about five years ago, but the whole experience is still so fresh in my memory that it could've been last year.

In Vietnam, that exam is basically treated as the most important test you'll ever sit, and mine was no exception. Back then, we had to cover three full years of high school content in one exam, which honestly felt like too much for anyone to handle - I remember pulling all-nighters almost every night in the final month. The competition was fierce too; you simply wouldn't get into a decent university unless your score was genuinely high.

On top of the academic pressure, I also had to figure out which major to choose, which stressed me out just as much as the studying itself. Some of my classmates seemed to know exactly what they wanted to do, but I had absolutely no idea, so I spent that whole period feeling completely on edge.

In hindsight, I wouldn't call it an enjoyable time exactly, but it's without doubt one of my most precious memories. It was the first experience that ever had that much of an impact on my life, and it forced me to actually think about who I wanted to become, rather than just drifting along. Looking back now, I can even laugh about those exhausting late nights with my old classmates - we still bring them up whenever we meet. It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say I properly grew up during that period, and that's really why it still matters so much to me.

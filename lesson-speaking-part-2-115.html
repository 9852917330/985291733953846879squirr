Simple Model Answer

An activity I enjoy by the sea is swimming. I live in Hai Phong, and the nearest beach is about fifteen minutes from my house. In summer, my friends and I often go there to cool down and spend time together.

I learned to swim when I was about eight. After some practice, I could use a few different strokes, including breaststroke and backstroke. Before entering the water, I usually do some gentle warm-up exercises. I enjoy the feeling of moving through the sea rather than staying on the hot sand.

One reason I like swimming is that it does not require much equipment compared with sports such as diving or windsurfing. It also gives my body exercise and helps me relax. Being with friends makes the experience more enjoyable because we can talk before and after swimming. I am thinking about visiting Nha Trang when I have another chance to travel. For me, a beach holiday feels incomplete without some time in the water, provided the conditions are suitable for swimming.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about one of my favourite things to do at the beach - swimming. I live in Hai Phong, a coastal city, and the nearest beach is only about fifteen minutes from my house, so in summer my friends and I often head down there to swim in the cool water and escape the heat.

Swimming's actually a pretty easy sport to pick up. I learned when I was about eight, and it only took me a week or so to get the hang of a few strokes - breaststroke, backstroke, that sort of thing. Before I get in the water, I always warm up and stretch properly, just to avoid pulling a muscle, and then it's straight in.

I love swimming at the beach for a few reasons, really. Unlike sports like diving or windsurfing, which need all sorts of expensive gear, swimming doesn't cost a thing. It's also brilliant for your health - it gives you an all-over body workout since you're using almost every muscle, and it builds up your stamina, strength and cardiovascular fitness at the same time. On top of that, it's just a great way to unwind and de-stress, and I get to spend that time with my mates too.

Summer's coming up again, so I'm already planning a trip to the beach soon. I'm thinking of heading to Nha Trang this time, since it's supposed to be one of the most spectacular beaches in the world.

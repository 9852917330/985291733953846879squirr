Person

A polite person you met

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a polite person you met

You should say: Who the person is How you know the person What the person looks like And explain why you think the person is polite.

OUTLINE

>>> Mai
good friend Mai, one of the nicest and most polite people I know, known since high school
what she looks like: tall, straight brown hair, dark brown eyes, gorgeous, dresses in trendy fashion, gets attention but stays down-to-earth about it
how met: first day of school, got lost, she helped, became desk-mates
why polite: good manners that feel genuine - thanks security guard by name, lets elders go first, stayed calm with a rude customer at her cafe job, never complains about helping classmates
manners feel genuine, not put on - that's why she stands out as a polite person

Simple Model Answer

A polite person I know is my friend Mai. We first met at high school and later attended the same college. On the first day of school, I could not find my classroom. She stopped to help me, and we were then asked to sit together.

Mai is tall, with straight brown hair and dark brown eyes. She dresses well, but she never acts as if she is better than other people. Her good manners seem natural. For example, she always thanks the security guard by name and lets older people go through a doorway first, even when she is busy.

One thing I remember clearly happened when she worked part time in a cafe. A customer was rude to her, but Mai stayed calm and spoke politely throughout the conversation. She also helped classmates with homework without making them feel uncomfortable. I admire her because she shows respect through everyday actions. Being around her reminds me to be more patient and considerate too.

Key words and phrases

good manners
Polite and respectful ways of behaving.
shows respect
Treats someone as a person whose feelings and needs matter.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, the person I'd like to talk about is a very good friend of mine, Mai. She has to be one of the nicest and most polite people I know. We've basically known each other since forever, because we were in the same class from high school right through to college, and we've stayed close ever since.

In terms of what she looks like, Mai's quite tall, with straight brown hair and these deep, dark brown eyes, and she's honestly pretty gorgeous — she gets a fair bit of attention wherever we go. She's always dressed in whatever's trendiest, too, though none of that ever seems to go to her head, which says a lot about her down-to-earth personality.

The first time I met her, she left a deep impression on me, because on the first day of school I got completely lost trying to find my classroom, and she went out of her way to help me. As luck would have it, the teacher then sat us together as desk-mates, and that was that.

The reason I think she's so polite is simply that she's got really good manners, and it never feels put on. I've noticed she always thanks the security guard at our building by name, and she'll let older people go through a door first even when she's in a rush herself. What really struck me, though, was watching her deal with a rude customer once, back when she had a part-time job at a cafe — she stayed calm and polite the whole time, even though the woman was being pretty unreasonable. And whenever classmates used to ask her for help with homework, she'd never once complain or make them feel like a bother.

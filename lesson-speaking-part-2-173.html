Situation

A time when you received good service in a restaurant or cafe

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when you received good service in a restaurant or cafe

You should say: - when this was - what kind of restaurant or cafe you were in - why you were there - and why you thought the service was good

Simple Model Answer

A time I received good restaurant service was at my friend Duc's birthday last Saturday. He booked a table at a small Italian restaurant near his flat. About ten of us attended, mostly friends from university.

I did not expect much before arriving, but the small, family-run place felt very welcoming. The waiters noticed when we needed something without standing too close or interrupting our conversation. The owner also checked that we were enjoying ourselves and seemed interested in our answers.

The nicest moment was when staff brought a cake with candles and sang Happy Birthday, although we had not asked them to. It made Duc feel special and gave everyone something to remember. Of course, the food mattered too, but the personal attention was what made the evening stand out. We were treated as guests rather than simply another order to complete. I would happily return, and I have already recommended it to friends. The experience showed me that good service often comes from small actions that make customers feel noticed and welcome.

Key words and phrases

family-run
Managed by members of one family.

Care given to someone's individual needs or experience.

Advanced Model Answer

I'll tell you about the service we got at a restaurant when a friend of mine, Duc, celebrated his birthday last Saturday evening. He'd booked a table at a small, family-run Italian place near his flat, and I wasn't expecting much from somewhere so tiny, but it turned out to be all the more welcoming for being so small and personal.

There were about ten of us altogether, mostly people I've known since university, and it was great to catch up with everyone and hear what they'd been up to since we'd last got together. We were there for Duc's birthday, but also because he'd been raving about the place for weeks, so the rest of us were curious to try it for ourselves.

What made the service stand out was that the staff didn't just take our order and disappear - they actually made an effort to make us feel at home. The waiters were attentive without hovering, and whenever anyone needed something, it was no trouble at all for them. At one point they brought out a cake with candles and sang 'Happy Birthday', even though nobody had asked them to, which was a really nice touch. The owner also came round a couple of times just to check we were happy with everything, and it didn't feel like a routine question - he genuinely seemed to care.

All in all, it was those little extra touches, more than the food itself, that made the evening so memorable. I'd definitely go back, and I've already recommended it to a couple of friends.

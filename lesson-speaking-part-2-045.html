Place

A place where you can read and write

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a place where you can read and write.

You should say: Where this place is How do you know this place What do you do there And explain why you think it's a good place for reading and writing.

Simple Model Answer

A place where I can read and write is the City Library in central Ho Chi Minh City. It is about five kilometres from my home. I first visited around three years ago, during my final university exams.

At that time, I had a lot of revision and homework. I wanted to relax with a book, so I decided to try the library. Although the surrounding streets are noisy, the rooms inside are peaceful. There are long tables, air conditioning and a large collection of books. I later began writing essays there because useful study materials were easy to find.

Before my first visit, I was not a keen reader and thought libraries might be boring. However, this one changed my opinion. I discovered novels, comics and books about new subjects. Reading there helps me put everyday worries aside for a while. I enjoy having somewhere quiet where I can either study seriously or simply read for pleasure. It has helped make reading a regular part of my life.

Key words and phrases

study materials
Books and other resources used for learning.
put everyday worries aside
Stop thinking about ordinary problems for a while.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about the City Library, which is one of the largest libraries in Ho Chi Minh City. I went there for the first time about 3 years ago, when I was in my final year of university. It was right in the middle of my final exams, so I was absolutely swamped with homework and revision. That's why I decided to try to ease my mind after all that stress by reading a few new books, and that's how I ended up visiting this library.

The library's roughly 5 kilometres from my house, in downtown Ho Chi Minh City, which is a pretty bustling area with loads of people, noise and traffic. It still attracts a huge number of visitors every day, mostly because of its enormous range of fascinating books.

This library's well known for how peaceful it is — it's basically heaven for anyone with a real passion for books, especially novels and comics. It's got these long tables and it's properly air-conditioned, so I ended up writing a lot of my essays there, since it gives you access to such a great range of learning materials. At first I thought the place was kinda boring, because I wasn't much of a reader back then. But after that first visit, it turned out to be genuinely wonderful, and I've actually become a big reader ever since.

Every time I read there, I feel like I can shake off everyday stress and just switch off after a hard day. On top of that, the books and other materials there help me expand my horizons and learn all sorts of new things. That's really why I like this building so much.

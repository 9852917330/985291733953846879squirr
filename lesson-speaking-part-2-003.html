Person

A family member that made you proud

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a family member that made you proud.

You should say: Who the person is How close you are What he/she did that made you proud And explain why they made you proud.

OUTLINE

>>> my mother
no one else in world I hold more respect for than my mother, best friend, talk daily
well into 40s, maintains youthful appearance, compassionate kind-hearted attentive personality
mother and friend, gives valuable advice, there through ups and downs, drives me crazy (perfectionist)
strong-willed since young - grandparents busy, she had to be independent, raised brother/chores herself
proudest moment: opened clothing store in her 30s despite family trying to talk her out of it
determination and perseverance made it a success, something to look up to, try to be as strong as her

Simple Model Answer

The family member who makes me proud is my mother. She is in her forties and still looks young. She is kind and caring, and I can talk to her like a close friend. Even though I have moved out, we speak on the phone most days.

She became independent quite early. My grandparents were busy, so she had to help with the housework and look after her younger brother. This taught her to deal with problems on her own.

I felt especially proud when she decided to open a clothing shop in her thirties. Most of our family thought it was too risky because there were already many shops nearby. However, she believed in her idea and worked hard to make it successful. She did not give up when things were difficult. Her shop showed me what she could achieve through patience and effort. Whenever I face a challenge, I think about her example and try to be as determined as she is.

Key words and phrases

on her own
Without help from other people.
did not give up
Continued trying even when something was difficult.

Advanced Model Answer

There's no one else in this world I hold more respect for than my mother. She's my guardian, my friend, and honestly my best friend too — we still talk on the phone most days, even now that I've moved out.

My mother's well into her forties now, but she still manages to maintain a youthful appearance. She's an extremely compassionate, kind-hearted person who's always looked after her children, and she's both a mother and a friend to me — she gives me valuable advice and is always there for me, no matter what. She drives me a bit crazy sometimes, because she's such a perfectionist, but I still hold huge pride and respect for her.

The reason I'm so proud of her is that she's such a strong-willed person. Ever since she was young, she's had to be independent, because my grandparents were always so busy, so she had to take care of almost everything herself — her younger brother and the household chores. Challenges like that shaped her into the strong woman she is today.

What made me proudest, though, was when she reached her thirties and decided she wanted to run her own business, a clothing store. Pretty much the whole family tried to talk her out of it, mainly because of all the competition from other shops nearby. But she went ahead anyway, and through sheer determination and perseverance, she's managed to make a real success out of it. It's honestly something for all of us to look up to, and I keep reminding myself every day to try to be as strong as my mother — a real-life "wonder woman".

Key words and phrases

maintain a youthful appearance
continue to look young
valuable advice
helpful guidance worth listening to
determination and perseverance
Strong will to continue despite difficulty.
look up to
admire and respect

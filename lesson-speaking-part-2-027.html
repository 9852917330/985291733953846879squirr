Describe two people from the same family

Simple Model Answer

Two people from the same family I know are twin sisters who live next door. We met about a year ago during a power cut. I asked to borrow candles, and they invited me in while we waited for the electricity to return.

Although they are twins, they look and behave differently. The older sister has short hair and is very outgoing. She can start a conversation with almost anyone. The younger one has long hair and is quieter. She listens carefully and sometimes makes unexpected jokes.

We often watch films at my flat, order food and talk until late. Their different personalities make these evenings interesting. One brings energy to the conversation, while the other gives thoughtful advice. They are also reliable, and we help each other with practical problems. I enjoy knowing them both because I feel comfortable asking for support. The older sister is considering a job abroad, so we may not always be neighbours, but I hope our friendship continues wherever we live.

Key words and phrases

outgoing
Friendly and comfortable meeting and talking to people.
reliable
Someone you can trust to help or do what they promise.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to describe twin sisters who live in the apartment next to mine. I met them about a year ago during a power cut, when I knocked on their door to borrow some candles, feeling a bit awkward about bothering total strangers. They invited me in until the electricity came back, and we've been friends ever since.

Although they're twins, they look nothing alike. The older sister has short hair, a mischievous smile and a very outgoing personality. She's a real social butterfly and can start a conversation with almost anyone. Her younger sister has long hair and is much quieter and more reserved. She usually listens carefully before speaking, but she's got a dry sense of humour that makes me laugh, even when I'm least expecting it.

I enjoy spending time with them because their personalities balance each other out. The older one brings energy to any gathering, while the younger one notices details and gives thoughtful advice. We often have film nights at my apartment, order food and talk until late. Because they see situations differently, we never run out of things to talk about.

What I value most is that they're both dependable. If I need practical help, there'll usually be one of them around, and they know they can rely on me too. I hope we'll stay close even if one of us eventually moves away, which feels more likely now that the older one's talking about a job abroad.

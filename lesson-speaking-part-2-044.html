Place

A place where people go to listen to music

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a place where people go to listen to music

You should say: What kind of music is performed there What type of people go there Why people go there Explain your impressions of this place

Simple Model Answer

A place I like for live music is Polygon, a music pub on Cat Linh Street. It is in a fairly hidden corner, so I might not have found it without a close friend who recommended it.

The music changes depending on the band performing. Western pop is common, but there are also jazz and ballad evenings. Many younger people go there with friends. Some have a drink and listen, while others dance. I mainly enjoy watching the musicians perform because they put so much feeling into the songs.

The atmosphere is friendly and relaxed. People who have never met sometimes start talking because they like the same music. Audience members can even join the performers on stage, and the musicians may play a requested song. This makes each evening feel a little different. I like Polygon because the audience is involved rather than simply watching quietly. It is a good place to spend time with friends and enjoy the energy of a live band.

live music

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about a music pub I go to pretty often, called Polygon. It's tucked away in a hidden corner of Cat Linh Street, which somehow makes it feel even better when you find it.

I first found out about it through a close friend of mine, who's a massive fan of live music. Instead of sticking to just one genre, the place offers a wide variety of styles depending on whoever's playing that night. Most of the bands play western pop, but on special occasions there are jazz nights or ballad nights too.

From what I can tell, it's a real hotspot for younger people who are into band music. Some are just there for a drink with friends while nodding along to the music, some want a place to dance the night away, and honestly, I'm mostly there to watch the artists sing their hearts out on stage — that's what I love most about it.

As for my impressions of the place, I'd describe it as somewhere that feels genuinely free. It's the kind of place where total strangers end up talking to each other just because of the music. The audience can even get up on stage if they fancy it, and the artists will happily improvise a tune if someone shouts out a request. I'd honestly recommend it to anyone who's into live band music.

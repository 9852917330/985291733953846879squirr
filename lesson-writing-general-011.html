Formal

Important papers left at a hotel

Formal

Prompt

After attending a meeting at a hotel, you realised that you had left important documents behind. Write to the hotel manager. In your letter: - state where you may have left the documents - explain why they are important - say what you would like the manager to do

Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I attended a business meeting at the Riverside Hotel yesterday and believe I left a folder containing important documents in the building.

The meeting took place in the Oak Conference Room from 2 p.m. to 5 p.m. I was seated near the window and may have left the folder either under my chair or on the table beside the projector. It is dark grey and marked “Northgate Project”.

The folder contains signed contracts and confidential documents relating to a client tender that closes on Friday. Replacing the originals would be difficult and could delay the submission, so I would be grateful if a member of staff could check the meeting room as soon as possible. Please also ask the reception desk and housekeeping team whether it has been handed in.

If the folder is found, could you keep it securely at reception? I can collect it this afternoon. Alternatively, I will cover the cost if you need to send it by registered post to my office.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

Replacing the originals would be difficult
Getting new copies would be hard.
I would be grateful if
A polite way to ask.
keep it securely at reception
Store it safely at the front desk.
I will cover the cost
I will pay for it.
send it by registered post
Use a recorded postal delivery service.

Sport & Leisure

Sport as a business

Positive or Negative

Prompt

Nowadays, sport is a big business, with high earnings for professional sportspeople and the companies involved. Is this a positive or negative development?

Model answer

Television, sponsorship and merchandising have turned professional sport into an industry worth hundreds of billions. I regard the development as broadly positive for the standard of sport, though it has damaged the relationship between clubs and the people who support them.

The money has clearly improved what spectators watch. Athletes can train full-time with proper coaching, nutrition and medical care, which is why records fall in almost every discipline, and injuries that once ended careers are now treated successfully. Commercial income has also opened sport to people who could not previously afford to pursue it, since a talented child from a poor family can now be supported through an academy, and women's competitions have grown rapidly on the same funding after decades of neglect. Broadcasting reaches audiences that would never have seen these events.

The costs fall on the sport itself. Wealth concentrates, so the same few clubs win repeatedly and competitions become predictable, and smaller clubs that formed the base of the game struggle or disappear. Fixtures are arranged for broadcasters rather than for supporters, with matches moved to inconvenient times and competitions restructured to suit the audiences that pay the most. Ticket prices have risen beyond what many lifelong supporters can pay, and a club that was a local institution can be sold to owners with no connection to the place.

There are consequences for the athletes too. Pressure to perform and to appear constantly on television produces injuries, exhaustion and a measurable amount of doping, and young players are signed and discarded before they are twenty.

In conclusion, commercial money has raised standards, widened access and made sport visible to millions, which is a genuine gain. It has also made competition less equal and pushed supporters aside, so the development is positive only where income is shared and clubs remain answerable to their communities.

Key words and phrases

suit the audiences that pay the most
Prioritise viewers who generate the most money.
signed and discarded before they are twenty
Recruited and then dropped while still teenagers.

Sport & Leisure

Olympic medal totals

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The chart below shows the total number of Olympic medals won by twelve different countries.

Model answer

The bar chart compares twelve countries in terms of the overall medal totals recorded for them in the chart at the Olympic Games.

It is clear that the USA was by far the most successful Olympic medal-winning nation in the chart. It is also noticeable that the figures for gold, silver and bronze medals won by any particular country tend to be fairly similar.

The USA had won a total of around 2,300 Olympic medals, including approximately 900 gold medals, 750 silver and 650 bronze. In second place in the chart was the Soviet Union, with just over 1,000 medals. Again, the number of gold medals won by this country is slightly higher than the number of silver or bronze medals.

Only four other countries - the UK, France, Germany and Italy - had won more than 500 Olympic medals, all with similar proportions of each medal colour. Apart from the USA and the Soviet Union, China was the only other country with a noticeably higher proportion of gold medals (about 200) compared to silver and bronze (about 100 each).

Key words and phrases

in the chart
Within the information presented by the graph.
by far the most successful
Much more successful than all others.
tend to be fairly similar
Are usually quite alike.
a noticeably higher proportion of gold medals
A clearly larger percentage of golds.

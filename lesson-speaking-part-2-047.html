Place

A place where you want to work

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a place where you want to work

You should say: - where it is - what kind of work you would like to do - when you would like to work there And explain why you want to work in this place.

OUTLINE

>>> Tokyo
always wanted to work in Tokyo, haven't been there yet

Hanoi's air pollution problem, cleaner air appealing on its own

excited, fingers crossed, completely new phase in life

Simple Model Answer

A place where I would like to work is Tokyo, the capital of Japan. I have not been there yet, but a travel and lifestyle programme first made me interested in it a few years ago.

What appealed to me was the combination of modern city life and Japanese traditions. There were tall office buildings near old temples, and people seemed to respect their culture while living in a busy international city. I currently live in Hanoi, where air pollution can be a problem, so the possibility of cleaner surroundings also attracts me.

I would like to work in marketing, ideally near Ginza, a busy shopping and business area. I have contacted an agency that helps graduates find internships in Japan. If my plans work out, I may begin a marketing placement in about six months. I know moving abroad would require preparation and adjustment, but I feel excited. It would be a chance to gain work experience, learn about another culture and become more independent.

Key words and phrases

internships
Short work placements that help people gain practical experience.
work out
Develop or finish successfully.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, the place I've always wanted to work, even though I haven't actually been there yet, is Tokyo, the capital of Japan.

I first fell for the idea after watching a travel and lifestyle programme about it a few years back. It's a major cosmopolitan city, obviously, but what struck me was how well it manages to preserve the cultural aspect of Japan at the same time — you'll see a beautiful old temple tucked right in among the skyscrapers, or women walking to work in kimonos, and it just looks amazing. On top of that, Hanoi, where I live now, has a pretty serious air pollution problem, so honestly, the idea of working somewhere with cleaner air is appealing on its own.

As for the kind of work, I'd like to go into marketing, ideally somewhere around Ginza, which is one of the busiest and most fashionable streets in Tokyo. There are quite a few agencies now that help graduates find internships in Japan, so I've actually been in touch with one, and if everything works out, I should be starting as a marketing intern there in about six months.

I'm genuinely excited about it. Fingers crossed it all comes together, because it feels like it could be the start of a completely new phase in my life.

Key words and phrases

a major cosmopolitan city
a large city with people from many countries and cultures
preserve the cultural aspect
protect what makes the local culture special
air pollution
Harmful substances in the air.
a completely new phase in my life
a very different period of my life beginning

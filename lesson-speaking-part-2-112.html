Activity

A car journey you went on

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a car journey you went on.

You should say: - Where did you go; - What did you do there; - Who you went there with; - Why you wanted to go there for a car journey; - and how you felt about it.

Simple Model Answer

A car journey I remember was a trip to Nha Trang with my best friend about three years ago. We had just graduated from university and decided to travel during the summer holiday. We left early because it was a long way from home.

The drive took about five hours. When we arrived, we were pleased to see clean beaches, white sand and clear water. We stayed with my uncle's family for three nights. During the day, we went swimming, explored the city and tried local food. We also helped my uncle's family with a few jobs around the house before returning home.

Before leaving, I had worried that travelling with just one friend might be boring. In fact, the trip was much better than I expected. We had time to talk properly and learn about a different way of life outside our usual surroundings. Going by car also made the journey itself part of the holiday. I would happily take another trip like it if we both had enough free time.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about a car journey I took with my best friend about three years ago, just after I'd graduated from university. It was the middle of summer vacation, and we decided on the spur of the moment to drive down to Nha Trang, a coastal city on the south-central coast of Vietnam. Because it's so far from my hometown, we knew we were facing a long drive, so we set out really early in the morning and chose to go by car rather than by motorbike, just to be safe.

After about five hours on the road, we finally arrived. The beaches there were stunning - fine white sand and clear, warm water, exactly what we'd been picturing the whole drive. We kept ourselves busy for the next few days: eating out most nights, swimming whenever the weather allowed, and just wandering around the city. We ended up staying with my uncle's family for three nights, and we lent a hand with a few jobs around their house before setting off home on the last day.

Honestly, I'd been under the impression that the trip might turn out a bit dull, since it was just the two of us and I didn't know many people down there. But it turned out to be one of the best trips I've had - we came away knowing so much more about country life and how differently people live outside the city. If I get the chance again, I'd jump at doing another trip like that.

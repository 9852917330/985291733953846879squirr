Customer Service

Customer service and how companies could improve it

Prompt

How important is customer service for you? What can companies do to improve their customer service? Why do you think employees sometimes fail to provide good customer service?

How important is customer service for you?

Simple Model Answer

It matters a lot because I want to feel welcome and respected. Friendly service makes me more likely to return. For example, I regularly visit one cafe largely because of its staff. Price still matters, but I am willing to pay a little more when I know I will be treated well.

Key words and phrases

willing to
Ready or happy to do something.

Advanced Model Answer

Customer service is really important for me because I like to feel welcome and valued as a customer. If companies want us to use their products or services, I believe they should treat us well and therefore encourage us to return. For example, I have a favourite cafe where I like to go, and the friendly staff are the main reason I've become a loyal customer. Price matters, obviously, but I'll pay slightly more to be treated well.

Key words and phrases

feel welcome and valued
feel accepted and appreciated
a loyal customer
a buyer who keeps returning to the same business

What can companies do to improve their customer service?

Simple Model Answer

Companies should train staff to welcome people, listen and solve problems. Employees also need enough freedom to take responsibility rather than pass a complaint around. When I called about a bill, one worker solved it quickly while another only followed a script. However, training cannot fix everything if there are too few staff and everyone is rushed.

Key words and phrases

take responsibility
Accept the job of dealing with something properly.

Advanced Model Answer

I think managers need to train their employees to deliver great customer service, because good intentions alone don't achieve much without real skills behind them. There are lots of things staff can do, like greeting customers properly, handling problems quickly, and making sure they're asked for feedback. I called my phone provider recently with a billing problem, and the difference was obvious: the first person I spoke to just read from a script, while the second actually solved it in five minutes because she took real ownership of the problem instead of passing it on. Training only goes so far, though, if staff are stretched too thin — even the best-trained call-handler can't do much when they're rushing through fifty calls an hour.

Key words and phrases

deliver great customer service
provide helpful and respectful service to buyers
asked for feedback
requested people's opinions on how to improve
stretched too thin
given more work than they can manage well

Why do you think employees sometimes fail to provide good customer service?

Simple Model Answer

Sometimes employees dislike their work because managers treat them badly or give them little support. That can affect how carefully they deal with customers. I was less attentive in my first job because I did not enjoy it. Still, poor service does not always show a permanent attitude; someone may simply be having a difficult day.

Key words and phrases

attentive
Paying careful attention to people's needs.

Advanced Model Answer

When employees' treatment of customers isn't good, it's usually because they're unhappy doing the job itself. That might be because they're treated badly by their own managers, or because they've never been encouraged to take pride in their work. I remember my first ever job — I didn't enjoy the work I'd been given, and I probably wasn't as thoughtful or attentive as I should've been when I had to deal with customers. It isn't always about the person, though; sometimes it really is just one bad day, not a sign of how they usually work.

Key words and phrases

take pride in
feel pleased with
just one bad day
only one difficult day

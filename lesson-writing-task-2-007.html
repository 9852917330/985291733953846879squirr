Animals & Wildlife

Wildlife protection or helping the poor?

Discuss Both Views

Prompt

Some people believe that money and time should be spent on protecting wild animals, while others argue that this money should be spent on populations living in the poorest areas of the world. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

Some people argue that money spent protecting wild animals would be better used helping the world's poorest communities. In my opinion this presents a false choice, since the two aims usually depend on each other.

The case for prioritising people is emotionally powerful. Millions still lack clean water, basic healthcare and enough food, and it is difficult to justify spending on nature reserves while children die of preventable diseases. Human suffering is immediate and measurable, whereas the benefits of conservation are gradual and often invisible.

However, this argument misunderstands where poverty comes from. The poorest communities are usually those most directly dependent on the natural environment: they fish, farm and gather fuel from it. When a forest is cleared or a river polluted, it is these families who lose their livelihoods first, so environmental destruction is itself a major cause of poverty rather than a separate concern. Protecting a wetland that supplies fish and controls flooding is therefore a form of poverty reduction.

There is also a practical point about scale. The sums spent on conservation worldwide are extremely small compared with military budgets, so redirecting them would make very little difference to global poverty. Conservation can also generate income directly, since wildlife tourism in countries such as Kenya and Costa Rica employs large numbers of local people.

In conclusion, I disagree that we must choose between wildlife and the poor. The most effective programmes protect habitats while paying the communities that live in them, which serves both purposes at once.

Key words and phrases

immediate and measurable
Happening now and possible to measure.
lose their livelihoods first
Are first to lose their income sources.
a form of poverty reduction
A way to make people less poor.
serves both purposes at once
Achieves both aims together.

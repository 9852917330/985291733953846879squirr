Animals & Wildlife

Is losing species the biggest problem?

Discuss Both Views

Prompt

Some people say that the main environmental problem of our time is the loss of particular species of plants and animals. Others say there are more important environmental problems. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

Environmental attention is divided between the disappearance of wildlife and problems such as warming, pollution and water shortage. Both sides describe something serious, and my own view is that the argument is less a ranking than a question of what causes what.

The case for treating species loss as central rests on permanence. A polluted river can be cleaned within a decade, but an extinct animal is gone for ever, and current rates of loss are far above anything in the geological record. Species also perform work that human beings depend on without paying for: pollination of crops, decomposition of waste and the regulation of pests. Losing enough of them weakens the systems that produce our food, and since we understand very little about how these connections operate, each loss carries unknown consequences.

The opposing view argues from immediacy. Air pollution kills millions of people every year, unsafe water kills hundreds of thousands of children, and climate change is already producing failed harvests and displacement. These affect human beings now, and where budgets are limited it is difficult to justify spending on a rare bird rather than on clean water. There is also a practical objection: a species protected on land that is drying out is not really protected.

My own position is that the second group of problems is more urgent while the first is more final, and they are not separate. Warming, pollution and habitat clearance are what is driving species loss, so extinction is largely a symptom, and treating the causes protects wildlife more effectively than any programme aimed at individual animals. That does not make conservation optional, since some species will disappear before the causes are fixed.

In conclusion, the disappearance of species is the least reversible of our environmental problems. Because it is driven by climate change, pollution and land use, addressing those is also the most effective form of conservation.

Key words and phrases

weakens the systems that produce our food
Damages the networks supporting food production.
is also the most effective form of conservation
Also protects nature most successfully.

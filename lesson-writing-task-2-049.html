News & Media

Global media and cultural diversity

Opinion

Prompt

When international media (including movies, fashion shows, advertisements and other TV programmes) convey the same messages to the global audience, people argue that the expansion of international media has negative impacts on cultural diversity. What is your opinion?

Model answer

International media gives people access to films, music and ideas from around the world. Although this can make popular culture more similar across countries, I do not think it must destroy cultural diversity. The outcome depends mainly on how local communities respond.

There is a genuine risk of cultural uniformity. Large media companies have the money to promote the same films, celebrities and consumer trends in many markets. Local artists may struggle to compete, and young audiences may start to see foreign lifestyles as more modern or attractive than their own. Over time, this can weaken local traditions and reduce demand for regional languages, music and crafts.

However, international media can also widen cultural understanding. Streaming platforms allow small producers to reach audiences far beyond their home countries, while viewers can discover stories that were previously unavailable to them. Governments and schools can support this positive effect by funding local content, teaching cultural history and ensuring that national broadcasters include regional voices. In this way, societies can engage with global culture without abandoning their identity.

Cultural influence is also rarely one-way. South Korean pop music, for example, borrows the production techniques and marketing methods of the American entertainment industry, yet its lyrics, choreography and star system remain distinctly Korean, and the genre has pulled millions of new listeners towards Korean language and fashion rather than away from them. Absorbing outside methods does not have to mean losing what is local: a culture confident enough to blend in this way can compete on more equal terms with the media companies that once threatened to drown it out.

In conclusion, global media can place pressure on cultural diversity, but cultural loss is not inevitable. If countries invest in local creative industries and people remain interested in their own heritage, international media can become a channel for exchange rather than a force for uniformity. The aim should be to preserve local identity while remaining open to outside influence.

Key words and phrases

weaken local traditions
Reduce the strength of local customs.
engage with global culture
Take part in international cultural activity.
Cultural influence is also rarely one-way
Cultures usually influence each other in both directions.
compete on more equal terms
Compete with more similar resources or advantages.
preserve local identity while remaining open
Keep local character while welcoming outside influences.

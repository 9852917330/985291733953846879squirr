Tourism & Travel

UK travel and destinations

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The charts below give information about travel to and from the UK, and about the most popular countries for UK residents to visit. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The line graph compares visits abroad by UK residents with visits to the UK by overseas residents between 1979 and 1999. The bar chart shows the five most popular overseas destinations for UK residents in 1999.

Overall, both types of visit increased, but outbound travel grew more strongly and remained higher throughout the period shown. France and Spain were the leading destinations among the five countries listed.

In 1979, UK residents made approximately 12 million visits abroad, compared with about 10 million visits to the UK by overseas residents. Outbound visits then rose substantially, reaching roughly 53 million in 1999, more than four times the initial figure. Inbound visits increased more moderately to approximately 27 million, or around half the outbound total at the end of the period.

France received about 11 million visits from UK residents in 1999, followed by Spain with roughly 9 to 10 million. The other three destinations were considerably less popular: the USA, Greece and Turkey each recorded between approximately 2 and 4 million visits. Turkey had the lowest figure, at around 2 million, less than one-fifth of the French total. The charts count visits, so the figures do not necessarily represent different individual travellers.

Key words and phrases

the period shown
The time interval covered by the data.
compared with
Used to show a difference or similarity between two things.
the lowest figure
The smallest numerical value in a comparison.

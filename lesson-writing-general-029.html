Semi-formal

Volunteering on a school trip

Semi-formal

Prompt

Your child's school is arranging a three-day trip abroad and has asked parents to volunteer. Write to the head teacher. In your letter: - explain why you would like to join the trip - describe how you could help - ask for further practical details

Model answer

Dear Mrs Brown,

I am writing to volunteer for the three-day school trip to Singapore next month.

I would be happy to assist because my daughter is travelling abroad without family for the first time, and I also believe parent volunteers can make the trip safer and less demanding for staff. I have previously accompanied two local day trips and am comfortable supervising groups of teenagers.

During the visit, I could take responsibility for a small group during airport transfers, museum visits and meal breaks. I am trained in first aid and speak conversational Mandarin, which may also be useful if the group needs assistance in public places. In addition, I can help keep attendance records and communicate updates to parents.

Before confirming, could you please clarify the practical arrangements? I would like to know the travel dates, estimated cost, sleeping arrangements and whether volunteers need a background check or additional insurance.

Thank you for considering my offer. I am available to discuss the trip after school any day this week.

Yours sincerely, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

happy to assist
Willing to help.
take responsibility for
Accept the duty of handling.
clarify the practical arrangements
Explain exactly how things will work.
175 words · minimum 150

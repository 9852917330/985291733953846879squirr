Object & Favourite

A TV programme you enjoy

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a TV programme you enjoy

You should say: What it is When you watch it Who you watch it with And explain why you like to watch it.

Simple Model Answer

A television programme I enjoy is The Walking Dead, an American drama about people trying to survive after society has been destroyed by zombies. My flatmate Duc introduced me to it, and we now watch together on Friday nights.

After he returns from work, we usually make instant noodles and put on an episode or two. It has become a regular activity that we both look forward to. I particularly like Andrew Lincoln's performance as a police officer trying to keep his group together. He makes the quieter emotional scenes feel believable.

The make-up is another impressive part. The zombies look frighteningly realistic, and I often wonder how much work each one requires. I have liked this type of story since childhood, so the subject already appealed to me. However, it is the unexpected events and difficult choices that keep me interested. The programme can be tense, but discussing it with Duc makes watching enjoyable. I am always curious to find out what will happen to the characters next.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about The Walking Dead, an American drama series I've recently become completely hooked on. It's set in the world after a zombie apocalypse, where society has collapsed and there's no government left to look after anyone.

I usually watch it late on Friday nights, once my flatmate Duc gets home from work — we've turned it into a bit of a ritual, actually, with a big bowl of instant noodles each and the lights off for the full effect. He got me into it in the first place, and now neither of us can imagine a Friday without an episode or two.

The show has a brilliant cast, but for me, Andrew Lincoln, who plays the leading role of a police officer trying to hold his group together, leaves the deepest impression. His acting feels completely genuine, especially in the quieter scenes where he's just trying to keep everyone calm.

What I find most fascinating, though, is the make-up work on the zombies — every single one goes through an incredibly detailed process to look properly rotten and realistic on screen, and I still don't fully understand how they pull it off.

I've been drawn to zombie stories since I was a kid, if I'm honest, so this show was always going to appeal to me. On top of that, the plot has plenty of unexpected twists, which keeps things genuinely tense. I can't get enough of this show, and I'm always counting down the days until the next season starts.

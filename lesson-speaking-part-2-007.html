Person

A helpful person at work or school

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a helpful person at work or school

You should say: Who it was What this person did How this person helped And explain how you felt after this person helped you.

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

A helpful person I remember is Mrs Linh, my high school English teacher. She taught me for three years and was also responsible for our class. She was in her late forties, but she looked younger.

Before meeting her, I was not very interested in English. However, she made lessons enjoyable by telling us about British and American culture. We also played games to practise vocabulary and grammar, so learning did not feel like simply remembering lists.

She helped me most during the three months before the university entrance exam. I was struggling with some questions, so she invited me to free extra classes after school. She explained difficult points patiently and taught us useful exam techniques. Thanks to her support, I passed with an almost perfect English score. I was so relieved that I called my parents straight away. I now study in Hanoi, but I still visit her during holidays. I will always be grateful for the time and care she gave me.

Key words and phrases

extra classes
Additional lessons outside the normal timetable.
straight away
Immediately, without waiting.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to tell you about one of my teachers from high school, who had a huge influence on me and was honestly the reason I managed to get into university. Her name's Linh, and she taught me English for all three years of high school — she was my form teacher too. She was in her late forties at the time, but she looked young for her age.

I was really influenced by the way she taught. Her thoroughness in teaching is what first got me interested in English, even though I hadn't cared much for the subject before that. We never felt bored in her classes — she'd tell us interesting stories about British and American culture, and she'd organise games so we could learn new vocabulary and grammar effortlessly instead of just memorising word lists.

What I remember most, though, is the three months before the university entrance exam, which was easily the most important exam of my life. She ran free extra classes after school for students who weren't quite up to standard, including me, and she patiently guided us through difficult questions and gave us loads of useful tips for the test. Thanks to her teaching and her dedication, I ended up passing the exam with an almost perfect score in English — I remember calling my parents the second I saw the results, almost too relieved to speak.

I've graduated now and I'm studying in Hanoi, but I still visit her whenever I'm back home for the holidays, and she's always ready to give me advice whenever I need it. I'm forever grateful to have had Mrs Linh in my life.

Key words and phrases

thoroughness in teaching
the care and completeness of the teaching
learn new vocabulary and grammar effortlessly
learn words and grammar with little apparent difficulty
guided us through difficult questions
helped us understand and answer hard questions
forever grateful
always feeling thankful

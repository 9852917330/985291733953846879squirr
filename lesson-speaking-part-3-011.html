Celebrations

Why parties matter, and why some people avoid them

Prompt

What types of parties do people have, and why are parties important? Why do you think some people like parties but others hate them? Do you think parties will become more popular in the future?

What types of parties do people have, and why are parties important?

Simple Model Answer

People hold parties for birthdays, weddings and other important occasions. They bring family and friends together, including people who normally live far apart. They also give everyone a chance to relax and have fun. Without these occasions, people might meet less often. However, large, noisy parties can be tiring for people who prefer quieter gatherings.

Key words and phrases

gatherings
Events where people meet and spend time together.

Advanced Model Answer

People have parties to celebrate special occasions like birthdays, weddings, or the start of a new year. I think it's important to mark these things because they're landmarks in our lives — a wedding, for instance, is really the one time an entire scattered family ends up in the same room. Parties are a good way to bring people together, and they're an opportunity to let off some steam. Without occasions like these, families and old friends would probably drift apart, since everyone's scattered across different cities and nobody would otherwise make the effort. That said, not everyone enjoys them equally — plenty of people find a big, noisy gathering more draining than joyful.

Key words and phrases

bring people together
Help people form closer relationships.
let off some steam
release stress or strong feelings

Why do you think some people like parties but others hate them?

Simple Model Answer

Some people enjoy the food, conversation and dancing. Others feel shy or dislike talking to strangers, so the same party may feel stressful. Size matters too: someone who dislikes a crowded hall may enjoy dinner with six close friends. I think the type of party is as important as whether a person enjoys socialising.

Key words and phrases

socialising
Spending time with people for enjoyment.

Advanced Model Answer

Most people like parties because they have a good time - eating a nice meal, chatting to friends, or having a dance. People who don't like them might find social situations difficult because they're shy, or maybe they don't enjoy having to make small talk with people they don't know. I'd also say a lot depends on the size of the party. Plenty of people who dread a room of two hundred strangers are perfectly happy at a dinner with six friends.

Key words and phrases

make small talk with
have a brief, casual conversation with

Do you think parties will become more popular in the future?

Simple Model Answer

I don't expect a big increase because parties are already common. People will continue celebrating, but gatherings may become smaller and more casual. A meal or barbecue with a few friends costs less and is easier to organise than a huge event. People may prefer meeting regularly instead of hosting one large party each year.

Key words and phrases

casual
Relaxed and not very formal.

Advanced Model Answer

I don't think parties will necessarily become more popular; they've always been a basic form of social life, so there isn't really room for them to grow much further. The format may well change, but people will keep celebrating and socialising together one way or another. If anything, I'd expect gatherings to get smaller and less formal — a barbecue with ten friends rather than a hall booked for two hundred. Big parties are expensive and take a great deal of organising, and younger people I know increasingly prefer seeing a few friends regularly to hosting one enormous event a year.

Key words and phrases

a basic form of social life
a simple way people spend time together
take a great deal of organising
require a lot of planning

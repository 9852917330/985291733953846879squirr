Names

How you were named and what names mean

Prompt

How did your parents choose your name? Does your name have any special meaning? Is your name common or unusual in your country? If you could change your name, would you?

How did your parents choose your name?

Simple Model Answer

I'm not sure because I've never asked my parents. I imagine they chose a name that they both liked and thought would be right for me.

Key words and phrases

imagine
Think something is probably true without knowing for certain.

Advanced Model Answer

To be honest, I'm not sure how they chose it; I've never asked them. I suppose it was just a name that they both liked, and maybe they thought it suited me.

Key words and phrases

To be honest
Introducing a frank personal opinion.

Does your name have any special meaning?

Simple Model Answer

It might have a special meaning, but I haven't checked. I may search for it online later because this question has made me want to find out.

Key words and phrases

find out
Discover or learn some information.

Advanced Model Answer

It might do, but I don't know what that meaning is; I've never looked it up. Maybe I'll google it later today; you've made me curious to find out!

Key words and phrases

looked it up
Searched for the information.
made me curious to find out
Made me want to discover more.

Is your name common or unusual in your country?

Simple Model Answer

It is quite common. When I was at school, there were two other people in my class with the same name, so it wasn't unusual there.

Key words and phrases

common
Happening often or found in many people or places.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, it's quite a common name. I remember that there were two other people with the same name as me in my class at school.

Key words and phrases

same name as me
Sharing my name.

If you could change your name, would you?

Simple Model Answer

No, I wouldn't change it. My name feels like part of who I am, so a different one would seem strange. I don't think my family or friends would like it either.

Key words and phrases

part of who I am
Something closely connected with my identity.

Advanced Model Answer

No, I definitely wouldn't. My name is part of my identity now, and it would feel strange to suddenly change it. I don't think my friends and family would like that either.

Key words and phrases

part of my identity
Something that helps define who I am.

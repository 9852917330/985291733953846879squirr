Art

Art lessons, drawing and galleries

Prompt

Did you enjoy doing art lessons when you were a child? Do you ever draw or paint pictures now? When was the last time you went to an art gallery or exhibition? What kind of pictures do you like having in your home?

Did you enjoy doing art lessons when you were a child?

Simple Model Answer

No, I didn't really enjoy them. I wasn't good at drawing or painting, and the lessons felt slow and boring. I liked language and science classes more.

Key words and phrases

wasn't good at
Did not have much skill in something.

Advanced Model Answer

No I didn't. I was never very good at drawing or painting, and I found art lessons quite slow and boring. I preferred language and science lessons.

Key words and phrases

language and science lessons
Classes about languages and science.

Do you ever draw or paint pictures now?

Simple Model Answer

No, I don't draw or paint now. I don't need to do it for my job, and it is still not a hobby that I enjoy.

Key words and phrases

for my job
As part of the work I do.

Advanced Model Answer

No, I don't draw or paint these days, because there's never any need for me to create art at work, and I still don't enjoy art as a hobby.

Key words and phrases

create art at work
Make artistic things as your job.

When was the last time you went to an art gallery or exhibition?

Simple Model Answer

I can't remember my last visit because I'm not very interested in art. I usually choose a concert, a film or a play instead of an art exhibition.

Key words and phrases

instead of
In place of something else.

Advanced Model Answer

I honestly can't remember the last time I went to any kind of art exhibition; I'm not much of an art fan, and I tend to go to music concerts, the cinema or the theatre instead of art shows.

Key words and phrases

art exhibition
A public display of artworks.
not much of
Not especially good at or interested in.

What kind of pictures do you like having in your home?

Simple Model Answer

I don't have paintings at home, but I do have family photos and pictures of beautiful places. They make the rooms look brighter and more welcoming.

Key words and phrases

more welcoming
Making people feel more comfortable and happy to be there.

Advanced Model Answer

I don't have any paintings or artistic images in my home, but I do have photos of family members and some nice photos of landscapes on the walls to brighten the place up.

Key words and phrases

brighten the place up
Make the space more cheerful.

News & Media

Will newspapers survive online news?

Opinion

Prompt

Although more and more people read news on the Internet, newspapers will remain the most important source of news. Do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

The Internet is beginning to rival newspapers as the best place to find information about what is happening in the world. I believe that this trend will continue, and the Internet will soon be just as important as the traditional press.

On the one hand, newspapers will probably remain a vital source of information even in the Internet age. Not everyone wants to, or is able to, go online instead: older people and those in rural areas may have limited internet access, while plenty of readers with a perfectly good connection still prefer newsprint out of habit. Newspapers also carry a level of trust that many websites lack, since their stories pass through professional journalists and editors before publication, and for many readers there remains something satisfying about holding an actual paper rather than staring at a screen.

However, the Internet is likely to become just as popular as newspapers, largely because it delivers news faster and follows readers everywhere, updating on a phone the moment a story breaks rather than the next morning. Online news is also more interactive: readers can share articles, argue about them in comment sections, and post their own updates on platforms such as Twitter and YouTube, so that the audience becomes part of the reporting rather than a passive recipient of it. Producing news digitally also avoids the paper, ink and delivery lorries that a print run requires, which makes it the less wasteful option environmentally.

In conclusion, I disagree with the view that newspapers will continue to be the main source of news, because I believe that the Internet will soon be equally important.

Key words and phrases

a vital source of information
An essential provider of knowledge.
still prefer newsprint out of habit
Choose printed newspapers because they are familiar.
the audience becomes part of the reporting
Readers or viewers help gather the news.
the less wasteful option environmentally
The choice using fewer environmental resources.

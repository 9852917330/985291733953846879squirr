Something you enjoy doing with an old person in your family

Part 2

Prompt

Describe something you enjoy doing with an old person in your family

You should say: - What it is - How often you do it - Who you do it with - Why you enjoy doing it with this person

Simple Model Answer

Something I enjoy doing with an older family member is watching television with my eighty-year-old grandmother. At the moment, we follow an Indian drama called Eight-Year-Old Bride on Today TV.

We discovered it about four months ago after seeing the first episode by chance. The story follows a girl called Anandi who is forced into marriage when she is only eight. We became interested in her life and have continued watching. The programme begins at eight each evening, so I try to finish other tasks before then.

My grandmother often notices small details that I miss. We discuss the characters' choices and what might happen next. The story has also encouraged serious conversations about the problems caused by marrying too young. However, the most important part for me is our shared routine. It gives us a regular reason to sit together and talk. If the programme ended, I would miss that time more than the story itself. I like an activity that allows two different generations to enjoy something and learn from each other's opinions.

Key words and phrases

shared routine
An activity that people regularly do together.
generations
Groups of people born around the same period, such as grandparents and grandchildren.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to tell you about something I really enjoy doing with my 80-year-old grandmother - watching an Indian soap opera together. The one we're currently hooked on is called Eight-Year-Old Bride, and it's on Today TV every evening.

We first stumbled upon it about four months ago, after catching the very first episode almost by accident. We were both completely drawn in by one of the characters, a girl called Anandi, and we haven't missed a single episode since. The show follows Anandi's life after she's forced into marriage at just eight years old, and everything she goes through afterwards.

It airs every evening at eight, so I always try to arrange my schedule around it so I can watch with my grandma. While we watch, she often points out little details in the story that I'd probably miss completely on my own - she's surprisingly sharp when it comes to picking up on things like that.

Honestly, I've learned a lot from both the show and from her, and I think the biggest lesson has to be that getting married too young can honestly ruin a person's whole life. But more than the story itself, it's really just become our little routine together, and I think that's what I'd miss most if it ever ended.

Key words and phrases

hooked on
Very interested and unable to stop.
stumbled upon
Found unexpectedly.
picking up on things
Noticing or learning small details.
getting married too young
Marrying before being ready for responsibilities.
our little routine
The small habit we share.

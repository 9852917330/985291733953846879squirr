Object & Favourite

A film that you did not enjoy

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a film that you did not enjoy.

You should say: What the film was When you watched it Who you watched it with And explain why you did not enjoy this film

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

A film I did not enjoy was Deadpool. I watched it with my best friend, Linh, about a year ago. We had no plans on a Friday afternoon, saw a poster outside a cinema and decided to buy tickets.

The story follows a man who receives unusual healing powers after medical experiments. I expected an exciting superhero film, but there was much more violence than I wanted. The fighting and blood made me uncomfortable, and I did not enjoy many of the jokes.

Linh found the film very funny, while I developed a headache and considered leaving early. I stayed because she wanted me to give it a chance. I understand that many people like this kind of humour and action, but it did not suit my taste. The experience reminded me that friends can enjoy very different things. Next time, I would check a film's style more carefully before choosing it. Linh has suggested watching the next film, but I think I would prefer a different cinema trip.

Key words and phrases

give it a chance
Try something properly before deciding whether you like it.
suit my taste
Match the kind of thing I personally enjoy.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to tell you about the worst film I've ever sat through — Deadpool. I watched it about a year ago with my best friend, Linh, on a lazy Friday afternoon when neither of us had any plans.

We'd been wandering around town with nothing to do, and then we spotted the poster outside the cinema and thought, why not. Fifteen minutes later we were in our seats with a box of popcorn, completely unprepared for what we were about to watch. Looking back, it was one of the worst decisions I've made in ages.

The plot follows a man whose body is experimented on to try to cure his cancer, and it ends up giving him superhuman healing powers, but the film itself is packed with over-the-top violence — constant fighting, blood everywhere, and jokes I found more crude than funny. While Linh was practically crying with laughter next to me, I ended up with a splitting headache and was seriously tempted to walk out about a third of the way through. I only stayed because she kept nudging me to give it a chance.

I know plenty of people, especially younger viewers, absolutely love it, and it clearly did well at the box office. It's just not my kind of humour, and I couldn't get past how casually cruel it was, even toward the villains. Linh's already asked if I want to see the sequel with her, but honestly, once was more than enough for me.

Key words and phrases

nothing to do
No activity available to do.
over-the-top violence
Unrealistically extreme violent scenes.
splitting headache
A very painful headache.
give it a chance
Try it before judging it.
not my kind of humour
I do not enjoy that style of comedy.
once was more than enough
I did not want to repeat the experience.

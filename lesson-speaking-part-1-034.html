Games

Games children and adults play

Part 1

Prompt

Do you enjoy playing games? What kind of games do children play in your country? Do you think adults should play games? What kinds of games were popular when you were younger?

Do you enjoy playing games?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I especially enjoy strategy games and word games. They are fun, but I also have to think carefully and pay attention, which gives my mind some exercise.

Key words and phrases

pay attention
Listen, watch or think carefully about something.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, particularly strategy and word games. They're entertaining but also require concentration, so they provide a healthy mental challenge.

Key words and phrases

a healthy mental challenge
A beneficial task that requires thinking.

What kind of games do children play in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Children play phone games, football, hide-and-seek and games at school. Phone games are common now, but active games still help children learn to move well and play with others.

Key words and phrases

active games
Games that involve moving your body.

Advanced Model Answer

Children play mobile games, football, hide-and-seek and a variety of classroom games. Digital games are more visible now, but physical games remain important for social and motor skills.

Key words and phrases

social and motor skills
Abilities to interact and control body movements.

Do you think adults should play games?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, as long as games leave enough time for work, exercise and relationships. They can help adults relax and keep their minds busy, so they are not just for children.

Key words and phrases

as long as
On the condition that something is true.

Advanced Model Answer

Certainly, provided gaming doesn't replace work, exercise or relationships. Games can reduce stress and keep the mind active, so they're not only for children.

Key words and phrases

keep the mind active
Continue using your thinking abilities.
only for children
Intended exclusively for young people.

What kinds of games were popular when you were younger?

Simple Model Answer

Outdoor games and simple board games were popular when I was younger. We had fewer electronic devices, so we used our imagination more and played with other children in person.

Key words and phrases

electronic devices
Equipment such as phones, tablets and computers.

Advanced Model Answer

When I was younger, outdoor games and simple board games were common. Children had fewer devices, so entertainment depended more on imagination and face-to-face interaction.

Key words and phrases

face-to-face interaction
Communicating in the same physical place.

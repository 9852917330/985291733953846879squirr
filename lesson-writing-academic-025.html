Crime & Safety

US violent crime

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The line chart below shows serious violent crime levels from 1978 to 2000 in the US. Serious violent crimes include rape, robbery, aggravated assault and homicide. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The line chart compares four measures of serious violent crime in the United States between 1978 and 2000: the total level, victimisations reported to the police, crimes recorded by the police and arrests. The vertical axis expresses the figures in thousands of offences.

Overall, the total crime level remained the highest series throughout. It rose to a peak in the late 1980s before declining, whereas police-recorded offences and arrests generally increased until the 1990s and then fell towards the end.

The total stood at approximately 3.5 thousand offences in 1978. After an initial fall to just over 3 thousand in 1980, it climbed to around 4.5 thousand in 1988. A downward trend followed, bringing the figure to about 3 thousand in 2000. Reported victimisations fluctuated at a lower level, generally around 2 to 2.5 thousand in the earlier years, before falling to roughly 1.4 thousand by the end.

Police-recorded crime increased from about 0.8 thousand in 1978 to a peak of approximately 1.8 thousand in 1994, then declined to around 1.5 thousand. Arrests were consistently the lowest measure, rising from roughly 0.3 thousand to about 0.9 thousand in 1996 before ending near 0.7 thousand.

Key words and phrases

A downward trend
A general pattern of decreasing values.
a peak of
The highest level reached by a figure.

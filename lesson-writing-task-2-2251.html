Environment

Food and drink in plastic

Advantages outweigh disadvantages

Prompt

In many countries today, more and more food and drink is sold in plastic containers. Do you think the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?

Model answer

Almost everything in a modern supermarket arrives wrapped in plastic. The material solves genuine problems, but in my view its disadvantages now outweigh its benefits.

The advantages are worth stating fairly. Plastic is light, cheap and airtight, so a sealed package keeps meat and salad edible for days longer than an open one, which reduces the amount of food thrown away. Because it weighs so little, transporting goods in plastic burns far less fuel than transporting them in glass, and sealed containers protect food from contamination between the factory and the shelf. In countries without reliable refrigeration, this prevents a great deal of illness.

The difficulty is what happens afterwards. Food packaging is used for minutes and lasts for centuries, and most of it is not recycled: it is thin, often made of mixed layers that cannot be separated, and contaminated by the food it held. What is collected is frequently exported to countries with weaker regulation, where it is burned or dumped. The result is visible on coastlines and invisible in the food chain, since plastic breaks into fragments that enter fish and eventually people.

The cost also falls in the wrong place. Manufacturers gain the savings while councils and taxpayers pay for collection and disposal, so there is no pressure on producers to use less.

In conclusion, plastic packaging genuinely reduces food waste and illness, and simply banning it would create new problems. Its disadvantages nonetheless outweigh those gains, because the damage is permanent while the benefit lasts a few days, and this will only change when producers are made responsible for what happens to the packaging they create.

prevents a great deal of illness
Stops many people becoming sick.
enter fish and eventually people

no pressure on producers to use less
Manufacturers have no reason to reduce use.
what happens to the packaging they create

The Little Prince

Core Story

Prompt

Describe a book that you have read and enjoyed.

You should say: - what the book was - when and where you read it - what it was about - and explain why you enjoyed reading it.

Simple Model Answer

A book I enjoyed is The Little Prince by Antoine de Saint-Exupery. It follows a young prince travelling between tiny planets and meeting unusual adults. At first, I thought it was mainly for children because it is short, has simple language and includes pictures.

However, the conversations explore ideas that adults recognise too. They are about friendship, loneliness, responsibility and becoming too interested in status or numbers. Instead of giving long explanations, the writer puts these ideas into short, memorable scenes.

I like the book because different readers can enjoy it in different ways. A child can follow the adventure, while an adult may think about the criticism of adult behaviour. I also enjoy reading it again because different lines feel important at different times in my life. Although it looks simple, it gives me a lot to consider. It is imaginative, easy to follow and more thoughtful than I expected.

Key words and phrases

responsibility
A duty to care for something or act properly.

Advanced Model Answer

A book I have read and genuinely enjoyed is The Little Prince by Antoine de Saint-Exupery.

On the surface, it is a short story about a young prince who travels from one tiny planet to another and meets a series of strange adults. The language is simple and the illustrations make it look like a children's book, which is partly why I underestimated it at first.

What makes the book interesting is that many of the conversations are really about adult behaviour. It touches on loneliness, friendship, responsibility and the way people can become obsessed with status or numbers. The ideas are not presented as long arguments; they appear in small scenes that are easy to remember.

I enjoyed it because the book works on two levels. A child can follow the story, while an adult can recognise the criticism underneath it. I also like books that can be reread without feeling repetitive, and this is one of them. Different lines stand out depending on what is happening in your own life. It is short, imaginative and much more thoughtful than its simple appearance suggests.

Activity

A plan

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a plan

You should say: What the plan is/was When you thought of the plan Who is involved in the plan And say how you did/think you will achieve the plan

Simple Model Answer

A plan I am excited about is travelling to Singapore with three close friends. I turned eighteen this year, and the idea came after we finished our university entrance exams in June. We felt we deserved a break after many years of school.

We chose Singapore because it seems clean, modern and convenient to reach from Vietnam. However, we still need our parents to feel comfortable with the idea. To make the trip easier to approve, we are looking for an organised tour instead of arranging every part ourselves.

Price is important, so we need an affordable package and reasonably priced flights. Booking early should also give us time to prepare properly. Once there, we would follow the guide and behave responsibly rather than treating the holiday as complete freedom from every rule. I know a tour may offer less flexibility, but it seems a sensible choice for our first trip as young adults. I hope the plan works because it would be both a reward for studying and a chance to share a new experience with friends.

Key words and phrases

affordable package
A group of travel services sold together at a price someone can manage.
flexibility
The freedom to change plans or choose different options.

Advanced Model Answer

I just turned eighteen this year, so I've been thinking it's about time I planned something a bit special. There are loads of things I could do, but the plan I'm most excited about right now is taking a trip to Singapore.

I came up with the idea right after finishing my university entrance exams back in June, and three of my closest friends are keen to come along too. After twelve years of nonstop studying, we all felt that taking a break is really essential. We settled on Singapore because it's such a clean, modern city, and it's also just a short flight from Vietnam.

The tricky part is that we're technically only just adults, so it might take a bit of convincing to get our parents on board. First, we've decided to book an organised tour rather than travel independently, since that'll put our parents' minds at ease - even if it does mean a bit of a lack of spontaneous activities along the way. Price matters too: if the tour's too expensive, I doubt our parents will agree to it at all, so finding something affordable is a must.

On top of that, we'll need to book our plane tickets in advance, both to get a decent price and to make sure everything's properly sorted before we go. Once we're actually there, I think we'll just try to be sensible tourists - listen to the tour guide, stay out of trouble, that sort of thing. So that's the plan, really - our reward for twelve years of hard work.

Key words and phrases

keen to come along
Excited to join the activity.
taking a break is really essential
Rest is very important.
put our parents' minds at ease
Help our parents stop worrying.
lack of spontaneous activities
Too few activities that happen without planning.
book our plane tickets in advance
Reserve plane seats before the travel date.
stay out of trouble
Avoid dangerous or harmful situations.

An outdoor meal or picnic that you had

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an outdoor meal or picnic that you had

You should say: - Where it took place - What was the occasion - What you ate and drank there And explain whether you enjoyed it or not

Simple Model Answer

An outdoor meal I enjoyed was a picnic beside West Lake in Hanoi last autumn. There were four of us, all university friends who had recently started full-time jobs. We were seeing less of each other, so we chose a Sunday to catch up.

Each person brought something homemade. I prepared fried rice with sausage, while the others brought spring rolls, fruit and iced lotus tea. We spread a mat on the grass near the water and shared everything. We stayed for most of the afternoon, eating, playing cards and taking short walks along the lake.

At one point, a stray dog came over and stayed near us for almost an hour. We found this unexpectedly funny. Most of the time, nobody checked their phone. I enjoyed the picnic more than many restaurant meals because we had enough time to talk without rushing. It cost little, but it helped us feel close again. We agreed to repeat it before the weather became cold. The afternoon showed me that good food and good company do not need an expensive setting.

Advanced Model Answer

I'll talk about a picnic four of us had by West Lake in Hanoi back in early autumn last year. There wasn't really a special occasion behind it — we're all old university friends who'd just started full-time jobs, and once term ended we hardly saw each other, so one of us suggested we set aside a whole Sunday just to sit by the lake and catch up properly.

We didn't buy anything ready-made. Each of us brought something we'd cooked ourselves — I made a big batch of fried rice with sausage, one friend brought spring rolls, another turned up with a huge bag of fresh fruit, and someone else made this incredibly sweet iced lotus tea in a flask. We spread an old picnic mat out on the grass near the water and laid everything out in the middle.

We ended up staying there for almost the whole afternoon, eating, playing cards, and occasionally getting up to walk along the path by the lake. At one point a stray dog wandered over and practically adopted us for an hour, which had all of us in stitches. Barely anyone checked their phone, which almost never happens these days.

I really did enjoy it, probably more than any restaurant meal I've had this year. It wasn't fancy or expensive, but it reminded me how easy it is to have a genuinely good time as long as you're with the right people. We've already agreed to make it a regular thing before the weather turns cold.

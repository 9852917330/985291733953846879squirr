Friendship

The qualities that matter in a friend

Prompt

What do you think are the most important qualities for friends to have? How important do you think it is for a person to spend some time alone?

What do you think are the most important qualities for friends to have?

Simple Model Answer

Honesty, reliability and respect matter most. Similar interests make a friendship enjoyable, but trust helps it last. A close friend listens and supports you when things are difficult, not just when life is fun. However, lighter friendships still have value. Not every person we enjoy spending time with needs to become someone we rely on in a crisis.

Key words and phrases

reliability
The quality of being trustworthy and dependable.

Advanced Model Answer

The most important qualities are honesty, reliability and mutual respect. Shared interests make a friendship enjoyable, but trust is what makes it durable. A close friend should be able to listen, tell the truth tactfully and remain dependable when circumstances become difficult. That last part is really the test: plenty of people are good company while everything's going well, but a genuine friend is the one who still answers the phone when you're the one with the problem. That doesn't mean every friendship needs to survive a crisis to count, though — a lighter, purely social friendship is still perfectly valid, just a different kind of relationship.

Key words and phrases

tell the truth tactfully
be honest while avoiding unnecessary hurt
a lighter, purely social friendship
a less close friendship based mainly on enjoying company

How important do you think it is for a person to spend some time alone?

Simple Model Answer

Some time alone helps people rest, understand their experiences and think clearly. A quiet walk or coffee may be enough. However, being alone too much can become unhealthy isolation. People also need different amounts: some recover energy with friends, while others need silence. I think the right balance matters more than expecting everyone to prefer the same thing.

Key words and phrases

isolation
Being separated from other people or having very little contact.

Advanced Model Answer

Spending some time alone is essential because it allows people to process experiences and recover from constant social demands. Even a short walk or quiet coffee can create space for reflection. Solitude becomes unhealthy only when it turns into persistent isolation. I'd add that people differ enormously in how much they need: some recover their energy in company and find being alone draining, whereas others can't think clearly until the room's quiet. Neither one is a flaw; the mistake is assuming everyone works the same way.

Key words and phrases

constant social demands
continual pressure to meet and speak with others
persistent isolation
being alone and disconnected for a long time
recover their energy in company
feel refreshed after spending time with others

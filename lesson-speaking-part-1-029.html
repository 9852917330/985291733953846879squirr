Food

Favourite foods and eating out

Part 1

Prompt

What are your most/ least favourite foods? If you invited a friend over for a meal, what would you serve? Do you prefer eating at home or in a restaurant and why? What special dish from your country would you recommend to a visitor?

What are your most/ least favourite foods?

Simple Model Answer

I like fresh Vietnamese food with vegetables, herbs and light soup. I dislike very oily or very sweet food because it makes me feel uncomfortable and tired afterwards.

Key words and phrases

oily
Containing a lot of oil or fat.

Advanced Model Answer

I particularly enjoy fresh Vietnamese dishes with herbs, vegetables and light broths. I'm least fond of food that's extremely greasy or sweet because it leaves me feeling heavy and tired.

Key words and phrases

particularly enjoy
Like especially much.
heavy and tired
Feeling physically sluggish and lacking energy.

If you invited a friend over for a meal, what would you serve?

Simple Model Answer

I'd make grilled chicken with rice, vegetables and a light soup. It is easy to prepare for a group, and I can change parts of the meal to suit what people eat.

Key words and phrases

suit
Be right for someone's needs or preferences.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd probably serve grilled chicken, rice, vegetables and a light soup. It's easy to prepare for several people and can be adapted to different dietary preferences.

Key words and phrases

different dietary preferences
Different choices about what to eat.

Do you prefer eating at home or in a restaurant and why?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer eating at home most days because it costs less and I can choose healthier ingredients. I still enjoy restaurants for special events, but I like having control over my everyday meals.

Key words and phrases

having control over
Being able to decide how something is done.

Advanced Model Answer

I prefer eating at home on ordinary days because it's healthier and less expensive. Restaurants are enjoyable for special occasions, but home cooking gives me more control over ingredients.

Key words and phrases

more control over ingredients
More ability to choose the food's contents.

What special dish from your country would you recommend to a visitor?

Simple Model Answer

I'd suggest Vietnamese noodle soup. Visitors can find it easily, and it is an important part of our food culture. The clear soup, herbs and rice noodles taste good together.

Key words and phrases

food culture
The foods and eating traditions connected with a group of people.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd recommend Vietnamese noodle soup because it's widely available yet closely associated with Vietnamese cuisine. Its clear broth, herbs and rice noodles create a balanced combination of flavours.

Key words and phrases

widely available
Easy to find in many places.
closely associated with
Strongly linked with.
a balanced combination of flavours
Different tastes that work well together.

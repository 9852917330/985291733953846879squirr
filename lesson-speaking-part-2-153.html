Situation

A change that you would like to see in your local area

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a change that you would like to see in your local area.

You should say: - what the change would be - who would be involved in making the change - how it would be done - and why you would like to see the change happen.

Simple Model Answer

A change I would like in my local area is making the main shopping street pedestrian-only. At present, many vehicles pass through the town centre even when their drivers are not stopping there. This creates noise, pollution and traffic for local people.

I would like the council to move this traffic onto another route. It might require a bypass or changes to nearby roads, so careful planning would be necessary. Residents could begin by discussing the problem and asking the council to consider possible designs.

If the street were closed to normal traffic, it could become safer and more pleasant for shopping. Cafes might have outdoor tables, and families could walk without constantly watching for cars. I think local shops could benefit too, because people might stay longer in the area. I understand that deliveries and access would need to be considered, but the idea seems worth exploring. A town centre should be a place people enjoy using, rather than simply a road they have to cross quickly.

Key words and phrases

pedestrian-only
Reserved for people walking rather than normal vehicle traffic.
bypass
A road taking traffic around a town or busy area.

Advanced Model Answer

The change that I would like to see in my local neighbourhood is the pedestrianisation of the main shopping street in the town centre. I would like to see traffic diverted around this area in order to make it safer and more attractive for visitors and shoppers. Most of the traffic that clogs up the main street is just passing through the town, but it's the local people who have to put up with the congestion, noise and pollution. It's the job of the local council to make changes like the one I am suggesting.

But before the council does anything, there usually needs to be some pressure from local people to bring a problem to light. So, perhaps we need a campaign by local residents who want to see this change. Then the council might contract a town planner or an architect to come up with some possible designs for a new road system. I'm not sure exactly how the process of creating a pedestrian street would work, but I imagine it would require detailed planning.

Perhaps a bypass would need to be built around the town centre first. This would give drivers a faster alternative to driving through the town, and it would allow the main shopping street to be blocked off and pedestrianised. I would like to see this change because it would really improve the quality of life of residents in the town where I live. I can imagine the new pedestrian street having cafes and restaurants with outdoor seating.

It would be a safer and more pleasant place for families to go shopping, and I'm sure this would have a positive knock-on effect on local shops and businesses.

Key words and phrases

put up with the congestion
accept and deal with crowded traffic
bring a problem to light

the quality of life

a positive knock-on effect
an indirect beneficial result

Object & Favourite

A piece of good news you heard

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a piece of good news you heard

You should say: what this news was when and where you heard it how you heard it And explain why you think it was good news

Simple Model Answer

A piece of good news I heard was receiving a full scholarship for an exchange programme in Japan about three years ago. I was a second-year university student and had applied for a two-month cultural programme for students from Southeast Asia.

My application was accepted, and I was also considered for financial support. About two months later, I was checking email in the university library between classes. A message from the scholarship committee said I had been chosen and that my fees and living costs would be covered.

I was so surprised that I made a noise in the quiet library. Being selected from many applicants made me feel proud. The news also brought relief because paying for the trip would have been difficult for my parents. I could now enjoy the programme without adding that pressure to the family. I still keep the email as a reminder. It represents both an opportunity to experience another culture and a moment when my effort created a result better than I had expected.

Key words and phrases

full scholarship
Financial support that pays all the costs included in a study award.
living costs
Money needed for everyday needs such as food and accommodation.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about the moment I got official confirmation of a scholarship to join an exchange programme in Japan, about three years ago now. It feels like a while back, but I can still remember that day really clearly.

At the time I was a second-year student at university, and one day I came across an advert for a two-month cultural exchange programme in Japan for students from Southeast Asia. I was really keen to apply, and luckily my application got accepted — I was even nominated for a scholarship on top of that.

About two months later, I was sitting in the university library, half-heartedly checking my email between classes, when I saw a message from the Scholarship Committee saying I'd been chosen for a full scholarship that would cover all my fees and living costs for the whole programme. My heart skipped a beat the moment I saw the word 'Congratulations', and I actually gasped out loud right there in the library. I was so proud of myself for being picked out of hundreds of other candidates.

It was honestly the best news I got that whole year. If I hadn't won it, covering my living costs would've been a serious financial burden for my parents, but thanks to that scholarship, I got to enjoy my time in Japan without worrying about money at all. I still keep this lucky email sitting in my inbox to this day, just to remind me of that moment.

Key words and phrases

official confirmation
An authorised statement proving something is true.
cultural exchange programme
An organised opportunity to experience another culture.
My heart skipped a beat
I felt sudden strong excitement or surprise.
gasped out loud
Made a surprised sound suddenly.
proud of myself
Pleased with my own achievement.
financial burden
A financial responsibility that is hard to manage.
keep this lucky email
Save this important message as a keepsake.

Place

An interesting house or apartment that you have visited

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an interesting house or apartment that you have visited.

You should say: - where the house or apartment was - who lived there - what it looked like on the outside and/or inside - and explain why you found this house or apartment interesting.

Simple Model Answer

An interesting flat I visited was on the tenth floor of a new building in central Manchester. I went with a friend who was considering moving there. Nobody lived in it yet because it had only recently been offered for sale.

The building looked modern from outside, with lots of glass. Inside, the flat had pale walls, light tiles, silver kitchen equipment and dark grey work surfaces. A huge window covered most of the living-room wall, allowing plenty of sunlight inside. The kitchen and living room shared one open space.

What interested me most was the technology. A phone app could control the lights, heating, blinds and even some kitchen equipment. There were also connected security cameras. Although the white and silver decoration was not exactly my style, I liked how bright, clean and spacious the flat felt. Seeing so many things controlled through one app was new to me. The visit made me curious about how technology might change ordinary homes in the future.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe an apartment that I recently visited in the city centre of Manchester. The apartment was on the 10th floor of a brand new block that had just been built. This building's on the edge of the main commercial area of the city, and the apartment overlooks several of Manchester's busiest shopping streets. Nobody was living in the apartment when I went there, because it had only just been put on the market.

I went to see it with a friend who's thinking of moving to Manchester; he wanted to view a few different properties, and I decided to go along and see what the newest apartments were like. From the outside, it was a tall, shiny glass building — nothing too remarkable. On the inside, as far as I remember, there weren't many different colours: the walls were an off-white colour, the floors were done in whitish tiles, and the kitchen appliances were all silver metal coloured. I think the kitchen surfaces were made from dark grey granite.

The main feature, in terms of the look of the apartment, was the huge floor-to-ceiling window in the living room, which let the sunlight flood in. Although the white and silver decor wasn't really to my taste, I did like the fact that the apartment seemed so cool, clean and spacious. I particularly liked the open-plan kitchen and living room, which had all sorts of gadgets you could operate through a mobile phone app. For example, I learned how to use the app to control the lighting, heating, blinds, security cameras, even the cooker.

I found it fascinating that the apartment contained so much up-to-the-minute technology, and that everything seemed to be connected via wifi. It felt like I was getting a glimpse into the future.

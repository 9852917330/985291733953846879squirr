A Power Cut in a Mountain Cabin

Core Story

Prompt

Describe an unusual event you experienced.

You should say: - when and where it happened - what happened - how you reacted - and explain why the event was memorable.

OUTLINE

>>> A Power Cut in a Mountain Cabin
unusual event • storm • sudden power cut • mountain cabin
weekend escape • entire valley dark • no TV • weak phone signal
candles • camping stove • hot drinks • childhood stories
devices off • more conversation • unexpectedly warm atmosphere
inconvenient but enjoyable • dependence on electricity • unexpected connection

Simple Model Answer

An unusual event happened during a weekend with friends in a small mountain cabin. We had rented it for a peaceful break from the city. Late one evening, a storm arrived and the electricity suddenly went off. We expected a short interruption, but the whole valley stayed dark.

Without lights, television or a reliable phone signal, we had to find other things to do. We found two candles and made hot drinks using a camping stove. Then we sat near the window listening to the rain. Someone began telling funny childhood stories, and soon we were laughing together.

The power returned around midnight, but nobody hurried to use their devices. Although the darkness had felt a little frightening, it had also created a lovely evening. I remember it because it showed how much we rely on electricity, but also how enjoyable time together can be without our normal entertainment.

Key words and phrases

went off
Stopped working or supplying power.
rely on
Need or depend on something.

Advanced Model Answer

One unusual event I remember clearly was a sudden power cut during a weekend stay in a small mountain cabin.

A few friends and I had rented the cabin because we wanted a quiet break from the city. Late in the evening, a storm arrived and the electricity disappeared without warning. At first we assumed it would return in a few minutes, but the entire valley went dark.

What made the experience memorable was how quickly the atmosphere changed. Without lights, television or reliable phone signal, we had to improvise. We found two candles, used a camping stove to make hot drinks and sat near the window listening to the rain. Someone started telling ridiculous childhood stories, and before long we were laughing far more than we had when all our devices were working.

The power came back around midnight, but by then nobody rushed to turn anything on. The event reminded me how dependent modern life is on electricity, yet it also showed me that losing convenience can occasionally create a better kind of evening. It was inconvenient, slightly eerie and unexpectedly enjoyable at the same time.

Key words and phrases

without warning
Suddenly, with no advance notice.
had to improvise
Needed to invent a solution immediately.
before long
After a short time.
dependent modern life is on
How much today's life relies on something.
unexpectedly enjoyable
More pleasant than expected.

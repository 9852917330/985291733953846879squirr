Travel & Holidays

Travelling alone or with friends

Part 1

Prompt

Do you like travelling? Why? Do you like to travel alone or with your friends? Which other places have you travelled to? What do you usually do when you first arrive in a new place?

Do you like travelling? Why?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, travelling gives me a break from normal life and helps me see how other people live. Even a short trip can teach me something if I pay attention to the place.

Key words and phrases

pay attention to
Notice and think carefully about something.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, because it's a break from my usual routine and it exposes me to different ways of living. Even a short trip can broaden my horizons if I pay attention to the place rather than simply taking photographs.

Key words and phrases

broaden my horizons
Expand my knowledge and experience.
pay attention to
Notice and think carefully about.

Do you like to travel alone or with your friends?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer travelling with one or two friends I get along with. It feels safer and more enjoyable, while a small group still lets us change our plans fairly easily.

Key words and phrases

get along with
Have a friendly and comfortable relationship with someone.

Advanced Model Answer

I prefer travelling with one or two people I get on with well. It's safer and more enjoyable that way, and a small group still leaves room for personal flexibility.

Key words and phrases

personal flexibility
Freedom to change my own plans.

Which other places have you travelled to?

Simple Model Answer

I've visited several places in Vietnam, including Hue, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City. Hue stands out because of its historic places and more relaxed way of life.

Key words and phrases

stands out
Is especially noticeable or memorable compared with other things.

Advanced Model Answer

I've travelled to several places in Vietnam, including Hue, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City. Each trip was different, but Hue was especially memorable because of its historic sites and slower pace of life.

Key words and phrases

pace of life
The speed of everyday activity.

What do you usually do when you first arrive in a new place?

Simple Model Answer

I usually walk around the area, find out how the transport works and look for useful places nearby. Doing that helps me feel comfortable for the rest of my stay.

Key words and phrases

find out
Learn information you did not know before.

Advanced Model Answer

I usually check the neighbourhood on foot, learn the basic transport routes and identify a few practical places nearby — it doesn't take long, and it helps me feel at ease for the rest of the trip.

Key words and phrases

feel at ease
Feel relaxed and comfortable.

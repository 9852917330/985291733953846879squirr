Environment

Raising fuel prices to protect the environment

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

It is said that the best way to solve the world's environmental problems is to increase the price of fuel. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

It has been suggested that raising the price of fuel is the most effective way to tackle the world's environmental problems. I agree that higher fuel prices would reduce emissions, but I disagree that this measure alone could solve problems of such variety and scale.

The argument for it is straightforward. Burning petrol, diesel and coal is the largest single source of the greenhouse gases that cause climate change, and price is the most reliable influence on behaviour. When fuel becomes expensive, people drive less, use public transport, buy smaller cars and insulate their homes, while manufacturers redesign products to use less energy. Countries with high fuel taxes, such as those in northern Europe, consistently record lower emissions per person than countries where fuel is cheap.

There are, however, two serious objections. The first is fairness. Fuel costs represent a much larger share of a poor family's income than of a wealthy one's, and a rural worker with no bus service has no realistic alternative to driving. A price rise would therefore punish those least able to change their behaviour, unless the money raised were returned to them.

The second is that fuel is only one part of the problem. Deforestation, intensive agriculture, plastic waste, overfishing and habitat loss would continue regardless of what petrol costs, and these require regulation and international agreement rather than pricing.

In conclusion, higher fuel prices are a useful and probably necessary tool, and they should be introduced gradually so that households can adjust. I disagree, however, that they represent the best or only solution to problems of this range and complexity.

Key words and phrases

the most reliable influence on behaviour
The factor most consistently changing actions.
has no realistic alternative
Has no other workable choice.
punish those least able to change their behaviour
Penalise people with the fewest options.
the best or only solution
The strongest or sole possible answer.

Sport & Leisure

Hosting international sporting events

Discuss both views + opinion

Prompt

Some people think hosting major international sporting events brings a lot of advantages to a country, while others believe there are more disadvantages. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

Countries compete fiercely for the right to stage events such as the Olympic Games or the World Cup, yet many host cities are still paying for them a decade later. I believe such events can be worthwhile, but only for countries that already need the infrastructure they require.

Supporters point to benefits beyond the sport. Hosting forces a government to complete transport and housing projects that might otherwise be delayed for years, as Barcelona did in 1992 when the Olympics were used to reconnect the city to its own coastline. The weeks of global coverage also work as advertising that no tourist board could afford, and success by home athletes tends to raise participation in sport among children who watch it.

Critics answer that the costs are consistently understated. Bids are approved on the basis of optimistic budgets, and the final figure is routinely several times the original estimate, with the difference met from taxation. The stadiums themselves are the clearest evidence: purpose-built venues in cities without a team large enough to fill them stand empty afterwards while still costing money to maintain. Poorer residents may also be moved out of areas earmarked for development.

My own view is that the decisive question is what the country would have built anyway. Where a host uses the deadline to deliver a metro line or housing that residents will use for fifty years, the expense is justified. Where it builds arenas that serve no purpose once the visitors leave, the money would have been better spent elsewhere.

In conclusion, hosting is neither automatically beneficial nor automatically wasteful. It repays a country that plans for the years after the event, and punishes one that plans only for the fortnight itself.

Key words and phrases

raise participation in sport
Encourage more people to play sports.
the costs are consistently understated
Expenses are repeatedly presented as too low.
routinely several times the original estimate
Usually costs many times more than predicted.
moved out of areas earmarked for development
Forced to leave places chosen for construction.
what the country would have built anyway

the fortnight itself

Friends

The age of your friends and when you see them

Prompt

Are your friends mostly your age or different ages? Do you usually see your friends during the week or at weekends? The last time you saw your friends, what did you do together? In what ways are your friends important to you?

Are your friends mostly your age or different ages?

Simple Model Answer

Most are around my age because we met at school or university. I also have one or two older or younger friends whom I met through work.

Key words and phrases

around my age
About as old as I am.

Advanced Model Answer

Most of my friends are about the same age as me because we met at school or university. I've got one or two friends who are older or younger that I met through work.

Key words and phrases

about the same age
Of a similar age.
met through work
Became acquainted because of a job.

Do you usually see your friends during the week or at weekends?

Simple Model Answer

I usually see my friends at weekends. We are all busy with work or other things during the week, so it is easier to find time then.

Key words and phrases

at weekends
On Saturdays and Sundays.

Advanced Model Answer

I tend to meet up with my friends at weekends because everyone's too busy during the week.

Key words and phrases

meet up with my friends
Get together with friends.

The last time you saw your friends, what did you do together?

Simple Model Answer

We went out for dinner last weekend because it was a friend's birthday. There were six of us, and we had a meal together to celebrate.

Key words and phrases

celebrate
Do something enjoyable to mark a special occasion.

Advanced Model Answer

It was one of my friends' birthday last weekend. Six of us went out for a meal to celebrate.

Key words and phrases

out for a meal
Eating at a restaurant or elsewhere.

In what ways are your friends important to you?

Simple Model Answer

My friends give me people to talk to and share experiences with. They make me laugh, and I know they will help me when I need support.

Key words and phrases

support
Help or encouragement when you need it.

Advanced Model Answer

I think it's important to have friends that you can talk to and share experiences with. My friends make me laugh, but I know I can also rely on them whenever I need help or support.

Key words and phrases

make me laugh
Cause me to find something funny.

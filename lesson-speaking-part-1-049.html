Mathematics

Studying mathematics and whether everyone needs it

Prompt

At what age did you start studying mathematics? Do you like mathematics? Is it necessary for everyone to learn mathematics? Do you prefer to use a calculator when doing mathematics?

At what age did you start studying mathematics?

Simple Model Answer

I don't remember exactly, but I probably started at primary school when I was five. At that age, I think we learned simple things such as adding numbers together.

Key words and phrases

adding
Putting numbers together to find a total.

Advanced Model Answer

I can't exactly remember, but it was probably in my first year at primary school when I was 5 years old. I probably learned simple addition at that age.

Key words and phrases

simple addition
Adding small or straightforward numbers.

Do you like mathematics?

Simple Model Answer

I don't mind basic maths, but it has never been my favourite subject. I can do simple calculations, while more difficult maths is hard for me to understand.

Key words and phrases

calculations
Ways of using numbers to find an answer.

Advanced Model Answer

I don't dislike maths, but I wouldn't say that I've ever really liked the subject either. I'm happy to do simple calculations, but my brain can't cope with the complex stuff!

Key words and phrases

simple calculations
Basic work with numbers.

Is it necessary for everyone to learn mathematics?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, everyone needs some basic maths. We use it in ordinary life, for example when we manage money, check a bill or work out how much something costs.

Key words and phrases

work out
Find an answer by thinking or calculating.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I think it is. We all need a basic grounding in maths so that we can do daily tasks, like managing our money, working out bills, and so on.

Key words and phrases

a basic grounding in maths
An understanding of fundamental maths.
managing our money
Planning how to use our money.

Do you prefer to use a calculator when doing mathematics?

Simple Model Answer

For easy sums, I like doing them in my head because it gives my brain some practice. If a calculation is more difficult, I use the calculator on my phone.

Key words and phrases

in my head
By thinking, without writing or using a device.

Advanced Model Answer

It depends. I quite like exercising my brain with anything that's easy enough, but I use the calculator on my phone for anything tricky.

Key words and phrases

exercising my brain
Challenging my mind to keep it active.

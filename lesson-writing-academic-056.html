Processes & Cycles

Honey bee life cycle

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The diagram below shows the life cycle of the honey bee. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The diagram illustrates the stages in the life of honey bees. Overall, the cycle shown comprises six stages from egg to full maturity, and takes approximately five weeks (34 to 36 days to be exact) to complete.

The first stage is when the female bee lays up to a couple of oval eggs once every 72 hours. These eggs hatch between nine and ten days later, and the immature forms labelled nymphs in the diagram emerge.

Over the next 3 weeks the nymphs go through three moulting stages, shedding their skins to allow further growth to occur: the first moulting happens 5 days after the eggs hatch, and the second one takes place a week later. Nine days after that, the nymphs moult for a third time and young adults emerge. These take four more days to reach full maturity, and the cycle starts over again. Of the individual intervals given, the nine-to-ten-day period before hatching is the longest, while the final maturation stage lasts only four days.

Key words and phrases

allow further growth to occur
Let the organism grow more.
the cycle starts over again
The sequence begins again.

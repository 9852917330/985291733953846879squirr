Transport

The transport system and traffic in your hometown

Part 1

Prompt

Can you describe the transport system in your hometown? What is the most common means of transport in your country? Do traffic jams often occur in your city? How can we solve this problem?

Can you describe the transport system in your hometown?

Simple Model Answer

People in Hanoi use motorbikes, buses and cars, and the metro is growing. Transport is improving, but changing from one type to another still needs to become easier.

Key words and phrases

metro
A city train system, often partly underground or above street level.

Advanced Model Answer

Hanoi relies heavily on motorbikes, buses, cars and an expanding metro system. The network's improving, but transfers between different modes still need to become more seamless.

Key words and phrases

more seamless
Smoother, with fewer interruptions or difficulties.

What is the most common means of transport in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Motorbikes are still very common because they are easy to use in crowded streets. Buses and the metro are becoming important too, especially for people who want to spend less on travel.

Key words and phrases

crowded streets
Roads with many people or vehicles using them.

Advanced Model Answer

Motorbikes are still the most common personal vehicle because they're flexible in dense streets. Buses and the metro are increasingly important for people who want to avoid the cost of driving.

Key words and phrases

the cost of driving
Money needed to use a car.

Do traffic jams often occur in your city?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, especially when people travel to work and home again. The city has grown quickly and there are many private vehicles, while the roads do not have enough space for them all.

Key words and phrases

private vehicles
Cars or motorbikes used by individuals rather than shared public services.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, especially during morning and evening peaks. Rapid urban growth and high private-vehicle use have placed considerable pressure on roads that weren't built to handle this many vehicles.

Key words and phrases

considerable pressure
A large amount of stress or demand.

How can we solve this problem?

Simple Model Answer

We need buses and trains that run often, safe places to walk and better control of traffic. Before limiting private vehicles, the city should give people other dependable ways to travel.

Key words and phrases

dependable
Able to be trusted to work well when needed.

Advanced Model Answer

The city needs frequent public transport, safe walking routes and better traffic management. Restrictions on private vehicles won't work unless people have a reliable alternative.

Key words and phrases

a reliable alternative
Another option that can be trusted.

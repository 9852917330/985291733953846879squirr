Numbers

Special numbers and remembering them

Prompt

Do you have a favourite number or a number that is special to you? Do any numbers have special importance in your country? Are you good at remembering numbers? What numbers do people often have to memorise?

Do you have a favourite number or a number that is special to you?

Simple Model Answer

Seven is special to me because my birthday is on the seventh. I don't really believe it brings good luck, but I like it because of that personal connection.

Key words and phrases

personal connection
A link with your own life or experiences.

Advanced Model Answer

My favourite number is seven because it's the date of my birthday, and I've always had a soft spot for it. I wouldn't call it genuinely lucky, but I do feel a strong attachment to it.

Key words and phrases

a soft spot for it
A particular fondness for it.

Do any numbers have special importance in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, seven is seen as special, for example. There are seven days in a week and seven main musical notes. Shakespeare also described human life as having seven stages.

Key words and phrases

stages
Different parts of a process or period of development.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes. The number 7, for example, is supposed to be a special number in my country. It appears in so many places, like the seven days of the week, the seven main notes in music, and the seven ages of man that Shakespeare described.

Key words and phrases

supposed to be a special number
Thought to have special meaning.

Are you good at remembering numbers?

Simple Model Answer

No, I find numbers difficult to remember. I usually save them on my phone rather than try to remember every one, which is more practical for me.

Key words and phrases

practical
Useful and suitable for everyday needs.

Advanced Model Answer

No, I'm not very good at remembering numbers. I don't think it's necessary to learn them by heart; I just save them on my phone.

Key words and phrases

learn them by heart
Memorise them completely.

What numbers do people often have to memorise?

Simple Model Answer

A bank card PIN is an important number to remember. People often need it to pay by card or take money from a cash machine, so forgetting it can cause problems.

Key words and phrases

PIN
A secret number used to confirm that you can use a card or account.

Advanced Model Answer

The most important number to remember on a daily basis is the PIN number for your bank card. Without this you can't pay for anything or get cash out.

Key words and phrases

on a daily basis
Every day.
get cash out
Withdraw money from a bank or machine.

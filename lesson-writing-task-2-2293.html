Technology & Science

Who should control research?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

Many people believe that scientific research should be carried out and controlled by governments rather than by private companies. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

An increasing share of the world's research is now funded by companies rather than states. I partly agree that governments should control it, but the answer depends on the kind of research rather than on a general principle.

The argument for public control is strongest where the incentives of business point the wrong way. A company must produce a return, so it works on problems with a paying market: treatments for wealthy patients rather than diseases of the poor, and products rather than the basic knowledge those products rest on. Firms also own their results, so findings are protected by patents instead of shared, and research that threatens the value of an existing product may never be published at all. Public funding is what supports work with no obvious application, which is where the largest discoveries have historically come from.

Private research nonetheless does things governments do badly. Turning a discovery into something people can actually use requires enormous investment and a tolerance for failure that no minister enjoys explaining, and companies are far quicker at development, manufacture and distribution. They also respond to demand faster than a committee allocating grants, and public research has weaknesses of its own, since political priorities can shape findings as easily as commercial ones.

The arrangement that works is division rather than control. Governments should fund basic science, medicine for conditions with no market, and anything affecting public safety, while regulating private research strictly: registering trials in advance, requiring publication of negative results, and preventing a company from suppressing what it does not like.

In conclusion, government funding is essential for research that markets will not pay for and for knowledge that should belong to everyone. Private research delivers the practical results, so the sensible policy is public money for the questions and firm rules for the answers.

Key words and phrases

threatens the value of an existing product
Could make a current product less profitable.

Dancing

Dancing, and whether traditional dance will last

Prompt

Do you like dancing? Has anyone ever taught you to dance? Do you think that traditional dancing will be popular in the future?

Do you like dancing?

Simple Model Answer

No, I don't really enjoy dancing. I'm not very interested in music, and I feel uncomfortable when I dance because I worry about how I look.

Key words and phrases

worry about
Feel nervous or concerned about something.

Advanced Model Answer

No, I don't like dancing. I'm not a big music fan, and dancing just makes me feel uncomfortable and self-conscious.

Key words and phrases

uncomfortable and self-conscious
Uneasy and worried about others' opinions.

Has anyone ever taught you to dance?

Simple Model Answer

No, nobody has taught me. I've never really wanted to learn, so I haven't taken classes or asked anyone to teach me any dance steps.

Key words and phrases

dance steps
The movements people make when dancing.

Advanced Model Answer

No, I've never been interested in learning to dance, so I've never taken any lessons or asked anyone to show me how to do it.

Key words and phrases

taken any lessons
Attended classes to learn something.

Do you think that traditional dancing will be popular in the future?

Simple Model Answer

I'm not sure because I don't know much about dance. However, I think some people will continue to enjoy traditional dancing, even if it isn't popular with everyone.

Key words and phrases

continue to
Keep doing something.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm afraid I have no idea, because I don't follow what's happening in the dancing world! I suppose it will always be popular with some people.

Key words and phrases

follow what's happening
Understand the events as they happen.
be popular with
Be liked by.

Tourism & Travel

Why people travel more, and what they gain

two-part

Prompt

Today people are travelling more than ever before. Why is this the case? What are the benefits of travelling for the traveller?

Model answer

People travel more than in the past because journeys have become cheaper, faster and easier to arrange. Higher incomes and flexible working patterns have also made travel possible for a wider group of people. Overall, this trend brings substantial personal and economic benefits.

Low-cost airlines, online booking and improved transport networks have reduced both the price and difficulty of travel. Social media also exposes people to destinations that they might never have considered. In addition, many people now study, work or have relatives abroad, so international travel has become part of ordinary life rather than a rare luxury.

Travel can broaden people's understanding by placing them in unfamiliar environments. Visitors learn how others live, communicate and solve everyday problems. Tourism also creates employment in transport, accommodation and local services. When managed well, it can provide income for rural areas and help fund the protection of historic sites and natural attractions.

However, these benefits depend on responsible behaviour. Overcrowding, pollution and rising housing costs can harm local communities. Governments should therefore control development, improve public transport and ensure that tourism revenue reaches residents. With sensible planning, travel can support cultural exchange and local prosperity without overwhelming destinations.

Travel also builds a kind of practical confidence that a familiar routine at home rarely offers. Even a short trip forces travellers to solve unfamiliar problems alone, such as reading a foreign menu or rearranging a delayed connection, and most people manage far better than they expected. At the same time, employers and universities increasingly value international experience, treating it as evidence of independence. A well-planned trip can therefore combine personal enjoyment with practical learning, rather than serving only as a break from routine.

Key words and phrases

part of ordinary life
Something common in everyday experience.
support cultural exchange and local prosperity
Help cultures connect and local economies grow.
combine personal enjoyment with practical learning
Enjoy yourself while gaining useful knowledge.

A dream house or apartment you want to live in

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a dream house or apartment you want to live in.

You should say: - Where it is - What your dream home is like - Why you like this dream home - What you would do to get it.

Simple Model Answer

My dream home would be a villa in District 7 of Ho Chi Minh City. I like that area because it has good roads, schools, hospitals and shopping centres. It would be a convenient place for a family.

I imagine a three-storey house with a large garden. There would be an old tree and a small pond with fish beside it. I would grow flowers and give my cats and dogs space to play. Inside, I would choose yellow and white walls and hang paintings by local artists.

I want a house rather than a flat because I hope to have a large family. My parents could live with my wife, our future children and me. Buying a villa would be expensive, so I am saving part of my salary every month. I also hope for a promotion and may buy a smaller property first. This home represents a long-term goal for me: somewhere comfortable where the people I love can live together.

Key words and phrases

a promotion
A move to a higher position at work.
a long-term goal
Something you hope to achieve over a period of years.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about my vision of an ideal home — the place I really want to live in one day.

My long-term goal is to settle down in one of the villas in District 7, or somewhere similarly upscale in HCMC. I don't want to settle for anything less when it comes to housing, and District 7 has great access to infrastructure facilities — good schools, hospitals, roads, and plenty of shopping centres.

There's actually a specific villa in that area I have in mind: three storeys, a big garden, and a small pond right next to an old tree, which I'd stock with a few fish. I'd plant all sorts of trees and flowers around the garden and let my cats and dogs run around out there — I love animals, so there'd definitely be a few of those. Inside, I'd paint the walls in bright colours like yellow and white, my favourites, and hang up paintings from local artists.

Part of the reason I want a villa specifically, rather than an apartment, is that I want a big family one day. I'd bring my parents to live with me and my wife, and together we'd bring up my future children right there in that garden.

As for actually getting there, I know it won't just fall into my lap. I'm saving a fixed amount from my salary every month, and hoping a promotion at work in the next couple of years will speed things up considerably. Longer term, I'm also thinking about investing in a smaller property first, as a stepping stone, rather than trying to buy the villa outright in one go.

This villa is going to be the symbol of everything I've worked for, and I honestly believe that if I stay focused on it, I can make it happen.

settle for anything less

basic services and structures, such as roads and transport

Technology & Science

Has technology made our leisure less creative?

Opinion

Prompt

The leisure industry is growing rapidly. The active role of modern technology in people's leisure lives nowadays has made them less creative. Do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

Technology now occupies a large part of leisure time, leading some people to believe that it has reduced creativity. I disagree with this generalisation. Passive screen use can certainly become repetitive, but modern technology also gives ordinary people powerful tools to create, learn and share original work.

The negative argument is understandable. Recommendation systems often encourage users to watch similar videos for hours, and many games provide fixed goals rather than genuine freedom. When entertainment becomes automatic, people may spend less time reading, making things or inventing their own activities. This is mainly a problem of habit and design, not proof that all digital leisure is uncreative.

In contrast, affordable software allows people to edit films, compose music, draw, write and build games at home. Online courses teach techniques that were once available only through specialist schools, while creative communities provide feedback and collaboration. Even gaming can involve strategy, storytelling and the design of new worlds. Technology therefore lowers the barrier between consuming and creating.

Education can improve the balance by teaching people how digital platforms compete for attention. When users understand recommendation systems and set limits on passive viewing, they have more time for demanding activities such as writing, design or music production. Schools and families can reinforce this habit by valuing finished creative projects, not simply hours spent online.

In conclusion, modern leisure can become passive when people use technology without purpose. However, the same devices can greatly expand creative opportunity. The important issue is whether users merely scroll through content or choose to use digital tools actively and deliberately.

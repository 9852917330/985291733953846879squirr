Crime & Punishment

Will technology reduce crime?

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

Crime rates are likely to decline due to the advancements in technology, which will help to prevent and solve crimes in an easier way. Do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

It is often predicted that advances in technology will make crime easier to prevent and to solve, and that offending will therefore fall. I accept that technology has made certain traditional crimes far more difficult, but I disagree with the wider claim, because the same developments have created entirely new forms of criminal activity.

There is no doubt that technology assists the police. Street cameras, number-plate recognition and mobile phone records make it much harder to commit a burglary or a robbery without being identified, and DNA analysis has allowed investigators to solve cases once considered hopeless. Banks now use automated systems that detect unusual transactions instantly. Rates of car theft and street crime have indeed fallen sharply in many countries over the past twenty years, and better security technology is a major reason for this.

However, criminals adopt new technology just as quickly as the authorities do. Fraud, identity theft and online scams have grown enormously, and in several countries these offences now outnumber all traditional crimes combined. Such crimes are extremely difficult to investigate, because the offender may be in another country, may be hidden behind encryption, and may target thousands of victims at once. Overall crime figures have not fallen; the crime has simply moved.

It is also worth remembering that technology does not address the underlying causes of crime, which are usually poverty, addiction, poor education and inequality. Cameras may displace offending rather than prevent it.

In conclusion, technology has clearly reduced some categories of crime, but I do not believe it will produce a general decline in crime rates.

Key words and phrases

solve cases once considered hopeless
Resolve investigations previously thought impossible.
hidden behind encryption
Protected by codes that prevent unauthorised reading.
the crime has simply moved
Criminal activity has shifted to a different place or form.
underlying causes of crime
The deeper reasons people commit crimes.
displace offending
Move criminal activity to another location.

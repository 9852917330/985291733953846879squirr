Trade & Industry

Oil use and employment

Academic Task 1

Prompt

Chart A below shows the rate of oil consumption (in millions of barrels) in a small country from 1950 to 2010. Chart B shows the number of people employed in the oil industry in that country, and also the rate of unemployment throughout the country over the same time. Write a report summarising the information. Select and describe the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The two charts show the rate of oil consumption, the number of people employed in the oil industry, and the rate of unemployment in a small country between 1950 and 2010.

Overall, the rise in oil use in this country was matched by a roughly similar rate of growth in the number of people employed in the oil business. At the same time, unemployment in the country as a whole dropped by more than half.

Oil consumption fluctuated at around 20 million barrels per year until 1970, but it then rose steadily, reaching 40 million barrels in 2000 and around 50 million by 2010.

Turning to the second chart, the number of people employed in the oil industry roughly tripled over the same period. This figure also fluctuated at around 1,000 until 1970, before climbing consistently, passing 1,800 in 1980 and reaching 3,100 in 2010. National unemployment, on the other hand, rose slightly from 4% in 1950 to 4.1% in 1960, before falling steadily to 3.1% in 1980 and 1.6% in 2010.

Key words and phrases

rate of growth
The speed at which an amount increases.
roughly tripled over the same period
Became about three times larger.

Cities that look alike

Reasons / Advantages outweigh disadvantages

Prompt

In the past, people used to travel abroad to look for many differences from their home country. Nowadays, cities throughout the world are becoming more and more similar. What are the reasons? Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

Model answer

A traveller arriving in a large city today is likely to find the same coffee chains, the same clothing shops and the same glass towers as at home. This convergence has clear commercial causes, and in my view its disadvantages outweigh its benefits.

The main reason is the reach of international business. A successful retailer opens in fifty countries with an identical shopfront, because a recognisable brand is precisely what customers are paying for. Construction has followed the same path: the materials, engineering methods and even the architects behind a modern office tower are international, so a new district in Jakarta is designed by much the same process as one in Frankfurt. Local authorities encourage this, since attracting a known company or a famous architect is treated as evidence that a city has arrived on the world stage.

There are advantages. Familiarity lowers the barrier to travel for people who find unfamiliar places intimidating, and international standards have brought better plumbing, safer buildings and more reliable transport to cities that previously lacked them.

The losses are heavier, however. Distinctive local architecture, markets and trades are what gave a place its character, and once a historic quarter is replaced by a shopping centre it cannot be recreated. Tourism suffers directly, because visitors travel to see what they cannot see at home, and a city that looks like everywhere else gives them no particular reason to come. Local businesses also lose the ground on which they competed.

In conclusion, cities are converging because commerce and construction now operate globally. On balance the disadvantages are greater, since the practical gains could be achieved without demolishing what made each city worth visiting in the first place.

Key words and phrases

arrived on the world stage
Became important or recognised internationally.
cannot be recreated
Cannot be made again in the same meaningful form.
no particular reason to come
No special attraction encouraging a visit.
worth visiting in the first place
Having qualities that justify visiting at all.

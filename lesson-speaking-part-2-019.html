Person

An adventurous person who you know

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an adventurous person who you know.

You should say: Who the person is How you know this person What this person does that is adventurous And explain why you think this person likes to take risks.

OUTLINE

>>> Bear Grylls
don't know him personally - famous adventurer with his own survival show; first came across him by accident on TV, hooked ever since
embodiment of adventure and courage - goes to remote places, hunts/eats bugs, drank his own urine, ate a sheep's eye
why he takes risks: confident in survival skills built up from military training; also a genuine purpose - the show teaches people what to do in similar situations
in awe of him, can't imagine going through what he does; a tough guy even as he gets older

Simple Model Answer

An adventurous person I know about is Bear Grylls. I do not know him personally, but I have watched his survival programmes for years. I first saw one by chance while changing television channels late at night.

In his shows, he visits remote places where conditions are difficult. He demonstrates ways to find water, get food and survive outdoors. Sometimes he eats insects or other unusual things. I find these scenes hard to watch, but they show how challenging life can be in those environments.

I think he is willing to take risks because of his training and experience. He had military training before becoming a television presenter, so he has developed useful survival skills. His programmes also aim to explain what someone might do in a difficult outdoor situation. I admire his confidence and courage, although I would not want to copy the dangerous activities myself. Watching him makes me realise how much knowledge and preparation are needed for an adventure in a wild place.

Key words and phrases

by chance
Without planning or expecting something.
survival skills
Abilities that help a person stay alive in difficult conditions.

Advanced Model Answer

Although I don't know this person personally, I'm going to talk about Bear Grylls, a famous adventurer with his own survival show on the National Geographic channel. I first came across him years ago, completely by accident, flicking through channels late one night, and I've been hooked on his programmes ever since.

You don't really need much explanation once people hear the name — Bear Grylls is pretty much the embodiment of adventure and courage. In his show, he goes to remote places with harsh conditions and has to try to survive for several days, finding food and water in the most unlikely places, sometimes hunting wild animals or eating insects just to get by. I don't think there's much this guy's afraid to do — he's drunk his own urine and eaten a sheep's eye on camera, and he didn't even flinch.

I think the reason he takes so many risks is that he's just incredibly confident in his own survival skills, built up from years of military training before he ever got a TV show. There's also a genuine purpose behind it — the show's meant to teach people what to do if they're ever stuck in a similar situation, so in a way he has to push himself to those extremes to make the lessons convincing.

I'm honestly in awe of the guy. I can't even manage eating insects myself, so I really can't imagine going through half of what he puts himself through. He's getting a bit older now, so I don't know how much longer the show will carry on, but either way, he's a tough one.

Key words and phrases

his own survival show
a television programme about his survival adventures
the embodiment of adventure and courage
a person who perfectly represents bravery and adventure
remote places with harsh conditions
distant areas where living conditions are difficult
try to survive
make an effort to stay alive
in awe of
feeling deep admiration for

Object & Favourite

Something you made yourself

Part 2

Prompt

Describe something you made yourself.

You should say: What you made. Why you make it. How you make it. And explain how you felt about it.

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

Something I made myself was a tiramisu cake for my mother's birthday a couple of years ago. I had enjoyed the dessert at a restaurant in District 1 and thought a homemade version would be a thoughtful present.

I had only learned basic baking a few months earlier, so it was quite a challenge. On her birthday morning, I bought the ingredients and started following a recipe. Unfortunately, the cream did not become firm as expected. I had used a different bowl from the one suggested, and before long the cake had turned into a mess.

When my mother came into the kitchen, I expected her to be annoyed. Instead, she smiled and said she appreciated the effort. I cleaned up and later bought her a dress to replace the failed cake. We still enjoyed her birthday together. I have tried the recipe again since then and succeeded. The experience taught me to practise before making an important gift, but it also showed me that my mother valued the care behind the present more than a perfect result.

Key words and phrases

thoughtful present
A gift chosen or made with care for the person receiving it.
firm
Solid enough to keep its shape rather than remaining liquid.

Advanced Model Answer

A couple of years ago I tried to make a Tiramisu cake for my mother's birthday, and that's what I want to talk about — though I'll admit, it wasn't exactly a success.

I'd first tasted Tiramisu at a restaurant in District 1 ages before that, and I loved it so much I decided it would make the perfect birthday present. I'd only learned to bake at all a few months earlier, after picking up a cookbook from a local bookshop, so this was ambitious for someone with about three cakes' worth of experience.

On the morning of her birthday, I got up early and bought all the ingredients from the supermarket, and everything was going fine until I got to the mascarpone cream. The recipe called for a stainless-steel bowl to whip it in, but I only had a porcelain one, so I used that instead — and it turned out that actually mattered. The cream refused to hold its shape, stayed completely liquid, and the whole cake pretty quickly turned into a big mess.

My mother walked in right in the middle of this disaster, and I braced myself for her to be annoyed about the state of the kitchen. Instead she just smiled and said she was touched that I'd even tried to make her something myself. I cleaned everything up, and later that day I bought her a dress to compensate for the cake I'd ruined.

We still had a lovely birthday together in the end, and I've actually had another go at that Tiramisu recipe since then — successfully, this time — so hopefully next year I'll finally get to give her the cake I originally had in mind.

the perfect birthday present
an ideal gift for a birthday

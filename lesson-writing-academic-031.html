Environment & Climate

European waste management

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The chart below compares levels of recycling, as well as some less environmentally friendly forms of waste management, in fifteen European countries.

Model answer

The bar chart shows the percentage of waste that is recycled, put into landfill or incinerated in various European countries.

Landfill is the largest waste-management category in most of the fifteen countries shown. In fact, while around half of the countries put the majority of their waste into landfill sites, only two countries recycle at least 50% of the waste that they produce.

Austria and Belgium are the highest-recycling of the fifteen countries, recycling around 60% and 50% of their waste respectively. Germany and Sweden also recycle roughly 40% of their waste.

Other European countries recycle far less. Greece, Ireland and the UK, for example, only recycle around 10% of refuse, and put between 80 and 90 per cent of their waste into landfill. Finally, four countries, namely Luxembourg, Sweden, the Netherlands and Denmark, incinerate more waste than they bury. Denmark has the highest incineration share, at just under 60%, whereas Greece and Ireland show no waste treated in this way.

Key words and phrases

respectively
In the same order as the things already mentioned.
incinerate more waste than they bury
Burn more rubbish than they bury.

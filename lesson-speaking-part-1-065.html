Sea

Living near the sea and how it makes you feel

Part 1

Prompt

Do you live near the sea? How do you feel when you are near the sea? What do you like and dislike about the sea? Would you like to live on an island and why?

Do you live near the sea?

Simple Model Answer

No, I don't live close to the sea. It takes some time to get there, so visiting the coast feels special and gives me a pleasant change from normal life.

Key words and phrases

the coast
The land beside the sea.

Advanced Model Answer

I don't live right by the sea, so going to the coast still feels like a bit of a treat. It takes a while to get there, but that's what makes each trip a refreshing change.

Key words and phrases

a refreshing change
A pleasantly different experience.

How do you feel when you are near the sea?

Simple Model Answer

I feel much calmer there. Seeing the open sea and hearing the waves help me stop worrying and relax. It is a place where I can really take a mental break.

Key words and phrases

waves
The moving lines of water that rise and fall on the sea.

Advanced Model Answer

I usually feel a lot calmer near the sea, because the open horizon and the sound of the waves just clear my head. It's one of the few places where I can switch off completely.

Key words and phrases

switch off completely
Completely stop thinking about work or worries.

What do you like and dislike about the sea?

Simple Model Answer

I enjoy the fresh air and beautiful views. However, I don't like busy beaches or plastic rubbish. The sea is more enjoyable when visitors look after the environment.

Key words and phrases

environment
The natural world around us, including land, water and air.

Advanced Model Answer

I like the fresh air and the views, but I'm not so keen on crowded beaches or plastic waste. The coast's always nicer when tourism doesn't end up damaging the environment.

Key words and phrases

damaging the environment
Harming nature.

Would you like to live on an island and why?

Simple Model Answer

I'd like to try it for a short time, but not forever. An island might be peaceful, but fewer hospitals, jobs and transport options could make normal life difficult.

Key words and phrases

options
Different choices that are available.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd enjoy living on an island for a short while, but probably not permanently. Isolation might be peaceful, but limited healthcare, transport and job options would make everyday life less convenient.

Key words and phrases

less convenient
Harder or less comfortable to use.

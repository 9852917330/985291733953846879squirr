A newly-opened shop

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a newly-opened shop

You should say: Where the shop is When it opened What it sells And how you feel about the shop.

Simple Model Answer

A newly opened shop in my neighbourhood is Circle K. It is a convenience store, part of an American chain with several branches in Hanoi. The branch near my home opened about four months ago.

I noticed it while it was being built because it is just up the road. Its red K logo inside a white circle is easy to recognise. After it opened, I went in and discovered that it sold my favourite drink, Vodka Cruiser. This made me start visiting more regularly.

Since then, the shop has also become a place where I meet friends. We sometimes sit outside and talk about our lives. I find these relaxed conversations helpful after a stressful day or a personal problem. Of course, the shop is convenient for everyday purchases too, but the social side is what makes it memorable for me. I like having a familiar place nearby where my friends and I can meet without arranging a formal evening out.

Key words and phrases

convenience store
A small shop selling everyday items, often open for long hours.
everyday purchases
Ordinary things that people buy regularly.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about a newly-opened convenience store in my neighbourhood called Circle K. Actually, Circle K's an extremely popular chain from America, and it's got multiple outlets around Hanoi now.

But the branch I'm talking about opened just up the road from where I live about four months ago, and I noticed it going up almost as soon as they started building it. It's also got this eye-catching logo — a giant red letter K inside a white circle — so it's pretty hard to miss.

I think the main reason I became a regular customer there was one particular item they sell. I was on cloud nine when I discovered it — my favourite drink, Vodka Cruiser. They only contain about 4.5% alcohol, which is perfect for a lightweight like me.

After that discovery, Circle K pretty much became a regular hangout spot for me and my mates. We'd sometimes meet up and sit outside the store with a couple of Cruisers, just chatting about life. It's actually a bit embarrassing to admit, but it's also become the place I go to let off some steam after a break-up, or whenever there's some other drama going on in my life.

So yeah, as long as Circle K keeps selling Vodka Cruisers, it'll always be my favourite shop.

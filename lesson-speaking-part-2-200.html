Object & Favourite

Something that makes you excited

Part 2

Prompt

Talk about something that makes you excited.

You should say: - What it is - How it is exciting - Why you feel excited about it

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

Something that makes me excited is my weekly coffee meeting with my best friend. We were extremely close at school and could spend hours talking without getting bored. Since graduating, however, work has made it harder to meet during the week.

We now try to keep time free at the weekend. Usually, we choose a cafe and talk for a few hours. Sometimes we go shopping or have brunch instead. The exact activity is less important than knowing we will have a proper chance to see each other.

I look forward to it because I can speak honestly about whatever is worrying me. She listens, makes me laugh and often gives useful advice. I normally feel lighter afterwards, even if the problem itself has not disappeared. The meeting also helps us keep our friendship strong while our lives become busier. It may seem like a small thing to be excited about, but familiar relationships matter a lot to me. Having this regular plan gives me something enjoyable to think about during a difficult working week.

Key words and phrases

brunch
A meal eaten late in the morning, combining breakfast and lunch.
feel lighter
Feel less worried or emotionally burdened.

Advanced Model Answer

Honestly, there isn't a huge amount of excitement in my life at the moment, but if I have to pick one thing, it'd be the weekly coffee date I have with my best friend — that's genuinely what I look forward to most.

The reason it means so much is that we barely get the chance to talk during the week. Back in school the two of us were like the white on rice, completely inseparable, and I could spend entire evenings just gossiping with her without it ever feeling like enough. Since we graduated, though, we've both been buried in work, so staying in regular contact has got a lot harder. Weekends are really the only time we can meet up properly now, and just thinking about it never fails to excite me.

We do all sorts of things together, honestly. Most weeks we just pick a nice cafe and talk for hours, but sometimes we'll go shopping instead, or have brunch somewhere a bit fancier. What we actually do doesn't matter that much to me — it's really just about seeing her. I always feel so much lighter after I've confided in her about whatever's stressing me out, because I know she'll cheer me up and give me genuinely useful advice.

So yes, out of everything going on in my life right now, that weekly coffee date is probably the one thing that excites me the most.

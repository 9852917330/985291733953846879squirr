Object & Favourite

A memorable story told by someone

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a memorable story told by someone

You should say: Who told you the story? When you heard the story What the story was about Why you found it memorable.

Simple Model Answer

A memorable story I heard was told by my friend Julie during our second year at university. Our group was sitting at our usual table in a cafe near campus. I had ordered an unfamiliar fruit drink instead of my normal coffee.

I disliked the taste, but Julie tried it and said she loved it because it reminded her of toothpaste. We were surprised and asked what she meant. She explained that she had enjoyed eating toothpaste as a child. Her parents had stopped her, but later she secretly saved money, bought a tube and ate it in her room.

For a moment, everyone was silent. Then we all started laughing, and I laughed so much that my eyes watered. It was the unexpected detail about saving money that made the story especially funny. We knew eating toothpaste was not a good idea, but her way of telling this childhood memory was unforgettable. Years later, we still mention it as a private joke whenever we see a strange-looking drink.

Key words and phrases

my eyes watered
Tears came into my eyes, for example from laughing a lot.
a private joke
Something funny understood mainly by a particular person or small group.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to share a funny story I heard from my friend Julie a couple of years ago, back when we were both in our second year at university. Our little group used to hang out most weekends at a coffee shop near campus, always at the same circular table by the window — it was our favourite spot.

That particular day, instead of ordering my usual cappuccino, I decided to try a completely new drink — some kind of mojito mixed with fruit, though I honestly can't remember which fruit anymore. It looked great, but the taste was unbearable to me. Julie insisted on trying a sip anyway, and to my surprise she loved it — she said it tasted exactly like the toothpaste she used to eat as a kid.

We were all a bit stunned, so naturally we made her explain herself. She told us she'd genuinely thought toothpaste tasted amazing, and even though her parents banned her from eating it, she secretly saved money to buy her own tube a few years later, hid it in her room, and finished the whole thing in one sitting. There was this dead silence for a couple of seconds while we all just stared at her, and then the whole table burst into laughter. I remember laughing so hard my eyes were watering.

It wasn't the first time I'd heard of someone enjoying the taste of toothpaste, but the secret saving-up-to-buy-it part was such an unexpected detail that it made the whole story hilarious. It's honestly become a running joke between the two of us ever since.

Key words and phrases

share a funny story
Tell an amusing personal account.
our favourite spot
The place we most like visiting.
a completely new drink
A beverage unlike what we had before.
unbearable to me
Too unpleasant for me to accept.
saved money to buy
Put money aside to purchase something.
burst into laughter
Suddenly start laughing.

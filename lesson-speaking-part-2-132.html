Something you do to keep healthy

Part 2

Prompt

Describe something you do to keep healthy.

You should say: - What the activity is - When you do it - How often you do it And explain why it is a good way to look after your health.

Simple Model Answer

Something I do to stay healthy is jogging. I started about two years ago while preparing for my graduation exams. I was spending most of the day at my laptop, and the pressure of studying made me feel stressed.

I decided to try running because I needed an active break. At first, it was difficult. My muscles ached, and I was not used to exercising regularly. However, after a few days I began to feel more comfortable. Now I try to go out five or six evenings a week.

I have noticed both physical and mental benefits. I feel fitter, and I am happier with my appearance. More importantly, time outside helps me stop thinking about work and study for a while. I usually return home with a clearer mind. I plan to continue rather than treating jogging as a short-term way to change my body. It has become a habit I enjoy, and I may try other sports later. Starting slowly helped me discover an activity that fits well into my daily life.

Key words and phrases

active break
Time away from work or study spent moving or exercising.
a clearer mind
A calmer state in which it is easier to think.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about something I'm keen on doing in my free time to stay healthy - jogging.

I took up this habit about two years ago, back when I was up to my ears with study for my graduation exams and feeling pretty stressed out. I remember thinking I needed something energetic to burn off the stress, so I decided to have a go at jogging.

It was tough to begin with, since I'd been living a pretty sedentary lifestyle up to that point - just sitting in front of my laptop all day doing assignments. After a few days of aching muscles, though, I got used to it, and these days I try to get out most evenings, five or six times a week.

Honestly, it's helped me a lot, both physically and mentally. I lost my belly and got a lot leaner thanks to it, and I'm pretty happy with how I look now - I try to keep it that way. It also just helps me unwind and get some fresh air; every time I go, I come back feeling like my head's been cleared out a bit. I'm planning to keep it up long-term, and maybe branch out into other sports eventually too.

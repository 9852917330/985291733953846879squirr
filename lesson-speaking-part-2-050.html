A public place that you think needs improvements

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a public place that you think needs improvements.

You should say: - What the place is. - When you visit the place. - What do you like and dislike about the public place

Simple Model Answer

A public place that needs improvement is Dam Sen Park in Ho Chi Minh City. It is a large amusement park, and I recently went there with my nieces and nephews. They enjoyed the rides, but I noticed some problems.

Several areas needed cleaning and repairs. There were not enough rubbish bins, some walls were covered in moss, and unused equipment had weeds around it. Most worrying was some discarded needles in a quiet corner. This made me concerned about children walking nearby.

I do not think the whole park needs to be rebuilt. Basic maintenance would make a big difference. Staff should remove dangerous rubbish, repair paths, improve lighting and add bins in busy places. Regular cleaning and security would also help. Later, the old toilets and empty buildings could be repaired. I still like the park because it is an affordable place for families to spend a day. However, visitors need to feel safe. A cleaner, better-maintained park would encourage people like me to come back.

Key words and phrases

discarded needles
Used or unwanted needles that have been thrown away.
Basic maintenance
Regular simple work to keep a place safe and in good condition.

Advanced Model Answer

A public place that needs improvement is Dam Sen Park in Ho Chi Minh City. It's a large amusement park that many families visit at weekends, and parts of it still feel like a green oasis in a crowded city.

I went there recently with my nieces and nephews. The children enjoyed the rides, but I noticed that several areas had fallen into disrepair. There weren't enough rubbish bins, some of the walls were covered in moss, and weeds had grown up around equipment nobody uses any more. The thing that really worried me, though, was finding discarded needles near a quiet corner of the park — that's a serious safety hazard, especially for kids in thin sandals.

I don't think the authorities need to redesign the whole park. They should just carry out basic maintenance first — clear away anything dangerous, fix the broken paths, improve the lighting, and put more bins in the busy areas. Regular cleaning and a visible security presence would also stop people littering or messing about. Longer term, they could renovate the old toilets and the buildings that have just been left abandoned.

I still like the park — it's affordable and it's a great spot for a family day out — but people won't keep coming back if they don't feel safe. A fairly modest programme of cleaning and repairs could restore public confidence and make the park feel welcoming again.

Activity

A project or a piece of work you did together with someone

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a project or a piece of work you did together with someone

You should say: - What it was - What you have done - Why you have chosen this person(s) to work with And explain what you did to finish the project.

Simple Model Answer

A project I completed with someone was organising my mother's fiftieth birthday last year. I worked with my younger sister, Khanh. She suggested a surprise party and persuaded me to help, although I was not very enthusiastic at first.

My first idea was simply to buy a present. However, Khanh wanted us to make a video using family memories. She was more organised than me, so she helped plan the tasks while we collected material and prepared the surprise. We kept everything secret from our mother.

On her birthday, we left a note directing her to the kitchen and turned off the lights. When she arrived, we played the video with her favourite song. She stood still for a moment and then began crying with happiness. Seeing her reaction made all the preparation worthwhile. I felt proud of what my sister and I had achieved together. The project showed me that working with another person can produce something more meaningful than taking the quickest and easiest option alone.

made all the preparation worthwhile

Having personal importance or a special purpose.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about a project I did with my younger sister that I've always been quite proud of - organising my mum's fiftieth birthday party last year.

To be honest, I'm not usually much of a team player, and most group projects I've been part of end in some kind of disaster, so when my sister, Khanh, first suggested throwing mum a surprise party, I couldn't have cared less. But Khanh was so determined that she basically forced me to help - and to be fair, she's always been far more organised about that kind of thing than I am. I hesitated at first, but on second thoughts, I was curious to see mum's reaction the moment she'd be taken by surprise, so I agreed to get involved.

At first, I wanted to take the easy way out and just buy mum a gift, but Khanh wasn't having it - she told me to help her make a video of our memories together instead, and I couldn't really argue.

When mum's birthday came around, she had no idea what was going on, since we'd kept it secret. The moment she walked in, she was taken aback by a note telling her to go straight to the kitchen - we'd turned off every light, so she had to feel her way there in the dark. Once we played the video and her favourite song, she stood still for a second, then burst into tears of joy.

In the end, the whole thing turned out to be a real success, and on top of a sense of accomplishment, I felt genuinely thankful to see mum that happy for the first time in ages.

Key words and phrases

on second thoughts
After thinking about it again.
be taken by surprise
Experience an unexpected event.
taken aback by
Suddenly surprised or shocked by.
turned out to be
Was discovered to be in the end.
a sense of accomplishment
Pride in completing something worthwhile.

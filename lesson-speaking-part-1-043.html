Hometown

Where you were born and how it has changed

Part 1

Prompt

Describe a place where you were born? Tell me about the main types of buildings there? How have buildings in your hometown changed since you were a child? Can you describe your hometown?

Describe a place where you were born?

Simple Model Answer

I was born in Hanoi, an old city in northern Vietnam. A lot of people live there, but its lakes, old streets and large trees make many areas feel special.

Key words and phrases

northern
In the north of a country or region.

Advanced Model Answer

I was born in Hanoi, a historic city in northern Vietnam. It's densely populated, but the lakes, old streets and mature trees give a lot of areas a distinctive atmosphere.

Key words and phrases

a distinctive atmosphere
A special, recognisable feeling in a place.

Tell me about the main types of buildings there?

Simple Model Answer

There are traditional houses, French-style old buildings, blocks of flats and modern offices. The city has developed at different times, so there is a mixture of building styles.

Key words and phrases

a mixture of
Several different things together.

Advanced Model Answer

The city's got a real mix — traditional houses, French colonial buildings, apartment blocks and modern offices. It's more a case of different stages of development than a single architectural style.

Key words and phrases

a single architectural style
One consistent type of building design.

How have buildings in your hometown changed since you were a child?

Simple Model Answer

There are more tall flats and business buildings now. The city has better modern services, but some older neighbourhoods no longer have quite the same look and feeling.

Key words and phrases

neighbourhoods
Areas of a town where people live.

Advanced Model Answer

There are a lot more high-rise apartments and commercial buildings now. The city's gained some modern facilities, but some neighbourhoods have lost part of their original character.

Key words and phrases

original character
The qualities a place had before changes.

Can you describe your hometown?

Simple Model Answer

Hanoi is busy and full of culture, with good food and useful services. However, it can also feel crowded and confusing, and traffic and pollution make everyday life harder.

Key words and phrases

pollution
Harmful substances or dirty air in the environment.

Advanced Model Answer

Hanoi's lively, culturally rich and sometimes a bit chaotic. It's got great food and services, but the traffic and pollution place considerable pressure on daily life.

Key words and phrases

place considerable pressure on
Create a lot of strain for.

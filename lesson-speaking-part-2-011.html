Person

A person who gave a clever solution to a problem

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a person who gave a clever solution to a problem

You should say: Who the person is When and where it happened What the problem was And explain why it was a clever solution

OUTLINE

>>> My brother Nam
A calm person who thinks ahead and notices practical details
University entrance exam: busy checking documents and travelling
Skipped most of breakfast and forgot to pack food
Nam had put a snack and water in my bag
His preparation removed a small problem at a stressful time
Thoughtful anticipation can be a clever form of problem-solving

Simple Model Answer

A person who found a clever solution to a problem is my older brother, Nam. He is calm and careful, while I often make quick decisions. He has helped me by thinking ahead on several occasions.

One example happened before my university entrance exam. I was so busy checking my papers and travelling to the exam centre that I had barely eaten breakfast. I sometimes forget practical things when I am nervous, and that morning was no different. As the exam approached, I realised I was hungry and had not brought a snack.

Then I found two small chocolate bars in my bag. My brother had put them there with a bottle of water before I left home. I ate the snack and felt more ready to concentrate. I was grateful because he had noticed a simple problem before I did. His solution was not complicated or expensive. What made it clever was understanding my habits and preparing something useful at exactly the right time.

Key words and phrases

thinking ahead
Considering what might happen and preparing for it.
at exactly the right time
At the moment when something is most useful.

Advanced Model Answer

A person who is particularly good at finding practical solutions is my older brother, Nam. We have rather different personalities: I tend to make quick decisions, whereas he thinks ahead and notices details that I overlook.

One example that stays with me happened on the day of my university entrance exam. I had spent the morning checking documents and worrying about the journey, so breakfast had become an afterthought. By the time I arrived, I realised I was hungry and had not packed anything to eat. It was a small problem, but one that felt much larger when I was already under pressure.

Then I discovered a couple of chocolate bars and a bottle of water in my bag. My brother had quietly packed them before I left. He knew that nervousness made me forget ordinary practical things, so he had prepared a simple solution in advance. I could have a snack and settle down instead of wasting the remaining time searching for a shop.

What impressed me was not technical brilliance but thoughtful anticipation. Nam had recognised a predictable difficulty and dealt with it before I even noticed. I think that is an underrated form of problem-solving. His action reminded me that people can look out for each other through small, well timed decisions, rather than only through dramatic acts of help.

Key words and phrases

thinks ahead
Prepares for likely future needs or problems.
under pressure
Feeling demands or stress while needing to perform.
in advance
Before the time when something is needed.
thoughtful anticipation
Careful preparation based on understanding a likely need.
look out for each other
Take care of one another and notice possible difficulties.

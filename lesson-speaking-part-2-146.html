Event

An interesting event in your school

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an interesting event in your school

You should say: - What the event was. - When the event was. - Why it was interesting. - How you felt about it.

Simple Model Answer

An interesting school event was our sports day when I was about thirteen. It took place on the school field on a sunny Saturday in early summer. There were running races, jumping events, shot put and a tug-of-war competition.

I entered the hundred-metre race, long jump and shot put. Before the first event, I was nervous, but hearing teachers, parents and classmates cheering helped me try harder. Each class had a banner, and the music and announcements made the day feel special.

I won the long jump with my best distance so far and came second in my other two events. Our class finished second overall. We would have liked to win, but we were still proud. At the end, the head teacher gave out trophies, and everyone applauded despite being tired. I remember the day because success felt shared. My results helped my class, and other students contributed in their own ways. It showed me that school sport can build confidence and bring people together, not simply reward the fastest or strongest person.

Key words and phrases

shot put
A sport in which someone throws a heavy metal ball as far as possible.
second overall
In second place after all the results are combined.

Advanced Model Answer

An interesting event at my school was the annual sports day when I was about thirteen. It took place on the school field on a bright Saturday morning in early summer, and included running races, long jump, high jump, shot put and a tug-of-war competition between the different year groups.

What made the day enjoyable was the atmosphere. Teachers and parents stood around the field and cheered us on, while each class had its own banner. The music, announcements and friendly rivalry created a real sense of occasion. I entered the 100-metre race, the long jump and shot put. I'd been nervous before the first event, but once the competition began, I pushed myself harder than usual.

I eventually won the long jump with a personal best distance, and came second in the other two events. My class finished as the overall runner-up, so we weren't exactly thrilled about it, but we were still proud of our result. The head teacher presented the trophies at the end of the afternoon, and everyone stayed behind to applaud the winners, even though most of us were exhausted by then.

I remember the event because it wasn't just an ordinary school day, it was a memorable shared experience. It showed me that sport can bring people together and give quieter students a chance to contribute. I also felt proud of contributing to my class, not just of winning an individual medal.

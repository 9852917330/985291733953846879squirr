Clothes

Clothes for different situations

Prompt

Are clothes and clothing fashions important to you? What different clothes do you wear for different situations? Do you wear different styles of clothes now compared to 10 years ago? Do you think the clothes we wear say something about who we are?

Are clothes and clothing fashions important to you?

Simple Model Answer

Not really. I care more about feeling comfortable than looking fashionable. I usually choose clothes that are easy to wear and suitable for what I need to do.

Key words and phrases

suitable for
Right or useful for a particular situation.

Advanced Model Answer

No, clothes and fashion aren't really important to me. I tend to wear clothes that are comfortable and practical rather than fashionable.

Key words and phrases

comfortable and practical
Pleasant to use and useful.

What different clothes do you wear for different situations?

Simple Model Answer

I wear a shirt and trousers at work because I need to look formal. At home, I prefer jeans and a T-shirt. For a special event, I might wear a suit.

Key words and phrases

a special event
An important occasion that is different from an ordinary day.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, I have to dress quite formally for work, so I wear a shirt and trousers. At home I prefer to wear jeans and a T-shirt, and on special occasions I might wear a suit.

Key words and phrases

dress quite formally
Wear clothes suitable for serious occasions.

Do you wear different styles of clothes now compared to 10 years ago?

Simple Model Answer

No, my style has stayed much the same. I don't follow fashion closely, so I have worn similar kinds of clothes for about ten years.

Key words and phrases

stayed much the same
Changed very little.

Advanced Model Answer

No, not really, because I don't follow fashion. I've dressed in a similar way for the last 10 years.

Key words and phrases

in a similar way
Using a comparable method or style.

Do you think the clothes we wear say something about who we are?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, clothes can tell us something about people. Some choose them to look fashionable, while others buy famous brands to show that they have money or a high social position.

Key words and phrases

famous brands
Product names that many people recognise.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, they probably do. Some people are really careful about what they wear because they want to be seen as stylish. Other people wear clothes that show wealth or status, such as clothes by famous designers.

Key words and phrases

be seen as
Be considered or understood as.
show wealth or status
Display money or social position.

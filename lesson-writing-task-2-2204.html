News & Media

Newspapers versus other media

Discuss Both Views

Prompt

Some people think that newspapers are the best way to learn about news. However, others believe that they can learn news more effectively through other media. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

Although newspapers were once the main source of information about current events, most people now follow the news online, on television or through social media. I would argue that other media are faster and more convenient, but that newspapers still provide the most reliable and thorough coverage.

The advantages of newspapers concern depth and accuracy. Articles are researched over days or weeks, checked by editors and written by journalists who specialise in a particular field, so a newspaper report on an election or a court case is likely to be far more detailed and more carefully verified than anything else available. Reading a paper also exposes people to stories they would never have chosen themselves, which gives a broader picture of what is happening in the world.

Other forms of media, however, have obvious strengths. Television and news websites can report an earthquake or a political announcement within minutes, whereas a newspaper reader must wait until the following morning. Online sources are free, can be reached anywhere on a phone, and offer video, audio and live updates that print cannot match. Social media also allows ordinary people to report events directly from places where journalists are not present.

In my opinion the two are best used together. I rely on my phone for immediate headlines, but I turn to established newspapers, whether printed or online, when I want to understand why something has happened. The main danger of social media is that it spreads unverified claims extremely quickly, and professional journalism remains the only effective check on this.

In conclusion, other media are better for speed and access, but newspapers continue to offer the most trustworthy analysis.

Key words and phrases

more carefully verified than anything else available
Checked more thoroughly than alternative information.
must wait until the following morning
Cannot publish or read it until tomorrow morning.
unverified claims
Statements not checked for accuracy.
the only effective check on this
The only measure that successfully limits it.

Activity

A long journey you travelled by car

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a long journey you travelled by car

You should say: what it was when and where it happened who was involved And explain if you enjoyed it or not.

OUTLINE

>>> Hue → Hanoi road trip
travelled w/ three close friends agreed meet house 5 a.m
like describe long exciting car journey took last week hometown Hue
After driving day arrived Hanoi late around 6 30 p.m. continued
great chance see country different angle road trip w/ mates
car trip helped clear mind bc hit open road leave worries
great opportunity friends enjoy other's company exchange stories along way

Simple Model Answer

A long car journey I took was from Hue, my hometown, to Hanoi last week. I travelled with three close friends. We met at my home at five in the morning because we wanted to leave before the roads became too busy.

We shared the driving and enjoyed good weather for most of the journey. We listened to music, talked and watched the countryside pass. Going by car meant we could stop for food or a view whenever we wanted. That freedom made the long distance feel less tiring.

We arrived in Hanoi at about half past six in the evening. Before going to our accommodation, we spent some time near the lake and stopped at a familiar cafe. Looking back, the journey was as enjoyable as the destination. Usually, I would fly or take a train and miss the smaller places along the way. Being together in the car also gave us a chance to catch up properly. It felt good to put everyday worries aside and spend a whole day with friends.

Key words and phrases

catch up properly
Spend enough time sharing news after not talking much for a while.
put everyday worries aside
Stop thinking about ordinary problems for a period of time.

Advanced Model Answer

I'll tell you about a long car journey I took last week, driving up from my hometown, Hue, all the way to Hanoi with three close friends.

We'd planned to leave really early to miss the worst of the traffic, so we all met at my place at 5 a.m. I'm not exactly a morning person, but somehow I managed to get up that early and be ready on time! We took it in turns driving, and since the weather was gorgeous that day, we drove most of the way with the windows down, blasting music and just chatting. There's something about the fresh air and the quiet countryside rolling past that made me feel relaxed but energetic at the same time.

We didn't actually reach Hanoi until around half six in the evening, and instead of heading straight to where we were staying, we drove a couple of laps around the lake as the sun went down and the streets started to quieten. Then we parked up and grabbed coffee at this little cafe we always go to, just to unwind after being in the car all day.

What made the trip so good, honestly, wasn't really Hanoi itself - it was the drive there. Normally I'd just fly or take the train and miss all of it, but going by car meant we could stop wherever we felt like, whether that was a roadside food stall or just some random viewpoint that looked nice. It really let me hit the open road and leave my worries behind for a day, and being stuck in a car together for that long meant we actually talked properly instead of just messaging each other - catching up properly for the first time in ages.

Key words and phrases

get up that early
Wake and leave bed at that early hour.
made me feel relaxed but energetic
I felt calm while still full of energy.
hit the open road
Begin travelling on a road without a fixed route.
leave my worries behind
Stop thinking about things troubling me.
catching up properly
Sharing recent news in a meaningful conversation.

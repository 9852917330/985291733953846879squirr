Semi-formal

Request to pay rent late

Semi-formal

Prompt

You are having financial difficulties and need to ask your landlord for permission to pay this month's rent late. Write to your landlord. In your letter: - explain the reason - state when you will pay - explain how you will avoid the problem in future

Model answer

Dear Mr Lewis,

I am writing to ask whether I may pay this month's rent on 15 July rather than on the usual first day of the month.

The delay is due to a temporary cash-flow problem. My employer changed its payroll provider last week, and several salaries, including mine, will not be processed until 12 July. I have sufficient income to cover the rent, but the funds will not reach my account before the normal payment date.

I will make the full payment of £950 immediately after my salary is deposited and will send you the transfer confirmation that day. I apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate that regular payment is important.

To prevent a similar delay, I will keep one month's rent in a separate savings account and set up a standing order for future payments. This means the rent will be transferred automatically even if my salary date changes again.

I hope this temporary arrangement is acceptable. Please let me know if you need written confirmation from my employer.

Yours sincerely, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

make the full payment
Pay the whole amount.
set up a standing order
Arrange automatic regular bank payments.

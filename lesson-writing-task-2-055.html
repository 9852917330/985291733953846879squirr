Education

Is a gap year a good idea?

Essay

Prompt

Some people believe that a gap year between school and university is a good idea, while others disagree strongly. Consider both sides of this debate and present your own opinion.

Model answer

Gap years, in which school leavers delay university for a year to travel or volunteer, have become increasingly common, and opinion is divided about whether they are worth the disruption. In my opinion the benefits usually justify a gap year provided it has a clear purpose, though the arguments against it are real.

The case against is mainly practical. A year of flights, accommodation and living costs is a real burden that often falls on the family or has to be borrowed against. Travelling for months can also blunt academic momentum, making it harder to return to essay deadlines and exam discipline than for a student who has come straight from school.

Safety is a further concern, particularly for young people travelling independently for the first time. Without the structure of a school trip or a university term, a gap-year traveller is more exposed to medical emergencies, transport accidents and petty crime, often somewhere language barriers make help harder to find.

On the other hand, supporters of gap years say that the skills learned outweigh the disadvantages. They highlight the independence and assertiveness that a student will acquire, in areas such as time management and interpersonal skills. They also say that these skills lead to greater career success later in life, which cancels out the costs involved. A student who spends six months teaching English or volunteering on a conservation project, for instance, returns with organisational experience and a self-reliance that few school leavers have, and this is precisely what stands out on an application.

In conclusion, my own feeling is that a year off can indeed be a useful activity, with the experience leading to improved skills that are invaluable in the future. Of course, this is provided that the year is planned carefully and used for something genuinely worthwhile.

Key words and phrases

blunt academic momentum
Slow progress and motivation in studying.
a self-reliance that few school leavers have
Unusual confidence in managing without others' help.
what stands out on an application
The feature that makes an application noticeable.
genuinely worthwhile
Truly worth the time or effort.

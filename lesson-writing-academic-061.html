Crime & Safety

UK vehicle offences

Academic Task 1

Prompt

The table below shows motor vehicle offences in England and Wales between 1994 and 2004. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.

Model answer

The table shows the numbers of six different types of motor vehicle offence recorded in England and Wales between 1994 and 2004.

Overall, speed limit offences and licence offences were the two main types of motor vehicle offence over this period, and speed limit offences stood out because they rose so sharply.

In 1994, licence, insurance and record keeping offences were the most common type of offence, and the figure rose to 957 thousand in 2004 after a ten-year period of fluctuations. Speed limit offences initially ranked second in 1994 but became the main type of offence by 2004, recording a ten-year high of 2,076 thousand, more than three times the figure ten years earlier. Neglect of traffic signs and directions came third, averaging at around 250 thousand per year.

Dangerous, careless or drunken driving was the fourth main type of offence, falling for much of the period from 1994 to 2004 but finally hitting a high for the period shown of 231 thousand. Two other types, accident offences and the unauthorised taking or theft of motor vehicles, were the least common, also declining gradually.

Computers

Using computers and their effect on health

Part 1

Prompt

When did you first use a computer? Do you think computers are bad for your health? Which computer software do you use most? What did you mainly use your first computer for?

When did you first use a computer?

Simple Model Answer

I first used a desktop computer at primary school. We practised typing and used simple drawing programs. I was amazed that it could do things so quickly when we gave it instructions.

Key words and phrases

amazed
Very surprised by something impressive.

Advanced Model Answer

I first used a desktop computer at primary school. We learned basic typing and simple drawing programs, and I remember being fascinated by how quickly the machine responded to instructions.

Key words and phrases

responded to instructions
Acted according to commands.

Do you think computers are bad for your health?

Simple Model Answer

They can be bad for your health if you sit badly or use them for hours without a break. I think the main issue is how we use them.

Key words and phrases

without a break
Continuously, without stopping to rest.

Advanced Model Answer

They can be harmful if people sit for hours with poor posture or stare at the screen without breaks. The problem usually isn't the computer itself, but the way it's used.

Key words and phrases

the way it's used
How people use it.

Which computer software do you use most?

Simple Model Answer

I mainly use internet browsers, programs for writing documents and spreadsheets. They help with most of my work, including preparing lessons and keeping information organised.

Key words and phrases

keeping information organised
Arranging facts or records so they are easy to use.

Advanced Model Answer

I use web browsers, word-processing software and spreadsheets most often. They support almost every part of my work, from lesson preparation to organising information efficiently.

Key words and phrases

organising information efficiently
Arranging facts without wasting time.

What did you mainly use your first computer for?

Simple Model Answer

I mainly used it for homework, finding information and educational games. Later, it became important for talking to people and learning new things on my own.

Key words and phrases

on my own
Without help from other people.

Advanced Model Answer

My first computer was mainly for school assignments, basic research and educational games. Over time it became an essential tool for communication and independent learning.

Key words and phrases

independent learning
Learning through your own efforts.

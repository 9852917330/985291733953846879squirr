Object & Favourite

A useful electric rice cooker

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a piece of technology (not computer—related) you like to use

You should say: What the technology is When you started using it What it is used for How you feel about it

OUTLINE

>>> An electric rice cooker
Bought it when I began cooking more meals at home
A simple bowl, lid and cook/keep-warm switch
Previously had to watch a saucepan to avoid boiling over
Measure rice and water, start it, then prepare the other dishes
Still needs suitable quantities and regular cleaning
A reliable tool that makes an everyday task easier

Simple Model Answer

A piece of technology I like using is my electric rice cooker. I bought it when I began preparing more meals at home. It is a simple model with a removable bowl and a switch for cooking or keeping the rice warm.

Before owning it, I cooked rice in a saucepan and often had to watch it carefully. Sometimes the water boiled over, or the rice stuck to the bottom. With the cooker, I measure the rice and water, close the lid and let it work while I prepare the rest of the meal.

I like it because it makes an ordinary task more reliable. I still need to use suitable amounts and clean the bowl, so it does not do everything for me. However, it saves attention at a busy time of day. It is not an exciting new invention, but I use it regularly and would miss it if it stopped working. For me, useful technology solves a real everyday problem.

Key words and phrases

keeping the rice warm
Maintain the rice at a warm temperature.
stuck to the bottom
Attached to the base of a container instead of coming away easily.

Advanced Model Answer

A piece of technology I genuinely appreciate is a basic electric rice cooker. I bought it after deciding to prepare more meals at home, and it has become one of the most frequently used appliances in my kitchen. It has no connection to the internet and very few controls, which is part of its appeal.

Previously, I cooked rice in a saucepan and had to keep checking it while preparing other dishes. I occasionally let the water boil over or ended up with rice stuck to the bottom. The cooker makes the process more predictable. Once I have measured the ingredients and started it, I can focus on the rest of the meal rather than divide my attention between several pans.

Of course, I still need to use appropriate quantities and clean it properly. What I value is not complete automation but the reduction of a small, repetitive demand on my attention. The device is neither fashionable nor particularly expensive, yet it makes something I do almost every day easier. It is a good reminder that technological value does not always come from adding more features. Sometimes a dependable tool that performs one task consistently is more useful than a complicated device that promises to do everything.

Key words and phrases

part of its appeal
One reason why something is attractive or enjoyable.
makes the process more predictable
Makes the result easier to expect in advance.
divide my attention
Try to focus on several things at once.
a dependable tool
A device that can be trusted to work reliably.

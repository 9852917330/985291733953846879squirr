Shopping

Brands, and whether they matter

Part 1

Prompt

Are you loyal to any brands? Do you want other people to know you buy a particular brand? Do you buy any products without considering the brand name? How do you feel about going shopping?

Are you loyal to any brands?

Simple Model Answer

I keep buying a brand only after its products have worked well for me. I compare quality, price and help after purchase, because those matter more than the name.

Key words and phrases

after purchase
After you have bought something.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm only loyal to a brand once it's proved consistently reliable. I usually compare quality, after-sales service and price first, because I care more about how well something works than the brand name.

Key words and phrases

consistently reliable
Dependable on repeated occasions.

Do you want other people to know you buy a particular brand?

Simple Model Answer

No, I don't need people to notice the brands I buy. I mainly want products that work properly. Buying something just to impress others matters less to me than getting good value.

Key words and phrases

good value
Worth the money you pay for it.

Advanced Model Answer

Not really. I just want products that work well, and I don't really see brand names as a way to show off. That kind of shopping can easily become more about image than value.

Key words and phrases

show off
Try to impress others with your abilities or possessions.
more about image than value
Focused on appearance rather than real usefulness.

Do you buy any products without considering the brand name?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, with basic food, pens, paper and things for the home. I usually check what they contain, how long they last and their price rather than choosing a familiar name.

Key words and phrases

familiar
Well known to you or easy to recognise.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, definitely, especially with things like basic food, stationery and household goods. For simple stuff like that, I'll compare ingredients, durability or price rather than relying on brand recognition.

Key words and phrases

relying on brand recognition
Depending on customers knowing the brand name.

How do you feel about going shopping?

Simple Model Answer

I see shopping mainly as a task, not entertainment. I enjoy it when I know what I need, but crowds and too much choice can make it take longer than necessary.

Key words and phrases

necessary
Needed for a particular purpose.

Advanced Model Answer

I don't really see shopping as entertainment, more of a practical task. I enjoy it when I know exactly what I need, but crowded shops and too many choices can make it needlessly time-consuming.

Key words and phrases

a practical task
A job with a useful real-world result.

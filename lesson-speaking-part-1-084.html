Travel & Holidays

The benefits of visiting other countries

Part 1

Prompt

What are the benefits of visiting different countries? What are the negative effects of tourism? What do you need to do before you go abroad on holiday? What type of problems can people have on holiday?

What are the benefits of visiting different countries?

Simple Model Answer

Travelling abroad can help us question our ideas, communicate better and learn through real experiences. It is especially useful when we spend time with local people and see how they live.

Key words and phrases

abroad
In or to another country.

Advanced Model Answer

Visiting other countries can challenge assumptions, improve communication and provide first-hand knowledge you just can't get from books. It's especially valuable when travellers engage with local life rather than staying in a tourist bubble.

Key words and phrases

engage with local life
Participate in the community's everyday activities.

What are the negative effects of tourism?

Simple Model Answer

Too many tourists can make housing more expensive, crowd public places and damage nature. Tourism becomes a problem when visitor numbers grow beyond what an area can comfortably support.

Key words and phrases

beyond
Further or more than a particular limit.

Advanced Model Answer

Uncontrolled tourism can raise housing costs, overcrowd public spaces and damage natural sites. The problem isn't tourism itself — it's growth that ignores local carrying capacity.

Key words and phrases

local carrying capacity
How many visitors an area can support.

What do you need to do before you go abroad on holiday?

Simple Model Answer

I check the rules for entering the country, insurance, travel, a place to stay and local customs. Planning helps me avoid problems, but I still leave time for unexpected activities.

Key words and phrases

customs
The usual traditions and ways of behaving in a place.

Advanced Model Answer

I check entry rules, insurance, transport, accommodation and basic local customs. Careful preparation means I don't get caught out, while still leaving enough freedom for spontaneous experiences.

Key words and phrases

spontaneous experiences
Activities that happen without advance planning.

What type of problems can people have on holiday?

Simple Model Answer

People may face travel delays, illness, lost passports or costs they did not expect. Insurance, copies of documents and a sensible second plan can make those problems easier to handle.

Key words and phrases

delays
Situations in which something happens later than planned.

Advanced Model Answer

Common holiday problems include delayed transport, illness, lost documents and unexpected costs, but they're much easier to manage with travel insurance, digital copies of documents and a realistic backup plan.

Key words and phrases

a realistic backup plan
A workable alternative if the original plan fails.

Core Story · Famous Person

David Attenborough

Core Story

Prompt

Describe a famous person you admire.

You should say: - who this person is - how you know about him - what he has achieved - and explain why you admire him.

OUTLINE

nature documentaries • calm voice • clear explanations

Simple Model Answer

A famous person I admire is David Attenborough, the British presenter known for nature documentaries. I first learned about him through his wildlife programmes. I noticed his calm voice and the clear way he explained animal behaviour without using too much difficult language.

His career has lasted many years, but that is not the main reason I respect him. His work has helped people become interested in wildlife and understand why it needs protection. He can make a tiny insect seem as interesting as a large animal. More recently, he has also discussed climate change and the loss of species.

What I admire most is his ability to share knowledge. He usually keeps attention on the subject rather than himself. I think that is an important quality in an expert. Knowing a great deal is useful, but helping other people become curious can have an even wider effect. His programmes show how powerful good explanations can be.

Key words and phrases

protection
Action taken to keep something safe from harm.
curious
Interested in learning or discovering more.

Advanced Model Answer

A famous person I admire is David Attenborough, the British broadcaster and natural historian known for presenting wildlife documentaries.

I first became familiar with his work through nature programmes. What immediately stood out was his voice and the way he explained complex behaviour without sounding dramatic or technical. He could describe a tiny insect or a huge ecosystem with the same level of curiosity.

His career is impressive because it has lasted for decades, but longevity is not the main reason I admire him. What matters more is the effect of his work. He has helped millions of people see wildlife as something worth understanding and protecting, rather than as background scenery. In recent years, he has also spoken clearly about climate change and biodiversity loss.

I admire him because he combines expertise with communication. He rarely makes himself the centre of the story; the subject remains more important than the presenter. That is a quality I respect in any expert. Being knowledgeable is valuable, but being able to make other people curious is a much rarer skill.

Key words and phrases

What immediately stood out
The thing I noticed straight away.
the effect of his work
The changes his work produces.
worth understanding and protecting
Important enough to learn about and keep safe.
combines expertise with communication
Brings together specialist knowledge and clear explanation.
make other people curious
Encourage others to want to learn more.

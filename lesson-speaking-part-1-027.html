Films

Watching films alone, with friends or at the cinema

Part 1

Prompt

Do you enjoy watching movies? Do you prefer to see a movie alone or with your friends? Do you prefer watching films at the cinema or at home? What is your most/least favourite type of movie?

Do you enjoy watching movies?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, especially dramas with good stories and documentaries. Watching a film helps me relax, and it can also help me understand a situation from someone else's point of view.

Key words and phrases

point of view
The way a person thinks about or understands something.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, especially well-written dramas and documentaries. A good film allows me to relax while seeing a situation from a different point of view.

Key words and phrases

a different point of view
Another way of understanding something.

Do you prefer to see a movie alone or with your friends?

Simple Model Answer

I usually watch with friends because I enjoy talking about the film afterwards. However, I sometimes watch complicated or serious films alone so I can concentrate on the details.

Key words and phrases

concentrate on
Give your full attention to something.

Advanced Model Answer

I usually watch films with friends because discussing them afterwards adds to the experience. For serious or complex films, however, I sometimes prefer watching alone so I can pay close attention to the details.

Key words and phrases

pay close attention to
Watch or consider something carefully.

Do you prefer watching films at the cinema or at home?

Simple Model Answer

For films with impressive pictures, I prefer the cinema. For other films, home is easier because I can pause or choose my own speed. Both have advantages, depending on the film.

Key words and phrases

pause
Stop something for a short time before continuing.

Advanced Model Answer

I prefer the cinema for visually impressive films, but home's more convenient for ordinary viewing. The cinema offers immersion, while home allows me to pause and watch at my own pace.

Key words and phrases

visually impressive
Remarkable to look at.
at my own pace
At a speed that suits me.

What is your most/least favourite type of movie?

Simple Model Answer

I like realistic dramas most because I enjoy believable people and meaningful feelings. I am least interested in horror films that repeat the same ideas and have predictable stories.

Key words and phrases

predictable
Easy to guess before it happens.

Advanced Model Answer

My favourite genre is realistic drama, and I'm least interested in formulaic horror films. I prefer stories with convincing characters and emotional depth.

Key words and phrases

emotional depth
Rich, believable and complex feelings.

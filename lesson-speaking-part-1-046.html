Your job and your plans for your career

Part 1

Prompt

Can you tell me about the job you do? What do you like or dislike about it? What plans do you have for your career in the future? How serious is unemployment in your country?

Can you tell me about the job you do?

Simple Model Answer

I'm an English teacher. I plan lessons, explain difficult language and give students advice on their work. My main aim is to help them improve step by step.

Key words and phrases

step by step
Gradually, through a series of small stages.

Advanced Model Answer

I work as an English teacher. My job involves planning lessons, explaining tricky points, and giving feedback that helps students make steady progress.

Key words and phrases

make steady progress
Improve at a regular pace.

What do you like or dislike about it?

Simple Model Answer

I like seeing students become more confident and able to learn by themselves. However, preparing lessons and checking work takes a lot of time, especially when several tasks are due together.

Key words and phrases

due
Needing to be finished or handed in by a particular time.

Advanced Model Answer

I like seeing students become more confident and independent. The hard part's the amount of preparation and marking, especially when a few deadlines fall in the same week.

Key words and phrases

fall in the same week
Occur during one particular week.

What plans do you have for your career in the future?

Simple Model Answer

I want to become a better teacher and use technology more effectively in class. Later, I'd like to make learning materials that are useful, clear and carefully checked.

Key words and phrases

effectively
In a way that produces the result you want.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to develop my teaching skills further and get better at using technology in my classes. Longer term, I'd love to create learning materials that are both practical and rigorous.

Key words and phrases

both practical and rigorous
Useful in real situations and carefully demanding.

How serious is unemployment in your country?

Simple Model Answer

It depends on the type of work. Many young graduates find it hard to get jobs related to their studies. Sometimes jobs exist, but applicants do not have the skills employers need.

Key words and phrases

graduates
People who have completed a university course.

Advanced Model Answer

It varies a lot by sector, but young graduates often struggle to find work that matches what they've trained for. It's not just about the number of jobs available — it's also the mismatch of skills.

Key words and phrases

the mismatch of skills
Skills people have do not match job needs.

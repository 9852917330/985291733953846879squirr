Food

Feeding a guest, and popular food in your country

Part 3

Prompt

If you invite a friend, what meal are you likely to offer them? What kinds of food are popular in your country? What are people's eating habits like in your country?

If you invite a friend, what meal are you likely to offer them?

Simple Model Answer

I'd offer shared dishes such as grilled meat or tofu, rice, vegetables and soup. Guests can choose what they enjoy, and I can spend time with them instead of cooking all evening. However, I would ask about serious allergies or dietary needs beforehand. A flexible meal is useful, but it does not replace checking important restrictions.

Key words and phrases

dietary needs
Particular requirements about what a person can or should eat.

Advanced Model Answer

Something shared, usually — grilled meat or tofu, rice, a couple of vegetable dishes and a soup, all placed in the middle for people to help themselves. That style works well because everyone simply takes what they want, so it accommodates different tastes without my having to ask anybody in advance. It also means I'm not stuck in the kitchen all evening, which rather defeats the purpose of inviting somebody. The one time it doesn't work is a serious allergy or dietary restriction, where I do still have to ask ahead rather than leave it to chance.

Key words and phrases

accommodates different tastes
offers choices for people with different preferences
a serious allergy or dietary restriction
a dangerous reaction to food or a need to avoid certain foods

What kinds of food are popular in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Rice and noodles are central, and street food is popular because it is quick, affordable and often very good. Different regions also take pride in their special dishes. International chains and convenience foods are growing, especially among younger people. Even so, traditional meals remain common because they are affordable and closely connected with local culture.

Key words and phrases

take pride in
Feel pleased and proud about something connected with you.

Advanced Model Answer

Rice and noodle dishes are the foundation of almost every meal, and street food is enormously popular because it's cheap, fast and often better than what restaurants serve, and regional specialities matter a great deal too; people are quite proud about which province prepares a particular dish properly. Convenience food and international chains have grown steadily, especially among younger people, but traditional dishes still dominate because they're affordable and tied closely to cultural identity.

Key words and phrases

regional specialities
foods particularly associated with a local area

What are people's eating habits like in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Some city residents eat more processed meals and order food because work leaves little time to cook. Others are becoming more careful about ingredients and avoiding too much sugar. Both trends can happen in the same area. Many older people, meanwhile, continue preparing the same simple meals they have always eaten, outside either new trend.

Key words and phrases

processed meals
Food changed or prepared in a factory before being sold.

Advanced Model Answer

They're changing in two opposite directions at once. On one side, people in cities eat far more processed food and order delivery constantly, mainly because working hours leave no time to cook. On the other, a growing group pays close attention to ingredients, buying organic produce and avoiding sugar. So you find health food shops opening on the same street as fast food outlets, and both doing well at the same time. Older generations sit slightly outside both trends, mind, since plenty of grandparents cook the same simple, unprocessed meals they always have, largely untouched by either shift.

Key words and phrases

leave no time to cook
make people too busy to prepare a meal
both doing well
both succeeding or staying healthy

Situation

An experience when you spent time with a child

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an experience when you spent time with a child.

You should say: When you spent time with this child Who this child was What you did together And how you felt about the experience.

Simple Model Answer

A time I spent with a child was when I looked after my two-year-old nephew. I was about fifteen and visiting my brother's family for the weekend. His wife was at work, and he needed to go shopping for groceries.

He asked me to stay with his son for about half an hour. I was nervous because I had never cared for a small child alone. However, he said I could phone if there was a problem. Once he left, I played my nephew's favourite cartoon, which kept him interested for about fifteen minutes.

Then we played with toys on the living-room floor. When he became bored again, I found some snacks and we watched more cartoons. Soon afterwards, he fell asleep and stayed asleep until my brother returned. I felt relieved that everything had gone well. The experience was easier than I had feared, although I noticed how quickly a young child needs a new activity. It gave me more confidence and a better understanding of the attention involved in looking after children.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to tell you about the time I looked after a young child, my two-year-old nephew. I was about fifteen at the time, and it happened one weekend when I was visiting my brother, his wife and their son. My brother's wife was at work that day, and he needed to nip out to the shops for groceries. Instead of all of us going together, he decided it'd be much more convenient if he went alone and I stayed home to look after my nephew.

At first I was honestly a bit shocked he'd even suggest it - I'd never looked after a small child alone before, and I didn't have a clue what to do if something went wrong. But he reassured me he'd only be gone about half an hour, and that I should call him for any emergency. So, reluctantly, I agreed.

Once he'd left, I put on one of my nephew's favourite cartoons to try to keep him occupied, but that only bought me about fifteen minutes before he got bored and wanted something else. So I brought all his toys out and tipped them onto the lounge floor, and we made a proper mess playing with those for a while, though that only lasted about ten minutes too. After that I found some of his favourite snacks in the kitchen, and we sat back down to watch more cartoons while he ate. About five minutes later, to my relief, he actually fell asleep, and stayed asleep until my brother got back.

All in all, it turned out to be a pretty painless experience. I was relieved nothing had gone wrong, and it taught me that looking after small kids isn't nearly as daunting as it seems.

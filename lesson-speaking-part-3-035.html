What makes a good lesson, and teaching yourself

Prompt

What do you think makes a good lesson? Do you think it is better to have a teacher or to teach yourself? Do you think the traditional classroom will disappear in the future?

What do you think makes a good lesson?

Simple Model Answer

A good lesson makes students interested and actively involved. They should understand why the topic is useful, and the teacher needs to explain it in a way they can follow. My university teacher sometimes asked us to teach parts ourselves. That worked well for confident students, but quieter students needed a different way to participate.

Key words and phrases

actively involved
Taking part rather than only watching or listening.

Advanced Model Answer

I think a good lesson is one that's interesting and engaging. By 'engaging' I mean the students should feel actually involved, not just present — they should feel they're learning something relevant to them. A lot depends on how the teacher delivers the content, too, in a way students actually respond to. My favourite teacher at university used to get us to teach parts of the lessons ourselves, which worked brilliantly for confident students, though it left the quieter students visibly uncomfortable.

Key words and phrases

relevant to them
important to their own lives
delivers the content
provides the information to learners

Do you think it is better to have a teacher or to teach yourself?

Simple Model Answer

Usually a good teacher helps people learn faster by noticing and correcting mistakes. Language learners especially benefit from feedback on their speaking. Teachers also provide structure when motivation falls. However, an adult with a clear goal and strong motivation may study successfully alone, particularly for a specific exam. So it depends partly on the learner.

Key words and phrases

motivation
The desire or reason that makes you want to do something.

Advanced Model Answer

Well, there's no substitute for a good teacher, in most cases. You can teach yourself, but you'll usually learn a lot faster with the guidance of a teacher who can see your mistakes as you make them. When you're learning a language, for instance, you really need someone to correct your mistakes — books can give you grammar and vocabulary, but they can't tell you where you're actually going wrong as you speak. A teacher also keeps you working when your motivation drops, which is usually what defeats people studying entirely alone. The exception's a highly motivated adult learner with a specific, narrow goal — someone cramming for a work exam can often manage perfectly well with self-study materials alone.

Key words and phrases

there's no substitute for
nothing else can do the same job as
with the guidance of a teacher
with help and direction from a teacher
correct your mistakes
show you where you are wrong so you can improve

Do you think the traditional classroom will disappear in the future?

Simple Model Answer

I don't think it will disappear. Students may learn facts online and use class time for discussion, practice and feedback. However, many still need a set time, a teacher and a shared space to keep working steadily. Online-only study can suit independent adults, but a mixture is likely to work better for many learners.

Key words and phrases

independent adults
Adults who can organise and manage their learning without much help.

Advanced Model Answer

The traditional classroom won't disappear, but it may become less dominant. Students can learn factual content online now and use classroom time for discussion, feedback and practical work instead, so the teacher's role may shift from delivering information to guiding and evaluating learning. Teaching moved online during the pandemic and that made the limits fairly clear: students could reach the material perfectly well, but attainment still fell, because most people need the structure of a fixed time, a room, and somebody who notices when they've stopped trying. The exception's a small minority of highly self-directed adult learners, for whom online-only study genuinely does work.

Key words and phrases

traditional classroom
a place where teacher and students meet in person
guiding and evaluating learning
helping people learn and checking what they understand

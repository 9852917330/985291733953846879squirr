Education

A year of work experience before university

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

Some people believe that students who graduate from high school should spend a year gaining work experience before going to university. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

It is sometimes suggested that school leavers should spend a year gaining work experience before beginning a university course. I agree with this proposal, provided that the year is genuinely structured rather than simply a delay.

The strongest argument concerns the choice of course. Most students select a degree at seventeen, with almost no knowledge of what the corresponding profession actually involves, and a significant proportion later change subject or abandon their studies altogether. A year in a hospital, an office or a laboratory gives a far more realistic impression, and a student who confirms their interest in this way studies with much greater purpose.

Work experience also develops qualities that school does not. Handling money, dealing with difficult customers, arriving punctually and working with colleagues of different ages teach responsibility in a way that examinations cannot. Students who have supported themselves financially also tend to manage their time more carefully at university, having learned what an hour of work is actually worth.

There are objections worth acknowledging. Some students lose academic momentum and find it hard to return to studying, and a year of unpaid work is easier for wealthy families to arrange than for poor ones, which risks creating a further advantage for those who already have one.

In conclusion, I agree that a year of work experience benefits most school leavers, since it improves their choice of course and their maturity. It should, however, be organised and paid, so that it is available to every student rather than only to those who can afford it.

Key words and phrases

abandon their studies altogether
Stop studying completely.
a far more realistic impression
A much more accurate understanding of reality.
manage their time more carefully
Plan and use their time more thoughtfully.
what an hour of work is actually worth
The real value of time spent earning.
lose academic momentum
Lose their rhythm and progress in studying.

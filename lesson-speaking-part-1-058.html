Parks

Visiting parks and why towns need them

Prompt

Do you like parks? How often do you visit parks? Why are parks an important part of many towns and cities? What do you think could be done to make parks better?

Do you like parks?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I like them because I can relax or walk there. Busy cities need green places where people can take a break from crowds and noise.

Key words and phrases

green places
Areas with grass, trees or other plants.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I like parks because they're great places to relax or go for a walk. I think every city needs some green space where people can escape from the crowds.

Key words and phrases

escape from the crowds
Get away from places where there are many people.

How often do you visit parks?

Simple Model Answer

There is a park near my home, so I usually visit once or twice a week when the weather is good. Being close makes it easy to go regularly.

Key words and phrases

once or twice a week
One or two times during a week.

Advanced Model Answer

There's a park very close to where I live, so I probably go there once or twice a week if the weather's nice.

Key words and phrases

once or twice a week
One or two times each week.

Why are parks an important part of many towns and cities?

Simple Model Answer

Parks give people a quieter place away from busy streets. People can eat lunch, exercise or stop thinking about work for a while, so they make city life more pleasant.

Key words and phrases

for a while
For a short period of time.

Advanced Model Answer

Parks are like an oasis where people get away from the hustle and bustle of city life. They are where people go to have lunch, to take some exercise, or to forget about work for a while.

Key words and phrases

the hustle and bustle
Busy, noisy activity.
take some exercise
Do physical activity.

What do you think could be done to make parks better?

Simple Model Answer

Cities could improve paths, add shaded seats and toilets that everyone can use, and look after parks better. Quiet areas should also be protected from too many shops or other business activities.

Key words and phrases

shaded
Protected from direct sunlight.

Advanced Model Answer

Even well-designed parks have room for improvement. Cities could add safer paths, accessible toilets, shaded seating and better maintenance, while protecting quiet areas from excessive commercial activity.

Key words and phrases

room for improvement
Things that could become better.

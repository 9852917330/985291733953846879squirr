Writing

How computers changed writing, and the future of it

Part 3

Prompt

What effect have computers had on the way we write? What are the advantages and disadvantages of email? Will writing remain an important skill in the future?

What effect have computers had on the way we write?

Simple Model Answer

Computers make editing and reorganising writing much easier. People no longer need to copy everything again to change a structure. Sharing and searching also help people work together. However, easy editing may encourage starting before thinking, and automatic spelling correction can make people less aware of mistakes. The tools help, but careful attention is still needed.

Key words and phrases

editing
Changing written work to improve or correct it.

Advanced Model Answer

The biggest change is that writing became revisable. On paper, restructuring a piece meant copying the whole thing out again, so people planned carefully before they started. Now you can rearrange anything instantly, which is liberating but does encourage writing before thinking. Sharing and searching have transformed collaboration as well. The loss is at the level of detail, though — with autocorrect handling spelling, a lot of people have simply stopped noticing their own errors.

Key words and phrases

writing before thinking
beginning to write before deciding what to say

What are the advantages and disadvantages of email?

Simple Model Answer

Email is fast, cheap and creates a record that can be searched later. This is useful for checking agreements. However, written tone can be misunderstood, and too many unnecessary messages can hide important information. Copying everyone into every conversation increases that problem. Email works best when people think about both the wording and who actually needs the message.

Key words and phrases

wording
The particular words chosen to express something.

Advanced Model Answer

It's fast, essentially free, and creates a searchable record, which is genuinely valuable when you need to establish what was agreed. The disadvantages are mostly about tone and volume. Written messages strip out the signals that stop a conversation going wrong, so something meant neutrally reads as cold. And because sending costs nothing, people get copied into everything, so important messages get lost among dozens that never needed to exist in the first place.

Key words and phrases

a searchable record
stored information that can be found by searching
get lost among dozens
be overlooked among many other messages

Will writing remain an important skill in the future?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, because people still need to judge whether writing is clear and accurate, even when AI produces a first draft. Someone must notice a wrong number or weak argument. Writing also helps us discover whether we understand an idea ourselves. Routine typing may become less important, but the ability to organise and evaluate meaning will remain valuable.

Key words and phrases

evaluate
Examine something carefully to judge its quality or usefulness.

Advanced Model Answer

More important, not less, I'd say. Artificial intelligence can produce a competent draft, but somebody still has to judge whether it's accurate, whether the argument holds and whether it says what was actually intended — a manager still has to notice when an AI-drafted report gets a figure wrong or misses the actual point, and you can't judge that if you can't write yourself. There's a deeper point too: writing something down is how you discover whether you genuinely understand it. That said, for routine writing — a quick email, a simple summary — nobody's going to miss doing it by hand, so the skill that'll matter is judgement, not typing practice.

Key words and phrases

a competent draft
a written first version that is good enough
genuinely understand it
really understand its meaning and workings

Formal

Problems with shared college accommodation

Formal

Prompt

You share a college room with another student, but the arrangement is making it difficult for you to study. Write to the college accommodation officer. In your letter: - describe the current situation - explain why it is affecting your work - state what type of accommodation you would prefer

Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to request a change to my current accommodation in West Hall, where I share Room 214 with another student.

Although my roommate is friendly, our routines are completely incompatible. He regularly invites several friends into the room late in the evening and often plays online games without headphones. In addition, he works night shifts and returns at around 2 a.m., which wakes me almost every day.

This situation is seriously affecting my ability to study because I am preparing for final examinations and need several uninterrupted hours of study each evening. I have tried discussing the matter with him, but the improvements have only been temporary. As a result, I am sleeping poorly and have already missed two morning lectures.

I would therefore be grateful if I could move to a single room, ideally in a quieter part of the residence. If no single room is available, I would be willing to share with a student who follows a more regular daytime schedule. I hope the college can help me find a quieter environment at your earliest convenience.

Yours faithfully, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

I am writing to request
I want to ask for.
seriously affecting my ability to study
Making studying much harder.
a quieter environment
A place with less noise.
at your earliest convenience
As soon as reasonably possible.

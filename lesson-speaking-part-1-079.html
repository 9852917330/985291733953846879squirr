Transport

Public transport where you live

Prompt

Do you use public transport? Which type of public transport do you prefer? How good is the public transport where you live? Do you think people should use public transport more?

Do you use public transport?

Simple Model Answer

I mainly drive now because it is faster for my usual trips. A journey I make twice a week takes half an hour by car but about three times longer by train and bus.

Key words and phrases

usual trips
The journeys you normally make.

Advanced Model Answer

These days I mostly drive, and the main reason is that my usual journeys are much faster by car. For example, the half-hour drive that I do twice a week would take me three times as long by train and bus.

Key words and phrases

three times as long
Lasting three times the amount of time.

Which type of public transport do you prefer?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer trains because I can get up and walk around. On some, I can reserve a seat with a table and a power socket, which lets me work during the journey.

Key words and phrases

reserve a seat
Arrange in advance to have a particular place to sit.

Advanced Model Answer

I definitely prefer the train. I like being able to stroll up and down, and on some trains I can book a seat with a table and a socket, so that I can do some work.

Key words and phrases

stroll up and down
Walk slowly back and forth.

How good is the public transport where you live?

Simple Model Answer

It is quite good. A nearby station has trains straight to the city centre, and there are many bus routes. Central Manchester also has trams, so there are several ways to travel.

Key words and phrases

trams
Passenger vehicles that travel on rails along city streets.

Advanced Model Answer

It's quite good. There's a train station not far from my house, and it's a direct line straight into the city centre. There are lots of bus routes too, and there's even a tram network in the centre of Manchester.

Key words and phrases

a direct line
A route without changing transport.

Do you think people should use public transport more?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, if it is convenient for their journeys. Using buses and trains instead of separate cars can reduce pollution, so I think it is a good choice when people can manage it.

Key words and phrases

convenient
Easy and suitable for your needs.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, as long as it's convenient for them. Public transport is obviously more environmentally friendly than private cars, so we could reduce air pollution by using it more.

Key words and phrases

environmentally friendly
Causing little harm to nature.
reduce air pollution
Decrease harmful substances in the air.

Leisure & Holidays

Morning or evening people, and what evenings are for

Part 3

Prompt

Are you a morning or evening type of person? What activities do people often do in the evening in your country? Is evening a good time for doing exercises?

Are you a morning or evening type of person?

Simple Model Answer

I'm a morning person because I concentrate best soon after waking, before messages and other demands build up. I try to do difficult work then and leave simpler tasks for the evening. Working late gives me more hours but poorer results. However, some people genuinely work better at night, so my routine would not suit everyone.

Key words and phrases

demands
Things requiring your time, effort or attention.

Advanced Model Answer

Definitely a morning person. My concentration's sharpest in the first few hours after waking, before messages start arriving and the day fills up, so that's when I try to do anything demanding. By the evening I'm fine for routine tasks but not for anything requiring real thought — I've tried working late plenty of times and it never lasts more than a few days, because I get the hours in but the quality drops noticeably. I know plenty of genuine night owls whose brains work the opposite way, though, so I wouldn't say it's a universal rule.

Key words and phrases

the quality drops noticeably
it becomes clearly less good
a universal rule
a rule that applies equally to everyone

What activities do people often do in the evening in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Many families eat dinner together, then people exercise, walk in parks or meet friends at cafes. Shopping areas can also become busy. For many workers, evening is their main personal time after a full day. However, people working from home may have more freedom to take breaks earlier, so patterns depend on work and household arrangements.

Key words and phrases

household arrangements
The way people organise living and responsibilities at home.

Advanced Model Answer

Eating together is probably the main one — dinner's still a family occasion in most households, far more so than lunch. After that it varies quite a bit. Loads of people exercise, either at a gym or walking and jogging in parks, which stay busy surprisingly late. Younger people meet friends at cafes, and shopping streets are often livelier in the evening than during the day. For working adults in big cities, evening is realistically the only personal time they get, though that's starting to change now that more people work from home and can grab an hour during the day instead.

Key words and phrases

the only personal time they get
the only period of the day they can use for themselves
grab an hour during the day
find one free hour during daytime

Is evening a good time for doing exercises?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, if it is the time people can manage regularly. Doing exercise in the evening is more useful than planning a morning session that never happens. However, very hard training close to bedtime may make it difficult to relax. I would leave time to calm down afterwards and choose gentler activity when it is already late.

Key words and phrases

calm down
Become more relaxed and less physically or mentally excited.

Advanced Model Answer

For most people, yes — often it's the only time available, and exercise you actually do beats exercise you theoretically should do at six in the morning before work. The one caution is intensity. Hard training raises your body temperature and leaves you too alert to sleep, so I'd finish anything intense at least an hour or two before bed. Anything moderate, like a walk or gentle swim, is fine right up until bedtime, but leave a proper gap to wind down afterwards.

Key words and phrases

leaves you too alert to sleep
makes you too awake to fall asleep
wind down
relax gradually before resting or sleeping

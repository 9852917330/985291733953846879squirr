Sports & Exercise

How people keep fit and why exercise matters

Part 3

Prompt

What do most people in your country do to keep fit? How important is it for people to do some regular physical exercise? Why do you think that modern lifestyles are not healthy?

What do most people in your country do to keep fit?

Simple Model Answer

Walking is very common, especially around parks and lakes early in the day. Younger city residents also use gyms, play badminton or football, and follow workout videos at home. Popular activities tend to be affordable and easy to fit around work. Rural areas may have fewer gyms and classes, though, so available choices differ by location.

Key words and phrases

fit around work
Be arranged at times that do not conflict with work.

Advanced Model Answer

Walking is the most common by a distance — parks and lakesides are busy from early morning, particularly with older people. Gym membership has grown enormously among younger adults in cities, and badminton and football are the popular team sports. There's also been a big rise in following workout videos at home, which took off during the pandemic and never really went away. What the popular activities share is that they're cheap and fit around a working day — though that's much truer in the cities than in rural areas, where gyms and classes simply aren't as available.

Key words and phrases

fit around a working day
be possible alongside regular working hours

How important is it for people to do some regular physical exercise?

Simple Model Answer

It is extremely important because normal life now involves so much sitting. People often need to plan movement or it never happens. Exercise supports health and helps maintain independence later in life. However, it does not solve everything: poor food habits or lack of sleep still matter. It should work alongside other healthy habits, not replace them.

Key words and phrases

independence
The ability to manage your life without needing constant help.

Advanced Model Answer

Extremely important, and more so than it used to be. Work and travel once involved a great deal of incidental movement, and modern life has removed almost all of it — we sit to work, sit to travel and sit to relax. So exercise now has to be deliberately scheduled or it simply doesn't happen. Beyond the cardiovascular benefits, it largely determines whether people stay independent in old age. It isn't a cure-all, though — it won't undo a genuinely poor diet or make up for years of bad sleep, so it has to work alongside the other things, not instead of them.

Key words and phrases

incidental movement
physical activity that happens during ordinary tasks
stay independent in old age
continue managing daily life without help when older
a cure-all
a solution wrongly claimed to fix every problem

Why do you think that modern lifestyles are not healthy?

Simple Model Answer

Several habits build on one another. People sit for long hours, stay up with screens, choose quick processed food and feel constantly available for work. Poor sleep can then make eating and activity habits worse. It becomes a cycle rather than one isolated problem. Improving health may therefore require several manageable changes instead of one dramatic solution.

Key words and phrases

a cycle
A repeated pattern in which effects lead back to similar causes.

Advanced Model Answer

It's an accumulation of small things rather than one large cause. People sit for eight or nine hours, sleep irregularly because screens keep them up, eat processed food because it's quick and cheap, and live under constant low-level stress from being permanently contactable. Any one of these alone would be manageable. The problem is that they reinforce one another — poor sleep makes you eat badly, and eating badly makes you inactive, so it becomes a cycle that's hard to break from just one direction.

Key words and phrases

reinforce one another
each makes the other stronger

Film & Television

Television for education or entertainment

Prompt

Do you think most people watch TV for education or for entertainment? Should TV play a role in educating children; how? How do you think TV viewing habits change as people get older?

Do you think most people watch TV for education or for entertainment?

Simple Model Answer

Mostly for entertainment. After work, people often want to relax with talent shows, dramas or other enjoyable programmes. There are many more of these than programmes mainly designed to teach. However, viewers can still learn from documentaries and news without making education their main goal, so entertainment and learning are not always completely separate.

Key words and phrases

entertainment
Activities or programmes people enjoy in their free time.

Advanced Model Answer

I think people watch TV primarily for entertainment. There are far more entertainment programmes than educational ones, and in my experience most people treat television as a form of relaxation in the evening. If I think about the most popular TV programmes in the UK, such as talent shows like 'X Factor' or soap operas like 'Eastenders', the focus is definitely on entertainment rather than education. That's not the whole picture, though — people do pick up a great deal by accident from documentaries and news programmes, so the education is often incidental rather than intended.

Key words and phrases

in my experience
based on what I have personally seen or done
incidental rather than intended
happening by chance instead of being planned

Should TV play a role in educating children; how?

Simple Model Answer

Yes. Stories can help children think about behaviour and right and wrong, while science programmes can build interest in the world. Fairy tales such as Cinderella can carry useful messages. However, young children may follow the story without understanding the lesson, so an adult discussing it with them can make the experience more valuable.

Key words and phrases

carry useful messages
Communicate ideas that people can learn from.

Advanced Model Answer

In my opinion, yes — TV should definitely play a role in educating children. Good children's TV programmes should tell stories that contain some kind of lesson about how to behave or what's morally right and wrong. Many of the traditional fairy tales, such as 'Cinderella', have been made into TV programmes, and there's always a positive message in those stories. Programmes about science and the natural world do much the same job for older children, and they often spark an interest that lasts. It can't replace an adult explaining things, though — a five-year-old watching alone will pick up the story but often miss the lesson underneath it.

Key words and phrases

play a role in
have an effect or take part in
spark an interest that lasts
cause someone to become interested for a long time
miss the lesson underneath it
fail to understand the deeper message it teaches

How do you think TV viewing habits change as people get older?

Simple Model Answer

Young children may watch talking animals, while teenagers often prefer action, sport or adventure. Adults may become more interested in news and politics; that happened to me. However, these are general patterns, not fixed rules. Some adults still enjoy cartoons or action films, and keeping those interests does not mean there is anything wrong with them.

Key words and phrases

general patterns
Common tendencies that do not apply to every individual.

Advanced Model Answer

TV viewing habits obviously change a lot as we get older. While toddlers might watch programmes about talking animals, teenagers prefer action and adventure or sports, and as adults we start taking more of an interest in news and politics. My own preferences, for example, have changed over the years — I'd never have watched news programmes when I was younger. That's not true for everyone, though; plenty of adults keep the same taste in cartoons or action films they had as teenagers, and I don't think that says anything bad about them.

Key words and phrases

changed over the years
become different as time passed

Describe a place in your city you want to go to

Simple Model Answer

A place in Ho Chi Minh City I want to visit again is the Bitexco Financial Tower. It is in the central business and entertainment area. The tower has sixty-eight floors, with offices, shops and restaurants inside.

Its shape was inspired by the lotus flower, which makes it different from many other tall buildings. The main attraction for me is the Sky Deck, where visitors can look across the city. I have been during the day, but next time I would like to go at sunset and watch the light change over the river and buildings.

I would take my parents because they have never seen the city from that height. I think they would be surprised by how much it has grown. Afterwards, we could have coffee upstairs and watch the traffic below. From far above, the busy roads look less stressful. I like this idea because it combines a new view with time spent together as a family.

Advanced Model Answer

Ho Chi Minh City is a popular tourist destination thanks to its fascinating culture, classic French architecture, sleek skyscrapers, and ornate temples and pagodas. Because of that, it's pretty difficult to pick out just one place among the millions of tourist attractions here.

Personally, I think Bitexco Financial Tower is the most impressive skyscraper. It's right in the heart of the city's business and entertainment district, and when it was completed in 2010, it was actually the tallest building in Vietnam — at least until January 2011.

The creative mastermind behind Bitexco drew inspiration for the skyscraper's unique shape from Vietnam's national flower, the lotus, which to Vietnamese people is a symbol of purity, commitment, and optimism. The 68-storey building's full of offices, shops, and restaurants, but the main attraction is the Sky Deck, where you can enjoy dramatic views right across the bustling city below.

There are lots of places I like to go in Ho Chi Minh City, depending on my mood, but if you want that hectic, modern buzz, Bitexco is an ideal attraction to consider.

As for what I'd actually like to do there, my main reason for wanting to go back is the Sky Deck at sunset. I've only ever been during the day, and I'm told that watching the light change over the river and the rooftops is a completely different experience. I'd also love to take my parents, since neither of them has seen the city from above, and I think they'd be genuinely surprised by how much it's grown since they moved here.

After that, I'd probably just stay for a coffee upstairs and watch the traffic, which from that height stops looking stressful and starts looking almost orderly.

Informal

Changing plans for a cinema trip

Informal

Prompt

You arranged to see a film with a friend, but you can no longer go. Another friend can take your place. Write to your friend. In your letter: - explain why you cannot attend - say who can go instead - explain why they would be good company

Model answer

Hi Ben,

I'm really sorry, but something urgent has come up and I won't be able to see the film with you on Saturday evening. My manager has asked me to cover a client presentation at short notice, and the meeting cannot be moved because the client is flying home on Sunday.

The good news is that Minh can step in at short notice. I checked with him before writing, and he's free at 7 p.m. He's already seen the first film in the series, so you won't have to explain the story, and he has offered to collect the tickets from the machine before you arrive.

I think you two will get on really well. You both love science-fiction films, follow the same football team and have a very similar sense of humour. I've given Minh your number, and he'll message you tomorrow to confirm where to meet.

I'm disappointed to miss it, but let's arrange another evening next week. Dinner is on me to make up for changing the plan.

All the best, Alex

Key words and phrases

something urgent has come up
An important unexpected matter needs attention.
step in at short notice
Help with very little warning.
get on really well
Have a very good relationship.

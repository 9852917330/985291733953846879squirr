Object & Favourite

A piece of clothing that you like wearing

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a piece of clothing that you like wearing.

You should say: - what the item of clothing is - where and when you bought it - when you wear it - and explain why you like wearing it.

Simple Model Answer

A piece of clothing I like is my dark blue coat. It is light, waterproof and warm enough for the cooler weather in the UK. It also has a hood that can be removed and several pockets inside and outside.

I bought it online at the start of autumn last year. I had owned the same model before, but my old coat was worn out. Because I already knew the right size, ordering a replacement was easy. It arrived a few days later, and I began wearing it immediately.

I use it almost every day, whether I am going to work or visiting the shops. My favourite thing is how practical it is. It keeps rain out, and the pockets hold my phone, wallet, keys and sometimes a small notebook. When the weather becomes warmer, I can fold it into my bag. It is not a special outfit for an important event, but it makes daily life easier. That is why it has become one of my most useful clothes.

Key words and phrases

waterproof
Designed to stop water passing through.
worn out
Damaged or no longer useful after being used a lot.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe a coat I wear all the time at the moment. It's dark blue, and it's made out of a lightweight, waterproof material, which makes it perfect for this time of year here in the UK. It's also got a detachable hood, which I actually used just yesterday when it was raining, plus various pockets on the outside and the inside.

I bought it last year, right at the beginning of autumn when the weather started turning colder. It was an easy purchase, actually, because I'd had the exact same coat before — my old one was starting to look pretty worn out, so I just replaced it. Since I already knew my size, I found the best offer online, ordered it through a shopping website, and it turned up at my door in a box a few days later.

Like I said, I'm wearing this coat constantly right now. It's my everyday coat — work, popping out to the shops, whatever else I'm doing. The reason I like it so much is that it's so practical and functional.

It's waterproof, it keeps me warm, and it comes in handy for carrying things — my phone, wallet, loose change, a pen, even a notebook; I basically stuff my pockets with whatever I've got on me. It even folds up small enough to fit in my bag on days when I don't need it.

Processes & Cycles

Urban waste sorting

Academic Task 1

Prompt

Write a description of how urban refuse is assorted for recycling. Use the information in the diagram and write a description of this process.

Model answer

The diagram shows how urban refuse is collected, sorted and processed for recycling, and how the resulting products return to consumers.

Overall, the system forms a loop. Recyclable materials pass from rubbish bins through recovery and manufacturing processes before reaching households, businesses and individual customers, who can then return suitable waste to the appropriate bins.

At the first stage, bottles made of plastic or glass, paper and durable items are collected from separate bins. They are taken to material recovery facilities, where the second stage involves sorting them into different streams. Soft drink containers are directed to soft drink companies, while newspapers and paper towels go to paper and pulp mills.

The remaining recyclables undergo further processing. They are cleaned, classified and crushed, after which businesses dealing in processed recyclables supply the materials to manufacturers. Engineering firms buy aluminium, whereas property developers purchase glass or plastic.

These different routes all lead to products that are supplied to households, businesses and individual customers. Once the products have been used, recyclable waste can be placed in the correct bins again, completing the cycle shown in the diagram.

Key words and phrases

material recovery facilities
Places where recyclable waste is separated and prepared for reuse.

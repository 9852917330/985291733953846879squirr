Technology & Science

Did the Moon landing matter?

Discuss Both Views

Prompt

The Moon landing is often described as one giant leap for mankind. Yet some people argue that this achievement made very little difference to ordinary people's daily lives. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

The first Moon landing is often described as one of humanity's greatest achievements, yet some argue that it changed very little for ordinary people. In my opinion both claims contain some truth, but the achievement was more significant than its critics allow.

The sceptical position is easy to understand. The programme cost an enormous sum at a time when the United States faced poverty and racial conflict at home, and it was driven largely by competition with the Soviet Union rather than by science. No permanent base was established, nobody returned for half a century, and the daily life of an average family was not visibly altered by the fact that twelve men had walked on the Moon.

Those who defend it point to consequences that were not obvious at the time. The programme required equipment that did not yet exist, and the effort to create it produced the miniaturised computing on which every modern device depends, along with advances in materials, medical monitoring and satellites. Satellite technology in particular now underpins weather forecasting, navigation and global communication, and few people realise how directly it descends from that programme.

There is also a less measurable effect. The landing demonstrated what a coordinated national effort could achieve, and photographs of the Earth taken from the Moon are widely credited with strengthening the environmental movement.

In conclusion, the Moon landing did not transform daily life directly, and its motives were undeniably political. Its technological and cultural consequences, however, were substantial enough to justify calling it a genuine leap forward rather than an expensive gesture.

Key words and phrases

competition with the Soviet Union
Rivalry with the former Soviet state.
miniaturised computing
Computing equipment made much smaller.
strengthening the environmental movement
Increasing support for protecting nature.
a genuine leap forward
A real, major improvement.

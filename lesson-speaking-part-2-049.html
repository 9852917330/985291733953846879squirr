A popular place

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a popular place

You should say: Where is this place? When did you first visit this place? How often do you go to this place? And explain why it is so popular.

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

A popular place in Hanoi is the Four Seasons swimming pool at my local sports complex. It is an indoor pool with heated water, so people can use it comfortably throughout the year, including winter.

I learned to swim at school, but I only started visiting this pool regularly about a year ago. My best friend invited me one weekend, and I enjoyed it so much that I kept going. Now I try to swim two or three mornings a week before work, when it is quieter.

I think the pool is popular because it is clean and well managed. A trained lifeguard is always there, and staff take safety seriously. The changing rooms have good showers and large lockers, which make the visit convenient. I also appreciate the comfortable water temperature. Swimming helps me exercise without feeling too hot or tired afterwards. Although the pool can be crowded, I think its good reputation is deserved. It has become an enjoyable and useful part of my weekly routine.

Key words and phrases

sports complex
A group of facilities where people can do different sports.
good reputation
A positive opinion that many people have about something.

Advanced Model Answer

So today I want to talk about a swimming pool called Four Seasons, which I'd say is probably the most popular pool in the city — it's packed pretty much every single day.

Four Seasons is an indoor public pool in my local sports complex. It's called Four Seasons because it's heated, so people can go and enjoy warm water all year round, even in winter. Swimming's become a really popular recreational activity here in Hanoi recently, for people of all ages, and I actually took swimming lessons back when I was at school, so I've always felt comfortable in the water.

I only started going there regularly about a year ago, when my best friend, who'd been a member for ages, finally dragged me along one weekend — and I've been hooked ever since. These days I try to go two or three times a week, usually early in the morning before work, because that's when it's quietest.

I think Four Seasons is popular for a couple of reasons, really. Safety's clearly their top priority, for one — there's always a well-trained lifeguard on duty, ready to enforce the pool rules and perform any water rescues if something goes wrong. And the facilities are excellent too: the water's kept at a nice temperature and regularly sanitised, and the changing rooms are clean, with proper showers and big lockers for your things. Honestly, I think the pool totally deserves its great reputation.

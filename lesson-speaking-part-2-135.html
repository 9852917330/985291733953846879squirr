What you would do if you were given a day off

Part 2

Prompt

Describe what you would do if you were given a day off

You should say: what you would like to do who you would like to be with where you would like to do it And explain how you would feel at the end of the day.

Simple Model Answer

If I were given a day off, I would spend it outdoors with my best friend Lan. We both enjoy travelling and discovering unfamiliar places, so I would choose a short trip rather than staying at home.

I would probably go to Ninh Binh, my hometown. Although I grew up there, I have not explored the whole province. We could cycle or walk through the countryside and visit places such as the Stone Church and Hoa Lu Palace. Lan would be a good companion because she enjoys these activities and knows basic first aid.

I would plan enough to avoid wasting time, but leave space to stop whenever we saw something interesting. Recently, my workload has been heavy, and I have spent too many hours indoors. A day of movement and fresh air would help me take my mind off those responsibilities. I would probably feel physically tired by the evening, but mentally refreshed. More than anything, I would enjoy having a whole day to share an experience with a close friend.

Key words and phrases

companion
Someone who goes somewhere or spends time with you.
take my mind off
Help me stop thinking about something worrying or tiring.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about something I'd love to do if I had a day off - spend the whole day out doing various outdoor activities, just to relieve some stress and recharge my batteries. I've already got a few ideas in mind for when I get the chance, like hiking or cycling somewhere mountainous, or visiting a few historical sites.

If I had a day off, I'd want to spend it with my best friend, Lan. She loves travelling and exploring new places just as much as I do, and she actually knows basic first aid and what to do if we ever got lost, so she'd be the perfect partner to go with.

I'd probably head to Ninh Binh, since it's actually my hometown. I grew up there, but I still haven't explored the whole province - it's huge, and there's a lot I've never seen. With so many attractions around, it'd let Lan and me enjoy some fresh atmosphere, go hiking, and visit places like the Stone Church and Hoa Lu Palace.

I've been dealing with a heavy workload at work recently, so I could really use a break like that. I imagine that by the end of the day, tired as I'd probably be after all that hiking, I'd feel completely recharged and just a lot happier - way more than any lazy day at home could manage.

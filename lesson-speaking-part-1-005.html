Buildings

An interesting building, and preserving old ones

Part 1

Prompt

Can you tell me about an interesting building in your hometown? Why should we preserve historical buildings? How have architecture and building materials changed in your country? What type of building do you prefer to live in and why?

Can you tell me about an interesting building in your hometown?

Simple Model Answer

The Hanoi Opera House is an interesting building in my city. It has a French-style front and stands in the city centre. It looks quite different from modern glass buildings.

Key words and phrases

city centre
The main central area of a city.

Advanced Model Answer

The Hanoi Opera House is one of the most striking buildings in my city. Its French-inspired façade and central location give it a distinctive character that modern glass towers often lack.

Key words and phrases

a distinctive character
A recognisable, unusual identity.

Why should we preserve historical buildings?

Simple Model Answer

Old buildings show us how a city has changed and help people feel connected to its past. If we knock them down, it is difficult to bring back their history and special feeling.

Key words and phrases

knock them down
Destroy buildings so they no longer stand.

Advanced Model Answer

Historical buildings preserve evidence of how a city developed and give residents a sense of continuity. Once they're demolished, the knowledge and atmosphere they carry can't be recreated convincingly.

Key words and phrases

a sense of continuity
Feeling connected to what came before.

How have architecture and building materials changed in your country?

Simple Model Answer

Modern buildings use more steel, glass and ready-made parts, while older houses used mainly bricks and wood. New designs also save space and energy because cities have different needs now.

Key words and phrases

save space
Use less room or make better use of it.

Advanced Model Answer

New buildings use more steel, glass and prefabricated materials, while older homes relied heavily on brick and timber. Designs have also become more compact and energy-efficient to meet urban needs.

Key words and phrases

meet urban needs
Provide what city residents require.

What type of building do you prefer to live in and why?

Simple Model Answer

I'd like a medium-sized flat with plenty of daylight, little noise and public transport nearby. It doesn't need to be expensive, but it should be comfortable and easy to keep in good condition.

Key words and phrases

in good condition
Working well and not damaged.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd prefer a medium-sized apartment with natural light, good soundproofing and access to public transport. I don't need luxury, but I value a home that's easy to maintain and comfortable throughout the year.

Key words and phrases

easy to maintain
Simple to keep in good condition.

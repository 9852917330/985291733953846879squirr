Technology & Science

Should we contact other worlds?

Discuss both views + opinion

Prompt

Some scientists think that there are intelligent life forms on other planets and that a message should be sent to contact them. Other scientists think that it is a bad idea and would be dangerous. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Model answer

Astronomers have identified thousands of planets capable of supporting life, which has revived an old argument about whether humanity should announce its presence. Both sides make serious points, and I favour the cautious position for reasons that are practical rather than fearful.

Those in favour argue that contact would be the most significant discovery in history. Confirming that we are not alone would transform biology, and any civilisation capable of receiving a signal would almost certainly be older than ours, so what could be learned about energy, medicine or survival is impossible to estimate in advance. There is also an argument from consistency: we already listen for signals, and a universe in which everyone listens and nobody transmits produces silence by design.

The opposing view rests on the asymmetry between us and anyone who replies. A civilisation able to cross interstellar distances would be so far ahead of us that we would have no way of protecting ourselves if its intentions were not friendly, and human history offers little comfort about what happens when a technologically advanced society meets a weaker one. Critics also object that a handful of researchers are speaking on behalf of the entire planet without any mandate to do so.

My own view rests on that last point. The risk cannot be estimated, since we have no evidence at all about who might be listening, and a decision whose consequences fall on everyone should not be taken by a small group.

In conclusion, the scientific case for contact is genuine, and I would not oppose transmission permanently. It should follow an international agreement rather than an individual initiative, because this is one experiment that cannot be stopped once it has begun.

Key words and phrases

impossible to estimate in advance
Cannot be predicted beforehand.
an argument from consistency
Reasoning that similar cases deserve similar treatment.
produces silence by design
Intentionally results in receiving no response.
the asymmetry between us and anyone who replies
An unequal relationship with any civilisation replying.
speaking on behalf of the entire planet
Claiming to represent everyone on Earth.
without any mandate to do so
Without authority or permission from those represented.
cannot be stopped once it has begun

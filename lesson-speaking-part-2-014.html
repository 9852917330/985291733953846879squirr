A person you know

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a person you know

You should say: - Who this person is. - How long you have known each other. - How you got to know him/her. - How you feel about this person.

Simple Model Answer

I'd like to describe my friend Moon, a Chinese student I met during the welcome activities at college. We have known each other for three years. During our first year, we shared many classes and often studied together before exams.

Moon is energetic and enjoyable to be around. She sometimes came to my home at weekends to help with homework. We also spent time together after class, and I never found her company boring. Although she seemed cheerful, her parents expected very high grades and wanted her to enter a good graduate school. This put her under a lot of pressure.

I admired the way she stayed positive and continued treating others kindly despite her worries. We are now in our final year, so we do not meet as often. However, she still messages me to ask how my studies are going. I appreciate these small signs of care. Whatever happens after college, I will remember our friendship and the happy time we spent learning together.

Key words and phrases

under a lot of pressure
Feeling strong expectations or demands to do well.
signs of care
Small actions that show someone is interested in your well-being.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about a friend I met in college, who goes by the name Moon.

I met her during our freshman orientation, and I honestly think I was really lucky to have been introduced to her. She's a Chinese student who pretty much everybody adores. I've known her for three years now, and in all that time, I don't think I've ever once been bored by her company. She's always energetic and fun to be with. We had loads of classes together in our first year, and she'd often come round to help me with my homework at the weekends. I got to know her properly through revising for exams together and hanging out after class.

As cheerful as she seemed on the surface, she was actually under a lot of pressure from her parents about getting good grades and getting into a decent graduate school. I really admire the way she always managed to stay positive despite all the stress she was under — she never let it show in how she treated the people around her.

Now that we're in our final year, I don't see her nearly as often. But once in a while she'll send me a message just to check how I'm getting on with my schoolwork, and I really appreciate that about her. Either way, I know I'll always cherish the time we spent together, and whether or not we manage to stay in each other's lives after this, I'll always look back on our college years with real fondness.

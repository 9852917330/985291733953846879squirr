Housing & Architecture

Renting or buying, and leaving the family home

Prompt

Do you think it is better to rent or to buy a place to live in? Do you agree that there is a right age for young adults to stop living with their parents?

Do you think it is better to rent or to buy a place to live in?

Simple Model Answer

I prefer buying if the cost is manageable. It gives more long-term control, and the home may keep or increase its value. Renting means paying an owner and having less certainty about staying. However, buying requires a large deposit, which many wages cannot support. So although ownership appeals to me, it is not a realistic choice for everyone.

Key words and phrases

deposit
An initial payment made before paying the rest of a purchase price.

Advanced Model Answer

I think it's better to buy your own home if you can afford to do that. Buying your own home is a better investment in the long term, because you can sell the home and perhaps even make a profit on it. The alternative is to pay rent to a landlord who can ask you to leave at any moment. The difficulty is that deposits have become enormous relative to wages, so a great many people can't realistically buy however sensible it might be.

Key words and phrases

in the long term
over a long period of time
make a profit on
earn more money than you spent on
ask you to leave at any moment
tell you that you must go with little warning

Do you agree that there is a right age for young adults to stop living with their parents?

Simple Model Answer

No, there is no right age for everyone. Money, education, housing costs and culture all affect the decision. More importantly, young adults should gradually learn to manage responsibilities and make choices. They can do that while living with parents too. In many cultures, staying home longer is normal rather than a sign that someone has failed to mature.

Key words and phrases

mature
Become more responsible and able to manage adult life.

Advanced Model Answer

There's no universal age at which young adults should leave home. Financial independence, housing costs, education and family culture all matter. The important point is that adults gradually take responsibility for money and daily decisions, whether they live alone at eighteen or remain with their parents longer. It's also worth saying that staying at home much longer is completely normal in many cultures, and nobody there regards it as a failure to grow up. That judgement is quite specific to certain Western countries.

Key words and phrases

take responsibility for
Accept the duty of handling.
a failure to grow up
a mistaken belief that someone is not mature enough

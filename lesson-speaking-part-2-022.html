Person

An interesting person from another country

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an interesting person from another country.

You should say: - Who this person is; - How you know this person; - What this person does; - Why this person is interesting.

OUTLINE

>>> Mr Vincent

Simple Model Answer

An interesting person from another country is Mr Vincent, my teacher at ZIM English Centre. He is American and has lived and taught in Vietnam for about two years. He joined our centre a year ago.

He is a hardworking teacher. I often see him arriving early or staying late to mark homework. He also explains things patiently when students need help. However, what interests me most is his effort to understand Vietnamese life. He has learned enough Vietnamese to bargain at markets, remembers to remove his shoes indoors and even learned to make Vietnamese baguette sandwiches from a local seller.

These actions show that he wants to be part of the culture around him. He is also polite and remembers students' names, as well as the names of people working in the canteen. I enjoy his classes because he combines good teaching with a friendly personality. He has shown me that learning about another country includes respecting the people and their everyday habits.

Key words and phrases

bargain at markets
Discuss a price with a seller to try to pay less.
everyday habits
Ordinary things that people do regularly.

Advanced Model Answer

Today I'd like to talk about an interesting person from another country who I really admire and respect — my English teacher, Mr Vincent. I'm currently studying at ZIM English Centre, where there are quite a few foreign teachers, but out of all of them, Vincent's easily the one I've grown closest to.

He's American, and he's been living and teaching in Vietnam for about two years now. He's got a real reputation at the school for being switched-on and genuinely dedicated — I often see him arriving early and still there marking homework long after everyone else has gone home. Since he joined a year ago, he's more or less won everyone over, students and staff alike.

What I find most interesting about him, though, is how hard he's worked to fit into Vietnamese culture rather than just expecting everyone else to adapt to him. He's picked up enough Vietnamese to haggle at the market, he always takes his shoes off without being asked, and he even learned to make Vietnamese baguette sandwiches from a stall owner near his flat, purely because he wanted to understand the culture better rather than just live alongside it. He's also incredibly patient — whenever a student messages him at midnight panicking about homework, he'll actually reply properly instead of brushing them off till the next morning.

On top of all that, he's got really good manners — small things like remembering the name of every student he's ever taught, or always thanking the canteen staff by name when he grabs lunch. It's that combination of curiosity about our culture and genuine warmth towards people that makes him stand out to me, and it's exactly why he's built such a good reputation here.

Key words and phrases

genuinely dedicated
truly committed to what they do
won everyone over
made everyone like or support them
picked up enough Vietnamese
learned enough Vietnamese through experience
reply properly
give a clear and suitable answer
good manners
polite and respectful behaviour

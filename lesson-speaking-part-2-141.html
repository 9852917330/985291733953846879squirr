A special day out (A day out which does not cost too much)

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a special day out (A day out which does not cost too much)

You should say: - Where did you go on that day. - What did you do. - Who did you go out with. - Why was it special to you.

Simple Model Answer

A special day out that cost little was an afternoon in a park with an old high school friend. We had not seen each other for almost a year and were both on a semester break. We planned to meet before going somewhere for dinner.

My friend arrived late, so we decided to stay in the park instead. We bought two milk teas and some kebabs from a nearby street seller, then found a comfortable place to sit. Although it had rained earlier that week, the evening turned out mild and pleasant.

We talked for hours about work, study and our future plans. We also watched people passing and laughed about old memories. Nothing expensive or carefully organised happened, but I enjoyed the evening more than I expected. It was reassuring to discover that our friendship still felt easy after so much time apart. The day reminded me that good company matters more than a fancy location. Sometimes a simple drink and enough time to talk are all that is needed for a memorable outing.

Key words and phrases

semester break
A holiday between periods of study at a school or university.
good company
People whose presence makes an activity enjoyable.

Advanced Model Answer

A day out that didn't cost much but that I really enjoyed was an afternoon in the park with an old friend from high school. We hadn't seen each other for almost a year, and since we were both on our semester break, we decided it was finally time to meet up and catch up over dinner.

We'd planned to meet at the park first and then decide where to go afterwards, but my friend turned up pretty late, so we just ended up staying there. We grabbed two takeaway milk teas and some kebabs from a small street vendor nearby and found a comfortable corner of the park to sit in. We spent ages just watching people walk past and chatting about whatever came into our heads. It had been raining on and off all week, so we were half expecting to get rained out, but it turned out to be a lovely, mild evening.

I think what made the day special wasn't really the plan — because we didn't stick to one — it was that even after waiting around and not going anywhere fancy, we still had far more fun than I'd expected. We caught up on everything going on in our lives and talked about our plans for the future. It was a nice feeling to realise that even though we can't meet up often, we can still fall straight back into easy conversation over milk tea and kebabs. It made me realise that sometimes all you really need is good company to make something memorable.

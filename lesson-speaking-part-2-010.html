Person

A person who does well in work

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a person who does well at work.

You should say: Who the person is How do you know this person What kinds of work this person does And explain why this person can do well

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

Someone who does well at work is Mr Lawrence, the head of the Maths department at the international school where I recently started teaching. Everyone calls him Laurie. I got to know him during a conversation over coffee in the school cafeteria.

He has taught maths since 1985 and clearly loves his job. He once joked that he would continue for another five hundred years if he could. I was surprised by how much he enjoyed teaching after so many years.

What impressed me most was watching him help students with algebra before their final exams. When they did not understand, he did not simply repeat himself. He explained the same idea in three different ways until everyone understood it. By the end, the students looked much more confident. I think this patience is why he is such a good teacher. He cares about whether students learn, rather than just finishing a lesson. As a new teacher, I can learn a lot from him.

Key words and phrases

got to know him
Learned more about him by spending time together.

Say the same thing again.

Advanced Model Answer

Recently I took a job as a maths teacher at an international school, and when I think of someone who genuinely does well at work, the first person who comes to mind is the head of the Maths department at my school, Mr Lawrence, or Laurie, as everyone calls him. I got to know him properly one lunchtime in the school cafeteria, over coffee, when we ended up talking about his role as head of Maths.

Laurie told me he's been a maths teacher since 1985, so that's over thirty-five years in the trade. He clearly loves the job — he joked that he'd happily keep teaching maths for another five hundred years if old age didn't get in the way first. To be honest, I was quite taken aback by that comment, because I usually feel something between admiration and sympathy for teachers who commit almost every aspect of their life to their work. Admiration for their dedication to their students; sympathy for how much they've had to give up along the way.

What actually convinced me he does well at work, though, wasn't just what he said — it was watching him with a group of students who were struggling with algebra before their final exams. He didn't just repeat the same explanation louder; he found three different ways to explain the same idea until it clicked, and by the end of the session, every one of them looked relieved rather than lost.

It's precisely this quality that explains why Laurie's so good at his job. His work and his life are intimately linked, and if Laurie wants to lead a good life, that's almost synonymous with being a good teacher.

Key words and phrases

the head of the Maths department
the person in charge of the mathematics department
in the school cafeteria
in the school dining area
in the trade
working in that kind of business or profession
taken aback by
Suddenly surprised or shocked by.

Education

University education

Discuss both views + opinion

Prompt

Some people believe the aim of university education is to help graduates get better jobs. Others believe there are much wider benefits of university education for both individuals and society. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Model answer

The purpose of university education is often debated. Some regard it primarily as professional preparation, whereas others emphasise its broader intellectual and social value. Although employability is an essential outcome, I believe universities serve society best when they combine vocational relevance with the development of independent judgement.

Those who prioritise employment have a strong practical case. Higher education requires substantial investment from students, families and governments, so graduates reasonably expect a measurable return on that investment. Degree programmes can provide specialist knowledge, professional accreditation and access to occupations that would otherwise remain closed. In fields such as medicine, engineering and law, universities also protect the public by ensuring that entrants meet rigorous standards. From this perspective, a course that ignores labour-market realities may leave students heavily indebted but poorly equipped for work.

However, reducing university to job training would overlook its wider contribution. Students learn to evaluate evidence, challenge assumptions and communicate across cultural or disciplinary boundaries. These capacities help individuals navigate complex public and private decisions long after specific technical knowledge becomes outdated. Universities also generate research, sustain cultural institutions and create spaces in which contested ideas can be examined rather than merely repeated. A society with citizens who can reason critically is better able to resist misinformation and hold powerful institutions accountable.

In my view, the two purposes are not mutually exclusive. Programmes should maintain academic depth while incorporating placements, collaborative projects and transferable skills. This approach would bridge the gap between scholarship and professional practice without turning every subject into narrow occupational training.

In conclusion, better employment is a legitimate aim of university education, but it is not the only one. The most valuable institutions prepare graduates both to earn a living and to participate intelligently in civic life.

Key words and phrases

independent judgement
Forming your own conclusions.
a measurable return on that investment
A benefit that can be clearly assessed.
navigate complex public and private decisions
Handle difficult choices in public and personal life.
hold powerful institutions accountable
Make influential organisations answer for their actions.
bridge the gap between scholarship and professional practice
Connect academic research with work in practice.
participate intelligently in civic life
Take an informed part in community affairs.

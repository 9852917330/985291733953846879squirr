A family celebration that you remember

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a family celebration that you remember.

You should say: - what you were celebrating - who was present - what you and your family did to make the celebration special - and why you enjoyed the occasion.

Simple Model Answer

A family celebration I remember is my sister Trang's wedding a few years ago. It took place in the small town where we grew up. The ceremony was in the old church where our parents had also married.

About a hundred people attended the ceremony, and even more joined the reception afterwards. Both families helped organise the day. My mother arranged decorations, including white lilies on the tables and small lights across the ceiling. There was a live band and a wedding cake, and everyone dressed carefully for the occasion.

I enjoyed seeing my sister so happy. The ceremony was emotional, especially when she entered the church. Afterwards, we celebrated by eating, talking and dancing until after midnight. What made the event particularly memorable was having so many relatives and old friends in one place. That does not happen often now that people live and work in different areas. The wedding was important for my sister, but it also gave the whole family a chance to reconnect and share a joyful day together.

Key words and phrases

reception
A celebration held after a wedding ceremony.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to describe my sister's wedding day, which took place a few years ago in the small town where I grew up. For my sister, Trang, it was the biggest and most important day of her life. There were around a hundred guests at the marriage ceremony itself, which was held in the old church on the edge of town where our parents got married too.

Even more people turned up for the reception afterwards. Most of my family were there, of course, along with the groom's family and a good number of the couple's friends and colleagues from work. To make the day feel special, we all pitched in — it wasn't just left to my parents to organise everything.

My mother made sure the church and the reception hall were beautifully decorated; there were white lilies on every table, and fairy lights strung along the ceiling that made the whole place glow once it got dark. We'd all dressed for the occasion, obviously, and there were bridesmaids, a live band, and a wedding cake that took my sister nearly an hour to choose. I enjoyed the day mainly because it was wonderful to see my sister looking so happy on her big day.

The ceremony itself was moving — there wasn't a dry eye in the church when my sister walked in — and we all had a fantastic time at the reception afterwards, dancing until well past midnight. It's rare for me to see my whole family and all our old friends together in one place, so that's probably what made the day so memorable for me.

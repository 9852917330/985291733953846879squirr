Technology

Inventions of the last fifty years and mobile phones

Part 3

Prompt

What do you think are the most important inventions of the last 50 years? How have mobile phones made communications easier? Which household appliances do you think people need most?

What do you think are the most important inventions of the last 50 years?

Simple Model Answer

Smartphones stand out because they combine a phone, camera, map and many other tools. For many people, they were also the first computer they owned. I would include the internet and medical scanning too. However, the benefits are not universal: some people still lack affordable devices or a dependable connection, so these inventions have not reached everyone equally.

Key words and phrases

universal
Applying to or available to everyone.

Advanced Model Answer

The smartphone's the obvious answer, because it collapsed a dozen separate devices into one — telephone, camera, map, bank, library and music player. For much of the world it was also the first computer people ever owned. I'd add the internet itself, since the phone would be useless without it, and medical imaging, which transformed diagnosis. The smartphone stands out mainly because its effects reached almost everybody, including in poorer countries — though that's not quite true even now, since plenty of people still can't afford one or don't have reliable coverage, so the benefits are less universal than the answer makes them sound.

Key words and phrases

the first computer people ever owned
the earliest computer someone bought for personal use
reliable coverage
a service or signal available dependably in most places

How have mobile phones made communications easier?

Simple Model Answer

They let us contact people quickly from almost anywhere, usually for much less than international calls once cost. This is especially useful in emergencies and for families living in different countries. However, easy contact creates pressure for immediate replies. People may see a slow response as rude because they assume the phone is always nearby.

Key words and phrases

immediate replies
Answers sent straight away, without delay.

Advanced Model Answer

They removed the two constraints that used to govern communication: location and cost. You can reach someone anywhere, instantly, and international calls that once cost a fortune are effectively free. For emergencies, and for families separated by migration, that's genuinely transformative in a way older generations find hard to imagine. The side effect is an expectation of immediate response — because people know you have the phone with you, not replying quickly is now read as rudeness.

Key words and phrases

an expectation of immediate response
the belief that people should reply straight away

Which household appliances do you think people need most?

Simple Model Answer

I'd put the refrigerator first because it keeps food safe, reduces waste and cuts shopping trips. Washing machines and cooking equipment are also essential because they replace heavy daily work. The order depends on the household, though. Someone washing clothes by hand for a large family might reasonably consider the washing machine the most important.

Key words and phrases

essential
Extremely important or necessary.

Advanced Model Answer

The refrigerator, without much question. It changed what people could safely eat, cut food waste enormously and reduced how often households had to shop. After that the washing machine, which returned hours of every week to whoever was doing the laundry. Cooking appliances too, obviously. What these three share is that they replaced genuinely heavy labour rather than merely adding convenience — though which one you'd rank first really depends on your circumstances, and someone hand-washing clothes for a big family would probably put the washing machine ahead of everything else.

Key words and phrases

returned hours of every week
saved several hours of time each week
genuinely heavy labour
physical work that is truly difficult and tiring

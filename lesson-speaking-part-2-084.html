Object & Favourite

A subject that you think should be removed from school

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a subject that you think should be removed from school

You should say: - what the subject is - why you think it is unnecessary for children to study it - and explain what you would replace it with.

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

If I had to choose a subject to remove from school, I would choose art as a compulsory lesson. In my own experience, we mainly drew pictures, painted and made collages from paper or fabric.

My first reason is that many students may not use these particular skills in their future jobs. Children who enjoy art can still draw and paint in their free time. My parents often did creative activities with me at weekends, so I did not rely only on school for that experience.

Another reason comes from the way my classes were taught. We often received little technical guidance and were simply left to finish a picture. It felt more like a break than a structured lesson. I would prefer extra time for maths, science or languages because those subjects seem more directly useful for many careers. This is based on my personal experience rather than a dislike of creativity itself. I understand that others enjoy art, but if timetable space were limited, I would give those core subjects priority.

Key words and phrases

compulsory lesson
A class that all students must attend.
give those core subjects priority
Treat those main subjects as more important when deciding how to use time.

Advanced Model Answer

If I had to pick one subject to take off the school curriculum, I'd probably go with art. In my experience, art lessons mostly involve drawing, painting, and making collages out of paper, fabric and whatever else is lying around. There are a few reasons I think it's not really worth keeping.

For one thing, I don't think drawing and painting are skills most children actually need once they leave school. They might enjoy the lessons at the time, but it's pretty unlikely they'll use them in most jobs. On top of that, kids can draw, paint and make collages at home in their own time anyway — my parents used to sit and do exactly that with me on weekends, no school lesson required.

And honestly, looking back at my own art classes, I don't think we picked up much real skill from them at all. The teachers mostly just left us to get on with it, without giving us much proper technical guidance, so it always felt more like free time than an actual lesson.

Instead of art, I'd rather see that time go towards core subjects like maths, science or a language. Those are the subjects that are actually going to help children later on, once they're out looking for a job, and I think both students and teachers would get more out of the extra time spent on them.

Key words and phrases

take off the school curriculum
Remove a subject from school lessons.
In my experience
Based on what I have personally seen.
left us to get on with it
Allowed us to work without much interference.
proper technical guidance
Appropriate expert instruction on how to do it.
out looking for a job
Trying to find employment.
get more out of
Gain more benefit from something.

Education

Pushing children to study hard

Agree / Disagree

Prompt

Some people believe that children should be pushed to study hard from a young age. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Model answer

It is sometimes argued that children should be pushed to work hard academically from an early age. I agree that high expectations benefit young people, but I believe that excessive pressure does more harm than good.

There is a strong case for expecting effort. Skills such as reading, mathematics and a foreign language are far easier to acquire when young, and a child who falls behind early rarely catches up. Habits also form early: a student who learns to work steadily, meet deadlines and persist through difficulty acquires something more valuable than the subject content itself. Left entirely to themselves, most children would choose immediate entertainment over long-term benefit, so a degree of adult direction is clearly necessary.

The difficulty is that pressure has a point beyond which it becomes destructive. In several countries where competition for university places is extreme, rates of anxiety and sleep deprivation among teenagers are among the highest in the world. Excessive pressure also damages learning itself, because a student who studies only to avoid punishment memorises material and forgets it immediately, and often develops a lasting dislike of the subject. Children additionally need time for play, sport and friendship, and these are not luxuries: they are where social skills and emotional resilience develop.

In my opinion the distinction lies between encouragement and coercion. Parents should set clear expectations and take an interest in progress. They should not threaten, compare a child unfavourably with siblings, or fill every evening with extra classes.

In conclusion, I agree that children should be expected to work hard, but pressure must be kept within limits that protect their health and their interest in learning.

Key words and phrases

immediate entertainment over long-term benefit
Choosing fun now over future advantages.
memorises material and forgets it immediately
Learns by memory without retaining knowledge.
a lasting dislike of the subject
A negative attitude that continues for years.
emotional resilience
The ability to recover from emotional difficulties.
between encouragement and coercion
Between helpful support and forcing someone.

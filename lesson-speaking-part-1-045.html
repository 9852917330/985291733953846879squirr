Internet

How you use the internet and its advantages

Part 1

Prompt

Do you use the internet much during the day? Which website have you visited recently? What are some advantages of the internet? Do people in your country use the internet a lot?

Do you use the internet much during the day?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, I use it throughout the day for work, messages and research. I try to have a clear purpose because too many alerts can make it difficult to focus.

Key words and phrases

a clear purpose
A definite reason for doing something.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, I'm on it throughout the day really — for work, communication and research. I try to use it deliberately though, because constant notifications can fragment my attention.

Key words and phrases

fragment my attention
Split my concentration among many things.

Which website have you visited recently?

Simple Model Answer

I recently used an education website to compare materials for learning languages. It was easy to follow, and the examples helped me understand how I could use the information.

Key words and phrases

easy to follow
Simple to understand or use.

Advanced Model Answer

I recently visited an educational website to compare language-learning resources. I liked the clear structure, and the fact that the information was supported by practical examples.

Key words and phrases

supported by practical examples
Explained using real situations.

What are some advantages of the internet?

Simple Model Answer

It helps people find information, use services and communicate quickly. One big benefit is that it makes education and starting a business more accessible to people who had fewer chances before.

Key words and phrases

accessible
Easy for people to get or use.

Advanced Model Answer

The internet gives people rapid access to information, services and communication. I'd say its greatest advantage is that it can lower the barrier to entry for education and business.

Key words and phrases

access to information
The ability to find or obtain facts.
lower the barrier to entry
Make it easier to begin participating.

Do people in your country use the internet a lot?

Simple Model Answer

Yes, especially in cities. People use it for shopping, banking, work and entertainment. However, not everyone has the same level of internet skills or a good connection.

Key words and phrases

connection
The link that allows a device to use the internet.

Advanced Model Answer

Yes, definitely, especially in the cities, where people rely on it for banking, shopping, entertainment and work. It's pretty widespread now, although digital skills and good-quality access are still unevenly distributed.

Key words and phrases

unevenly distributed
Not shared equally among people or places.

Place

A leisure facility for my hometown

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a leisure facility, such as a cinema, theatre or sports centre, that you would like in your hometown.

You should say: - What it is; - Why you would like to have this facility in your hometown; - When will you go there; - How the facility changes your life; - How you felt about it.

Simple Model Answer

A facility I would like in my hometown is an indoor sports and climbing centre. At the moment, we only have a small, old badminton hall, and it is usually fully booked. For better facilities, people often travel almost an hour to another city.

My ideal centre would have a climbing wall, courts for different sports and an indoor running track. It would also offer group exercise classes. I would probably go after work during the week and spend longer there on Saturday mornings.

I think it would help many people exercise more regularly. Rain would no longer stop us from training, and travelling there would be easy. It could also bring local people together. Children could attend after school, adults could come in the evening, and older residents could use it during the day. Our town has many young people who need enjoyable activities nearby. I would be excited if the local government built a place like this, and I would certainly try it as soon as it opened.

fully booked
Having no spaces available because all have been reserved.

Give people from the area opportunities to meet and interact.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about something my hometown doesn't have yet but really should: a proper indoor sports and climbing centre.

Right now, if you want to do any indoor sport where I live, your only real option is a small, old badminton hall that's usually fully booked. There's nowhere to climb, no proper indoor running track for when it rains, and no space for group fitness classes. I'd love to see a modern centre built with a climbing wall, a couple of multi-purpose courts, and a running track around the edge of the building.

If it existed, I'd probably go most weekday evenings after work, since that's really the only free time I get during the week, plus a longer session on Saturday mornings before the place gets busy.

The reason I think this matters is that our town has a lot of young people with plenty of energy and nowhere really to put it. At the moment, most of us either drive to the next city to use their facilities, which takes almost an hour, or we just don't exercise regularly at all. A well-designed centre close to home would remove that barrier completely.

I also think it would bring the community together in a way we're currently missing. Sports centres are somewhere people of different ages naturally mix — kids after school, working adults in the evening, older residents in the daytime — and that kind of casual social contact is hard to create any other way.

If the local government ever built something like this, I honestly think it would transform how people spend their free time here, and it would probably keep quite a few young people from moving away simply to have better facilities. I'd honestly be first in the queue on opening day.

the only free time I get
the only time when I am not busy
well-designed centre
a place planned so it works well for its users

Informal

Selling a television to a friend

Informal

Prompt

You want to sell your television and think a friend may be interested. Write to your friend. In your letter: - explain why you are selling it - describe the television - suggest when your friend could see it

Model answer

Hi Chris,

I remember you mentioned that you were looking for a television for your new flat, so I thought I'd offer you mine before advertising it online.

I'm only selling it because I'm moving into a furnished apartment next month and there's already a television there. The one I have is a 43-inch Samsung smart TV, about two years old, and it's in excellent working order. The screen has no scratches, the remote is included, and it supports Netflix, YouTube and wireless screen sharing. I still have the original box and receipt.

I paid £420 for it, but I think £230 would be a fair price, especially as you wouldn't need to arrange delivery. There's no pressure at all if it's not what you want.

You could come round and have a look on Saturday afternoon or Sunday morning. I can connect it to the internet so that you can test the picture, sound and apps properly.

Let me know what suits you.

All the best, Alex

Key words and phrases

before advertising it online
Before posting the sales advertisement.
in excellent working order
Working very well.
There's no pressure at all
You do not have to agree.
come round and have a look
Visit and examine it.
test the picture, sound and apps properly
Check every main feature carefully.

Government & Politics

Who should pay for schools and hospitals?

Discuss both views + opinion

Prompt

Some people think that governments should pay for education and health care, while others believe that it is not their responsibility. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Model answer

Whether the state should fund schools and hospitals is one of the oldest disagreements in politics. I believe governments should pay for both, although the argument for private provision alongside them is stronger than it is usually allowed to be.

The case for public funding is that these two services determine what a person can become. A child whose family cannot afford school fees loses opportunities permanently, and an untreated illness can end a working life, so leaving both to private purchase means that ability counts for less than the income a person happens to be born into. There is also an efficiency argument that is often overlooked: an educated, healthy population is what produces skilled workers and taxpayers, so the spending returns to the treasury over time rather than disappearing.

Those who disagree point to cost and quality. Health spending rises faster than any government's income as populations age, and state monopolies face no competition, which can leave patients waiting months for treatment they would receive privately in a week. Taxing everyone to fund a service that many choose not to use is, on this view, difficult to justify.

My own position accepts the criticism without accepting the conclusion. Waiting lists are an argument for funding and managing public services properly, not for withdrawing them, and countries that rely on private provision spend more per person while covering fewer of their citizens.

In conclusion, governments should fund education and health care, because both determine life chances and both repay the investment. Private alternatives can reasonably exist for those who want them, provided they operate alongside a public system rather than instead of one.

Key words and phrases

determine what a person can become
Shape a person's future possibilities.
returns to the treasury over time
Government receives money back over later years.
covering fewer of their citizens
Providing protection to a smaller share of people.

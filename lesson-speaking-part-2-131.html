Activity

One activity stopped by the weather

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an activity that was interrupted by bad weather.

You should say: When and where it happened Who was involved What the activity was And what you did after the activity was stopped

Simple Model Answer

An activity stopped by the weather was watching a tennis match between Novak Djokovic and Dominic Thiem at the French Open in 2017. Djokovic was my favourite player, and I was very excited to watch the match live.

Unfortunately, I had a mid-term test at the same time. Instead of making a sensible decision, I lied to my father so I could stay home. I knew it was wrong, but I let my interest in tennis become more important than the test.

Then the weather in Paris became so bad that the match did not go ahead as scheduled. I ended up watching old highlights instead of the game I had planned my day around. When my father discovered the truth, he was angry and disappointed. I felt guilty, especially because I had missed the test for nothing. I later took a replacement exam. The experience is memorable because the weather ruined my plan, but it also taught me to take my responsibilities seriously rather than letting excitement decide everything.

Key words and phrases

live
At the same time as an event is actually happening.
replacement exam
Another test arranged for someone who missed the original one.

Advanced Model Answer

Anyone who's a big fan of tennis will know the name Novak Djokovic, and today I want to tell you about the day I skipped school to watch a match between him and Dominic Thiem at the 2017 French Open - a match that, in the end, never actually happened.

I've been hooked on tennis since I first discovered it. My dad and I always turn into total couch potatoes whenever a big tournament's on - Novak's always been my favourite, while dad's more of a Roger Federer man. I love watching him play regardless of the result, and I've always admired his sportsmanship and the way he moves around the court, so powerfully but elegantly.

That particular day was the quarter-final, and Novak needed to beat Thiem to reach the semis. I was desperate to watch it live, especially since he'd lost the French Open final twice in a row before that. Unfortunately, I had a mid-term test scheduled for exactly that time, which meant there was no way to watch it properly. After a fair bit of wrestling with my conscience, I ended up lying to my dad so I could stay home and watch instead of sitting the test.

As it happened, though, the weather in Paris that day turned out to be so bad that the match got cancelled entirely. Rather than the final I'd been hoping for, I just watched old highlights of Novak's matches instead, feeling sorry for myself. I felt incredibly guilty once the truth came out, and I got into big trouble with my dad for skipping the test for nothing - though I did sit a makeup exam a few days later.

Key words and phrases

a big fan of tennis
Someone who enjoys tennis a lot.
wrestling with my conscience
Struggling to decide what is morally right.
turned out to be
Was discovered to be in the end.
got into big trouble
Faced serious consequences for misbehaviour.

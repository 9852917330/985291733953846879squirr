A Fountain Pen from a Former Teacher

Core Story

Prompt

Describe a meaningful gift you received.

You should say: - what the gift was - who gave it to you - when you received it - and explain why it was meaningful.

OUTLINE

Simple Model Answer

A special gift I received was a fountain pen from a former teacher. It was given to me at the end of an important short course. The pen had a dark metal body, a fine tip and a small case. It was simple rather than expensive.

The teacher included a note saying that writing things down helps us think clearly. At the time, I was facing a difficult career decision and felt confused. I began using the pen in a notebook to list my questions, plans and ideas. This helped me see the difference between my own wishes and other people's expectations.

I still keep it on my desk, although I do not use it every day. When a problem is complicated, writing with it helps me slow down. The gift matters because it reminds me of advice I received when I needed it, not because of its price.

Key words and phrases

expectations
Ideas about what someone thinks you should do.
slow down
Take more time and stop rushing.

Advanced Model Answer

One of the most meaningful gifts I have received was a simple fountain pen from a former teacher.

I received it at the end of a short course that had been quite important to me. The pen itself was not expensive or decorative. It had a dark metal body, a fine nib and a small case. What made it special was the note that came with it, which said that clear thinking usually begins with writing things down.

At the time, I was trying to make a difficult career decision and my thoughts were all over the place. I started using the pen to keep a small notebook of questions, plans and ideas. That habit helped me separate what I actually wanted from what I thought other people expected me to do.

I still keep the pen on my desk. I do not use it every day, but whenever I have a complicated problem, I tend to reach for it. The gift is meaningful because it represents much more than the object itself. It reminds me of useful advice given at exactly the right moment, and of the value of slowing down long enough to think clearly.

Situation

A time when you worked in a team

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a time when you worked in a team

You should say: what the activity was who you worked with you how easy or difficult it was And explain how you felt about being part of this team.

Simple Model Answer

A time I worked in a team was for a British and American Culture class at the Foreign Trade University in Hanoi. There were six of us. We had to compare the travel habits of British and American tourists, then produce a report and presentation.

We divided into two groups of three. One group designed a questionnaire, and the other prepared the report outline. Finding enough participants was difficult because we needed fifty people from each country. Eventually, we used Facebook to reach people who were willing to complete the survey online.

Once we had the responses, we compared them and identified the main patterns. Our presentation went well, and we felt pleased when the work was finished. There were a few disagreements, but everyone took responsibility for their part. I was proud not just of the final result, but of how we solved problems together. The experience showed me that dividing tasks is only the beginning of teamwork. People also need to communicate and help each other when one part of the project becomes difficult.

Key words and phrases

questionnaire
A set of questions used to collect information from people.
participants
People who take part in an activity or study.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about a team project I worked on during my 'British and American Culture' class at the Foreign Trade University in Hanoi. There were six of us on the team, and our task was to identify the differences in the travelling habits of British and American tourists. We had to produce a report and give a presentation on our findings.

The first thing we did was split into two groups of three. We had to conduct a survey, so three of us spent time designing the questionnaire while the other three drafted an outline for the report. One of the trickiest parts was finding enough respondents, since we needed fifty British and fifty American participants. Thankfully, Facebook came to the rescue - we managed to reach enough people from both countries who were willing to fill in the online survey for us.

In the end, I think it turned out to be a genuinely successful project, because we were able to work out the main travel patterns of people from the US and the UK, and our presentation went down really well with the class.

Looking back, it was a memorable experience for all of us, and I felt a real sense of accomplishment once we'd handed in the report and delivered the presentation. What I was proudest of, though, was how well we worked together as a team. There were a few minor disagreements along the way, as there always are, but overall everyone showed a good level of responsibility, and I think that's what made the whole thing work.

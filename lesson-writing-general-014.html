Formal

Offering a talk for an international day

Formal

Prompt

A local college is holding an international day, and you would like to give a presentation about your country. Write to the college principal. In your letter: - describe what you would talk about - explain why the students would find it useful - ask about the arrangements for speakers

Model answer

Dear Principal Harris,

I am writing to offer a short presentation about Vietnam for the college's international day in September.

My talk would cover three areas: regional food traditions, the importance of family celebrations, and the rapid changes taking place in major cities such as Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. I would use photographs, a short video and a few everyday objects to bring the subject to life rather than presenting a list of facts.

I believe the session would be useful because it would give students a balanced picture of a country that is often discussed mainly as a tourist destination. It could also encourage students from different backgrounds to compare customs and ask questions about cultural similarities.

Could you please tell me how long each presentation should be and how many students are expected? I would also like to know the technical requirements, particularly whether a projector, speakers and internet access will be available.

I would be pleased to send a brief outline in advance.

Yours sincerely, Alex Morgan

Key words and phrases

the rapid changes taking place in major cities
Fast developments in large cities.
bring the subject to life
Make the topic interesting and real.
give students a balanced picture
Show students different sides fairly.
compare customs and ask questions about cultural similarities
Discuss how cultures are alike or different.
send a brief outline in advance
Share a short plan beforehand.

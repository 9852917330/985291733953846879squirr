Situation

An enjoyable experience you had in the countryside

Part 2

Prompt

Describe an enjoyable experience you had in the countryside.

You should say: When and where you went Who you went with What you did there And explain why you think it was enjoyable.

Simple Model Answer

An enjoyable countryside experience was a family visit to my hometown village in northern Vietnam last summer. My parents, younger sister and I left at about five in the morning and arrived after a four-hour drive.

The village felt very different from the city. There were green rice fields, narrow dirt paths and small houses close together. My parents were excited to see relatives they had not met for a long time. We spent the morning talking, and at noon our relatives served a delicious meal made with local ingredients.

In the afternoon, they showed us the fields and a small lake. My sister and I tried fishing and helped feed chickens, which were new activities for us. I enjoyed being able to take part rather than only looking around. The whole family seemed cheerful and relaxed throughout the day. The trip helped me understand more about rural life and gave my parents time with people they missed. It remains special because we shared simple experiences together without the usual noise and pressure of the city.

Advanced Model Answer

I'd like to talk about last summer, when I had an unforgettable opportunity to visit my hometown - a small village located in the north of Vietnam.

My whole family, my parents, my younger sister and I, decided to spend the day driving out there together. For my parents, it was a chance to see relatives they hadn't seen in ages, and for my sister and me, it was more about getting a closer look at rural life and just spending time together as a family.

We hit the road really early, around 5 a.m., and after about four hours of driving we arrived at this peaceful, tranquil little village. I remember being genuinely surprised by how different the scenery was from where I live - we were surrounded by rolling green paddy fields, winding dirt paths, and small houses standing right next to each other.

Once we arrived, we went to see our relatives, who were incredibly warm and welcoming and greeted us like they hadn't seen us in years, which, to be fair, they hadn't. We spent the whole morning just chatting and reminiscing about old times. At noon we had this amazing home-cooked meal, all made with local, organically grown ingredients - honestly some of the best food I've had in ages. In the afternoon, one of our relatives took us out to see the paddy fields and the village's little lake, and my sister and I even got to try fishing and feeding the chickens, which neither of us had ever done before.

We were all in such high spirits the whole day. It really was a once-in-a-lifetime experience, and it's the kind of memory that's going to stay with me for a long time.

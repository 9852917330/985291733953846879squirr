Object & Favourite

A comedy TV series that you watch

Part 2

Prompt

Describe a comedy TV series that you watch.

You should say: What TV series is it? Who are the main characters there? Describe an interesting event from the series.

Simple Model Answer

A comedy series I enjoy is Friends, an American programme about six friends living in New York. It was made in the 1990s, but I still find it entertaining and often watch episodes again.

Each character has a different personality. Ross is rather awkward, Monica likes everything clean, Chandler makes jokes, Joey is friendly, and Rachel and Phoebe add their own kinds of humour. Their different ways of thinking create many funny situations.

One episode I remember clearly involves Ross trying to move a sofa up a narrow staircase with Rachel and Chandler. He keeps giving instructions, but they cannot get the sofa around the corners. The problem is ordinary, yet the actors make it extremely funny. My friends and I still mention that scene when we move furniture. I like the series because the humour mainly comes from the relationships between the characters. After a difficult day, watching a short episode helps me relax and usually puts me in a better mood.

Key words and phrases

awkward
Uncomfortable or unsure how to behave in social situations.
puts me in a better mood
Makes me feel happier or more relaxed.

Advanced Model Answer

I'm going to talk about Friends, which is probably the comedy series I've rewatched more than anything else in my life. It's an American sitcom from the nineties, set in New York, and even though it finished long before I was watching it properly, it's still one of those shows that never really gets old.

There are six main characters, and the whole show revolves around their friendship group. There's Ross, who's a bit awkward and obsessed with dinosaurs, his sister Monica, who's incredibly tidy and competitive about everything, Chandler, who can't say a sentence without making a joke, Joey, who's not the sharpest but has the biggest heart, and then Rachel and Phoebe, who are both hilarious in completely different ways — Rachel's more sarcastic, and Phoebe's just wonderfully strange.

One episode that really sticks in my mind is the one where Ross tries to move a sofa up a narrow staircase with Rachel and Chandler, and he keeps shouting 'pivot!' over and over while they get it stuck at every single turn. It sounds like such a small, silly thing, but the timing and the way the actors play off each other had me in stitches, and it's become a running joke among my own friends now whenever we're moving furniture.

I think what makes the series so rewatchable is that the humour doesn't really rely on anything dated — it's mostly just how the characters bounce off one another. Whenever I've had a rough day, I put an episode on in the background, and it never fails to cheer me up.
